# 1

- [O Head once full of bruises,]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，滿了傷痕的頭，]{lang="zh"}
- [So full of pain and scorn!]{lang="en"}\
  [滿了痛苦、誶詬，]{lang="zh"}
- [Mid other sore abuses,]{lang="en"}\
  [受盡萬般的試煉，]{lang="zh"}
- [Mocked with a crown of thorn:]{lang="en"}\
  [又戴荊棘冠冕；]{lang="zh"}
- [O Head e’en now surrounded]{lang="en"}\
  [這頭今日已得榮，]{lang="zh"}
- [With brightest majesty,]{lang="en"}\
  [已得聖徒誦詠，]{lang="zh"}
- [In death once bowed and wounded]{lang="en"}\
  [可憐當日受死傷，]{lang="zh"}
- [On the accursed tree:]{lang="en"}\
  [在加略木頭上。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Thou Countenance transcendent!]{lang="en"}\
  [你的面容原超凡，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou life-creating Sun!]{lang="en"}\
  [如同光明太陽，]{lang="zh"}
- [To worlds on Thee dependent]{lang="en"}\
  [父神一見就悅納，]{lang="zh"}
- [Yet bruised and spit upon:]{lang="en"}\
  [竟受罪人唾打。]{lang="zh"}
- [O Lord, what Thee tormented]{lang="en"}\
  [主，你所受的一切，]{lang="zh"}
- [Was our sins’ heavy load,]{lang="en"}\
  [都為我們罪孽；]{lang="zh"}
- [We had the debt augmented]{lang="en"}\
  [我們債務得清付，]{lang="zh"}
- [Which Thou didst pay in blood.]{lang="en"}\
  [你卻被人剪除。]{lang="zh"}
