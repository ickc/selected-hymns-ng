# 1

- [Oh, who’s like our Jesus? He’s all we desire!]{lang="en"}\
  [誰能像我耶穌？我全心愛慕！]{lang="zh"}
- [His love draws us near sets our spirits afire!]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的愛吸引我，將我靈燒著！]{lang="zh"}
- [We cannot but love Him As one we proclaim: “Lord]{lang="en"}\
  [我不能不愛祂，要宣告這話：]{lang="zh"}
- [Jesus, we love Thee exalt Thy dear name!”]{lang="en"}\
  [“主耶穌，我愛你，將你名高舉！”]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [O Jesus, our Jesus, with Thee we are blest—]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌，我的耶穌，我因你蒙福－]{lang="zh"}
- [Our Life, our Enjoyment, our Peace and our Rest:]{lang="en"}\
  [我的生命、供給，惟你是安息：]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy name be our theme, and Thy love be our song;]{lang="en"}\
  [你名是我題目，我愛的歌賦；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy love has inspired both our heart and our tongue!]{lang="en"}\
  [你愛感動我心，使我口誦吟。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [O Jesus, our Jesus, how can we express]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌，我的耶穌，我不能盡述]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy dearness, so precious, and Thy loveliness?]{lang="en"}\
  [你的可愛、寶貴、並你的甜美。]{lang="zh"}
- [When we see Thy beauty, all else fades away—]{lang="en"}\
  [當我見你美麗，一切都消跡——]{lang="zh"}
- [All shadows must flee at the breaking of day!]{lang="en"}\
  [猶如每晨破曉，陰影都竄逃。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [We love Thee, we love Thee, we love Thee, O Lord.]{lang="en"}\
  [我愛你，我愛你，哦主，我愛你。]{lang="zh"}
- [We love Thee, our Jesus, we love Thee, our God!]{lang="en"}\
  [主耶穌，我愛你，我神，我愛你！]{lang="zh"}
- [We love Thee, we love Thee, and that Thou dost know;]{lang="en"}\
  [我愛你，我愛你，你知我情意；]{lang="zh"}
- [As we’re in Thy presence, love for Thee will grow!]{lang="en"}\
  [你同在的面光，使我愛增長。]{lang="zh"}
