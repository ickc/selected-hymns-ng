# 1

- [His be the Victor’s name,]{lang="en"}\
  [得勝之名屬祂，]{lang="zh"}
- [Who fought the fight alone;]{lang="en"}\
  [惟祂獨自戰爭；]{lang="zh"}
- [Triumphant saints no honor claim,]{lang="en"}\
  [凱歌聖徒無何可誇，]{lang="zh"}
- [His conquest was their own.]{lang="en"}\
  [只誇祂的得勝。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [By weakness and defeat,]{lang="en"}\
  [似乎軟弱、失敗，]{lang="zh"}
- [He won a glorious crown,]{lang="en"}\
  [卻得榮耀冠冕；]{lang="zh"}
- [Trod all our foes beneath His feet,]{lang="en"}\
  [藉被仇敵踐踏、佈擺，]{lang="zh"}
- [By being trodden down.]{lang="en"}\
  [反將仇敵踏踐。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [He Satan’s power laid low;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂把撒但征服：]{lang="zh"}
- [Made sin, He sin o’erthrew,]{lang="en"}\
  [成罪，使罪失勢；]{lang="zh"}
- [Bowed to the grave, destroyed it so,]{lang="en"}\
  [進入墳墓，將它消除；]{lang="zh"}
- [And death by dying slew.]{lang="en"}\
  [被殺，卻敗壞死。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Bless, bless the Conquer’r slain,]{lang="en"}\
  [讚祂！為我死過——]{lang="zh"}
- [Slain in His victory;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂得勝的命斃；]{lang="zh"}
- [Who lived, Who died, Who lives again,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂曾活著，死過又活；]{lang="zh"}
- [For thee, His church, for thee!]{lang="en"}\
  [教會，祂今為妳。]{lang="zh"}
