# 1

- [My God, how wonderful Thou art,]{lang="en"}\
  [神阿，你是何等奇妙！]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy majesty how bright,]{lang="en"}\
  [何等威嚴莊重！]{lang="zh"}
- [How beautiful Thy mercy seat,]{lang="en"}\
  [施恩寶座純潔明耀，]{lang="zh"}
- [In depths of burning light.]{lang="en"}\
  [無限光明之中。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [How dread are Thine eternal years,]{lang="en"}\
  [永遠的神，何等尊貴，]{lang="zh"}
- [O everlasting Lord,]{lang="en"}\
  [諸天是你座位；]{lang="zh"}
- [By prostrate spirits day and night]{lang="en"}\
  [眾靈你前晝夜環跪，]{lang="zh"}
- [Incessantly adored!]{lang="en"}\
  [不停、不住讚美。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [How wonderful, how beautiful,]{lang="en"}\
  [何等奇妙，何等美麗！]{lang="zh"}
- [The sight of Thee must be,]{lang="en"}\
  [在你聖潔光中；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thine endless wisdom, boundless power,]{lang="en"}\
  [無邊智慧，無限能力，]{lang="zh"}
- [And awful purity!]{lang="en"}\
  [並你榮耀無窮。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Oh, how I fear Thee, living God,]{lang="en"}\
  [永生神前，我心敬畏，]{lang="zh"}
- [With deepest, tenderest fears,]{lang="en"}\
  [惟恐任意妄為；]{lang="zh"}
- [And worship Thee with trembling hope]{lang="en"}\
  [戰兢、盼望，向你拜跪，]{lang="zh"}
- [And penitential tears!]{lang="en"}\
  [並帶悔改眼淚。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Yet I may love Thee, too, O Lord,]{lang="en"}\
  [親愛的主，我深愛你，]{lang="zh"}
- [Almighty as Thou art,]{lang="en"}\
  [雖然你是主宰；]{lang="zh"}
- [For Thou hast stooped to ask of me]{lang="en"}\
  [因你降卑向我索取，]{lang="zh"}
- [The love of my poor heart.]{lang="en"}\
  [我這不配的愛。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [No earthly father loves like Thee;]{lang="en"}\
  [地上無父如此慈愛，]{lang="zh"}
- [No mother, e’er so mild]{lang="en"}\
  [無母如此仁慈，]{lang="zh"}
- [Bears and forbears as Thou hast done]{lang="en"}\
  [像你這樣背負、忍耐：]{lang="zh"}
- [With me, Thy sinful child.]{lang="en"}\
  [你的污穢孩子。]{lang="zh"}

# 7

- [Father of Jesus, love’s reward,]{lang="en"}\
  [主的慈父，因你愛賞，]{lang="zh"}
- [What rapture will it be]{lang="en"}\
  [使我歡天喜地，]{lang="zh"}
- [Prostrate before Thy throne to lie,]{lang="en"}\
  [在你座前俯伏頌揚，]{lang="zh"}
- [And gaze, and gaze on Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [我得瞻仰於你。]{lang="zh"}
