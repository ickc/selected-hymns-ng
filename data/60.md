# 1

- [Jesus, the name high over all,]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌這名超乎萬名，]{lang="zh"}
- [In hell, or earth, or sky:]{lang="en"}\
  [天上、地上、陰間：]{lang="zh"}
- [Angels and men before it fall,]{lang="en"}\
  [天使和人無不尊敬，]{lang="zh"}
- [And devils fear and fly.]{lang="en"}\
  [魔鬼懼怕、逃竄。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Jesus, the name to sinners dear,]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌這名可親、可愛，]{lang="zh"}
- [The name to sinners giv’n;]{lang="en"}\
  [賜給罪人之名；]{lang="zh"}
- [It scatters all their guilty fear,]{lang="en"}\
  [定罪、懼怕，為我趕開，]{lang="zh"}
- [It turns their hell to heav’n.]{lang="en"}\
  [帶來天上安寧。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Jesus the prisoner’s fetters breaks,]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌打碎罪囚枷鎖，]{lang="zh"}
- [And bruises Satan’s head;]{lang="en"}\
  [打傷撒但的頭；]{lang="zh"}
- [Pow’r into strengthless souls He speaks,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂用大能吞滅軟弱，]{lang="zh"}
- [And life into the dead.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂用生命拯救。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Oh, that the world might taste and see,]{lang="en"}\
  [但願世人都能嘗到]{lang="zh"}
- [The riches of His grace!]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的豐富恩典！]{lang="zh"}
- [The arms of love that compass me,]{lang="en"}\
  [都能享受祂的懷抱，]{lang="zh"}
- [Would all mankind embrace.]{lang="en"}\
  [投在祂的胸間。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [His only righteousness I show,]{lang="en"}\
  [我願彰顯祂的公義，]{lang="zh"}
- [His saving truth proclaim:]{lang="en"}\
  [把祂救恩傳揚；]{lang="zh"}
- [’Tis all my business here below,]{lang="en"}\
  [一生一世，只此信息：]{lang="zh"}
- [To cry, Behold the Lamb!]{lang="en"}\
  [“看哪，神的羔羊！”]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [Happy, if with my latest breath]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌這名永遠可靠，]{lang="zh"}
- [I may but gasp His name:]{lang="en"}\
  [夠我度過今生；]{lang="zh"}
- [Preach Him to all, and cry in death,]{lang="en"}\
  [直到永世可愛、可寶，]{lang="zh"}
- [“Behold, behold the Lamb!”]{lang="en"}\
  [永遠是我見證。]{lang="zh"}
