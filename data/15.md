# 2

- [Thy mercy found us in our sins,]{lang="en"}\
  [我在罪中，你愛來尋，]{lang="zh"}
- [And gave us to believe;]{lang="en"}\
  [感動我心相信；]{lang="zh"}
- [Then, in believing, peace we found,]{lang="en"}\
  [叫我因信得著安樂，]{lang="zh"}

  [因著基督得活。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [All that we are, as saints on earth,]{lang="en"}\
  [我為聖徒，現在所享，]{lang="zh"}
- [All that we hope to be,]{lang="en"}\
  [和我將來盼望，]{lang="zh"}
- [When Jesus comes and glory dawns,]{lang="en"}\
  [無一是我自己配得，]{lang="zh"}
- [We owe it all to Thee.]{lang="en"}\

# 4

- [O God, how rich, how vast Thy love,]{lang="en"}\
  [神，你這樣豐富的愛，]{lang="zh"}
- [Whoe’er can Thee repay?]{lang="en"}\
  [誰能還清這債？]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy love is past man’s finding out,]{lang="en"}\
  [你愛真是遠超人意，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy grace no man can say.]{lang="en"}\
  [神聖！不可思議！]{lang="zh"}

  [哦父，但願你今使我，]{lang="zh"}
- [More clearly may I see,]{lang="en"}\
  [時刻明白更多：]{lang="zh"}
- [That I may e’er more fully know]{lang="en"}\
  [像你這樣慈愛的神，]{lang="zh"}
