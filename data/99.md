# 2

- [Lo! Jesus meets us, risen from the tomb;]{lang="en"}\
  [看！復活基督，與我們相見；]{lang="zh"}
- [Lovingly He greets us, scatters fear and gloom;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂那可愛問安，驅盡懼與暗；]{lang="zh"}
- [Let the Church with gladness, hymns of triumph sing,]{lang="en"}\
  [但願教會歡樂，高唱得勝詩，]{lang="zh"}
- [For her Lord now liveth, death hath lost its sting.]{lang="en"}\
  [因主現在活著，死已失權勢。]{lang="zh"}

  [無你就無生命，有你勝死亡。]{lang="zh"}
- [Make us more than conjurors, through Thy deathless love;]{lang="en"}\
