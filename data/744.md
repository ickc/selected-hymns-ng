# 1

| What shall I give unto the Lord
| 我拿甚麼來報答神，
| For all, for all, for all He’s done for me?
| 向我，向我所賜一切厚恩？
| I’ll take the cup of salvation,
| 我要舉起救恩的杯，
| And call, and call, and call upon the name of the Lord.
| 稱揚，稱揚，稱揚我神耶和華的名。
