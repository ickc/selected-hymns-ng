# 1

- [Can you be obedient To the Lord of all,]{lang="en"}\
  [你們能否順從 你一切的主，]{lang="zh"}
- [Though the earth should totter, Though the heav’ns should fall?]{lang="en"}\
  [如果地要震動， 天象要翻覆？]{lang="zh"}
- [Face e’en a disaster With a faith-filled heart,]{lang="en"}\
  [你們能否相信， 神與你同在，]{lang="zh"}
- [Knowing naught can harm him Who with Christ will start?]{lang="en"}\
  [就是災禍忽臨， 必定不遭害？]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Can you be obedient To the Lord you serve,]{lang="en"}\
  [你們能否順從　　　所事奉的主，]{lang="zh"}
- [Never even flinch, friend, Never even swerve;]{lang="en"}\
  [不稍軟弱、驚恐，　也不稍讓步？]{lang="zh"}
- [Though your next step onward Seem to lead to death?]{lang="en"}\
  [雖然你的前途　　　好像是死路，]{lang="zh"}
- [Can you then obey Him Without bated breath?]{lang="en"}\
  [此時能否順服，　　而不一自顧？]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Can you trust your Leader When He bids you go]{lang="en"}\
  [你真能否順從，　　如果主召你]{lang="zh"}
- [Right into a battle With a mighty foe?]{lang="en"}\
  [加入前線進攻，　　抵禦凶仇敵？]{lang="zh"}
- [Can you step up briskly And with joy obey?]{lang="en"}\
  [你真能否立前，　　歡喜受差遣？]{lang="zh"}
- [Can you fight the battle, Till the end of day?]{lang="en"}\
  [你真能否爭戰，　　直到晚色遍？]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Can you? Then beloved, Christ just waits for you;]{lang="en"}\
  [能否？我的弟兄，　你神已久等，]{lang="zh"}
- [Listen for His orders, Glad His will to do;]{lang="en"}\
  [應當服祂權柄，　　遵行祂命令。]{lang="zh"}
- [Then when soldiers muster At the set of sun,]{lang="en"}\
  [你若作主精兵，　　當祂再降臨，]{lang="zh"}
- [And your name is mentioned, Christ will say, “Well done.”]{lang="en"}\
  [祂要題起你名，　　並說你“忠心！”]{lang="zh"}
