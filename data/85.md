# 1

- [Crowned with thorns upon the tree,]{lang="en"}\
  [頭戴荊冕釘十架，]{lang="zh"}
- [Silent in Thine agony;]{lang="en"}\
  [甘受痛苦無怨詞；]{lang="zh"}
- [Dying, crushed beneath the load]{lang="en"}\
  [神的忿怒和刑罰，]{lang="zh"}
- [Of the wrath and curse of God.]{lang="en"}\
  [將你壓碎以至死。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [On Thy pale and suff’ring brow,]{lang="en"}\
  [你的蒼白、受苦額，]{lang="zh"}
- [Mystery of love and woe;]{lang="en"}\
  [顯出愛、憂的奧祕；]{lang="zh"}
- [On Thy grief and sore amaze,]{lang="en"}\
  [你的憂、痛，我驚愕，]{lang="zh"}
- [Savior, I would fix my gaze!]{lang="en"}\
  [救主，我今注目你！]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [On Thy holy, loving breast]{lang="en"}\
  [主，你聖、愛的胸懷，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou dost bid the weary rest;]{lang="en"}\
  [是疲倦者的安息；]{lang="zh"}
- [Rest there from the world’s false ways,]{lang="en"}\
  [此世虛偽我拋開，]{lang="zh"}
- [Rest there from its vanities.]{lang="en"}\
  [從它空虛轉向你。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Rest in pardon and relief,]{lang="en"}\
  [脫下罪、愁的重擔，]{lang="zh"}
- [From the load of guilt and grief;]{lang="en"}\
  [得著赦免和釋放；]{lang="zh"}
- [Rest in Thy redeeming blood,]{lang="en"}\
  [因你寶血心安然，]{lang="zh"}
- [Rest in perfect peace with God.]{lang="en"}\
  [在神平安裏歡暢。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

  [在我眼中價無計；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou alone my rest shall be,]{lang="en"}\
  [惟有你是我安息，]{lang="zh"}
