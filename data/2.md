# 1

| Come, Thou Almighty King,
| 全能君王我神！
| Help us Thy name to sing,
| 教我頌讚你名，
| Help us to praise.
| 頌讚不停：
| Father, all glorious,
| 讚美父神尊榮，
| O’er all victorious,
| 讚你超越得勝，
| Come, and reign over us,
| 讚你權能統治，
| Ancient of Days.
| 直到永世。

# 2

| Come, Thou incarnate Word,
| 神子成了肉身！
| Gird on Thy mighty sword,
| 顯出無限權能，
| Our prayer attend:
| 聽我呼聲：
| Come, and Thy people bless,
| 賜你子民福分，
| And give Thy word success;
| 使你應許得成——
| Spirit of holiness,
| 降下聖潔之靈，
| On us descend.
| 使我成聖。

# 3

| Come, holy Comforter,
| 保惠之靈降臨！
| Thy sacred witness bear
| 負起神聖見證，
| In this glad hour:
| 在此良辰：
| Thou who Almighty art,
| 以你生命大能，
| Now rule in every heart,
| 在我心中執政，
| And ne’er from us depart,
| 永遠住我裏面，
| Spirit of power.
| 運行、成全。

# 4

| To Thee, great One in Three,
| 大哉，三一之神！
| Eternal praises be
| 永遠配得稱頌，
| Hence evermore.
| 永世無終！
| Thy sov’reign majesty
| 直到榮耀之中，
| May we in glory see,
| 瞻你無上尊榮，
| And to eternity
| 獻上愛戴、尊敬，
| Love and adore.
| 永無止境！
