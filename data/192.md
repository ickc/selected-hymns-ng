# 1

- [Lord, Thou didst know when in the flesh,]{lang="en"}\
  [當你住在肉身生命，]{lang="zh"}
- [As such I’d know Thee shallowly;]{lang="en"}\
  [知我識你至不深刻，]{lang="zh"}
- [Then as the Spirit Thou didst come]{lang="en"}\
  [你就定規住在聖靈，]{lang="zh"}
- [Within my spirit, known to be.]{lang="en"}\
  [要住我靈啟示明白。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [In flesh Redeemer mine Thou art;]{lang="en"}\
  [從前你是我救贖主，]{lang="zh"}
- [As Spirit now my Comforter.]{lang="en"}\
  [現今你是我保惠師，]{lang="zh"}
- [The outward touch has passed away]{lang="en"}\
  [不再是那外面接觸，]{lang="zh"}
- [For inward union worthier.]{lang="en"}\
  [乃是裏面合一不弛。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Because Thou art the Spirit now,]{lang="en"}\
  [我今因你所成聖靈，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thyself revealing ceaselessly,]{lang="en"}\
  [住在靈裏不斷啟示]{lang="zh"}
- [Within my spirit I may know]{lang="en"}\
  [你的自己、你的性情，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy presence and reality.]{lang="en"}\
  [覺你同在，感你真實。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [I know Thy life surpassing far]{lang="en"}\
  [我能認識你的生命，]{lang="zh"}
- [The knowledge Thy disciples had,]{lang="en"}\
  [遠超當日門徒經歷；]{lang="zh"}
- [Although they walked and lived with Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [雖然他們與你同行、]{lang="zh"}
- [When Thou on earth in flesh wast clad.]{lang="en"}\
  [同食、同住、同勞、同息。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Because Thy Spirit dwells within,]{lang="en"}\
  [哦主，因著內住聖靈，]{lang="zh"}
- [How real, O Lord, Thou art to me;]{lang="en"}\
  [你是何等實在、實在！]{lang="zh"}
- [Not touched, yet more reliable,]{lang="en"}\
  [不摸，比摸更為可憑；]{lang="zh"}
- [Not seen, yet loved more fervently.]{lang="en"}\
  [不見，但已心中深愛。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [Thy Holy Spirit deep within]{lang="en"}\
  [聖靈現今是從衷裏，]{lang="zh"}
- [Supplies Thyself as righteousness,]{lang="en"}\
  [時刻將你供應給我，]{lang="zh"}
- [As holiness, redemption full,]{lang="en"}\
  [作我救贖、聖潔、公義，]{lang="zh"}
- [As wisdom and as fruitfulness.]{lang="en"}\
  [作我智慧和我結果。]{lang="zh"}

# 7

- [Thy Spirit in my spirit now]{lang="en"}\
  [因你的靈，住我靈裏，]{lang="zh"}
- [Supplies Thyself, unites with me,]{lang="en"}\
  [將你供應，與我合一，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thus I am all the time myself]{lang="en"}\
  [時時刻刻是我自己，]{lang="zh"}
- [And constantly am also Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [時時刻刻也都是你。]{lang="zh"}
