# 1

  [坐在我主耶穌腳前，]{lang="zh"}

  [聽祂向我說美言！]{lang="zh"}
- [Happy place—so near, so precious!]{lang="en"}\
  [與主親近，喜樂之源，]{lang="zh"}
- [May it find me there each day!]{lang="en"}\
  [天天願在此流連！]{lang="zh"}
- [Sitting at the feet of Jesus,]{lang="en"}\
  [坐在我主耶穌腳前，]{lang="zh"}
- [I may look upon the past,]{lang="en"}\
  [回首看見我既往，]{lang="zh"}
- [For His love has been so gracious—]{lang="en"}\
  [祂愛寶貴而且甘甜——]{lang="zh"}

  [贏得我心來欣賞。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Sitting at the feet of Jesus,]{lang="en"}\
  [坐在我主耶穌腳前，]{lang="zh"}
- [Where can mortals be more blest?]{lang="en"}\
  [蒙恩之人何有福！]{lang="zh"}
- [There I lay my sins and sorrows,]{lang="en"}\
  [在此卸下憂慮、罪愆；]{lang="zh"}
- [And, when weary, find sweet rest,]{lang="en"}\
  [疲心在此蒙安撫。]{lang="zh"}
- [Sitting at the feet of Jesus,]{lang="en"}\
  [坐在我主耶穌腳前，]{lang="zh"}
- [There I love to weep and pray,]{lang="en"}\
  [向祂傾訴並流淚，]{lang="zh"}
- [While I from His fullness gather]{lang="en"}\
  [天天從祂領受豐滿，]{lang="zh"}
- [Grace and comfort every day.]{lang="en"}\
  [得祂恩典和安慰。]{lang="zh"}
