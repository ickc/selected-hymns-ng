# 1

- [Our goal—the holy city with the Lord,]{lang="en"}\
  [聖城同救主是我們目標，]{lang="zh"}
- [It is our portion, ’tis where He’s adored;]{lang="en"}\
  [我們的永分，燦爛又榮耀；]{lang="zh"}
- [Although our bodies still are living here,]{lang="en"}\
  [我身雖在此，靈卻已在彼，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our spirits with our glorious Lord are there.]{lang="en"}\
  [深望與恩主立即在一起。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Our goal divine—the new Jerusalem—]{lang="en"}\
  [神聖的目標，新耶路撒冷，]{lang="zh"}
- [That holy city made for God with men;]{lang="en"}\
  [乃是神為人豫備的聖城；]{lang="zh"}
- [For all redeemed, who have Thy pathway trod,]{lang="en"}\
  [所有蒙寶血救贖的罪人，]{lang="zh"}
- [Shall have the right to share the bliss with God.]{lang="en"}\
  [都能有權利享受這福分。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [And every time we toward that city gaze,]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，那座聖城，我每一瞻仰，]{lang="zh"}
- [Then every time we long to see Thy face;]{lang="en"}\
  [我心渴想見恩主的面光；]{lang="zh"}
- [We long to hear the shout that calls to Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [巴不得早日聽見祂呼聲]{lang="zh"}
- [With Thee to share Thy God eternally.]{lang="en"}\
  [得進入那城，永享祂豐盛。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Our heart’s desire is not the peace so sweet,]{lang="en"}\
  [我心所渴慕，並非甜安息，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our longed for hope is not the joy we’ll meet;]{lang="en"}\
  [我久所盼望，也非何福氣；]{lang="zh"}
- [But Thou Thyself, Thou art “our hope,” O Lord,]{lang="en"}\
  [乃是主自己——我榮耀盼望，]{lang="zh"}
- [For ’tis Thyself that will be our reward.]{lang="en"}\
  [就是我永分，作我的獎賞。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [O Jesus, Lord, we pray Thee hear our cry, Come!]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，求主耶穌！快照我心願，]{lang="zh"}
- [Bring us to that city from on high!]{lang="en"}\
  [來接愛你者到你的面前！]{lang="zh"}
- [That in that glory we may share with Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [在那聖城中享受你自己，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy loving Self partake eternally .]{lang="en"}\
  [同住並同榮，永不再分離。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [O Lord, Thou sayest, “I make all things new,”]{lang="en"}\
  [那時的一切要更新完成，]{lang="zh"}
- [New heav’ns, new earth, and all within them too!]{lang="en"}\
  [新天並新地、新耶路撒冷！]{lang="zh"}
- [Our God Himself our portion then shall be;]{lang="en"}\
  [神榮耀自己要作我永分；]{lang="zh"}
- [O Lord, come quickly! Take us up with Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [主，願你快來！與我永不分！]{lang="zh"}
