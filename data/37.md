# 1

- [O God our Father, we would come to Thee]{lang="en"}\
  [靠著我們救主流血作祭，]{lang="zh"}
- [In virtue of our Savior’s precious blood;]{lang="en"}\
  [父阿，我們前來一同就你；]{lang="zh"}
- [All distance gone, our souls by grace set free,]{lang="en"}\
  [距離已去，藉恩已得自由，]{lang="zh"}
- [We worship Thee, our Father and our God.]{lang="en"}\
  [前來敬拜向你讚美、歌謳。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [We would, O God, present before Thy face]{lang="en"}\
  [哦父，我們願在你前獻上]{lang="zh"}
- [The fragrant name of Thy beloved Son;]{lang="en"}\
  [你的愛子之名所有馨香；]{lang="zh"}
- [By faith we view Him through Thy boundless grace,]{lang="en"}\
  [藉著祂死為我所得恩賞，]{lang="zh"}
- [Which, by His dying, He for us has won.]{lang="en"}\
  [在此憑信我們將祂瞻仰。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Thy joy in Him who is with Thee we share;]{lang="en"}\
  [你的喜樂我今與祂同享，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our hearts delight in Thy delight in Him;]{lang="en"}\
  [在祂裏面我心因你歡暢；]{lang="zh"}
- [Chiefest of thousands, fairer than the fair;]{lang="en"}\
  [萬人之上，祂比美者更美，]{lang="zh"}
- [His glory nought can tarnish, nought can dim.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的榮耀永不衰落敗褪。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [We bow in worship now before Thy throne,]{lang="en"}\
  [在你座前我們俯伏仰望，]{lang="zh"}
- [By faith the Object of Thy love would see;]{lang="en"}\
  [憑信看見你愛所有對象；]{lang="zh"}
- [Who, in the midst, His brethren’s song doth lead.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂今在此率領許多弟兄—]{lang="zh"}
- [To Him, our Savior, shall the glory be!]{lang="en"}\
  [你的眾子，同聲向你歌頌。]{lang="zh"}
