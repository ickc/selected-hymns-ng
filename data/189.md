# 1

- [We have come, we have come to the house of God;]{lang="en"}\
  [我們來，我們來，到神殿裏來；]{lang="zh"}
- [We have come to the house, whence out flows the flood.]{lang="en"}\
  [我們來，因神殿有水流出來。]{lang="zh"}
- [On the right, day and night, constant is its flow,]{lang="en"}\
  [這水流，在殿右，晝夜湧流著，]{lang="zh"}
- [Watering us and causing fruits of life to grow.]{lang="en"}\
  [滋潤我們，結出生命的美果。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [From the house, from the house flows this living stream,]{lang="en"}\
  [從這殿，從這殿，湧出這水流，]{lang="zh"}
- [From the house, to the earth, with the life supreme.]{lang="en"}\
  [從這殿到全地，生命在湧流，]{lang="zh"}
- [Yet more deep, Lord, we seek that the flow may be;]{lang="en"}\
  [願這水流加深，主，聽我們求，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thus we must be measured and possessed by Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [我們就必被你量過並佔有。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Measure us, measure us, measure every day;]{lang="en"}\
  [量我們，量我們，天天量又量，]{lang="zh"}
- [Measure us, measure more, measure all the way,]{lang="en"}\
  [量我們，量更多，一直量又量，]{lang="zh"}
- [Till we know that the flow is a mighty flood,]{lang="en"}\
  [直等到我們見水流成江河，]{lang="zh"}
- [Sweeping over all the earth for Christ the Lord.]{lang="en"}\
  [流通全地，使人全被主點活。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Take us through, take us through, take us through the flow;]{lang="en"}\
  [領我們，領我們，從水流經過，]{lang="zh"}
- [Take us through, through and through, everywhere we go.]{lang="en"}\
  [領我們從水流，經過再經過，]{lang="zh"}
- [Flow increase, never cease, till we swim in Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [水流漲，不可洑，成可䞶的水，]{lang="zh"}
- [Till we are immersed in God eternally.]{lang="en"}\
  [我們被浸透，永遠享神恩惠。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [All shall live, all shall live where the river comes;]{lang="en"}\
  [都必活，都必活，一切都必活，]{lang="zh"}
- [All shall live, really live, everywhere it runs.]{lang="en"}\
  [都必活，真正活，百物隨流活，]{lang="zh"}
- [Let the fount from this mount life abundant bring,]{lang="en"}\
  [願這水流帶來豐盛的生命，]{lang="zh"}
- [Till the deserts of the earth with churches spring!]{lang="en"}\
  [使教會在荒漠地一一顯明。]{lang="zh"}
