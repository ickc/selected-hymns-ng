# 1

- [Master, speak! Thy servant heareth,]{lang="en"}\
  [我主，請說，僕人敬聽，]{lang="zh"}
- [Waiting for Thy gracious word,]{lang="en"}\
  [我今等候你恩言；]{lang="zh"}
- [Longing for Thy voice that cheereth,]{lang="en"}\
  [我心渴望聞你慈聲，]{lang="zh"}
- [Master, let it now be heard.]{lang="en"}\
  [我願現在就聽見。]{lang="zh"}
- [I am list’ning, Lord, for Thee;]{lang="en"}\
  [哦主，我今正傾聽，]{lang="zh"}
- [What hast Thou to say to me?]{lang="en"}\
  [是否就向我發言？]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Often through my heart is pealing]{lang="en"}\
  [我主，請說，僕人敬聽；]{lang="zh"}
- [Many another voice than Thine,]{lang="en"}\
  [雜音常侵入我心，]{lang="zh"}
- [Many an unwilled echo stealing]{lang="en"}\
  [你從聖所所發命令，]{lang="zh"}
- [From the walls of this Thy shrine.]{lang="en"}\
  [竟不能清晰聽聞。]{lang="zh"}
- [Let Thy longed-for accents fall;]{lang="en"}\
  [求主今向我發聲，]{lang="zh"}

  [其他雜音使寂沉。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

  [讓我得聞你微聲；]{lang="zh"}
- [Speak! for oh, Thou knowest]{lang="en"}\
  [鑒察我的渴慕心情，]{lang="zh"}
- [All the yearning of my heart.]{lang="en"}\
