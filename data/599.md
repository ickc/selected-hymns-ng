# 1

- [Praise the Savior, ye who know Him!]{lang="en"}\
  [蒙恩群眾，將主頌揚！]{lang="zh"}
- [Who can tell how much we owe Him?]{lang="en"}\
  [其恩何深，其愛何厚；]{lang="zh"}
- [Gladly let us render to Him]{lang="en"}\
  [無以圖報，]{lang="zh"}
- [All we are and have.]{lang="en"}\
  [甘心獻上所是並所有。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Jesus is the name that charms us;]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌聖名，將我吸引；]{lang="zh"}
- [He for conflicts fits and arms us;]{lang="en"}\
  [賜我裝備，戰勝敵軍；]{lang="zh"}
- [Nothing moves and nothing harms us]{lang="en"}\
  [作我保護，不受傷損；]{lang="zh"}
- [When we trust in Him.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂有信心。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Trust in Him, ye saints, forever;]{lang="en"}\
  [願眾聖徒投靠救主，]{lang="zh"}
- [He is faithful, changing never;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的信實今仍如初，]{lang="zh"}
- [Neither force nor guile can sever]{lang="en"}\
  [不論強暴或是騙術，]{lang="zh"}
- [Those He loves from Him.]{lang="en"}\
  [不能隔絕主。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Keep us, Lord, oh, keep us cleaving]{lang="en"}\
  [求我救主容我親近，]{lang="zh"}
- [To Thyself and still believing,]{lang="en"}\
  [永不疏遠，永不失信，]{lang="zh"}
- [Till the hour of our receiving]{lang="en"}\
  [等我得了主所應允，]{lang="zh"}
- [Promised joys with Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂是我永分。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Then we shall be where we would be;]{lang="en"}\
  [那時到我所愛樂地，]{lang="zh"}
- [Then we shall be what we should be;]{lang="en"}\
  [那時我得全然更新；]{lang="zh"}
- [Things which are not now, nor could be,]{lang="en"}\
  [世間所無全備福氣，]{lang="zh"}
- [Then shall be our own.]{lang="en"}\
  [全都歸我們。]{lang="zh"}
