# 1

- [Deep down into the depths of this Thy Name,]{lang="en"}\
  [神阿，你名何等廣大泱漭！]{lang="zh"}
- [My God, I sink and dwell in calm delight;]{lang="en"}\
  [我今投身其中，心頂安然；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou art enough how ever long the day,]{lang="en"}\
  [有你夠了，無論日有多長；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou art enough however dark the night.]{lang="en"}\
  [有你夠了，無論夜有多暗。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Thou art my God—the All-Sufficient One,]{lang="en"}\
  [你是我神！全有！全足！全豐！]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou canst create for me whate’er I lack;]{lang="en"}\
  [你能為我創造我所缺乏；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy mighty hand has strewn the lonely track]{lang="en"}\
  [有你自己，在我回家途中，]{lang="zh"}
- [With miracles of love and tender care.]{lang="en"}\
  [無論有何需要，都必無差。]{lang="zh"}
