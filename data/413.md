# 1

- [Gracious Spirit, Holy Ghost,]{lang="en"}\
  [恩惠聖靈我導師，]{lang="zh"}
- [Taught by You, we covet most,]{lang="en"}\
  [感動我心來等待，]{lang="zh"}
- [Of Your gifts at Pentecost,]{lang="en"}\
  [五旬所賜的恩賜：]{lang="zh"}
- [Holy heavenly love.]{lang="en"}\
  [屬天的聖愛。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Faith that mountains could remove,]{lang="en"}\
  [雖有移山的信心、]{lang="zh"}
- [Tongues of earth or heaven above,]{lang="en"}\
  [知識、萬人的方言，]{lang="zh"}
- [Knowledge, all things, empty prove]{lang="en"}\
  [卻無屬天的愛心，]{lang="zh"}
- [Without heavenly love.]{lang="en"}\
  [一切仍枉然。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Love is kind, and suffers long,]{lang="en"}\
  [雖我殉道並捨己，]{lang="zh"}
- [Love is meek, and thinks no wrong,]{lang="en"}\
  [一切為窮人傾倒，]{lang="zh"}
- [Love than death itself more strong:]{lang="en"}\
  [若是無愛仍空虛；]{lang="zh"}
- [Therefore give us love.]{lang="en"}\
  [“愛”是我所要。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Though I as a martyr bleed,]{lang="en"}\
  [愛是溫柔、肯原諒，]{lang="zh"}
- [Give my goods the poor to feed,]{lang="en"}\
  [愛是仁慈、常忍耐，]{lang="zh"}
- [All is vain if love I need;]{lang="en"}\
  [這愛比死更堅強；]{lang="zh"}
- [Therefore give me love.]{lang="en"}\
  [主，賜我這愛。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Prophecy will fade away]{lang="en"}\
  [講道之能和知識，]{lang="zh"}
- [Melting in the light of day;]{lang="en"}\
  [有日終必歸無有；]{lang="zh"}
- [Love will ever with us stay:]{lang="en"}\
  [惟有愛永無終日；]{lang="zh"}
- [Therefore give us love.]{lang="en"}\
  [“愛”是我所求。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [Faith, and hope, and love we see]{lang="en"}\
  [如今主裡所常存，]{lang="zh"}
- [Joining hand in hand, agree;]{lang="en"}\
  [有信，有望，並有愛，]{lang="zh"}
- [But the greatest of the three,]{lang="en"}\
  [有如三股合成繩，]{lang="zh"}
- [And the best, is love.]{lang="en"}\
  [但最大是愛。]{lang="zh"}
