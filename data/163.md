# 1

- [Praise Him! praise Him! Jesus our blessed Redeemer!]{lang="en"}\
  [讚美！讚美！讚美耶穌，我救贖主！]{lang="zh"}
- [Sing, ye saints! His wonderful love proclaim!]{lang="en"}\
  [聖徒歌唱，將祂奇愛宣明；]{lang="zh"}
- [Hail Him! hail Him Mightiest angels in glory;]{lang="en"}\
  [稱頌！稱頌！稱頌基督，天使俯伏；]{lang="zh"}
- [Strength and honor give to His holy name!]{lang="en"}\
  [能力、尊貴，歸於祂的聖名！]{lang="zh"}
- [Like a shepherd, Jesus will feed His people,]{lang="en"}\
  [有如牧者，主使祂的羊飽足，]{lang="zh"}
- [In His arms He carries them all day long;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的膀臂懷抱他們安寧。]{lang="zh"}
- [O ye saints that live in the light of His presence,]{lang="en"}\
  [你們眾聖，當在光中與祂同住；]{lang="zh"}
- [Praise Him! praise Him! ever in joyful song!]{lang="en"}\
  [讚美！稱頌，帶著快樂心情。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Praise Him! praise Him! Jesus, our blessed Redeemer,]{lang="en"}\
  [讚美！讚美！讚美耶穌，我救贖主！]{lang="zh"}
- [For our sins He suffered and bled and died;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂為我罪受苦、流血、受刑；]{lang="zh"}
- [He, our Rock, our Hope of eternal salvation,]{lang="en"}\
  [稱頌！稱頌！稱頌祂是磐石穩固，]{lang="zh"}
- [Hail Him! hail Him! Jesus, the Crucified;]{lang="en"}\
  [為成永遠救恩，十架被釘！]{lang="zh"}
- [Loving Savior, meekly enduring sorrow,]{lang="en"}\
  [愛的救主，祂擔當我的痛苦，]{lang="zh"}
- [Crowned with thorns that cruelly pierced His brow;]{lang="en"}\
  [頭戴荊冕，為我忍受羞辱；]{lang="zh"}
- [Once for us rejected, despised, and forsaken,]{lang="en"}\
  [從前為我，被人棄絕、受人輕忽，]{lang="zh"}
- [Prince of Glory, ever triumphant now.]{lang="en"}\
  [永遠得勝，作我榮耀之主！]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Praise Him! praise Him! Jesus, our blessed Redeemer,]{lang="en"}\
  [讚美！讚美！讚美耶穌，我救贖主！]{lang="zh"}
- [Heavenly portals, loud with hosannahs ring!]{lang="en"}\
  [響徹天門，“和散那”聲飛揚！]{lang="zh"}
- [Jesus, Savior, reigneth for ever and ever;]{lang="en"}\
  [加冠！加冠！加冠祂作萬有之主，]{lang="zh"}
- [Crown Him! crown Him! Prophet and Priest and King!]{lang="en"}\
  [復興萬物，永作祭司、君王！]{lang="zh"}
- [Death is vanquished! Tell it with joy, ye faithful,]{lang="en"}\
  [死已廢除！我們當歡樂宣佈：]{lang="zh"}
- [Where is now thy victory, boasting grave?]{lang="en"}\
  [死阿，那裏是你所誇墳場？]{lang="zh"}
- [Jesus lives! No longer thy portals are cheerless;]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌活著！不要靜默，同來歡呼；]{lang="zh"}
- [Jesus lives, the mighty and strong to save.]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌活著，救恩有力、剛強！]{lang="zh"}
