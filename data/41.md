# 1

- [Immortal, invisible, God only wise,]{lang="en"}\
  [不能朽，不能見，獨一至尊神，]{lang="zh"}
- [In light in accessible hid from our eyes,]{lang="en"}\
  [隱藏在光輝中，肉眼不可尋；]{lang="zh"}

  [最可頌，最榮耀，亙古永長存，]{lang="zh"}

  [又全能，又全勝，頌讚神聖名。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Unresting, unhasting, and silent as light,]{lang="en"}\
  [你運作不停止，如光無聲息，]{lang="zh"}
- [Nor wanting, nor wasting, Thou rulest in might;]{lang="en"}\
  [無缺乏，無耗損，掌管以能力；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy justice like mountains high soaring above,]{lang="en"}\
  [你公義不動搖，如高山屹立，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy clouds which are fountains of goodness and love.]{lang="en"}\
  [你施恩，又施愛，永作我遮蔽。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [To all life Thou givest, to both great and small;]{lang="en"}\
  [賜生命與氣息予大小生靈，]{lang="zh"}
- [In all life Thou livest, the true life of all;]{lang="en"}\
  [你活在萬有中，乃是真生命；]{lang="zh"}
- [We blossom and flourish as leaves on the tree,]{lang="en"}\
  [我們有如花草，今朝雖茂盛，]{lang="zh"}
- [And wither and perish, but nought changeth Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [明朝到，即枯殘，惟你不變更。]{lang="zh"}
