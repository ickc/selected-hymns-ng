# 1

  [主阿，我們聚集你前，]{lang="zh"}

  [求你賜給更深恩典，]{lang="zh"}
- [To testify of grace received,]{lang="en"}\
  [使我得著更豐生命，]{lang="zh"}
- [Or offered now in Jesus’ name;]{lang="en"}\
  [使我能作更美見證；]{lang="zh"}
- [Dear Lord, to Thee our spirits cry,]{lang="en"}\
  [恩主，這是我靈所求，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our ev’ry longing satisfy.]{lang="en"}\
  [我心所慕，願你成就。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [We ask Thee, Lord, for Thou art here,]{lang="en"}\
  [在此多人靈裏乾燥，]{lang="zh"}
- [Make this a Pentecostal hour,]{lang="en"}\
  [多人心裏需要光照；]{lang="zh"}
- [When hung'ring souls from Thee receive]{lang="en"}\
  [願你賜下恩膏滋潤，]{lang="zh"}
- [Pardon, or purity, or pow'r;]{lang="en"}\
  [賜下恩光照亮人心，]{lang="zh"}
- [Unstop deaf ears, let blind eyes see,]{lang="en"}\
  [使人復興，愛你更深，]{lang="zh"}
- [Bring souls into Thy liberty.]{lang="en"}\
  [使人自審，追求上進。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Thou knowest sin-sick souls are here,]{lang="en"}\

- [Sick with depravity within,]{lang="en"}\
  [使我脫離外面捆綁；]{lang="zh"}
- [Restless, discouraged, weary ones,]{lang="en"}\
  [使我靈裏更有能力，]{lang="zh"}
- [And Thou art here to cleanse from sin;]{lang="en"}\
  [使我心裏更脫自己；]{lang="zh"}
- [Oh, speak the word ""I will, be whole;""]{lang="en"}\
  [使你生命在我成形，]{lang="zh"}
- [And save and sanctify each soul.]{lang="en"}\
  [使你旨意藉我通行。]{lang="zh"}
