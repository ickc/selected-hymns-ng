# 1

- [The King of love my Shepherd is,]{lang="en"}\
  [是愛的神作我牧人，]{lang="zh"}
- [Whose goodness faileth never;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂常餧養並施恩；]{lang="zh"}
- [I nothing lack if I am His,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂是屬我，我是屬祂，]{lang="zh"}
- [And He is mine forever.]{lang="en"}\
  [我必不至於缺乏。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Where streams of living water flow]{lang="en"}\
  [祂帶我到活水溪邊，]{lang="zh"}
- [My ransomed soul He leadeth,]{lang="en"}\
  [享受滋潤和甘甜；]{lang="zh"}
- [And, where the verdant pastures grow,]{lang="en"}\
  [又帶我到嫩草地場，]{lang="zh"}
- [With food celestial feedeth.]{lang="en"}\
  [飽嘗屬天的食糧。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Perverse and foolish oft I strayed,]{lang="en"}\
  [固執、愚昧，我常失迷，]{lang="zh"}
- [But yet in love He sought me,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂愛來將我尋覓；]{lang="zh"}
- [And on His shoulder gently laid,]{lang="en"}\
  [將我輕放在祂肩上，]{lang="zh"}
- [And home rejoicing brought me.]{lang="en"}\
  [帶我回家樂洋洋。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [In death’s dark vale I fear no ill]{lang="en"}\
  [死蔭幽谷，不怕遭害，]{lang="zh"}
- [With Thee, dear Lord, beside me;]{lang="en"}\
  [因主與我總同在；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy rod and staff my comfort still,]{lang="en"}\
  [你仗、你竿都安慰我，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy Cross before to guide me.]{lang="en"}\
  [十架在前引導我。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Thou spread’st a table in my sight;]{lang="en"}\
  [在我面前擺設筵席，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy unction grace bestoweth;]{lang="en"}\
  [你的恩膏常賜與；]{lang="zh"}
- [And oh, what transport of delight]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，我真是滿懷歡喜，]{lang="zh"}
- [From Thy pure chalice floweth!]{lang="en"}\
  [你賜福杯常滿溢。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [And so through all the length of days]{lang="en"}\
  [你的甘甜、奇妙的愛，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy goodness faileth never;]{lang="en"}\
  [測量我一生年代；]{lang="zh"}
- [Good Shepherd, may I sing Thy praise]{lang="en"}\
  [我要歌頌良牧不衰，]{lang="zh"}
- [Within Thy house forever.]{lang="en"}\
  [在你家永遠感戴。]{lang="zh"}
