# 1

- [My glorious Victor, Prince Divine,]{lang="en"}\
  [我的榮耀、得勝君王，]{lang="zh"}
- [Clasp these surrendered hands in Thine;]{lang="en"}\
  [我舉雙手降服於你；]{lang="zh"}
- [At length my will is all Thine own,]{lang="en"}\
  [我的意志向你投降，]{lang="zh"}
- [Glad vassal of a Savior’s throne.]{lang="en"}\
  [作你座前快樂奴隸。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [My Master, lead me to Thy door;]{lang="en"}\
  [我主，領我到你門口，]{lang="zh"}
- [Pierce this now willing ear once more;]{lang="en"}\
  [我耳甘願被你穿透；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy bonds are freedom; let me stay]{lang="en"}\
  [你的捆索是真自由；]{lang="zh"}
- [With Thee, to toil, endure, obey.]{lang="en"}\
  [服你、事你，沒有怨尤。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Yes, ear and hand, and thought and will,]{lang="en"}\
  [思想、意志、我耳、我手，]{lang="zh"}
- [Use all in Thy dear slav’ry still!]{lang="en"}\
  [都歸你作愛的奴役！]{lang="zh"}
- [Self’s weary liberties I cast]{lang="en"}\
  [甘願捨棄我的自由，]{lang="zh"}
- [Beneath Thy feet; there keep them fast.]{lang="en"}\
  [服你腳下，永不背離。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Tread them still down; and then I know,]{lang="en"}\
  [在你腳下；我纔知曉]{lang="zh"}
- [These hands shall with Thy gifts o’erfiow;]{lang="en"}\
  [你的恩賜溢我雙手；]{lang="zh"}
- [And pierced ears shall hear the tone]{lang="en"}\
  [被穿耳朵方始聽到]{lang="zh"}
- [Which tells me Thou and I are one.]{lang="en"}\
  [細語告我：“我歸你有。”]{lang="zh"}
