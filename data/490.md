# 1

- [The Lord’s my Shepherd, I’ll not want;]{lang="en"}\
  [是愛的神作我牧人，]{lang="zh"}
- [He makes me down to lie]{lang="en"}\
  [我必不至乏困；]{lang="zh"}
- [In pastures green; He leadeth me]{lang="en"}\
  [使我躺在青草地上，]{lang="zh"}
- [The quiet waters by.]{lang="en"}\
  [領到安歇水旁。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [My soul He doth restore again,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂使我的靈魂蘇醒，]{lang="zh"}
- [And me to walk doth make]{lang="en"}\
  [使我信心堅定；]{lang="zh"}
- [Within the paths of righteousness,]{lang="en"}\
  [為祂自己名的緣故，]{lang="zh"}
- [E’en for His own name’s sake.]{lang="en"}\
  [引導我走義路。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Yea, though I walk in death’s dark vale,]{lang="en"}\
  [我雖行過死蔭幽谷，]{lang="zh"}
- [Yet will I fear no ill;]{lang="en"}\
  [不怕遭害受苦；]{lang="zh"}
- [For Thou art with me, and Thy rod]{lang="en"}\
  [你竿扶持，你杖引領，]{lang="zh"}
- [And staff me comfort still.]{lang="en"}\
  [一路有你同行。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [My table Thou hast furnished]{lang="en"}\
  [你在我的敵人面前，]{lang="zh"}
- [In presence of my foes;]{lang="en"}\
  [為我擺設盛筵；]{lang="zh"}
- [My head Thou dost with oil anoint,]{lang="en"}\
  [你用膏油膏我的頭，]{lang="zh"}
- [And my cup overflows.]{lang="en"}\
  [使我福杯溢流。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Goodness and mercy all my life]{lang="en"}\
  [一生一世必有恩惠、]{lang="zh"}
- [Shall surely follow me,]{lang="en"}\
  [慈愛與我相隨；]{lang="zh"}
- [And in God’s house forevermore]{lang="en"}\
  [我要住在神的家中，]{lang="zh"}
- [My dwelling-place shall be.]{lang="en"}\
  [直到永世無終。]{lang="zh"}
