# 1

  [神的行動奇妙、高超，]{lang="zh"}

  [深如海洋，動如風暴，]{lang="zh"}

# 2

  [祂的蘊藏，深不可測，]{lang="zh"}

  [巧妙永可信倚，]{lang="zh"}
- [He treasures up His bright designs,]{lang="en"}\
  [動用祂的智慧計策，]{lang="zh"}

# 3

  [畏怯聖徒，從此放心！]{lang="zh"}
- [The clouds ye so much dread]{lang="en"}\
  [你們所怕厚雲，]{lang="zh"}
- [Are big with mercy, and shall break]{lang="en"}\
  [現在滿載神的憐憫，]{lang="zh"}
