# 1

- [O my Savior, glorified!]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，我救主已得榮！]{lang="zh"}
- [Now the heavens opened wide,]{lang="en"}\
  [諸天如今已大開，]{lang="zh"}
- [Show to faith’s exultant eye]{lang="en"}\
  [顯給信心的眼睛，]{lang="zh"}
- [One in beauteous majesty.]{lang="en"}\
  [見祂榮美的丰采。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Worthy of the sweetest praise]{lang="en"}\
  [蒙救贖者感激心，]{lang="zh"}
- [That my ransomed heart can raise,]{lang="en"}\
  [發出最甜讚美頌；]{lang="zh"}
- [Is that Man in whom alone]{lang="en"}\
  [配得讚揚只一人，]{lang="zh"}
- [God Himself is fully known.]{lang="en"}\
  [因祂將神全顯明。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [For those clustering glories prove]{lang="en"}\
  [“神是愛！”何等福音，]{lang="zh"}
- [That glad gospel, “God is Love,”]{lang="en"}\
  [只有信眾能證明；]{lang="zh"}
- [Whilst those wounds, in glory bright,]{lang="en"}\
  [“神是光！”光中聖民，]{lang="zh"}
- [Voice the solemn, “God is light.”]{lang="en"}\
  [應當鄭重地宣明。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Hark, my soul! thy Savior sings;]{lang="en"}\
  [我魂當聽救主吟，]{lang="zh"}
- [Catch the joy that music brings,]{lang="en"}\
  [從這歌聲得歡樂；]{lang="zh"}
- [And, with that sweet flood of song,]{lang="en"}\
  [隨同甜美的音韻，]{lang="zh"}
- [Pour thy whisp’ring praise along.]{lang="en"}\
  [傾出微弱的謳歌。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [O my Savior, glorified,]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，我得榮的救主，]{lang="zh"}
- [Turn my eye from all beside,]{lang="en"}\
  [使我眼目轉向你，]{lang="zh"}
- [Let me but Thy beauty see,]{lang="en"}\
  [離開一切人、事、物，]{lang="zh"}
- [Other light is dark to me.]{lang="en"}\
  [惟見你美麗自己。]{lang="zh"}
