# 1

- [We come, O Christ, to Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [同來朝見基督，]{lang="zh"}
- [True Son of God and man,]{lang="en"}\
  [你是神而人者，]{lang="zh"}
- [By whom al things consist,]{lang="en"}\
  [萬有藉你造出，]{lang="zh"}
- [In whom all life began:]{lang="en"}\
  [生命從你開始：]{lang="zh"}
- [In Thee alone we live and move]{lang="en"}\
  [在你裏面，行動、生活；]{lang="zh"}

  [在你愛裏，我得存活。]{lang="zh"}
