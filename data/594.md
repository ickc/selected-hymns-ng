# 1

- [Pressed out of measure, pressed beyond all length;]{lang="en"}\
  [迫得太緊，迫得無處可逃生；]{lang="zh"}
- [Pressed so intensely, seeming beyond strength;]{lang="en"}\
  [迫到極點，好像力不能勝；]{lang="zh"}
- [Pressed in the body, pressed within the soul,]{lang="en"}\
  [壓迫在身，無處可算是平安；]{lang="zh"}
- [Pressed in the mind till darksome surges roll.]{lang="en"}\
  [壓迫在心，四圍都是黑暗。]{lang="zh"}

# 1-chorus

- [God is my hope and God is my joy;]{lang="en"}\
  [神是我盼望，是我喜樂；]{lang="zh"}
- [He is the resurrection life I enjoy.]{lang="en"}\
  [是祂使無變有，叫死人復活！]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Pressure by foes, and pressure from our friends;]{lang="en"}\
  [迫于仇敵，有時也迫于朋友；]{lang="zh"}
- [Pressure on pressure, till life nearly ends;]{lang="en"}\
  [迫上加迫，性命幾歸烏有。]{lang="zh"}
- [Pressed into knowing none to help but God,]{lang="en"}\
  [迫到除神之外，別無人幫助；]{lang="zh"}
- [Pressed into loving both the staff and rod.]{lang="en"}\
  [迫到接受鞭杖，如蒙雨露。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Pressed into liberty where nothing clings,]{lang="en"}\
  [迫到無一纏累，致得以自由；]{lang="zh"}
- [Pressed into faith for hard and hopeless things;]{lang="en"}\
  [迫到艱苦、絕望，惟神是求：]{lang="zh"}
- [Pressed into life, a life in Christ the Lord,]{lang="en"}\
  [迫到躲進生命——主裡的生命，]{lang="zh"}
- [Pressed into life, the life of Christ outpoured.]{lang="en"}\
  [得享基督生命，何等豐盈！]{lang="zh"}
