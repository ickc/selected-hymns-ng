# 1

- [Day by day, and with each passing moment,]{lang="en"}\
  [每一天所度過每一時刻，]{lang="zh"}
- [Strength I find to meet my trials here;]{lang="en"}\
  [我獲得力量勝過試煉；]{lang="zh"}
- [Trusting in my Father’s wise bestowment,]{lang="en"}\
  [我信靠我天父智慧所賜，]{lang="zh"}
- [I’ve no cause for worry or for fear.]{lang="en"}\
  [無理由再憂慮並掛念。]{lang="zh"}
- [He, whose heart is kind beyond all measure,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂心仁慈遠超一切度量，]{lang="zh"}
- [Gives unto each day what He deems best,]{lang="en"}\
  [逐日領受祂手所量給；]{lang="zh"}
- [Lovingly its part of pain and pleasure,]{lang="en"}\
  [憂或喜都顯明祂的慈愛，]{lang="zh"}
- [Mingling toil with peace and rest.]{lang="en"}\
  [勞苦中融主安慰。]{lang="zh"}
