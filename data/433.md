# 1

- [All for Jesus! All for Jesus!]{lang="en"}\
  [都歸基督，都歸基督，]{lang="zh"}
- [All my being’s ransomed pow’rs;]{lang="en"}\
  [都歸基督理當然；]{lang="zh"}
- [All my thoughts and words and doings,]{lang="en"}\
  [我既是主寶血所贖，]{lang="zh"}
- [All my days and all my hours.]{lang="en"}\
  [主就當得所有權。]{lang="zh"}
- [All for Jesus! All for Jesus! All my days and all my hours.]{lang="en"}\
  [都歸基督，都歸基督，每時每日歸基督；]{lang="zh"}

# 2

# 3

- [Worldlings prize their gems of beauty,]{lang="en"}\
  [世人貪愛虛榮、美色，]{lang="zh"}

  [追求屬地的事物；]{lang="zh"}
- [Boast of wealth, and fame, and pleasure;]{lang="en"}\
  [誇耀財富、名聲、享樂；]{lang="zh"}
