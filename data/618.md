# 1

- [Onward Christian soldiers!]{lang="en"}\
  [前進！基督精兵，前進！]{lang="zh"}
- [Marching as to war, With the cross of Jesus]{lang="en"}\
  [如出征；十架旌旗高撐，隨主向前行。]{lang="zh"}
- [Going on before. Christ, the royal Master, Leads against the foe;]{lang="en"}\
  [前進！走上疆場，進攻諸幽冥；]{lang="zh"}
- [Forward into battle, See, His banners go!]{lang="en"}\
  [必將仇敵掃蕩，若聽主號令。]{lang="zh"}

# 1-chorus

- [Onward, Christian soldiers!]{lang="en"}\
  [前進！基督精兵，前進！]{lang="zh"}
- [Marching as to war, With the cross of Jesus, Going on before.]{lang="en"}\
  [如出征；十架旌旗高撐，隨主向前行。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [At the name of Jesus Satan’s host doth flee;]{lang="en"}\
  [一聞耶穌大名，魔軍必竄逃；]{lang="zh"}
- [On then, Christian soldiers, On to victory!]{lang="en"}\
  [所以基督精兵，向前將敵剿。]{lang="zh"}
- [Hell’s foundations quiver At the shout of praise:]{lang="en"}\
  [若肯高聲讚頌，陰間必動搖；]{lang="zh"}
- [Brothers, lift your voices, Loud your anthems raise!]{lang="en"}\
  [所以親愛弟兄，讚頌聲要高。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Like a mighty army Moves the Church of God:]{lang="en"}\
  [基督教會出征，勢如大軍隊：]{lang="zh"}
- [Brothers, we are treading Where the saints have trod;]{lang="en"}\
  [古聖在前先行，我們在後隨；]{lang="zh"}
- [We are not divided, All one Body we—]{lang="en"}\
  [並非分成兩起，古聖與我們；]{lang="zh"}
- [One in faith and Spirit, One eternally.]{lang="en"}\
  [乃是一靈、一體、一望並一信。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Crowns and thrones may perish, Kingdoms rise and wane;]{lang="en"}\
  [諸王終必摧毀，邦國興而衰；]{lang="zh"}
- [But the Church of Jesus Constant will remain.]{lang="en"}\
  [惟有基督教會，永遠必存在。]{lang="zh"}
- [Gates of hell can never’ Gainst the Church prevail;]{lang="en"}\
  [陰間權勢雖凶，不能勝教會；]{lang="zh"}
- [We have Christ’s own promise, Which can never fail.]{lang="en"}\
  [教會隨主進攻，必要毀魔鬼。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Onward, then, ye people! Join our happy throng;]{lang="en"}\
  [前進！蒙恩聖徒，回應主教會；]{lang="zh"}
- [Blend with ours your voices In the triumph song.]{lang="en"}\
  [跟隨教會步武，必奏凱歌歸。]{lang="zh"}
- [Glory, laud and honor Unto Christ, the King;]{lang="en"}\
  [榮耀、尊貴、頌揚、歸給得勝王；]{lang="zh"}
- [This through countless ages Men and angels sing.]{lang="en"}\
  [聖徒天使合唱，頌揚永無疆。]{lang="zh"}
