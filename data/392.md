# 1

- [O Jesus Christ, grow Thou in me,]{lang="en"}\
  [哦主，求你長在我心，]{lang="zh"}
- [And all things else recede;]{lang="en"}\
  [你外再無他求！]{lang="zh"}
- [My heart be daily nearer Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [使我逐日與你更親，]{lang="zh"}

  [逐日向罪自由。]{lang="zh"}
