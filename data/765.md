# 1

- [Of all the gifts Thy love bestows,]{lang="en"}\
  [你愛所給雖然甚多，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou Giver of all good!]{lang="en"}\
  [恩賜眾善者哪！]{lang="zh"}
- [E’en heav’n itself no richer knows]{lang="en"}\
  [但在天上還未見過]{lang="zh"}
- [Than Jesus and His blood.]{lang="en"}\
  [甚麼比血更大。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Faith, too, that trusts the blood through grace,]{lang="en"}\
  [就是我的信心對血，]{lang="zh"}
- [From that same love we gain;]{lang="en"}\
  [也是你愛所給；]{lang="zh"}
- [Else, sweetly as it suits our case,]{lang="en"}\
  [不然，恩賜雖合所缺，]{lang="zh"}
- [The gift had been in vain.]{lang="en"}\
  [也是虛空無益。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [O Love divine, Thou vast abyss!]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，神的愛，你是深海，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our sins buried in Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [我罪都埋裏面；]{lang="zh"}
- [Cover'd is our unrighteousness,]{lang="en"}\
  [我的不義，你都遮蓋；]{lang="zh"}
- [From all debts we are free.]{lang="en"}\
  [虧欠，你都赦免。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [In Jesus' blood our hearts can trace]{lang="en"}\
  [在寶血裏，我已尋出]{lang="zh"}
- [The footprints of Thy love,]{lang="en"}\
  [你愛心的蹤跡；]{lang="zh"}
- [And know the riches of Thy grace,]{lang="en"}\
  [由此我知你的寬恕，]{lang="zh"}
- [The holy righteousness.]{lang="en"}\
  [並知你的公義。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Though heart may fail and flesh decay,]{lang="en"}\
  [心會軟弱，身會衰頹，]{lang="zh"}
- [On firm ground we remain,]{lang="en"}\
  [我總安然相信；]{lang="zh"}
- [When earth and heav'n shall pass away,]{lang="en"}\
  [天地雖然都要銷毀，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our anchor shall sustain.]{lang="en"}\
  [我錨已經拋穩。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [Until that day, we then shall prove,]{lang="en"}\
  [到了那時，我要證明]{lang="zh"}
- [The full worth of Thy love.]{lang="en"}\
  [你愛有何價值；]{lang="zh"}
- [But here on earth, my life still needs]{lang="en"}\
  [但在今日我的生命]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy great sustaining power.]{lang="en"}\
  [就先靠它扶持。]{lang="zh"}

# 7

- [We praise Thee, and would praise Thee more,]{lang="en"}\
  [當讚美你！再讚美你！]{lang="zh"}
- [To Thee our all we owe;]{lang="en"}\
  [有何不是你給；]{lang="zh"}
- [The precious Saviour, and the power]{lang="en"}\
  [寶貴救主，和那使彼]{lang="zh"}
- [That makes Him precious too.]{lang="en"}\
  [顯為寶貴的力。]{lang="zh"}
