# 1

- [Father, let Thy kingdom come,]{lang="en"}\
  [父阿，願你國降臨，]{lang="zh"}
- [Let it come with living power,]{lang="en"}\
  [滿帶生命的大能；]{lang="zh"}
- [Speak at length the final word,]{lang="en"}\
  [願你末後的發聲，]{lang="zh"}
- [Usher in the triumph hour.]{lang="en"}\
  [引進得勝的時辰。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [As it came in the days of old,]{lang="en"}\
  [正如從前的日子，]{lang="zh"}
- [In the deepest hearts of men,]{lang="en"}\
  [每當聖徒殉道時，]{lang="zh"}
- [When Thy martyrs died for Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [顯在人心的深處；]{lang="zh"}
- [Let it come, O God, again.]{lang="en"}\
  [願它快來不延遲。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Tyrant thrones and idol shrines,]{lang="en"}\
  [黑暗權勢的宮庭，]{lang="zh"}
- [Let them from their place be hurled;]{lang="en"}\
  [全都被你所除盡；]{lang="zh"}
- [Enter on Thy better reign,]{lang="en"}\
  [你的王權來執政，]{lang="zh"}
- [Wear the crown of this poor world.]{lang="en"}\
  [願人都尊你為聖。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Oh, what long, sad years have gone,]{lang="en"}\
  [自從教會受教導，]{lang="zh"}
- [Since Thy Church was taught this prayer;]{lang="en"}\
  [漫長歲月雖難熬；]{lang="zh"}
- [Oh, what eyes have watched and wept]{lang="en"}\
  [仍然儆醒並禱告，]{lang="zh"}
- [For the dawning everywhere.]{lang="en"}\
  [求使光明遍地照。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Break, triumphant day of God!]{lang="en"}\
  [得勝日子快降臨，]{lang="zh"}
- [Break at last our hearts to cheer;]{lang="en"}\
  [我們的心要歡欣；]{lang="zh"}
- [Throbbing souls and holy songs]{lang="en"}\
  [無數頌歌存此心，]{lang="zh"}
- [Wait to hail Thy dawning here.]{lang="en"}\
  [等你來臨同獻吟。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [Empires, temples, scepters, thrones,]{lang="en"}\
  [國度、王權、並寶座，]{lang="zh"}
- [May they all for God be won;]{lang="en"}\
  [都當歸我神得著，]{lang="zh"}
- [And, in every human heart,]{lang="en"}\
  [父阿，在地每顆心，]{lang="zh"}
- [Father, let Thy kingdom come.]{lang="en"}\
  [都願你國快降臨。]{lang="zh"}
