# 1

- [Jesus shall reign where’er the sun]{lang="en"}\
  [日光照耀所到之疆，]{lang="zh"}
- [Doth his successive journeys run;]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌國度普及四方；]{lang="zh"}
- [His Kingdom stretch from shore to shore,]{lang="en"}\
  [即使星月不再放光，]{lang="zh"}
- [Till moons shall wax and wane no more.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂仍掌權永世無疆。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [To Him shall endless prayer be made,]{lang="en"}\
  [不絕禱聲向祂奉獻，]{lang="zh"}
- [The prisoner leaps to lose his chains,]{lang="en"}\
  [無量頌歌成祂冠冕；]{lang="zh"}
- [His name like sweet perfume shall rise]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的聖名好比馨香，]{lang="zh"}
- [With every morning sacrifice,]{lang="en"}\
  [隨同晨祭升到天上。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [People and realms of every tongue]{lang="en"}\
  [萬國、萬族、萬方、萬民，]{lang="zh"}
- [Dwell on His love with sweetest song;]{lang="en"}\
  [不停頌讚妙愛奇恩；]{lang="zh"}
- [And infant voices shall proclaim]{lang="en"}\
  [嬰孩亦將同聲歌唱，]{lang="zh"}
- [Their early blessings on His name.]{lang="en"}\
  [救主聖名及早頌揚。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Blessings abound where’er He reigns:]{lang="en"}\
  [在主治下人人多福，]{lang="zh"}
- [And princes throng to crown His head,]{lang="en"}\
  [罪人脫離捆綁束縛；]{lang="zh"}
- [The weary find eternal rest,]{lang="en"}\
  [勞苦之人永享安息，]{lang="zh"}
- [And all the sons of want are blest.]{lang="en"}\
  [貧窮之人滿得福氣。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Where He displays His healing power]{lang="en"}\
  [在此祂有醫治大能，]{lang="zh"}
- [Death and the curse are known no more;]{lang="en"}\
  [死亡、咒詛失去蹤影；]{lang="zh"}
- [In Him the tribes of Adam boast]{lang="en"}\
  [亞當所失生命之福，]{lang="zh"}
- [More blessings than their father lost.]{lang="en"}\
  [在基督裏全都恢復。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [Let every creature rise and bring]{lang="en"}\
  [受造眾生興起頌揚，]{lang="zh"}
- [Peculiar honors to our King;]{lang="en"}\
  [獨特榮耀歸我君王；]{lang="zh"}
- [Angels descend with songs again,]{lang="en"}\
  [天使再來高聲唱吟，]{lang="zh"}
- [And earth repeat the loud Amen.]{lang="en"}\
  [地上萬民同應阿們。]{lang="zh"}
