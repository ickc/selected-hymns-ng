# 1

- [Jesus, the sinner’s Friend,]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌，罪人朋友，]{lang="zh"}
- [We hid ourselves in Thee;]{lang="en"}\
  [我來藏身你懷，]{lang="zh"}
- [God looks upon Thy sprinkled blood,]{lang="en"}\
  [神見你血，已為我流，]{lang="zh"}
- [It is our only plea.]{lang="en"}\
  [我就蒙神喜愛。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [He hears Thy precious Name,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂樂聞你尊名，]{lang="zh"}
- [We claim it as our own;]{lang="en"}\
  [我奉此名求恩；]{lang="zh"}
- [The Father must accept and bless]{lang="en"}\
  [父必接受、祝福、傾聽，]{lang="zh"}
- [His well-beloved Son.]{lang="en"}\
  [愛子名裏求懇。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Thou hast fulfilled the law,]{lang="en"}\
  [律法你已完成，]{lang="zh"}
- [And we are justified:]{lang="en"}\
  [我得稱義、蒙恩：]{lang="zh"}
- [Ours is the blessing, Thine the curse;]{lang="en"}\
  [我享祝福，因你受懲，]{lang="zh"}
- [We live, for Thou hast died.]{lang="en"}\
  [你死，使我得生。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Jesus, the sinner’s Friend!]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌，罪人朋友，]{lang="zh"}
- [We cannot speak Thy praise;]{lang="en"}\
  [遠超我能頌揚；]{lang="zh"}
- [No mortal voice can sing the song]{lang="en"}\
  [人間也無一個歌喉，]{lang="zh"}
- [That ransomed hearts would raise.]{lang="en"}\
  [能將你恩盡唱。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [But when before the throne,]{lang="en"}\
  [有日座前下拜，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy face we all shall see,]{lang="en"}\
  [我們得見你面，]{lang="zh"}
- [Clothed in our blood-bought robes of white,]{lang="en"}\
  [身穿白袍——你血所買，]{lang="zh"}
- [We’ll stand complete in Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [坦然侍立你前。]{lang="zh"}

# 6
