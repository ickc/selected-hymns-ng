# 1

- [Jesus, I my cross have taken,]{lang="en"}\
  [我今撇下一切事物，]{lang="zh"}
- [All to leave and follow Thee;]{lang="en"}\
  [背十架跟耶穌；]{lang="zh"}
- [Destitute, despised, forsaken,]{lang="en"}\
  [甘受藐視、厭棄、貧苦，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou, from hence, my all shalt be.]{lang="en"}\
  [一心跟主走窄路。]{lang="zh"}
- [Perish every fond ambition,]{lang="en"}\
  [前我所愛、所慕、所求，]{lang="zh"}
- [All I’ve sought, and hoped, and known;]{lang="en"}\
  [今與雄心同歸盡；]{lang="zh"}
- [Yet how rich is my condition,]{lang="en"}\
  [但我又是何等豐優，]{lang="zh"}
- [God and Christ are still my own!]{lang="en"}\
  [神與基督是我分。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Let the world despise and leave me,]{lang="en"}\
  [讓這世界棄我、嫌我，]{lang="zh"}
- [They have left my Savior, too;]{lang="en"}\
  [恩主也曾被棄嫌；]{lang="zh"}
- [Human hearts and looks deceive me;]{lang="en"}\
  [人面、人心，欺我、騙我，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou art not, like man, untrue;]{lang="en"}\
  [惟你永遠不欺騙。]{lang="zh"}
- [And, while Thou shalt smile upon me,]{lang="en"}\
  [當你向我顯出笑臉，]{lang="zh"}
- [God of wisdom, love, and might,]{lang="en"}\
  [友雖生疏敵雖險，]{lang="zh"}
- [Foes may hate, and friends disown me;]{lang="en"}\
  [但我心安，處之泰然，]{lang="zh"}
- [Show Thy face, and all is bright.]{lang="en"}\
  [因我得見你歡顏。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Man may trouble and distress me,]{lang="en"}\
  [人雖逼我使我苦痛，]{lang="zh"}

  [不過驅我到你前；]{lang="zh"}
- [Life with trials hard may press me,]{lang="en"}\
  [世間苦難壓我愈重，]{lang="zh"}
- [Christ will bring me sweeter rest.]{lang="en"}\
  [天上平安愈甘甜。]{lang="zh"}
- [O ’tis not in grief to harm me,]{lang="en"}\
  [有何憂苦能將我襲，]{lang="zh"}
- [While Thy love is left to me;]{lang="en"}\
  [當你賜我你愛憐。]{lang="zh"}
- [O ‘twere not in joy to charm me,]{lang="en"}\
  [有何快樂能將我迷，]{lang="zh"}
- [Were that joy unmixed with Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [若你不在它裡面。]{lang="zh"}
