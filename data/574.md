# 1

- [I’m a pilgrim and a stranger,]{lang="en"}\
  [我是一個異鄉旅客，]{lang="zh"}
- [Rough and thorny is the road.]{lang="en"}\
  [跋涉遙遠的道路；]{lang="zh"}
- [Often in the midst of danger,]{lang="en"}\
  [雖然有時遇見阻隔，]{lang="zh"}
- [But it leads to God.]{lang="en"}\
  [引我親天父。]{lang="zh"}
- [Clouds and darkness oft distress me,]{lang="en"}\
  [途中雖有烏雲籠罩，]{lang="zh"}
- [Great and many are my foes;]{lang="en"}\
  [也有意外的打擾，]{lang="zh"}
- [Anxious cares and tho’ts oppress me,]{lang="en"}\
  [思慮、煩惱把我壓倒，]{lang="zh"}
- [But my Father knows.]{lang="en"}\
  [但我父知道。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Oh, how sweet is this assurance,]{lang="en"}\
  [父是知道，何等保證！]{lang="zh"}
- [‘Mid the conflict and the strife!]{lang="en"}\
  [沿途試煉有何害？]{lang="zh"}
- [Although sorrows past endurance]{lang="en"}\
  [縱然它們隨我一生，]{lang="zh"}
- [Follow me through life.]{lang="en"}\
  [不容易忍耐。]{lang="zh"}
- [Home in prospect still can cheer me,]{lang="en"}\
  [天家在望，莫大鼓勵，]{lang="zh"}
- [Yes, and give me sweet repose,]{lang="en"}\
  [使我安息不動搖！]{lang="zh"}
- [While I feel His presence near me;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的同在何其甜蜜，]{lang="zh"}
- [For my Father knows.]{lang="en"}\
  [我天父知道。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Yes, He sees and knows me daily,]{lang="en"}\
  [我父每日都知道我，]{lang="zh"}
- [Watches over me in love;]{lang="en"}\
  [看守我在愛裡面；]{lang="zh"}
- [Sends me help when foes assail me—]{lang="en"}\
  [艱難來襲，祂來助我，]{lang="zh"}
- [Bids me look above.]{lang="en"}\
  [叫我往上看。]{lang="zh"}
- [Soon my journey will be ended,]{lang="en"}\
  [不久旅程到達止境，]{lang="zh"}
- [Life is drawing to a close;]{lang="en"}\
  [今生就要告終了，]{lang="zh"}
- [I shall then be well attended;]{lang="en"}\
  [我將如何進入光明，]{lang="zh"}
- [This my Father knows.]{lang="en"}\
  [這事父知道。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Face to face my Father see;]{lang="en"}\

- [Fall with rapture, and adore Him]{lang="en"}\
  [滿心歡喜，俯伏朝見，]{lang="zh"}
- [For His love to me.]{lang="en"}\
  [感謝祂恩典。]{lang="zh"}
- [Nothing more shall then distress me]{lang="en"}\

  [再無今生的攪擾，]{lang="zh"}
- [Jesus stands engaged to bless me;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的應許全得應驗，]{lang="zh"}
- [This my Father knows.]{lang="en"}\
  [這事父知道。]{lang="zh"}
