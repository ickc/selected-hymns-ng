# 1

O come, let us adore Him,
哦來，歡欣敬拜祂；
O come, let us adore Him,
哦來，歡欣敬拜祂；
O come, let us adore Him,
哦來，歡欣敬拜祂——
Christ the Lord.
基督我主。

# 2

We’ll praise His name forever,
永遠讚美祂的名；
We’ll praise His name forever,
永遠讚美祂的名；
We’ll praise His name forever,
永遠讚美祂的名——
Christ the Lord.
基督我主。

# 3

We’ll give Him all the glory,
將榮耀全都歸祂；
We’ll give Him all the glory,
將榮耀全都歸祂；
We’ll give Him all the glory,
將榮耀全都歸祂——
Christ the Lord.
基督我主。

# 4

For He alone is worthy,
惟有祂配得榮耀；
For He alone is worthy,
惟有祂配得榮耀；
For He alone is worthy,
惟有祂配得榮耀——
Christ the lord.
基督我主。
