# 1

- [Under His wings I am safely abiding;]{lang="en"}\
  [在祂翼下，平安穩妥我居住，]{lang="zh"}
- [Though the night deepens and tempests are wild,]{lang="en"}\
  [不管夜色多深，且有風雨；]{lang="zh"}
- [Still I can trust Him, I know He will keep me;]{lang="en"}\
  [但我能信靠，我知祂必眷顧，]{lang="zh"}
- [He has redeemed me, and I am His child.]{lang="en"}\
  [因祂已救我，我是祂兒女。]{lang="zh"}
