# 1

- [This is My rest forever;]{lang="en"}\
  [「我已揀選了錫安，]{lang="zh"}
- [Here will I dwell,]{lang="en"}\
  [作我居所。]{lang="zh"}
- [For the Lord hath chosen Zion,]{lang="en"}\
  [是我永遠安息之所，]{lang="zh"}
- [He hath desired it for His habitation.]{lang="en"}\
  [我要住這裡，這是我所願意。]{lang="zh"}
- [He will abundantly bless her provision:]{lang="en"}\
  [我要使其中的糧食極豐滿，]{lang="zh"}
- [He will satisfy her poor with bread.]{lang="en"}\
  [使其中的窮人都飽足。]{lang="zh"}
- [He will clothe her priests with salvation,]{lang="en"}\
  [我要使祭司披上救恩，]{lang="zh"}
- [And her saints shall shout aloud for joy.]{lang="en"}\
  [使聖民大聲讚美歡呼。」]{lang="zh"}
