# 1

- [”A little while!” Our Lord shall come,]{lang="en"}\
  [“一點時候”——主就回來，]{lang="zh"}
- [And we shall wander here no more;]{lang="en"}\
  [我們就要不再流落；]{lang="zh"}
- [He’ll take us to our Father’s home,]{lang="en"}\
  [迎接我們歸家雲外，]{lang="zh"}
- [Where He for us has gone before—]{lang="en"}\
  [祂所豫備天上居所－]{lang="zh"}
- [To dwell with Him, to see His face,]{lang="en"}\
  [與祂同居瞻祂榮面，]{lang="zh"}
- [And sing the glories of His grace.]{lang="en"}\
  [高聲歌頌奇妙恩典。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [""A little while”—He’ll come again;]{lang="en"}\
  [“一點時候”——祂就再來，]{lang="zh"}
- [Let us the precious hours redeem;]{lang="en"}\
  [我們須要贖回光陰；]{lang="zh"}
- [Our only grief to give Him pain,]{lang="en"}\
  [使祂痛心是我悲哀，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our joy to serve and follow Him.]{lang="en"}\
  [跟祂、事祂，是我歡欣；]{lang="zh"}
- [Watching and ready may we be,]{lang="en"}\
  [願我豫備，儆醒、禱告，]{lang="zh"}
- [As those that wait their Lord to see.]{lang="en"}\
  [有如僕人等候主到。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [""A little while”—’twill soon be past,]{lang="en"}\
  [“一點時候”——轉瞬即過，]{lang="zh"}
- [Why should we shun the promised cross?]{lang="en"}\
  [主賜十架為何推辭？]{lang="zh"}
- [O let us in His footsteps haste,]{lang="en"}\
  [步祂腳蹤，效祂所作，]{lang="zh"}
- [Counting for Him all else but loss;]{lang="en"}\
  [為祂，利益算作損失；]{lang="zh"}
- [For how will recompense His smile]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的笑臉是為報酬]{lang="zh"}
- [The suff’rings of this “little while.”]{lang="en"}\
  [這苦痛的“一點時候。”]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [""A little while”—come, Savior, come!]{lang="en"}\
  [“一點時候”——求你就來！]{lang="zh"}
- [For Thee Thy bride has tarried long;]{lang="en"}\
  [主，你新婦盼望已久；]{lang="zh"}
- [Take Thy poor waiting pilgrims home,]{lang="en"}\
  [疲倦客旅切切等待，]{lang="zh"}
- [To sing the new eternal song,]{lang="en"}\
  [歸家高唱永遠歌謳；]{lang="zh"}
- [To see Thy glory, and to be]{lang="en"}\
  [見你真體榮耀無比，]{lang="zh"}
- [In ev’rything conformed to Thee!]{lang="en"}\
  [並要改變完全像你。]{lang="zh"}
