# 1

- [In Revelation chapter one]{lang="en"}\
  [在啟示錄第一章裏，]{lang="zh"}
- [God gives a vision of the Son,]{lang="en"}\
  [神將兒子異象賜給；]{lang="zh"}
- [Of Him who was and is to come;]{lang="en"}\
  [讓我們看昔在、今在、]{lang="zh"}
- [Oh, let us to this One now come.]{lang="en"}\
  [並以後永在的那位。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [In spirit hear His trumpet voice;]{lang="en"}\
  [在靈裡聽見祂號聲，]{lang="zh"}
- [We must be turned to see His choice]{lang="en"}\
  [必須轉身纔能看明——]{lang="zh"}
- [The seven lampstands golden fair;]{lang="en"}\
  [那位人子行走在那]{lang="zh"}
- [The Son of Man is walking there.]{lang="en"}\
  [七金燈臺中的光景。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [The great high priestly robe He wears,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂身穿大祭司衣袍，]{lang="zh"}
- [For every church He fully cares:]{lang="en"}\
  [充分照顧每一教會：]{lang="zh"}
- [He trims the lamp, the oil supplies;]{lang="en"}\
  [修理燈盞，添加燈油；]{lang="zh"}
- [He makes them bum, flames in His eyes.]{lang="en"}\
  [使燈常燃，發出光輝。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [A golden girdle on His breast—]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的胸間束著金帶——]{lang="zh"}
- [His work is done, and from His rest]{lang="en"}\
  [工作已成，從安息裡]{lang="zh"}
- [He unto all the churches pours]{lang="en"}\
  [向眾教會傾出豐富，]{lang="zh"}
- [Himself in love, the treasure store.]{lang="en"}\
  [就是祂愛中的自己。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [His head, His hair is white as wool]{lang="en"}\
  [祂頭與髮，白如羊毛——]{lang="zh"}
- [The ancient One with youth is full.]{lang="en"}\
  [亙古的主，仍是常新。]{lang="zh"}
- [His face is shining as the sun]{lang="en"}\
  [面貌如同烈日照耀，]{lang="zh"}
- [To burn and lighten every one.]{lang="en"}\
  [來焚燒並光照我們。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

  [我們一見這永活者，]{lang="zh"}

  [都要仆倒如同死了。]{lang="zh"}
- [But then the Lord His comfort gives]{lang="en"}\
  [但主賜給祂的安慰——]{lang="zh"}
- [He once was dead, but now He lives.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂曾死過，今又活了。]{lang="zh"}
