# 1

- [Sweet hour of prayer, sweet hour of prayer,]{lang="en"}\
  [禱告之時，甘甜之時！]{lang="zh"}
- [That calls me from a world of care,]{lang="en"}\
  [使我離開煩惱之事，]{lang="zh"}
- [And bids me at my Father’s throne]{lang="en"}\
  [來到神的座前，發表]{lang="zh"}
- [Make all my wants and wishes known:]{lang="en"}\
  [所有意願、所有需要；]{lang="zh"}
- [In seasons of distress and grief]{lang="en"}\
  [當我遇到重壓、憂愁，]{lang="zh"}
- [My soul has often found relief,]{lang="en"}\
  [我靈卻常得著自由，]{lang="zh"}
- [And oft escaped the tempter’s snare]{lang="en"}\
  [不陷惡者所有佈置，]{lang="zh"}
- [By thy return, sweet hour of prayer.]{lang="en"}\
  [因為有你——禱告之時！]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Sweet hour of pray’r! sweet hour of pray’r,]{lang="en"}\
  [禱告之時，甘甜之時！]{lang="zh"}
- [The joy I feel, the bliss I share,]{lang="en"}\
  [何等蒙福，快樂安適；]{lang="zh"}
- [Of those whose anxious spirits bum]{lang="en"}\
  [我靈深處，猶如火焚，]{lang="zh"}
- [With strong desires for Thy return!]{lang="en"}\
  [我心焦渴，望你來臨。]{lang="zh"}
- [With such I hasten to the place]{lang="en"}\
  [此情催我，進到你前，]{lang="zh"}
- [Where God, my Savior, shows His face,]{lang="en"}\
  [我神我主，瞻仰你面；]{lang="zh"}
- [And gladly take my station there,]{lang="en"}\
  [我樂逗留在此位置，]{lang="zh"}
- [And wait for thee, sweet hour of pray’r.]{lang="en"}\
  [將你等候——禱告之時！]{lang="zh"}
