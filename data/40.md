# 1

- [The spacious firmament on high,]{lang="en"}\
  [仰看穹蒼，浩瀚無垠，]{lang="zh"}
- [With all the blue ethereal sky,]{lang="en"}\
  [蔚藍、深邃，沒有止境；]{lang="zh"}
- [And spangled heav’ns, a shining frame,]{lang="en"}\
  [其中千萬星光閃爍，]{lang="zh"}
- [Their great Original proclaim.]{lang="en"}\
  [共同宣告神的創作。]{lang="zh"}
- [Th’unwearied sun, from day to day,]{lang="en"}\
  [不息太陽日日奔馳，]{lang="zh"}
- [Does his Creator’s pow’r display;]{lang="en"}\
  [將主創造大能顯示，]{lang="zh"}
- [And publishes to every land]{lang="en"}\
  [普照天下一切受造，]{lang="zh"}
- [The work of an almighty hand.]{lang="en"}\
  [說明全能手段奇妙。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Soon as the evening shades prevail,]{lang="en"}\
  [夕陽西下，夜色來襲，]{lang="zh"}
- [The moon takes up the wondrous tale;]{lang="en"}\
  [明月溫柔靜照大地；]{lang="zh"}
- [And nightly to the listening earth]{lang="en"}\
  [長夜漫漫，一再自述：]{lang="zh"}
- [Repeats the story of her birth;]{lang="en"}\
  [當初主怎將它造出。]{lang="zh"}
- [While all the stars that round her burn,]{lang="en"}\
  [四圍星辰發光如熾，]{lang="zh"}
- [And all the planets in their turn,]{lang="en"}\
  [一一述說自己故事，]{lang="zh"}
- [Confirm the tidings as they roll,]{lang="en"}\
  [一面運行，一面傳揚，]{lang="zh"}
- [And spread the truth from pole to pole.]{lang="en"}\
  [信息傳遍地極、四方。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [What though in solemn silence all]{lang="en"}\
  [雖然星辰奔馳夜空，]{lang="zh"}
- [Move round the dark terrestrial ball?]{lang="en"}\
  [莊嚴靜默，來復運行；]{lang="zh"}
- [What though no real voice nor sound]{lang="en"}\
  [緊依軌道發出光明，]{lang="zh"}
- [Amid the radiant orbs be found?]{lang="en"}\
  [無言無語，無聲可聽；]{lang="zh"}
- [In reason’s ear they all rejoice,]{lang="en"}\
  [但在智者耳中常聞]{lang="zh"}
- [And utter forth a glorious voice,]{lang="en"}\
  [它們發出榮耀聲音，]{lang="zh"}
- [Forever singing as they shine,]{lang="en"}\
  [一面照耀﹐一面頌稱：]{lang="zh"}
- [“The hand that made us is divine.”]{lang="en"}\
  [“ 造我的手何其神聖！”]{lang="zh"}
