# 1

- [Christians, make haste, your mission high fulfilling,]{lang="en"}\
  [信徒趕快遵命出去普天下，]{lang="zh"}
- [To tell to all the world that God is Light,]{lang="en"}\
  [通知他們：神是如何慈愛，]{lang="zh"}
- [That He who made all nations is not willing]{lang="en"}\
  [使之深曉：神已成功祂救法，]{lang="zh"}
- [One soul should perish, lost in shades of night.]{lang="en"}\
  [並不願意一人滅亡、悲哀。]{lang="zh"}

# 1-chorus

- [Publish glad tidings, tidings of peace;]{lang="en"}\
  [傳揚好信息、和平福音，]{lang="zh"}
- [Tidings of Jesus, redemption and release.]{lang="en"}\
  [傳揚主耶穌已經救贖罪人。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Behold how many thousands still are lying,]{lang="en"}\
  [請看，何止千萬的人在罪中，]{lang="zh"}
- [Bound in the darksome prison-house of sin,]{lang="en"}\
  [受了捆綁，一直事奉鬼魔，]{lang="zh"}
- [With none to tell them of the Savior’s dying]{lang="en"}\
  [無人前去通知：救贖已成功，]{lang="zh"}
- [Or of the life He died for them to win.]{lang="en"}\
  [救主已經得勝，死而復活。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [’Tis yours to save from peril of perdition]{lang="en"}\
  [主能救人脫離罪惡的捆綁，]{lang="zh"}
- [The soul for whom the Lord His life laid down;]{lang="en"}\
  [亞當族類，因祂捨命結束；]{lang="zh"}
- [Beware lest, slothful to fulfill your mission,]{lang="en"}\
  [我們應當不畏蹈火與赴湯，]{lang="zh"}
- [You lose one jewel that should deck His crown.]{lang="en"}\
  [庶免攔阻一人來信耶穌。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Proclaim to every people, tongue, and nation]{lang="en"}\
  [世界各民、各國、各方並各人，]{lang="zh"}
- [That God, in whom they move and live, is love;]{lang="en"}\
  [我們都當前往傳揚恩典，]{lang="zh"}
- [Tell how He stooped to save His lost creation,]{lang="en"}\
  [叫人知道：神是如何成肉身，、]{lang="zh"}
- [And died on earth that men He might approve.]{lang="en"}\
  [受死在地，使人重生屬天。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Give of your sons to bear the message glorious;]{lang="en"}\
  [不該攔阻兒女獻身往邊荒，]{lang="zh"}
- [Give of your wealth to speed them on their way;]{lang="en"}\
  [應當奉獻財物幫助福音，]{lang="zh"}
- [Pour out your soul for them in prayer victorious,]{lang="en"}\
  [並且禱告，求神祝福並推廣，]{lang="zh"}
- [And all you spend our Savior will repay.]{lang="en"}\
  [你的犧牲，主必紀念在心。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [He comes again; O brothers, ere you meet Him,]{lang="en"}\
  [應當記得，救主已將快再臨，]{lang="zh"}
- [Make known to every heart His saving grace;]{lang="en"}\
  [機會一過，就難再盡本分；]{lang="zh"}
- [Let none whom He hath ransomed fail to greet Him,]{lang="en"}\
  [只怕有人是因你我不熱心，]{lang="zh"}
- [Through your neglect unfit to see His face.]{lang="en"}\
  [不能得救，也不能親近神。]{lang="zh"}
