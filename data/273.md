# 1

- [Go labor on; spend, and be spent;]{lang="en"}\
  [殷勤作工，費財費力，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy joy to do the Father’s will;]{lang="en"}\
  [甘心樂意，行神旨意；]{lang="zh"}
- [It is the way the Master went;]{lang="en"}\
  [這是我主，所走的路，]{lang="zh"}
- [Should not the servant tread it still?]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的僕人，也當步武。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Go, labor on: ’tis not for nought;]{lang="en"}\
  [殷勤作工，絕不徒然，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy earthly loss is heav’nly gain;]{lang="en"}\
  [地上損失，獲賞在天；]{lang="zh"}
- [Men heed thee, love thee, praise thee not;]{lang="en"}\
  [人的喜厭，不必介意，]{lang="zh"}
- [The Master praises, what are men?]{lang="en"}\
  [有主讚賞，無何可比。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Go, labor on; your hands are weak,]{lang="en"}\
  [殷勤作工，手雖下垂、]{lang="zh"}
- [Your knees are faint, your souls cast down;]{lang="en"}\
  [腳雖發酸、心雖疲憊，]{lang="zh"}
- [Yet falter not; the prize you seek]{lang="en"}\
  [仍不動搖；賞賜已近——]{lang="zh"}
- [Is near, a kingdom and a crown.]{lang="en"}\
  [國度、冠冕，即將來臨。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Go, labor on while it is day,]{lang="en"}\
  [要趁白晝殷勤作工，]{lang="zh"}
- [The world’s dark night is hastening on;]{lang="en"}\
  [今世黑夜，轉瞬將終；]{lang="zh"}
- [Speed, speed thy work, cast sloth away,]{lang="en"}\
  [加緊作工，切莫懶惰，]{lang="zh"}
- [It is not thus that souls are won.]{lang="en"}\
  [否則難將靈魂得著。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Men die in darkness at your side,]{lang="en"}\
  [眼看人死在黑暗中，]{lang="zh"}
- [Without a hope to cheer the tomb;]{lang="en"}\
  [沒有指望，心中虛空；]{lang="zh"}
- [Take up the torch and wave it wide,]{lang="en"}\
  [拿起火把，將其高揚，]{lang="zh"}
- [The torch that lights time’s thickest gloom.]{lang="en"}\
  [好將四周幽暗照亮。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [Press on, faint not, keep watch and pray;]{lang="en"}\
  [儆醒禱告，切莫灰心；]{lang="zh"}
- [Be wise the erring soul to win;]{lang="en"}\
  [智者能得迷途靈魂；]{lang="zh"}
- [Go forth into the world’s highway,]{lang="en"}\
  [要往大路、岔路口去，]{lang="zh"}
- [Compel the wanderer to come in.]{lang="en"}\
  [勉強流蕩者來歸依。]{lang="zh"}

# 7

- [Press on, and in thy work rejoice;]{lang="en"}\
  [一直作工，並要歡暢；]{lang="zh"}
- [For work comes rest, the prize thus won;]{lang="en"}\
  [作工得息，且有獎賞；]{lang="zh"}
- [Soon shalt thou hear the Master’s voice,]{lang="en"}\
  [不久將聞夜半呼聲：]{lang="zh"}
- [The midnight cry, Behold, I come!]{lang="en"}\
  [“看哪，我來！”快樂時辰。]{lang="zh"}
