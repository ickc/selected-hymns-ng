# 1

- [My goal is God Him self, not joy, nor peace,]{lang="en"}\
  [我心所追求，乃是神自己，]{lang="zh"}
- [Nor even blessing, but Himself, my God;]{lang="en"}\
  [不是樂與安，也不是福氣；]{lang="zh"}
- [’Tis His to lead me there not mine, but His]{lang="en"}\
  [我能出代價，達到這聖地，]{lang="zh"}
- [At any cost, dear Lord, by any road.]{lang="en"}\
  [不是我自己，乃是祂恩力。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [So faith bounds forward to its goal in God,]{lang="en"}\
  [因信而向前，要得神自己，]{lang="zh"}
- [And love can trust her Lord to lead her there;]{lang="en"}\
  [憑愛而跟隨，永遠不他依；]{lang="zh"}
- [Upheld by Him, my soul is following hard]{lang="en"}\
  [因祂扶持我，我追隨不已，]{lang="zh"}
- [Till God hath full fulfilled my deepest prayer.]{lang="en"}\
  [直到神答應，我最深禱祈。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [No matter if the way be sometimes dark,]{lang="en"}\
  [雖然這道路，有時真可怕，]{lang="zh"}
- [No matter though the cost be oft-times great,]{lang="en"}\
  [雖然這代價，常是非常大，]{lang="zh"}
- [He knoweth how I best shall reach the mark,]{lang="en"}\
  [但是神知道，何者是我需，]{lang="zh"}
- [The way that leads to Him must needs be strait.]{lang="en"}\
  [得著神的路，永不能彎曲。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [One thing I know, I cannot say Him nay;]{lang="en"}\
  [一事我所知，祂永不會錯，]{lang="zh"}
- [One thing I do, I press towards my Lord;]{lang="en"}\
  [一事我所作，要將祂得著；]{lang="zh"}
- [My God my glory here, from day to day,]{lang="en"}\
  [一天過一天，神是我所要，]{lang="zh"}
- [And in the glory there my great Reward.]{lang="en"}\
  [至終的賞賜，神作我榮耀。]{lang="zh"}
