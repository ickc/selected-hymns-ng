# 1

- [Send Thou, O Lord, to every place]{lang="en"}\
  [哦主，遍地滿是罪人，]{lang="zh"}
- [Swift messengers before Thy face,]{lang="en"}\
  [求你感動信徒之心，]{lang="zh"}
- [The heralds of Thy wondrous grace,]{lang="en"}\
  [快去傳你奇妙救恩，]{lang="zh"}
- [Where Thou Thyself wilt come.]{lang="en"}\
  [好迎接你再臨。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Send men whose eyes have seen the King!]{lang="en"}\
  [差遣那曾親眼見神，]{lang="zh"}
- [Men in whose ears His sweet words ring;]{lang="en"}\
  [並親聞祂妙音的人，]{lang="zh"}
- [Send such Thy lost ones home to bring;]{lang="en"}\
  [去領失喪靈魂歸神，]{lang="zh"}
- [Send them where Thou wilt come.]{lang="en"}\
  [好迎接你再臨。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [To bring good news to souls in sin;]{lang="en"}\
  [傳福音給罪中靈魂，]{lang="zh"}
- [The bruised and broken hearts to win;]{lang="en"}\
  [贏回受傷、破碎之心，]{lang="zh"}
- [In every place to bring them in,]{lang="en"}\
  [從各地帶人歸羊群，]{lang="zh"}
- [Where Thou Thyself wilt come.]{lang="en"}\
  [好迎接你再臨。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Gird each one with the Spirit’s sword,]{lang="en"}\
  [人人佩帶聖靈寶劍，]{lang="zh"}
- [The sword of Thine own deathless word;]{lang="en"}\
  [就是你的活潑聖言，]{lang="zh"}
- [And make them conquerors, conquering Lord,]{lang="en"}\
  [使他們都因你得勝，]{lang="zh"}
- [Where Thou Thyself wilt come.]{lang="en"}\
  [好迎接你再臨。]{lang="zh"}
