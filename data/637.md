# 1

- [And is it so! I shall be like Thy Son?]{lang="en"}\
  [真是如此！我將像你兒子？]{lang="zh"}
- [Is this the grace which He for me has won?]{lang="en"}\
  [這是否祂為我成功恩慈？]{lang="zh"}
- [Father of glory—thought beyond all thought!]{lang="en"}\
  [何其高超，父的榮耀計畫！]{lang="zh"}
- [In glory, to His own blest likeness brought!]{lang="en"}\
  [要帶我進榮耀並且像祂。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Oh, Jesus, Lord, who loved me like to Thee?]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，主耶穌，誰能愛我如你？]{lang="zh"}
- [Fruit of Thy work, with Thee, too, there to see]{lang="en"}\
  [不僅見你榮耀在永世裡，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy glory, Lord, while endless ages roll,]{lang="en"}\
  [在彼並見你勞苦的果子，]{lang="zh"}
- [Myself the prize and travail of Thy soul.]{lang="en"}\
  [我這人乃你魂產難賞賜。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Yet it must be: Thy love had not its rest]{lang="en"}\
  [若非我們與你全然如一，]{lang="zh"}
- [Were Thy redeemed not with Thee fully blest;]{lang="en"}\
  [你愛就仍不能完全安息；]{lang="zh"}
- [That love that gives not as the world, but shares]{lang="en"}\
  [因你愛所賜不像這世界，]{lang="zh"}
- [All it possesses with its loved co-heirs.]{lang="en"}\
  [但與蒙愛者同享它一切。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Nor I alone; Thy loved ones, all complete]{lang="en"}\
  [有日蒙愛者榮耀裡顯現，]{lang="zh"}
- [In glory, round Thee there with joy shall meet;]{lang="en"}\
  [歡樂環繞你，全都已完全；]{lang="zh"}
- [All like Thee, for Thy glory like Thee, Lord,]{lang="en"}\
  [都要像你，因你榮耀像你，]{lang="zh"}
- [Object supreme of all, by all adored.]{lang="en"}\
  [你遠超一切，配萬眾頂禮。]{lang="zh"}
