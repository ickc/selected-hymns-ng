# 1

- [Charge, soldiers, charge in battle! The conflict has begun;]{lang="en"}\
  [沖過！戰士哪，沖過！前線正在開火；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy battle song triumphant—“The victory is won!”]{lang="en"}\
  [但各各他已得勝，請你常唱此歌。]{lang="zh"}
- [Hell’s power must be vanquished And Satan’s forces strewn;]{lang="en"}\
  [黑暗權勢應當退！撒但陰府已危！]{lang="zh"}
- [The Captain of the Army Will come and triumph soon.]{lang="en"}\
  [萬軍之主的元帥，快要奏凱歸回。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Charge, soldiers, charge in battle! And gaze upon thy Lord;]{lang="en"}\
  [“沖過！戰士哪，沖過！仰起你頭看我；]{lang="zh"}
- [If thou wilt fight His battle, The throne is thy reward.]{lang="en"}\
  [你若為我打勝仗，必得登我寶座。”]{lang="zh"}
- [This war is of the spirit, Not fought on earthly ground;]{lang="en"}\
  [這是屬靈的交戰，不在屬世地盤；]{lang="zh"}
- [The Lord well knows the foe is near, Encircling us around.]{lang="en"}\
  [主知仇敵正迫近，前後四圍佈滿！]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Charge, soldiers, charge in battle! Christ’s throne awaiteth thee;]{lang="en"}\
  [“沖過！戰士哪，沖過！ 你要登我寶座；]{lang="zh"}
- [His resurrection power Will lift thy spirit free.]{lang="en"}\
  [使我復活的能力，會使你靈超脫。]{lang="zh"}
- [Through Him thou wilt have power, The foe to battle down;]{lang="en"}\
  [藉我你要得權能，藉我你要得勝，]{lang="zh"}
- [Through Him thou wilt the triumph sing, And wear the victor’s crown.]{lang="en"}\
  [藉我你要唱凱歌，藉我你要高升。”]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [A feast He is preparing, How happy thou to own.]{lang="en"}\
  [我已為你留一席，你要歡喜快樂。”]{lang="zh"}
- [With Him we’re resurrected Above the battle din;]{lang="en"}\
  [我們與祂同復活，同升天上坐著，]{lang="zh"}
- [Transcending all that binds us, We’re far above in Him.]{lang="en"}\
  [遠離戰場的喊聲，遠離綁人罪惡。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Thou hast no part in darkness, Thy future, O how bright;]{lang="en"}\

- [With Christ to dwell forever And in His love delight.]{lang="en"}\
