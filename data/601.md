# 1

- [O eyes that are weary and hearts that are sore,]{lang="en"}\
  [你這奔跑正路、手垂腳酸的人，]{lang="zh"}
- [Look off unto Jesus and sorrrow no more;]{lang="en"}\
  [望斷以及於主，免得疲倦灰心；]{lang="zh"}
- [The light of His countenance shineth so bright,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂面光照燦爛，驅盡困人黑暗，]{lang="zh"}
- [That on earth as in heaven, there need be no night.]{lang="en"}\
  [你若仰望祂臉，就必明如中天。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [“Looking off unto Jesus,” my spirit is blest;]{lang="en"}\
  [望斷以及於主，你靈必蒙恩湛，]{lang="zh"}
- [In the world I have turmoil—in Him I have rest;]{lang="en"}\
  [在世上有苦楚，在祂裡面平安；]{lang="zh"}
- [The sea of my life all about me may roar,]{lang="en"}\
  [在人生苦海中，時有波浪澎湃，]{lang="zh"}
- [When I look unto Jesus I hear it no mom.]{lang="en"}\
  [若仰望祂面容，你心必不驚駭。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [“Looking off unto Jesus,” I go not astray;]{lang="en"}\
  [望斷以及於主，你就不會迷糊，]{lang="zh"}
- [My eyes are on Him, and He shows me the way;]{lang="en"}\
  [你若向祂舉目，祂必指你正路；]{lang="zh"}
- [The path may seem dark as He leads me along,]{lang="en"}\
  [當祂領你前走，即使遇到雲霧，]{lang="zh"}
- [But following Jesus I cannot go wrong.]{lang="en"}\
  [你若向祂仰首，也必不會錯誤。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [“Looking off unto Jesus,” my heart cannot fear,]{lang="en"}\
  [望斷以及於主，你心就必安寧，]{lang="zh"}
- [Its trembling is still when I see Jesus near;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂在你旁看顧，有何使你戰兢？]{lang="zh"}
- [I know that His power my safeguard will be,]{lang="en"}\
  [既知祂的大能，隨時作你保護，]{lang="zh"}
- [For, “Why are you troubled?” He saith unto me.]{lang="en"}\
  [“你為何感不寧？”實在無以答覆。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Soon, soon shall I know the full beauty and grace]{lang="en"}\
  [望斷以及於主，直到被提上天，]{lang="zh"}
- [Of Jesus my Lord when I stand face to face;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂榮耀的面目，完全顯你眼前；]{lang="zh"}
- [I shall know how His love went before me each day,]{lang="en"}\
  [今所仰望榮面，使你路中得力，]{lang="zh"}
- [And wonder that ever my eyes turned away.]{lang="en"}\
  [那日變為頌贊，使你永遠歡喜。]{lang="zh"}
