# 1

- [Teach me Thy way, O Lord, Teach me Thy way;]{lang="en"}\
  [指示你路，我主，指示你路；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy gracious aid afford, Teach me Thy way.]{lang="en"}\
  [用你恩力賜助，使知你路。]{lang="zh"}
- [Help me to walk aright; More by faith, less by sight;]{lang="en"}\
  [助我行走不偏，憑信不憑眼見；]{lang="zh"}
- [Lead me with heav’nly light, Teach me Thy way.]{lang="en"}\
  [用光將我指點，使知你路。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [When doubts and fears arise, Teach me Thy way;]{lang="en"}\
  [正當疑懼四湧，指示你路；]{lang="zh"}
- [When storms o’erspread the skies, Teach me Thy way.]{lang="en"}\
  [正當風雨蔽空，指示你路。]{lang="zh"}
- [Shine thro’ the cloud and rain, Thro’ sorrow, toil, and pain;]{lang="en"}\
  [陽光破雲而現，透過痛苦、愁煩；]{lang="zh"}
- [Make Thou my pathway plain, Teach me Thy way.]{lang="en"}\
  [使我前途平坦，指示你路。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Long as my life shall last, Teach me Thy way;]{lang="en"}\
  [盡我一生年日，使知你路；]{lang="zh"}
- [Where’er my lot be cast, Teach me Thy way.]{lang="en"}\
  [無論處何境遇，使知你路。]{lang="zh"}
- [Until the race is run, Until the journey’s done,]{lang="en"}\
  [直到全程跑完，到達路終標竿，]{lang="zh"}
- [Until the crown is won, Teach me Thy way.]{lang="en"}\
  [贏得公義冠冕，使知你路。]{lang="zh"}
