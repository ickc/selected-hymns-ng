  [平安如水流，正一路跟隨我；]{lang="zh"}

  [憂慮如怒濤勢洶湧；]{lang="zh"}
- [Whatever my lot Thou hast taught me to say,]{lang="en"}\
  [任何的遭遇，你已教我能說：]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Though Satan should buffet, though trials should come,]{lang="en"}\
  [撒但雖肆虐，試煉雖如烈火，]{lang="zh"}
- [Let this blest assurance control,]{lang="en"}\
  [我心仍應當有把握；]{lang="zh"}
- [That Christ hath regarded my helpless estate,]{lang="en"}\
  [因主明瞭我是何等的軟弱，]{lang="zh"}
- [And hath shed His own blood for my soul.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂已為我流血成工作。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [My sin—oh, the bliss of this glorious thought—]{lang="en"}\
  [我罪，哦，這是何等榮耀思想，]{lang="zh"}
- [My sin, not in part, but the whole,]{lang="en"}\
  [我罪，非局部，乃攏總，]{lang="zh"}
- [Is nailed to His Cross, and I bear it no more;]{lang="en"}\
  [全釘祂十架，卻不壓我身上，]{lang="zh"}
- [Praise the Lord, praise the Lord, O my soul!]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，我魂，要稱頌、要稱頌！]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [For me, be it Christ, be it Christ hence to live;]{lang="en"}\
  [活著是基督，是基督在執政，]{lang="zh"}
- [If dark hours about me shall roll,]{lang="en"}\
  [即便那死河起波瀾，]{lang="zh"}
- [No pang shall be mine, for in death as in life]{lang="en"}\
  [我也無苦痛，因雖死猶如生，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou wilt whisper Thy peace to my soul.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂對我正低聲賜平安。]{lang="zh"}
