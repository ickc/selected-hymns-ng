# 1

- [Eternal Light! Eternal Light!]{lang="en"}\
  [永恆之光！永恆之光！]{lang="zh"}
- [How pure the soul must be]{lang="en"}\
  [光照、鑒察我裡，]{lang="zh"}
- [When, placed within Thy searching sight,]{lang="en"}\
  [將我淨化，使我明亮；]{lang="zh"}
- [It shrinks not, but with calm delight]{lang="en"}\
  [面對這光，我心安詳，]{lang="zh"}
- [Can live and look on Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [因我得以見你。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [The spirits that surround Thy throne]{lang="en"}\
  [圍繞寶座天上眾靈，]{lang="zh"}
- [May bear the burning bliss;]{lang="en"}\
  [雖可同沐光明，]{lang="zh"}
- [But that is surely theirs alone,]{lang="en"}\
  [但是他們僅僅享用；]{lang="zh"}
- [Since they have never, never known]{lang="en"}\
  [墮落世界痛苦光景，]{lang="zh"}
- [A fallen world like this.]{lang="en"}\
  [他們絕不知情。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Oh, how shall I, whose native sphere]{lang="en"}\
  [像我生性愚昧昏蒙，]{lang="zh"}
- [Is dark, whose mind is dim,]{lang="en"}\
  [又居黑暗之中，]{lang="zh"}
- [Before th’Ineffable appear,]{lang="en"}\
  [怎能蒙神向我顯明？]{lang="zh"}
- [And on my natural spirit bear]{lang="en"}\
  [渾噩如我，竟也蒙寵，]{lang="zh"}
- [The uncreated beam?]{lang="en"}\
  [永恆之光照明！]{lang="zh"}
