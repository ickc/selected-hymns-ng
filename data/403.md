# 1

- [Oh, for a heart to praise my God,]{lang="en"}\
  [願我全心頌贊我神，]{lang="zh"}
- [A heart from sin set free,]{lang="en"}\
  [因我完全脫罪；]{lang="zh"}
- [A heart that’s sprinkled with the blood]{lang="en"}\
  [寶血已經灑我良心，]{lang="zh"}
- [So freely shed for me.]{lang="en"}\
  [我得平安無虧。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [A heart resigned, submissive, meek,]{lang="en"}\
  [在我親愛救主座前，]{lang="zh"}
- [My dear Redeemer’s throne;]{lang="en"}\
  [我心柔順、簡單；]{lang="zh"}
- [Where only Christ is heard to speak,]{lang="en"}\
  [在此只聞基督之言，]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [A humble, lowly, contrite heart,]{lang="en"}\
  [此心謙卑、憂傷、痛悔，]{lang="zh"}
- [Believing, true, and clean,]{lang="en"}\
  [因信得蒙洗淨，]{lang="zh"}
- [Which neither death nor life can part]{lang="en"}\
  [生死不能改我地位，]{lang="zh"}
- [From Him that dwells within.]{lang="en"}\
  [因主已住我心。]{lang="zh"}

# 4
