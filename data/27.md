# 1

- [“Abba, Father,” We approach Thee]{lang="en"}\
  [阿爸，父阿，我們現在]{lang="zh"}
- [In our Savior’s precious name.]{lang="en"}\
  [因主到你面前來；]{lang="zh"}
- [We, Thy Children, here assembling,]{lang="en"}\
  [你的兒女到此聚集，]{lang="zh"}
- [Now the promised blessing claim.]{lang="en"}\
  [願得應許的福氣。]{lang="zh"}
- [From our guilt His blood has washed us,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的寶血已洗我們，]{lang="zh"}
- [“Tis thro’ Him our souls draw nigh,]{lang="en"}\
  [我們藉祂來得恩；]{lang="zh"}
- [And Thy Spirit too has taught us]{lang="en"}\
  [你靈也已指教我們]{lang="zh"}
- [“Abba, Father,” thus to cry.]{lang="en"}\
  [呼喊阿爸，近你身。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Once as prodigals we wandered,]{lang="en"}\
  [我們從前好像浪子，]{lang="zh"}
- [In our folly, far from Thee;]{lang="en"}\
  [離你流蕩真無知；]{lang="zh"}
- [But Thy grace, o’er sin abounding,]{lang="en"}\
  [但你的恩比罪更多，]{lang="zh"}
- [Rescued us from misery.]{lang="en"}\
  [拯救我們脫災禍。]{lang="zh"}
- [Clothed in garments of salvation]{lang="en"}\
  [給我們穿救恩衣裳，]{lang="zh"}
- [At Thy table is our place;]{lang="en"}\
  [給我們坐你席上；]{lang="zh"}
- [We rejoice, and Thou rejoicest,]{lang="en"}\
  [我們快樂，你也歡喜，]{lang="zh"}
- [In the riches of Thy grace.]{lang="en"}\
  [因你恩典深無比。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Thou the prodigal hast pardoned,]{lang="en"}\
  [你用為父的愛親嘴，]{lang="zh"}
- [“Kissed us” with a Father’s love;]{lang="en"}\
  [表明你赦浪子罪；]{lang="zh"}
- [“Killed the fatted calf,” and made us]{lang="en"}\
  [你宰肥犢，你使我們]{lang="zh"}
- [Fit Thy purpose to approve.]{lang="en"}\
  [永遠與你不再分。]{lang="zh"}
- [“It is meet,” we hear Thee saying,]{lang="en"}\
  [“我們理當歡喜快樂，]{lang="zh"}
- [“We should merry be and glad;]{lang="en"}\
  [”我們聽你如此說：]{lang="zh"}
- [I have found My once-lost children,]{lang="en"}\
  [“因我兒子死而復活，]{lang="zh"}
- [Now they live who once were dead.”]{lang="en"}\
  [曾經失去又得著。”]{lang="zh"}

# 4
