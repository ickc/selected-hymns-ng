# 1

- [The tree of life, how sweet the fruit,]{lang="en"}\
  [生命樹果，甘甜、豐盈，]{lang="zh"}
- [With God as life complete.]{lang="en"}\
  [是神來作生命。]{lang="zh"}
- [I once was dead, but now I live,]{lang="en"}\
  [前我死枯，如今活著；]{lang="zh"}
- [Was starved, but now I eat.]{lang="en"}\
  [前饑，今享豐盛。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [’Twas God that brought me to the tree,]{lang="en"}\
  [是神領我到生命樹，]{lang="zh"}
- [With Christ Himself as meat;]{lang="en"}\
  [享受神的基督。]{lang="zh"}
- [How precious did that tree become]{lang="en"}\
  [其中滋味，甘甜、滿足，]{lang="zh"}
- [When I began to eat.]{lang="en"}\
  [當我親近我主。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [The Lord Himself is food to me,]{lang="en"}\
  [基督是我生命源頭，]{lang="zh"}
- [He is my life supply;]{lang="en"}\
  [作我屬天食物；]{lang="zh"}
- [He will my pure enjoyment be,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂是我的純淨享受，]{lang="zh"}
- [None else can satisfy.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂外再無滿足。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [I freely eat this living tree,]{lang="en"}\
  [我今歡享這生命樹，]{lang="zh"}
- [For eating is the way]{lang="en"}\
  [此路我已得著！]{lang="zh"}
- [To put God’s life inside of me,]{lang="en"}\
  [今藉吃喝，生命加多，]{lang="zh"}
- [To live by Him today.]{lang="en"}\
  [我就因祂活著。]{lang="zh"}
