# 1

- [Not all the blood of beasts,]{lang="en"}\
  [古時祭壇所流，]{lang="zh"}
- [On Jewish altars slain,]{lang="en"}\
  [所有祭牲之血，]{lang="zh"}
- [Could give the guilty conscience peace,]{lang="en"}\
  [不能免除良心內疚，]{lang="zh"}
- [Or wash away its stain.]{lang="en"}\
  [或清洗我罪孽。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [But Christ, the heavenly Lamb,]{lang="en"}\
  [惟有羔羊基督，]{lang="zh"}
- [Takes all our sins away;]{lang="en"}\
  [能將我罪全除，]{lang="zh"}
- [A sacrifice of nobler name,]{lang="en"}\
  [藉此更美、更貴祭物，]{lang="zh"}
- [And richer blood than they.]{lang="en"}\
  [我們真蒙救贖。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [My faith would lay her hand]{lang="en"}\
  [我伸信心之手，]{lang="zh"}
- [On that dear head of Thine,]{lang="en"}\
  [與我恩主相聯；]{lang="zh"}
- [While like a penitent I stand,]{lang="en"}\
  [我今肅立，坦承己羞，]{lang="zh"}
- [And there confess my sin.]{lang="en"}\
  [因此得蒙赦免。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [My soul looks back to see]{lang="en"}\
  [我回顧你所負]{lang="zh"}
- [The burdens Thou didst bear]{lang="en"}\
  [我的罪惡重擔]{lang="zh"}
- [When hanging on the cursed tree,]{lang="en"}\
  [你掛十架、為我受詛，]{lang="zh"}
- [And knows her guilt was there.]{lang="en"}\
  [從此不再虧欠。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Believing, we rejoice]{lang="en"}\

- [We bless the Lamb with cheerful voice,]{lang="en"}\
