# 1

- [As pants the hart for cooling streams]{lang="en"}\
  [我如困鹿切慕溪水，]{lang="zh"}
- [When heated in the chase,]{lang="en"}\
  [裏面乾渴難言；]{lang="zh"}
- [So longs my soul, O God, for Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [哦神，我心惟你是追，]{lang="zh"}
- [And Thy refreshing grace.]{lang="en"}\
  [並你復甦恩典。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Why restless, why cast down, my soul?]{lang="en"}\
  [我的心哪，為何憂悶？]{lang="zh"}
- [Trust God, who will employ]{lang="en"}\
  [應當信靠我神；]{lang="zh"}
- [His aid for thee, and change these sighs]{lang="en"}\
  [祂必使你歎息之聲，]{lang="zh"}
- [To thankful hymns of joy.]{lang="en"}\
  [變為讚美、頌稱。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [For Thee, my God, the living God,]{lang="en"}\
  [活的神哪，你是我神，]{lang="zh"}
- [My thirsty soul doth pine;]{lang="en"}\
  [我心飢渴要你；]{lang="zh"}
- [Oh, when shall I behold Thy face,]{lang="en"}\
  [深願我能親近你身，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou Majesty Divine?]{lang="en"}\
  [面見你的自己。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [God of my strength, how long shall I,]{lang="en"}\
  [神是我的生命、力量，]{lang="zh"}
- [Like one forgotten, mourn,]{lang="en"}\
  [何覺孤苦伶仃？]{lang="zh"}
- [Forlorn, forsaken, and exposed]{lang="en"}\
  [祂從未曾將我遺忘，]{lang="zh"}
- [To my oppressor’s scorn?]{lang="en"}\
  [任令仇敵欺凌。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Why restless, why cast down, my soul?]{lang="en"}\
  [我的心哪，為何憂悶？]{lang="zh"}
- [Hope still, and thou shalt sing]{lang="en"}\
  [祂是你的盼望；]{lang="zh"}
- [The praise of Him who is thy God,]{lang="en"}\
  [故當歌唱、讚美你神，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy health’s eternal spring.]{lang="en"}\
  [因祂活泉歡暢。]{lang="zh"}
