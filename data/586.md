# 1

- [Like a river, glorious is God’s perfect peace,]{lang="en"}\
  [神的完全平安，暢流如江河；]{lang="zh"}
- [Over all victorious in its bright increase;]{lang="en"}\
  [漫過各種艱難，越過越顯多。]{lang="zh"}
- [Perfect, yet it floweth fuller every day,]{lang="en"}\
  [奇妙、甜美平安，天天暢流過；]{lang="zh"}
- [Perfect, yet it groweth deeper all the way.]{lang="en"}\
  [奇妙、甜美平安，越流越深闊。]{lang="zh"}

  [仇敵無法追蹤， 對頭難站立。]{lang="zh"}
