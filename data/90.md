# 1

- [No blood, no altar now,]{lang="en"}\
  [沒有血、沒有壇，]{lang="zh"}
- [The sacrifice is o’er!]{lang="en"}\
  [祭祀已成過去；]{lang="zh"}
- [No flame, no smoke ascends on high,]{lang="en"}\
  [沒有煙冒、沒有火燃，]{lang="zh"}
- [The lamb is slain no more,]{lang="en"}\
  [犧牲再無必需；]{lang="zh"}
- [But richer blood has flowed from nobler veins,]{lang="en"}\
  [更美的血流自更貴的脈，]{lang="zh"}
- [To purge the soul from guilt,]{lang="en"}\
  [洗淨人的污穢，]{lang="zh"}
- [and cleanse the reddest stains.]{lang="en"}\
  [清償人的罪債。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [We thank Thee for the blood,]{lang="en"}\
  [神，我們感謝你，]{lang="zh"}
- [The blood of Christ, Thy Son:]{lang="en"}\
  [為你兒子的血，]{lang="zh"}
- [The blood by which our peace is made,]{lang="en"}\
  [靠它我們被稱為義，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our victory is won:]{lang="en"}\
  [靠它我們得捷；]{lang="zh"}
- [Great victory o’er hell, and sin, and woe,]{lang="en"}\
  [大勝地獄、死亡、黑暗勢力，]{lang="zh"}
- [That needs no second fight,]{lang="en"}\
  [毋需兩次爭戰，]{lang="zh"}
- [and leaves no second foe.]{lang="en"}\
  [不留一個仇敵。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [We thank Thee for the grace,]{lang="en"}\
  [神，我們感謝你，]{lang="zh"}
- [Descending from above,]{lang="en"}\
  [因有天來恩典，]{lang="zh"}
- [That overflows our widest guilt,]{lang="en"}\
  [漫過我們最闊不義，]{lang="zh"}
- [Th’ eternal Father’s love.]{lang="en"}\
  [赦免最深罪愆。]{lang="zh"}
- [Love of the Father’s everlasting Son,]{lang="en"}\
  [我們要讚美你所有的愛，]{lang="zh"}
- [Love of the Holy Ghost,]{lang="en"}\
  [像你榮耀、權柄、]{lang="zh"}
- [Jehovah, Three in One.]{lang="en"}\
  [能力，存到萬代。]{lang="zh"}

- [So glad, and sure, and clear;]{lang="en"}\
  [因為盼望堅固，]{lang="zh"}
- [It holds the drooping spirit up]{lang="en"}\
  [下沉的靈藉以再起，]{lang="zh"}
- [Till the long dawn appear;]{lang="en"}\
  [直至晨曦顯露；]{lang="zh"}
- [Fair hope! with what a sunshine does it cheer]{lang="en"}\
  [有福的盼望、何等的鼓舞、]{lang="zh"}
- [Our roughest path on earth,]{lang="en"}\
  [最疲倦的曠野、]{lang="zh"}
- [our dreariest desert here.]{lang="en"}\
  [最艱難的道路。]{lang="zh"}
