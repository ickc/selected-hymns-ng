# 1

- [Thy Word is like a storehouse, Lord,]{lang="en"}\
  [神的話如豐富寶庫，]{lang="zh"}
- [With full provision there,]{lang="en"}\
  [其中美善充足；]{lang="zh"}
- [And everyone who seeks may come,]{lang="en"}\
  [有心願者都可進入，]{lang="zh"}
- [Its glorious wealth to share.]{lang="en"}\
  [享受它的豐富。]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy Word is like a deep, deep mine,]{lang="en"}\
  [神的話像稀世寶礦，]{lang="zh"}
- [And jewels rich and rare]{lang="en"}\
  [珍寶深深內藏；]{lang="zh"}
- [Are hidden in its mighty depths]{lang="en"}\
  [留待尋求的人前來，]{lang="zh"}
- [For ev’ry searcher there.]{lang="en"}\
  [將它挖掘、分享。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Thy Word is like a starry host:]{lang="en"}\
  [神的話如萬星燦爛，]{lang="zh"}
- [A thousand rays of light]{lang="en"}\
  [射下閃爍光線，]{lang="zh"}
- [Are seen to guide the traveler,]{lang="en"}\
  [引導客旅，使能看見，]{lang="zh"}
- [And make his pathway bright.]{lang="en"}\
  [一路安然進前。]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy Word is like an armory,]{lang="en"}\
  [神的話如武器之倉，]{lang="zh"}
- [Where soldiers may repair,]{lang="en"}\
  [備足全副軍裝，]{lang="zh"}
- [And find, for life’s long battle day,]{lang="en"}\
  [在世一生，如上戰場，]{lang="zh"}
- [All needful weapons there.]{lang="en"}\
  [它使我打勝仗。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [O may I love Thy precious Word,]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，我寶愛你的話語，]{lang="zh"}
- [May I explore the mine,]{lang="en"}\
  [我要挖掘礦區，]{lang="zh"}
- [May I its glorious riches take,]{lang="en"}\
  [屬天珍寶容我盡取，]{lang="zh"}
- [May light upon me shine.]{lang="en"}\
  [並在光中沐浴。]{lang="zh"}
- [O may I find my armor there,]{lang="en"}\
  [找出適用軍裝一穿，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy Word my trusty sword;]{lang="en"}\
  [神的話作寶劍；]{lang="zh"}
- [I’ll learn to fight with every foe]{lang="en"}\
  [面對仇敵，勇往直前，]{lang="zh"}
- [The battle of the Lord.]{lang="en"}\
  [為主勇敢征戰！]{lang="zh"}

  [你屬天的教誨。]{lang="zh"}
