# 1

- [On a hill far away stood an old rugged cross,]{lang="en"}\
  [在遠山聳立著古老的十字架，]{lang="zh"}
- [The emblem of suffering and shame,]{lang="en"}\
  [作為羞辱、痛苦標誌；]{lang="zh"}
- [And I love that old cross where the Dearest and Best]{lang="en"}\
  [我愛這十字架，因主離榮耀家，]{lang="zh"}
- [For a world of lost sinners was slain.]{lang="en"}\
  [來在其上替人受死。]{lang="zh"}

# 1-chorus

- [So I’ll cherish the old rugged cross,]{lang="en"}\
  [我寶貴古老的十字架，]{lang="zh"}
- [Till my trophies at last I lay down;]{lang="en"}\
  [一直到我俯伏主腳前；]{lang="zh"}
- [I will cling to the old rugged cross,]{lang="en"}\
  [我堅持古老的十字架，]{lang="zh"}
- [And exchange it some day for a crown]{lang="en"}\
  [等有天，我將它換冠冕。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Oh, that old rugged cross, so despised by the world,]{lang="en"}\
  [古老的十字架，不少人很輕視，]{lang="zh"}
- [Has a wondrous attraction for me;]{lang="en"}\
  [對我卻有奇妙吸力；]{lang="zh"}
- [For the dear Lamb of God left His glory above,]{lang="en"}\
  [神聖潔的羔羊曾降臨到此世，]{lang="zh"}
- [To bear it to dark Calvary.]{lang="en"}\
  [將它背至髑髏死地。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [In the old rugged cross, stained with blood so divine,]{lang="en"}\
  [古老的十字架，染斑斑的血跡，]{lang="zh"}
- [A wondrous beauty I see;]{lang="en"}\
  [從我眼光何等可悅！]{lang="zh"}
- [For ’twas on that old cross Jesus suffered and died,]{lang="en"}\
  [就在這十字架，主受苦到至極，]{lang="zh"}
- [To pardon and sanctify me.]{lang="en"}\
  [為要賜我赦免、聖潔。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [To the old rugged cross I will ever be true,]{lang="en"}\
  [我永遠要效忠古老的十字架，]{lang="zh"}
- [It’s shame and reproach gladly bear;]{lang="en"}\
  [歡喜受它所受譏誚；]{lang="zh"}
- [Then He’ll call me some day to my home far away,]{lang="en"}\
  [有一天主要來召我回到天家，]{lang="zh"}
- [Where His glory forever I’ll share.]{lang="en"}\
  [永遠享受祂的榮耀。]{lang="zh"}
