# 1

- [Lord, I was blind; I could not see]{lang="en"}\
  [我本瞎眼，不能看見]{lang="zh"}
- [In Thy marred visage any grace,]{lang="en"}\
  [主的面貌所顯恩典，]{lang="zh"}
- [But now the beauty of Thy face]{lang="en"}\
  [但今你的美麗臉面]{lang="zh"}
- [In radiant vision dawns on me.]{lang="en"}\
  [向我射出燦爛光線。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Lord, I was deaf; I could not hear]{lang="en"}\
  [我本耳聾，不能聽見]{lang="zh"}
- [The thrilling music of Thy voice;]{lang="en"}\
  [主口所出一切美言；]{lang="zh"}
- [But now I hear Thee and rejoice,]{lang="en"}\
  [但今歡欣，因已聽見]{lang="zh"}
- [And all Thine uttered words are dear!]{lang="en"}\
  [你的聲音何其甘甜！]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Lord, I was dumb; I could not speak]{lang="en"}\
  [我本啞口，不能發表]{lang="zh"}
- [The grace and glory of Thy name;]{lang="en"}\
  [主名所含恩典、榮耀；]{lang="zh"}
- [But now, as touched with living flame,]{lang="en"}\
  [但今已被聖火點著，]{lang="zh"}
- [My lips Thine eager praises wake.]{lang="en"}\
  [我唇將你讚美、稱道。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Lord, I was dead; I could not stir]{lang="en"}\
  [我本死沉，對你冷漠，]{lang="zh"}
- [My lifeless soul to come to Thee;]{lang="en"}\
  [不肯前來得主豐富；]{lang="zh"}
- [But now, since Thou hast quickened me,]{lang="en"}\
  [但今你已將我點活，]{lang="zh"}
- [I rise from sin’s dark sepulcher.]{lang="en"}\
  [脫離罪的黑暗墳墓。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [For Thou hast made the blind to see,]{lang="en"}\
  [主，你已使盲者複明，]{lang="zh"}
- [The deaf to hear, the dumb to speak,]{lang="en"}\
  [聾者聽見，啞者發言，]{lang="zh"}
- [The dead to live; and, lo, I break]{lang="en"}\
  [死者得活；看哪，我今]{lang="zh"}
- [The chains of my captivity.]{lang="en"}\
  [斷開一切捆我鎖煉！]{lang="zh"}
