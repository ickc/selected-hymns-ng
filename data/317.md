# 1

- [Amazing grace! how sweet the sound,]{lang="en"}\
  [驚人恩典！何等甘甜，]{lang="zh"}
- [That saved a wretch like me!]{lang="en"}\
  [來救無賴如我！]{lang="zh"}
- [I once was lost, but now am found,]{lang="en"}\
  [前曾失落，今被尋見！]{lang="zh"}
- [Was blind, but now I see.]{lang="en"}\
  [前盲，今不摸索！]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [’Twas grace that taught my heart to fear,]{lang="en"}\
  [恩典教導我心懼怕，]{lang="zh"}
- [And grace my fears relieved;]{lang="en"}\
  [又將懼怕除掉；]{lang="zh"}
- [How precious did that grace appear]{lang="en"}\
  [恩典在我初信剎那，]{lang="zh"}
- [The hour I first believed!]{lang="en"}\

# 3

- [The Lord hath promised good to me,]{lang="en"}\
  [主已應許向我施恩，]{lang="zh"}
- [His word my hope secures;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂話就是保證；]{lang="zh"}
- [He will my shield and portion be]{lang="en"}\
  [祂要作我盾牌、永分，]{lang="zh"}
- [As long as life endures.]{lang="en"}\
  [帶我經過此生。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Through many dangers, toils and snares,]{lang="en"}\
  [歷經艱險、勞碌、痛苦，]{lang="zh"}
- [I have already come;]{lang="en"}\
  [我今前來就祂；]{lang="zh"}
- [’Tis grace hath brought me safe thus far,]{lang="en"}\
  [恩典領我跋涉長途，]{lang="zh"}
- [And grace will lead me home.]{lang="en"}\
  [並要帶我回家。]{lang="zh"}
