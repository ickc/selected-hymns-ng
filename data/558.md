# 1

- [Jesus, keep me near the cross,]{lang="en"}\
  [求主使我近十架！]{lang="zh"}
- [There a precious fountain,]{lang="en"}\
  [在此有一寶泉，]{lang="zh"}
- [Free to all—a healing stream,]{lang="en"}\
  [醫治活水，無代價，]{lang="zh"}
- [Flows from Calv’ry’s mountain.]{lang="en"}\
  [流自加略山巔。]{lang="zh"}

# 1-chorus

- [In the cross, in the cross,]{lang="en"}\
  [十字架！十字架！]{lang="zh"}
- [Be my glory ever;]{lang="en"}\
  [永是我的誇耀！]{lang="zh"}
- [Till my raptured soul shall find]{lang="en"}\
  [在此主付的贖價，]{lang="zh"}
- [Rest with Him forever.]{lang="en"}\
  [是我永遠倚靠！]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Near the cross, a trembling soul,]{lang="en"}\
  [前我戰兢就十架，]{lang="zh"}
- [Love and mercy found me;]{lang="en"}\
  [得蒙愛憐、寬饒；]{lang="zh"}
- [There the Bright and Morning Star]{lang="en"}\
  [明亮晨星\^\[「明亮晨星」指主基督\]的光華，]{lang="zh"}
- [Sheds its beams around me.]{lang="en"}\
  [在此仍將我照。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Near the cross! O Lamb of God,]{lang="en"}\
  [哦主，當我近十架，]{lang="zh"}
- [Bring its scenes before me;]{lang="en"}\
  [示我以其情景；]{lang="zh"}
- [Help me walk from day to day,]{lang="en"}\
  [在你十架蔭庇下，]{lang="zh"}
- [With its shadow o’er me.]{lang="en"}\
  [天天助我前行。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Near the cross I’ll watch and wait,]{lang="en"}\
  [就近十架而儆醒，]{lang="zh"}
- [Hoping, trusting ever,]{lang="en"}\
  [時時信靠、仰望，]{lang="zh"}
- [Till I see my Savior’s face,]{lang="en"}\
  [直到被提得上升，]{lang="zh"}
- [Leave His presence never.]{lang="en"}\
