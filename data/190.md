# 1

- [Lord, when the Father ne’er was known,]{lang="en"}\
  [主，當無人認識父時，]{lang="zh"}
- [The Father came through Thee below,]{lang="en"}\
  [父在你裏藉你來地，]{lang="zh"}
- [That we who lived in ignorance]{lang="en"}\
  [好叫我們雖然無知，]{lang="zh"}
- [Might through Thyself the Father know.]{lang="en"}\
  [如要識父只要識你。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [But, Lord, when Thou wast here on earth,]{lang="en"}\
  [但是，當你在世時候，]{lang="zh"}
- [How scarce were those Thyself who knew;]{lang="en"}\
  [識你的人何等的少！]{lang="zh"}
- [A veil there was twixt Thee and them;]{lang="en"}\
  [與你好像間隔很厚，]{lang="zh"}
- [The crowded ‘round, but saw not through.]{lang="en"}\
  [雖在擠你，摸你不到。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Now as the Spirit Thou hast come]{lang="en"}\
  [你今卻在靈裏再來，]{lang="zh"}
- [E’en as the Father came in Thee;]{lang="en"}\
  [有如當初父在你裏，]{lang="zh"}
- [As we through Thee the Father know,]{lang="en"}\
  [我要識父是看父懷，]{lang="zh"}
- [Now through the Spirit we know Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [我要識你在於靈力。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Not with the flesh Thou now art clothed—]{lang="en"}\
  [你今不是披前肉體，]{lang="zh"}
- [Then must Thou walk with toil around;]{lang="en"}\
  [四處奔波勞碌不息，]{lang="zh"}
- [But as the Spirit in our heart]{lang="en"}\
  [你今乃是住我靈裏，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou dost supply Thyself unbound.]{lang="en"}\
  [時刻用靈供應自己。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Thou, Lord, the Father once wast called,]{lang="en"}\
  [曾有一次你就是父，]{lang="zh"}
- [But now the Holy Spirit art;]{lang="en"}\
  [現今的你就是聖靈，]{lang="zh"}
- [The Spirit is Thine other form,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂是你的另一態度，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thyself to dwell within our heart.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂是你的第二情形。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [By knowing Thee as Spirit, Lord,]{lang="en"}\
  [我今因為認識聖靈，]{lang="zh"}
- [We realize Thy life’s outflow,]{lang="en"}\
  [我就認識你的榮耀、]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy glory and Thy character,]{lang="en"}\
  [你的性情和你生命，]{lang="zh"}
- [And all Thy being’s wonders know.]{lang="en"}\
  [認識你的本質奇妙。]{lang="zh"}

# 7

- [Praise to Thy Name now floods our heart;]{lang="en"}\
  [讚美你名，我們的心]{lang="zh"}
- [There is no one as dear as Thee;]{lang="en"}\
  [認識你是何等實在；]{lang="zh"}
- [For since we know how real Thou art,]{lang="en"}\
  [沒有親的比你可親，]{lang="zh"}
- [No other one could lovelier be.]{lang="en"}\
  [沒有愛的比你可愛。]{lang="zh"}
