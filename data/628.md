# 1

- [The Son of God goes forth to war, A kingly crown to gain;]{lang="en"}\
  [神的兒子出戰前方，去得冠冕為王；]{lang="zh"}
- [His blood-red banner streams afar: Who follows in His train?]{lang="en"}\
  [血染大旗遠遠飄揚，誰配加祂陣行？]{lang="zh"}
- [Who best can drink his cup of woe, Triumphant over pain,]{lang="en"}\
  [誰將苦杯飲之如飴，不顧痛苦創傷，]{lang="zh"}
- [Who patient bears his cross below, He follows in His train.]{lang="en"}\
  [甘負十架，忍耐到底，纔配加祂陣行！]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [The martyr first, whose eagle eye Could pierce beyond the grave,]{lang="en"}\
  [歷代殉聖眼光銳利，看透墳墓對方，]{lang="zh"}
- [Who saw his Master in the sky, And called to Him to save:]{lang="en"}\
  [看出主的復活能力，仰望祂的扶幫；]{lang="zh"}
- [Like Him, with pardon on his tongue, In midst of mortal pain,]{lang="en"}\
  [效祂發出赦免之求，在受致死之傷，]{lang="zh"}
- [He prayed for them that did the wrong: Who follows in his train?]{lang="en"}\
  [也求饒恕害己之仇，誰配加其陣行？]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [A glorious band, the chosen few on whom the Spirit came,]{lang="en"}\
  [蒙主選召英勇聖徒，滿得聖靈力量，]{lang="zh"}
- [Twelve valiant saints, their hope they knew, and mocked the cross and flame.]{lang="en"}\
  [充滿盼望，輕看羞辱，匯成榮耀陣行；]{lang="zh"}
- [They met the tyrant’s brandished steel, the lion’s gory mane,]{lang="en"}\
  [十架、烈火、刀劍、猛獸，他們面對死亡，]{lang="zh"}
- [They bowed their necks, the death to feel: who follows in their train?]{lang="en"}\
  [毫不畏懼，欣然接受，誰配加其陣行？]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [A noble army, men and boys, the matron and the maid,]{lang="en"}\
  [複有精兵、壯丁、孩童，賢婦、淑女繼起，]{lang="zh"}
- [Around the Savior’s throne rejoice, in robes of light arrayed.]{lang="en"}\
  [今繞寶座歡樂無窮，身服光明白衣；]{lang="zh"}
- [They climbed by strength divine from heaven, through peril, toil, and pain:]{lang="en"}\
  [他們登上乃靠聖力，歷經勞苦、創傷，]{lang="zh"}
- [O God, to us may grace be given to follow in their train.]{lang="en"}\
  [懇求我神賜我恩、力，配加他們陣行！]{lang="zh"}
