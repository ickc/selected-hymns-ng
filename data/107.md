# 1

- [The veil is rent: Lo! Jesus stands]{lang="en"}\
  [幔子裂開！看哪，]{lang="zh"}
- [Before the throne of grace;]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌站立施恩座前！]{lang="zh"}
- [And clouds of incense from His hands]{lang="en"}\
  [手執香爐，馨香如雲，]{lang="zh"}
- [Fill all that glorious place.]{lang="en"}\
  [榮耀充滿聖殿。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [His precious blood is sprinkled there,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的寶血，一次永遠，]{lang="zh"}
- [Before and on the throne;]{lang="en"}\
  [座上、座前灑遍！]{lang="zh"}
- [And His own wounds in heav’n declare]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的傷痕，在天宣告，]{lang="zh"}
- [The work that saves is done.]{lang="en"}\
  [救贖大工完全！]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [“’Tis finished!” on the cross He said,]{lang="en"}\
  [忽聞“成了！”聲音傳自]{lang="zh"}
- [In agonies and blood;]{lang="en"}\
  [痛苦流血山邱；]{lang="zh"}
- [’Tis finished! now He lives to plead]{lang="en"}\
  [救贖成功，今在父前，]{lang="zh"}
- [Before the face of God.]{lang="en"}\
  [長遠活著祈求。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [’Tis finished! here our souls can rest,]{lang="en"}\
  [“成了！”此聲安撫我魂，]{lang="zh"}
- [His work can never fail;]{lang="en"}\
  [救恩永不敗退；]{lang="zh"}
- [By Him, our Sacrifice and Priest,]{lang="en"}\
  [更美祭物，永遠祭司，]{lang="zh"}
- [We enter thro’ the veil.]{lang="en"}\
  [率領進入幔內。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Within the holiest of all,]{lang="en"}\
  [我們既蒙寶血所灑，]{lang="zh"}
- [Cleansed by His precious blood,]{lang="en"}\
  [坦然進入幔內；]{lang="zh"}
- [Before the throne we prostrate fall,]{lang="en"}\
  [施恩座前，完全俯伏，]{lang="zh"}
- [And worship Thee, our God.]{lang="en"}\
  [神阿！惟你是配！]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [Boldly our heart and voice we raise,]{lang="en"}\
  [靠著寶血，奉主聖名，]{lang="zh"}
- [His name, His blood, our plea;]{lang="en"}\
  [揚起無懼禱聲；]{lang="zh"}
- [Assured our prayers and songs of praise]{lang="en"}\
  [靠著基督，上達於你，]{lang="zh"}
- [Ascend, by Him, to Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [讚美之歌上升。]{lang="zh"}
