# 1

- [Our God and Father we respond anew]{lang="en"}\
  [我父、我神，你愛在子顯明，]{lang="zh"}
- [To love in Christ revealed, and praise Thy name.]{lang="en"}\
  [對此我們再來你前響應。]{lang="zh"}
- [He did Thy will; and for the joy in view,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂行你旨，滿足你的心意；]{lang="zh"}
- [Endured the cross, despising all the shame.]{lang="en"}\
  [忍受十架，使我得你歡喜。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [He, midst His brethren, strikes the note of praise;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂今率領許多弟兄歌唱，]{lang="zh"}
- [We hear His song, our hearts responsive swell;]{lang="en"}\
  [我們和聲，從心響應頌揚。]{lang="zh"}
- [And on this day when He His voice doth raise,]{lang="en"}\
  [當祂在此引聲向你讚美，]{lang="zh"}
- [In spirit we do join and praise Thee well.]{lang="en"}\
  [我們靈裏共鳴，歡然跟隨。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Chosen in Him, Thy well-beloved Son,]{lang="en"}\
  [在你愛子裏面蒙了揀選，]{lang="zh"}
- [We have our part in joy before Thy face;]{lang="en"}\
  [今得有分你前歡呼、頌讚；]{lang="zh"}
- [Predestined, ere this world had yet begun,]{lang="en"}\
  [創世之前己經蒙你豫定，]{lang="zh"}
- [For that blest world, where all bespeaks Thy grace.]{lang="en"}\
  [要我有分於你兒子生命。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Sons loved and loving, whom Thy spirit fills,]{lang="en"}\
  [蒙愛眾子，全由你靈更新，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our endless portion Christ in us to be;]{lang="en"}\
  [有你愛子作那無窮之分；]{lang="zh"}
- [Each with some trait of Him whose love us thrills,]{lang="en"}\
  [每個都有祂的一分特點，]{lang="zh"}
- [For Thy delight and joy eternally.]{lang="en"}\
  [供你喜悅欣賞，直到永遠。]{lang="zh"}
