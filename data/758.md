# 1

| A new commandment I give unto you
| 主賜給我們一條新命令：
| That you love one another As I have loved you,
| 主怎樣愛了我們，我們也相愛；
| That you love one another As I have loved you.
| 主怎樣愛了我們，我們也相愛。
| By this shall all men Know you are My disciples
| 叫眾人認出，我們是主的門徒；
| If you have love one to another.
| 我們若彼此切實相愛。
