# 1

- [Not what I am, O Lord, but what Thou art;]{lang="en"}\
  [非我所是，主阿，乃你所是，]{lang="zh"}

  [只有這個會叫我心安寧；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy love, not mine, bids fear and doubt depart,]{lang="en"}\
  [你（非我）愛會叫疑惑消失，]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Thy Name is Love! I hear it from yon Cross;]{lang="en"}\
  [我從十架聞悉：你名是愛！]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy Name is Love! I read it in yon tomb:]{lang="en"}\
  [我從你墓讀出：你名是愛！]{lang="zh"}
- [All meaner love is perishable dross,]{lang="en"}\

- [But this shall light me through time’s thickest gloom.]{lang="en"}\
