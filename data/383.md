# 1

- [Lord Jesus, I long in Thy presence to live,]{lang="en"}\
  [主耶穌，我羨慕活在你面前，]{lang="zh"}
- [From morning to evening my one world Thou art;]{lang="en"}\
  [在早晨，在晚上，只有一世間；]{lang="zh"}
- [O let not my heart be contented or rest]{lang="en"}\
  [不讓我心快樂，若我在愛慕、]{lang="zh"}
- [When loving or seeking what with Thee doth part.]{lang="en"}\
  [在感覺、或思想無你的事物。]{lang="zh"}
- [Each moment, each day, throughout suff’ring and pain,]{lang="en"}\
  [每一刻，每一天，不論何痛苦，]{lang="zh"}
- [When nought in the world can give comfort or cheer,]{lang="en"}\
  [當世上正沒有甚麼可鼓舞，]{lang="zh"}
- [When sighing and weeping encompasses me,]{lang="en"}\
  [當歎息正不禁，眼淚流滴滴，]{lang="zh"}
- [Lord, still all my sighing and wipe ev’ry tear.]{lang="en"}\
  [主，擦乾我眼淚，平靜我歎息。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Each time when I dream of the goodness of life,]{lang="en"}\
  [我每次如夢想人生的善美，]{lang="zh"}
- [I pray Thee, dear Lord, that Thou in it may be;]{lang="en"}\
  [親愛主，我求你，必須也在內！]{lang="zh"}
- [O do not allow me to choose by myself,]{lang="en"}\
  [不讓我離開你去尋求快樂，]{lang="zh"}
- [Or seek any pleasure that’s other than Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [不讓我憑自己去單獨選擇！]{lang="zh"}
- [Each night when alone in the stillness I lie,]{lang="en"}\
  [當每夜萬籟靜，孤單自處時，]{lang="zh"}
- [I pray Thee, Lord Jesus, that Thou wilt be near;]{lang="en"}\
  [主耶穌，我求你，仍與我同止；]{lang="zh"}
- [Each morning ere dawn comes, while still in my sleep,]{lang="en"}\
  [當每晨未破曉，我仍蒙昧中，]{lang="zh"}
- [Then whispering call me and open my ear.]{lang="en"}\
  [求你來低聲喚，將我耳開通。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Each time, Lord, when reading in Thy holy Word,]{lang="en"}\
  [當每次我虔讀你聖潔話語，]{lang="zh"}
- [I pray that Thy glory may shine on each line,]{lang="en"}\
  [求你用你榮耀照亮每一句；]{lang="zh"}
- [That clearly I’ll see what a Savior I have]{lang="en"}\
  [讓我能明看見：這寶貴救主，]{lang="zh"}
- [And how great salvation that Thou hast made mine.]{lang="en"}\
  [和祂的大救恩，無一不我屬。]{lang="zh"}
- [When helpless I come, Lord, to kneel at Thy throne,]{lang="en"}\
  [當我正無倚靠跪到你座前，]{lang="zh"}
- [I pray Thee to hear me and grant me Thy grace;]{lang="en"}\
  [求垂聽我禱告，賜夠用恩典；]{lang="zh"}
- [If thru my shortcomings Thou hear not my prayer,]{lang="en"}\
  [有時候因有錯，禱告你不聽，]{lang="zh"}
- [Withdraw not Thy presence, O hide not Thy face.]{lang="en"}\
  [別收回你同在，使我感不寧。]{lang="zh"}
