# 1

- [When I survey the wondrous cross]{lang="en"}\
  [我每靜念那十字架，]{lang="zh"}
- [On which the Prince of glory died]{lang="en"}\
  [並主如何在上受熬，]{lang="zh"}
- [My richest gain I count but loss,]{lang="en"}\
  [我就不禁渾忘身家，]{lang="zh"}
- [And pour contempt on all my pride.]{lang="en"}\
  [鄙視從前所有倨傲。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Forbid it, Lord, that I should boast,]{lang="en"}\
  [願主禁我別有所誇，]{lang="zh"}
- [Save in the cross of Christ my God;]{lang="en"}\
  [除了基督的十字架；]{lang="zh"}
- [All the vain things that charm me most,]{lang="en"}\
  [前所珍愛虛空榮華，]{lang="zh"}
- [I sacrifice them to His blood.]{lang="en"}\
  [今為祂血情願丟下。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [See from His head, His hands, His feet,]{lang="en"}\
  [看從祂頭！祂腳！祂手！]{lang="zh"}
- [Sorrow and love flow mingled down;]{lang="en"}\
  [憂情、慈愛和血而流！]{lang="zh"}
- [Did e’er such love and sorrow meet,]{lang="en"}\
  [那有愛憂如此相遘？]{lang="zh"}
- [Or thorns compose so rich a crown?]{lang="en"}\
  [荊棘編成如此冕旒？]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [His dying crimson like a robe,]{lang="en"}\
  [看祂全身滿被水血，]{lang="zh"}
- [Spreads o’er His body on the tree;]{lang="en"}\
  [如同穿上朱紅衣飾！]{lang="zh"}
- [Then am I dead to all the globe,]{lang="en"}\
  [因此，我與世界斷絕，]{lang="zh"}
- [And all the globe is dead to me.]{lang="en"}\
  [世界向我也像已死。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Were the whole realm of nature mine,]{lang="en"}\
  [假若宇宙都歸我手，]{lang="zh"}
- [That were an offering far too small:]{lang="en"}\
  [盡獻我主仍覺可羞；]{lang="zh"}
- [Love so amazing, so divine,]{lang="en"}\
  [愛既如此奇妙、深厚，]{lang="zh"}
- [Demands my heart, my life, my all!]{lang="en"}\
  [當得我心、我命——所有。]{lang="zh"}
