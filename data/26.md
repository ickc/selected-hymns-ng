# 1

- [We praise Thee, glorious Father,]{lang="en"}\
  [榮耀父神，讚美你，]{lang="zh"}
- [As objects of Thy grace,]{lang="en"}\
  [我是你恩對象；]{lang="zh"}
- [Who for Thine own heart’s pleasure]{lang="en"}\
  [今為使你心歡喜，]{lang="zh"}

  [前來向你頌揚：]{lang="zh"}

  [永世標出的眾子，]{lang="zh"}

  [同著基督你愛子，]{lang="zh"}
