# 1

- [How shall I follow Him I serve?]{lang="en"}\
  [我當如何愛我的主？]{lang="zh"}
- [How shall I copy Him I love?]{lang="en"}\
  [如何奉獻使祂歡喜？]{lang="zh"}
- [Nor from those blessed footsteps swerve,]{lang="en"}\
  [不稍離開祂的道路，]{lang="zh"}
- [Which lead me to His seat above?]{lang="en"}\
  [學習如何與祂親密？]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Privations, sorrows, bitter scorn,]{lang="en"}\
  [窮困、憂患、被人厭棄，]{lang="zh"}
- [The life of toil, the mean abode,]{lang="en"}\
  [勞苦重擔，寄身陋屋，]{lang="zh"}
- [The faithless kiss, the crown of thorn—]{lang="en"}\
  [荊棘冠冕，虛偽禮儀——]{lang="zh"}
- [Are these the consecrated road?]{lang="en"}\
  [難道這是奉獻道路？]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Lord, should my path through suff’ring lie,]{lang="en"}\
  [我的途徑若是痛苦，]{lang="zh"}
- [Forbid it I should e’er repine;]{lang="en"}\
  [主，我求你，禁我退縮，]{lang="zh"}
- [Still let me turn to Calvary,]{lang="en"}\
  [使我仍然向你順服，]{lang="zh"}
- [Nor heed my griefs, rememb’ring Thine.]{lang="en"}\
  [追憶你的十架重負。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [O let me think how Thou didst leave]{lang="en"}\
  [讓我思念，你曾如何]{lang="zh"}
- [Untasted every pure delight,]{lang="en"}\
  [離開許多純潔樂事，]{lang="zh"}
- [To fast, to faint, to watch, to grieve,]{lang="en"}\
  [來憂、來餓、來倦、來渴，]{lang="zh"}
- [The toilsome day, the homeless night:]{lang="en"}\
  [無家之夜，不息之日。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [To faint, to grieve, to die for me!]{lang="en"}\
  [為我來憂、來倦、來死，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou camest, not Thyself to please;]{lang="en"}\
  [你來不求自己喜悅；]{lang="zh"}
- [And, dear as earthly comforts be,]{lang="en"}\
  [地上安逸雖難推辭，]{lang="zh"}
- [Shall I not love Thee more than these?]{lang="en"}\
  [我豈愛你不勝這些？]{lang="zh"}
