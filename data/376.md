# 1

- [O Bread to pilgrims given,]{lang="en"}\
  [天程旅客的糧食，]{lang="zh"}
- [O Food for saints to eat,]{lang="en"}\
  [聖徒可食之餅，]{lang="zh"}
- [O Manna sent from heaven,]{lang="en"}\
  [每日嗎哪天所賜，]{lang="zh"}
- [For heav’n-born natures meet;]{lang="en"}\
  [適合屬天性情；]{lang="zh"}
- [Give us, for Thee long pining,]{lang="en"}\
  [長久渴慕享受你，]{lang="zh"}
- [To eat till richly filled;]{lang="en"}\
  [飽嘗你的豐富，]{lang="zh"}
- [Till, earth’s delights resigning,]{lang="en"}\
  [直到地樂全滅跡，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our ev’ry wish is stilled.]{lang="en"}\
  [所有心願滿足。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [O Water, life bestowing,]{lang="en"}\
  [湧流生命的活水，]{lang="zh"}
- [From out the Savior’s heart,]{lang="en"}\
  [流自裂開靈磐，]{lang="zh"}
- [A fountain purely flowing,]{lang="en"}\
  [明晶活泉何甘美，]{lang="zh"}
- [A fount of love Thou art.]{lang="en"}\
  [你是愛的泉源。]{lang="zh"}
- [Oh, let us, freely tasting,]{lang="en"}\
  [讓我白白來暢飲，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our burning thirst assuage;]{lang="en"}\
  [消除我的乾渴；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy sweetness, never wasting,]{lang="en"}\
  [你外，別水不再尋，]{lang="zh"}
- [Avails from age to age.]{lang="en"}\
  [永遠將你來喝。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Jesus, this feast receiving,]{lang="en"}\
  [在此歡然享受你，]{lang="zh"}
- [We Thee unseen adore;]{lang="en"}\
  [我們向你拜禮；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy faithful word believing,]{lang="en"}\
  [相信你信實話語，]{lang="zh"}
- [We take, and doubt no more.]{lang="en"}\
  [接受不再懷疑。]{lang="zh"}
- [Give us, Thou tree and loving,]{lang="en"}\
  [使我從你得餧養，]{lang="zh"}
- [On earth to live in Thee;]{lang="en"}\
  [憑你活在地上；]{lang="zh"}
- [Then, God the veil removing,]{lang="en"}\
  [除去帕子的遮藏，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy glorious face to see.]{lang="en"}\
  [得見你的面光。]{lang="zh"}
