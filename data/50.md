# 1

- [Thy name we love, Lord Jesus,]{lang="en"}\
  [主耶穌，我愛你名！]{lang="zh"}
- [And lowly bow before Thee;]{lang="en"}\
  [謙卑俯伏在你前；]{lang="zh"}
- [And while we live, to Thee we give]{lang="en"}\
  [榮耀、權柄、頌讚、尊敬，]{lang="zh"}
- [All blessing, worship, glory.]{lang="en"}\
  [我活時必向你獻；]{lang="zh"}
- [We sing aloud Thy praises, :]{lang="en"}\
  [向你高歌歌聲沛，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our hearts and voices blending,]{lang="en"}\
  [心口同聲來歌唱，]{lang="zh"}
- [‘Tis Thou alone we worthy own,]{lang="en"}\
  [我魂感佩，因你是配，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy beauty’s all-transcending.]{lang="en"}\
  [你的美麗真非常！]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Thy name we love, Lord Jesus:]{lang="en"}\
  [主耶穌，我愛你名！]{lang="zh"}
- [It tells God’s love unbounded]{lang="en"}\
  [未有天地未有時，]{lang="zh"}
- [To ruined man, ere time began,]{lang="en"}\
  [神愛無際，就已顯明，]{lang="zh"}
- [Or heav’n and earth were founded:]{lang="en"}\
  [要施恩典救亡世；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thine is a love eternal,]{lang="en"}\
  [這愛真是永遠愛！]{lang="zh"}
- [That found in us its pleasure,]{lang="en"}\
  [墮落如我也眷顧！]{lang="zh"}
- [That brought Thee low to bear our woe]{lang="en"}\
  [愛叫你來，為我負債，]{lang="zh"}
- [And make us Thine own treasure.]{lang="en"}\
  [使我成為你珍物。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Thy name we love, Lord Jesus;]{lang="en"}\
  [主耶穌，我愛你名！]{lang="zh"}
- [It tells Thy birth so lowly,]{lang="en"}\
  [你的降生何寒微！]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy patience, grace, and gentleness.]{lang="en"}\
  [你的德馨滿有恩情，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy lonely path, so holy;]{lang="en"}\
  [你的道路孤且卑；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou wast the “Man of Sorrows”]{lang="en"}\
  [我的痛苦你替背，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our grief, too, Thou didst bear it;]{lang="en"}\
  [我的荊冕你替戴，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our bitter cup Thou drankest up;]{lang="en"}\
  [我的禍杯你替嘗備，]{lang="zh"}
- [The thorny crown, didst wear it.]{lang="en"}\
  [常經憂患是為愛。]{lang="zh"}
