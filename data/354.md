# 1

- [Lord Jesus, I love Thee, I know Thou art mine;]{lang="en"}\
  [恩主，我愛你，我深知你屬我；]{lang="zh"}
- [For Thee all the pleasures of sin I resign;]{lang="en"}\
  [一切罪中樂，我為你全擺脫。]{lang="zh"}
- [My gracious Redeemer, my Savior art Thou,]{lang="en"}\
  [你是我救主，曾流血將我買；]{lang="zh"}
- [If ever I lov’d Thee, Lord Jesus, ‘tis now.]{lang="en"}\
  [如果我愛你，主耶穌，是現在！]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [I love Thee, because Thou hast first loved me,]{lang="en"}\

  [為我在加略將贖價已付妥。]{lang="zh"}
- [I love Thee for wearing the thorns on Thy brow;]{lang="en"}\
  [愛你，是因為荊棘冕你肯戴；]{lang="zh"}
- [If ever I loved Thee, Lord Jesus, ’tis now.]{lang="en"}\
  [如果我愛你，主耶穌，是現在！]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [I’ll love Thee in life, I will love Thee in death,]{lang="en"}\
  [活著我愛你，就是死也愛你；]{lang="zh"}
- [And praise Thee as long as Thou lendest me breath;]{lang="en"}\
  [並要讚美你，只要你給氣息。]{lang="zh"}
- [And say when the death-dew lies cold on my brow,]{lang="en"}\
  [即是死臨到，我仍然要表白：]{lang="zh"}
- [If ever I loved Thee, Lord Jesus, ’tis now.]{lang="en"}\
  [如果我愛你，主耶穌，是現在！]{lang="zh"}
