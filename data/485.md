# 1

- [I’ve found the One of peerless worth,]{lang="en"}\
  [我已尋到宇宙至寶，]{lang="zh"}
- [My heart doth sing for joy;]{lang="en"}\
  [我心因此歡呼；]{lang="zh"}
- [And sing I must, for Christ I have:]{lang="en"}\
  [歡呼基督給我得到，]{lang="zh"}
- [Oh, what a Christ have I!]{lang="en"}\
  [何等一位基督！]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Christ is my Prophet, Priest, and King;]{lang="en"}\
  [我這基督是我先知，]{lang="zh"}
- [My Prophet full of light;]{lang="en"}\
  [滿有啟示、亮光，]{lang="zh"}
- [My Priest that stands ‘twixt me and God,]{lang="en"}\
  [也是祭司，神前盡職，]{lang="zh"}
- [My King that rules with might.]{lang="en"}\
  [且是大能君王。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [My Christ, He is the Lord of lords,]{lang="en"}\
  [我這基督是王中王，]{lang="zh"}
- [He is the King of kings;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂是萬主之主；]{lang="zh"}
- [He is the Sun of Righteousness,]{lang="en"}\
  [又是神的公義太陽，]{lang="zh"}
- [With healing in His wings.]{lang="en"}\
  [醫治所有病苦。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Christ is my meat, Christ is my drink,]{lang="en"}\
  [基督是我食糧、活水，]{lang="zh"}
- [My medicine and my health;]{lang="en"}\
  [我的醫藥、健康；]{lang="zh"}
- [My peace, my strength, my joy, my crown,]{lang="en"}\
  [平安、力量、喜樂、安慰，]{lang="zh"}
- [My glory and my wealth.]{lang="en"}\
  [榮耀、冠冕、獎賞。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Christ is my Father, and my Friend,]{lang="en"}\
  [基督乃是我父、我友，]{lang="zh"}
- [My Brother, and my Love;]{lang="en"}\
  [我兄並我心愛；]{lang="zh"}
- [My Head, my Hope, my Counsellor,]{lang="en"}\
  [中保、策士、盼望、元首，]{lang="zh"}
- [My Advocate above.]{lang="en"}\
  [在天為我擔代。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [My Christ, He is the Heaven of heavens;]{lang="en"}\
  [我這基督充滿萬有，]{lang="zh"}
- [My Christ what shall I call?]{lang="en"}\
  [怎能述盡說竭？]{lang="zh"}
- [My Christ is First, my Christ is Last,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂是首先、祂是末後，]{lang="zh"}
- [My Christ is All in all.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂是一切一切！]{lang="zh"}
