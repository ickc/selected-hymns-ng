# 1

- [Rise, my soul, thy God directs thee;]{lang="en"}\
  [我魂，興起！你神領路，]{lang="zh"}
- [Stranger hands no more impede;]{lang="en"}\
  [誰也不能再攔阻；]{lang="zh"}
- [Pass thou on, His strength protects thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [只管前行，祂在保護，]{lang="zh"}
- [Strength that has the captive freed.]{lang="en"}\
  [加力獲釋的囚虜。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Is the wilderness before thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [是否曠野就在你前，]{lang="zh"}
- [Desert lands where drought abides?]{lang="en"}\
  [到處沙漠乾燥地？]{lang="zh"}
- [Heavenly springs shall there restore thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [但那天來新鮮甘泉，]{lang="zh"}
- [Fresh from God’s exhaustless tides.]{lang="en"}\
  [不斷湧出滋潤你。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Light divine surrounds thy going,]{lang="en"}\
  [神聖亮光繞你腳步，]{lang="zh"}
- [God Himself shall mark thy way;]{lang="en"}\
  [神在指定你路徑；]{lang="zh"}
- [Secret blessings, richly flowing,]{lang="en"}\
  [隱密之福，豐滿、充足，]{lang="zh"}
- [Lead to everlasting day.]{lang="en"}\
  [使你靈魂常蘇醒。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [God, thine everlasting portion,]{lang="en"}\
  [活神是你永遠福分，]{lang="zh"}
- [Feeds thee with the Mighty’s meat;]{lang="en"}\
  [大能食物賜給你；]{lang="zh"}
- [Saved from Egypt’s hard extortion,]{lang="en"}\
  [使你不受埃及擾困，]{lang="zh"}
- [Egypt’s food no more to eat.]{lang="en"}\
  [埃及糧食可撇棄。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Art thou weaned from Egypt’s pleasures?]{lang="en"}\
  [你若斷絕埃及福樂，]{lang="zh"}
- [God in secret shall thee keep;]{lang="en"}\
  [神就藏你於隱密，]{lang="zh"}
- [There unfold His hidden treasures,]{lang="en"}\
  [打開寶藏讓你來得，]{lang="zh"}
- [There His love’s exhaustless deep.]{lang="en"}\
  [永浸浸沉神愛裡。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [In the desert God will teach thee]{lang="en"}\
  [在這嚝野神要教你，]{lang="zh"}
- [What the God that thou hast found—]{lang="en"}\
  [認識祂是怎樣神：]{lang="zh"}
- [Patient, gracious, powerful, holy;]{lang="en"}\
  [忍耐、恩慈、神聖、有力；]{lang="zh"}
- [All His grace shall there abound.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的恩典何高深。]{lang="zh"}

# 7

- [On to Canaan’s rest still wending,]{lang="en"}\
  [迦南路上仍當力前，]{lang="zh"}
- [E’en thy wants and woes shall bring]{lang="en"}\
  [雖有缺乏和仇敵，]{lang="zh"}
- [Suited grace from high descending;]{lang="en"}\
  [但祂賜下夠用恩典，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou shalt taste of mercy’s spring.]{lang="en"}\
  [憐憫之泉常湧溢。]{lang="zh"}

# 8

- [Though thy way be long and dreary,]{lang="en"}\
  [路徑雖長並且荒涼，]{lang="zh"}
- [Eagle strength He’ll still renew;]{lang="en"}\
  [神會加力如鷹騰；]{lang="zh"}
- [Garments fresh, and feet unweary,]{lang="en"}\
  [衣裳仍新，腳未受傷，]{lang="zh"}
- [Tell how God hath brought thee through.]{lang="en"}\
  [見證一路神引領。]{lang="zh"}

# 9

- [When to Canaan’s long-loved dwelling]{lang="en"}\
  [神聖大愛領你腳步，]{lang="zh"}
- [Love divine thy feet shall bring,]{lang="en"}\
  [進入迦南那美地，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou, with shouts of triumph swelling,]{lang="en"}\
  [要與眾聖凱歌歡呼，]{lang="zh"}
- [Zion’s songs in rest shall sing.]{lang="en"}\
  [在那錫安永安息。]{lang="zh"}
