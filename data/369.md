# 1

- [Abide with me! fast falls the eventide;]{lang="en"}\
  [與我同住，夕陽西沉迅速；]{lang="zh"}
- [The darkness deepens; Lord, with me abide!]{lang="en"}\
  [黑暗漸深，求主與我同住；]{lang="zh"}
- [When other helpers fail and comforts flee,]{lang="en"}\
  [安慰消逝，且無何人能助；]{lang="zh"}
- [Help of the helpless, oh, abide with me.]{lang="en"}\
  [無助之助，求你與我同住。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Swift to its close ebbs out life’s little day;]{lang="en"}\
  [人生短日，轉瞬就已入暮，]{lang="zh"}
- [Earth’s joys grow dim, its glories pass away;]{lang="en"}\
  [地樂燈殘，世榮花謝草枯；]{lang="zh"}
- [Change and decay in all around I see;]{lang="en"}\
  [四境所見，盡是變遷朽腐；]{lang="zh"}
- [O Thou who changest not, abide with me.]{lang="en"}\
  [永不變者，求來與我同住。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Come not in terrors, as the King of kings;]{lang="en"}\
  [莫帶威嚴，有如王中之王，]{lang="zh"}
- [But kind and good, with healing in Thy wings:]{lang="en"}\
  [只帶慈良，並你施醫翅膀，]{lang="zh"}
- [Tears for all woes, a heart for every plea;]{lang="en"}\
  [淚來洗憂，心來聽我求訴；]{lang="zh"}
- [Come, Friend of sinners, thus abide with me.]{lang="en"}\
  [罪人之友，請來與我同住。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [I need Thy presence every passing hour:]{lang="en"}\
  [你的同在，時時我都需要；]{lang="zh"}
- [What but Thy grace can foil the tempter’s power?]{lang="en"}\
  [除你恩典，何能使魔敗逃；]{lang="zh"}
- [Who like Thyself my guide and stay can be?]{lang="en"}\
  [有誰像你，將我引導扶助；]{lang="zh"}
- [Through cloud and sunshine, oh, abide with me.]{lang="en"}\
  [或陰或晴，求你與我同住。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [I fear no foe, with Thee at hand to bless:]{lang="en"}\
  [有你賜福，我就不怕敵兇；]{lang="zh"}
- [Ills have no weight, and tears no bitterness:]{lang="en"}\
  [病而不苦，流淚也不酸痛；]{lang="zh"}
- [Where is death’s sting? where, grave, thy victory?]{lang="en"}\
  [甚麼毒鉤！甚麼死亡、墳墓！]{lang="zh"}
- [I triumph still, if Thou abide with me.]{lang="en"}\
  [我都勝過，若你與我同住。]{lang="zh"}
