# 1

- [“Consider Him,” let Christ thy pattern be,]{lang="en"}\
  [要思想耶穌，以祂作模型，]{lang="zh"}
- [And know that He hath apprehended thee]{lang="en"}\
  [好在你身上顯出祂榮形：]{lang="zh"}
- [To share His very life, His pow’r divine,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂已經賜你永遠的生命，]{lang="zh"}
- [And in the likeness of thy Lord to shine.]{lang="en"}\
  [並使你享受復活的大能。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [“Consider Him”; so shalt thou, day by day,]{lang="en"}\
  [要思想耶穌，天天都如此，]{lang="zh"}
- [Seek out the lowliest place, and therein stay,]{lang="en"}\
  [要自甘卑微，不再有大志；]{lang="zh"}
- [Content to pass away, a thing of nought,]{lang="en"}\
  [甘心願捨棄虛空的事物，]{lang="zh"}
- [That glory to the Father’s name be brought.]{lang="en"}\
  [將榮耀歸給在天上的父。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Shrink not, O child of God, but fearless go]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，神的兒女，莫退縮畏懼，]{lang="zh"}
- [Down into death with Jesus; thou shalt know]{lang="en"}\
  [與耶穌同死，是我們所需；]{lang="zh"}
- [The power of an endless life begin,]{lang="en"}\
  [纔認識無窮生命的大能，]{lang="zh"}
- [With glorious liberty from self and sin.]{lang="en"}\
  [並得以脫離罪、己的權能。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [“Consider Him,” and thus thy life shall be]{lang="en"}\
  [要思想耶穌，一生當這樣，]{lang="zh"}
- [Filled with self-sacrifice and purity;]{lang="en"}\
  [要活在幔內常見主榮光；]{lang="zh"}
- [God will work out in thee the pattern true,]{lang="en"}\
  [若棄掉自己，必知主心意，]{lang="zh"}
- [And Christ’s example ever keep in view.]{lang="en"}\
  [一脫離自己，純潔又安息。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [“Consider Him,” and as you run the race,]{lang="en"}\
  [要思想耶穌，當你在向前，]{lang="zh"}
- [Keep ever upward looking in His face;]{lang="en"}\
  [要一直向上瞻仰祂榮面，]{lang="zh"}
- [And thus transformed, illumined thou shalt be,]{lang="en"}\
  [必榮上加榮，變成祂形狀，]{lang="zh"}
- [And Christ’s own image shall be seen in thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [使祂的形像顯在你身上。]{lang="zh"}
