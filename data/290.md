# 1

- [And can it be that I should gain]{lang="en"}\
  [救主流血所成救恩，]{lang="zh"}
- [An int’rest in the Savior’s blood?]{lang="en"}\
  [我是何人竟也有分？]{lang="zh"}
- [Died He for me, who caused His pain?]{lang="en"}\
  [罪是我犯，死歸祂受，]{lang="zh"}
- [For me, who Him to death pursued?]{lang="en"}\
  [祂替我死，將我拯救！]{lang="zh"}
- [Amazing love! How can it be]{lang="en"}\
  [驚人之愛，何竟如此？]{lang="zh"}
- [That Thou, my God, shouldst die for me?]{lang="en"}\
  [我主我神為我受死！]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [‘Tis mystery all! The immortal dies!]{lang="en"}\
  [不能死者，竟然受死！]{lang="zh"}
- [Who can explore His strange design?]{lang="en"}\
  [全是奧祕，誰能盡知？]{lang="zh"}
- [In vain the firstborn seraph tries]{lang="en"}\
  [神聖之愛，長闊高深，]{lang="zh"}
- [To sound the depths of love Divine!]{lang="en"}\
  [最高天使也難詳盡！]{lang="zh"}
- [‘Tis mercy all! let earth adore,]{lang="en"}\
  [所有天使不必臆猜，]{lang="zh"}
- [Let angel minds inquire no more.]{lang="en"}\
  [全是憐憫，當得敬拜！]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [He left His Father’s throne above,]{lang="en"}\
  [離開天上父的寶座，]{lang="zh"}
- [So free, so infinite His grace;]{lang="en"}\
  [降世為人，恩何廣闊；]{lang="zh"}
- [Emptied Himself of all but love,]{lang="en"}\
  [虛己、捨身，流血、贖罪，]{lang="zh"}
- [And bled for Adam’s helpless race:]{lang="en"}\
  [為救亞當無望族類；]{lang="zh"}
- [‘Tis mercy all, immense and free;]{lang="en"}\
  [全是憐憫，無量、白給，]{lang="zh"}
- [For, O my God, it found out me.]{lang="en"}\
  [竟然臨及我這罪魁！]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Long my imprisoned spirit lay]{lang="en"}\
  [天性黑暗，邪惡滿心，]{lang="zh"}
- [Fast bound in sin and nature’s night;]{lang="en"}\
  [我靈久為罪惡監禁；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thine eye diffused a quickening ray,]{lang="en"}\
  [你眼垂顧，帶來恩光，]{lang="zh"}
- [I woke, the dungeon flamed with light;]{lang="en"}\
  [我立甦醒，囚牢明亮，]{lang="zh"}
- [My chains fell off, my heart was free,]{lang="en"}\
  [鎖鍊盡脫，全人釋放，]{lang="zh"}
- [I rose, went forth, and followed Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [起來隨主，心中歡暢。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [No condemnation now I dread;]{lang="en"}\
  [不再定罪，何必懼憂？]{lang="zh"}
- [Jesus, and all in Him, is mine!]{lang="en"}\
  [主和一切盡歸我有！]{lang="zh"}
- [Alive in Him, my living Head,]{lang="en"}\
  [活在元首基督裏面，]{lang="zh"}
- [And clothed in righteousness Divine,]{lang="en"}\
  [神聖之義將我遮掩；]{lang="zh"}
- [Bold I approach the eternal throne,]{lang="en"}\
  [坦然來到施恩座前，]{lang="zh"}
- [And claim the crown, through Christ my own.]{lang="en"}\
  [有主基督作我冠冕。]{lang="zh"}
