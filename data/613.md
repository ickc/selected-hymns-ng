# 1

- [“On toward the goal!” Press on! Alone, yet unafraid;]{lang="en"}\
  [當向標竿力前！雖然孤單，不變；]{lang="zh"}
- [He cut the path, who beckons thee, On then, and undismayed.]{lang="en"}\
  [那開路者現在召你，所以當前勿延。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [“On toward the goal!” Press on! The eyes that are a flame]{lang="en"}\
  [當向標竿力前！ 主眼目如火焰，]{lang="zh"}
- [Are watching thee, what then are men? What matter praise, or blame?]{lang="en"}\
  [正在看你；人算甚麼？ 何必管他喜厭！]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [“On toward the goal!” Press on! Look not behind thee now,]{lang="en"}\
  [當向標竿力前！不要再望後面，]{lang="zh"}
- [When just ahead lies His “Well done,” And crowns await thy brow.]{lang="en"}\
  [因為前頭就是獎賞，主要賜給冠冕。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [“On toward the goal!” Press on! Blind, deaf and sometimes dumb]{lang="en"}\
  [當向標竿力前！塞耳、啞口、閉眼，]{lang="zh"}
- [Along the blood-marked, uphill way, Hard after Christ, press on!]{lang="en"}\
  [在崎嶇的血跡路上，緊隨基督向前！]{lang="zh"}
