# 1

- [O Breath of Life, come sweeping through us,]{lang="en"}\
  [生命之氣，來掃蕩我們，]{lang="zh"}
- [Revive Thy Church with life and power;]{lang="en"}\
  [用你靈力復興教會；]{lang="zh"}
- [O Breath of Life, come, cleanse, renew us,]{lang="en"}\
  [生命之氣，來更新我們，]{lang="zh"}
- [And fit Thy Church to meet this hour.]{lang="en"}\
  [使你教會與你相配。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [O Wind of God, come bend us, break us,]{lang="en"}\
  [神的大風，來折服我們，]{lang="zh"}
- [Till humbly we confess our need;]{lang="en"}\
  [直到我們謙卑、順服；]{lang="zh"}
- [Then in Thy tenderness remake us,]{lang="en"}\
  [繼以柔和來再造我們，]{lang="zh"}
- [Revive, restore, for this we plead.]{lang="en"}\
  [祈使我們完全復甦。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [O Breath of Love, come breathe within us,]{lang="en"}\
  [愛的氣息，來吹進我們，]{lang="zh"}
- [Renewing thought and will and heart;]{lang="en"}\
  [更新我們心思、情、意；]{lang="zh"}
- [Come, Love of Christ, afresh to win us,]{lang="en"}\
  [基督之愛，來復新我們，]{lang="zh"}
- [Revive Thy Church in every part.]{lang="en"}\
  [復興教會每一肢體。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [O Heart of Christ, once broken for us,]{lang="en"}\
  [主的心腸，曾為我破碎，]{lang="zh"}
- [’Tis there we find our strength and rest;]{lang="en"}\
  [是我力量、安息之源；]{lang="zh"}
- [Our broken, contrite hearts now solace,]{lang="en"}\
  [今成安慰，若我心痛悔，]{lang="zh"}
- [And let Thy waiting Church be blest.]{lang="en"}\
  [願你教會多蒙恩眷。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Revive us, Lord! Is zeal abating,]{lang="en"}\
  [復興我們！使熱心不頹，]{lang="zh"}
- [While harvest fields are vast and white?]{lang="en"}\
  [眾多莊稼今已成熟。]{lang="zh"}
- [Revive us, Lord, the world is waiting,]{lang="en"}\
  [復興我們！裝備你教會，]{lang="zh"}
- [Equip Thy Church to spread the light.]{lang="en"}\
  [明光照耀，使成祝福。]{lang="zh"}
