# 1

- [Conflict today is fierce, The strength of Satan more;]{lang="en"}\
  [今日戰爭兇猛，仇敵聲勢更強；]{lang="zh"}
- [The cry of battle calling now Is louder than before.]{lang="en"}\
  [戰場呼喊、殺伐之聲，比前更為響亮。]{lang="zh"}
- [The rebel voice of hell E’en stronger now becomes;]{lang="en"}\
  [陰府反抗之聲，遠比以往厲害；]{lang="zh"}
- [But list, the midnight cry resounds, Behold, I quickly come!]{lang="en"}\
  [聽哪，卻有夜半呼聲：“看哪，我必快來！”]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Trials more bitter grow, The fighting doth enlarge;]{lang="en"}\
  [今日試煉更苦， 敵我交戰益烈；]{lang="zh"}
- [Hell’s forces rally all their pow’rs And gather for the charge.]{lang="en"}\
  [陰府軍兵一齊鼓舞， 集隊洶洶來劫 。]{lang="zh"}
- [Yet while we wait and watch And feel the war severe,]{lang="en"}\
  [我在此處守等， 更覺難以得勝；]{lang="zh"}
- [We hear the joyful song ring out, Jesus, the Lord, is near!]{lang="en"}\
  [卻聽傳來喜樂之聲： “耶穌我主已近！”]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [’Tis harder at the end The word to testify,]{lang="en"}\
  [更難證實主話， 在此末後之世；]{lang="zh"}
- [For Satan fights with all his pow’r Our witness to defy.]{lang="en"}\
  [因為撒但傾力攻打， 竭其一切權勢。]{lang="zh"}
- [Much greater strength we need The foe to overcome;]{lang="en"}\
  [我需更大靈力， 纔會擊敗仇敵；]{lang="zh"}
- [How happy when the Lord we see And all our sighing’s done!]{lang="en"}\
  [當主來時不再歎息， 那時喜樂無比！]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Who then will forward go Strong in His mighty power?]{lang="en"}\
  [誰願一直前進， 借著主的大能？]{lang="zh"}
- [Who then will firmly trust the Lord Until the vict’ry hour;]{lang="en"}\
  [誰要堅定一直相信， 直到爭戰全贏？]{lang="zh"}
- [Till with the conqu’rors blest, The triumph song’s begun?]{lang="en"}\
  [誰要靠主壯心， 直到得勝奏凱？]{lang="zh"}
- [That man will then rejoice to hear, Behold, I quickly come!]{lang="en"}\
  [如此之人何等樂聽： “看哪，我必快來！”]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Who then will choose God’s best, And take the narrow track,]{lang="en"}\
  [誰選神的最好， 走這十架窄路；]{lang="zh"}
- [Though passing thru the wildest storms, Yet never turning back?]{lang="en"}\
  [歷盡百般憂患、苦惱， 絲毫也不回顧？]{lang="zh"}
- [Who now will dare press on, Enduring pain and fear?]{lang="en"}\
  [誰敢奮勇直前， 忍受痛苦、懼驚？]{lang="zh"}
- [All such will then rejoice to see Jesus, the Lord, is near!]{lang="en"}\
  [因在此時，歡樂可見：“耶穌我主已近！”]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [Though deep the darkness be We still would onward go,]{lang="en"}\
  [現今黑暗深厚， 我們仍當力前，]{lang="zh"}
- [Till we the day of rapture greet And glory ‘round shall glow.]{lang="en"}\
  [直到我們被提時候， 榮耀就必顯現！]{lang="zh"}
- [’Tis there we’ll see the Lord, And Satan overcome;]{lang="en"}\
  [在彼我們見主， 仇敵已經失敗；]{lang="zh"}
- [The overcomers will rejoice, Jesus, the Lord, has come!]{lang="en"}\
  [得勝者要一同歡呼： 耶穌我主已來！]{lang="zh"}
