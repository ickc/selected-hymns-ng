# 1

- [Far away the noise of strife upon my ear is falling,]{lang="en"}\
  [前在埃及為罪奴僕，不知何為自由；]{lang="zh"}
- [Then I know the sins of earth beset on every hand;]{lang="en"}\
  [良心常有罪的重負，無處得拯救。]{lang="zh"}
- [Doubt and fear and things of earth in vain to me are calling,]{lang="en"}\
  [如今屬地事物纏累，對我再無關係；]{lang="zh"}
- [None of these shall move me from Beulah Land.\^\[Meaning, married (Isa, 62:4.)\]]{lang="en"}\
  [無何能使我離榮美之地。]{lang="zh"}

# 1-chorus

- [I’m living on the mountain, underneath a cloudless sky,]{lang="en"}\
  [今我登巍巍高山，在光天化日之中，]{lang="zh"}
- [I’m drinking at the fountain that never shall run dry;]{lang="en"}\
  [今我飲滾滾活泉，長年湧流無終，]{lang="zh"}
- [O yes, I’m feasting on the manna from a bountiful supply,]{lang="en"}\
  [今我得豐富，地滿乳蜜，路滴脂油，]{lang="zh"}
- [For I am dwelling in Beulah Land.]{lang="en"}\
  [恩重重，樂哉！今我居榮美之地。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Far below the storm of doubt upon the world is beating,]{lang="en"}\
  [前在埃及疑慮風暴，時常將我吹襲；]{lang="zh"}
- [Sons of men in battle long the enemy withstand;]{lang="en"}\
  [仇敵軍兵不斷攪擾，我幾不能敵。]{lang="zh"}
- [Safe am I within the castle of God’s word retreating,]{lang="en"}\
  [今我有主話為堡壘，在其中享安息；]{lang="zh"}
- [Nothing then can reach me, ’tis Beulah Land.]{lang="en"}\
  [無何困我，在此榮美之地。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Let the stormy breezes blow, their cry cannot alarm me,]{lang="en"}\
  [疑慮風暴儘管吹襲，我今不再畏懼；]{lang="zh"}
- [I am safely sheltered here, protected by God’s hand;]{lang="en"}\
  [神的能手足以護庇，我在此安居。]{lang="zh"}
- [Here the sun is always shining, here there’s naught can harm me,]{lang="en"}\
  [在此日光經常照耀，再無禍害臨及；]{lang="zh"}
- [I am safe forever in Beulah Land.]{lang="en"}\
  [何等安然，在此榮美之地。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Viewing here the works of God, I sink in contemplation,]{lang="en"}\
  [我今默念神的作為，何等甘甜、滿足；]{lang="zh"}

  [祂的恩言將我安慰，祂領我一路。]{lang="zh"}
- [Dwelling in the spirit, here I learn of full salvation,]{lang="en"}\
  [靈中享受完全救恩，其樂無何可比；]{lang="zh"}
- [Gladly will I tarry in Beulah Land.]{lang="en"}\
  [我愛長久居此榮美之地。]{lang="zh"}
