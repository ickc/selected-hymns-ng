# 1

- [Once I was bound by sin’s galling fetters,]{lang="en"}\
  [罪惡的鎖鍊，曾使我難當，]{lang="zh"}
- [Chained like a slave I struggled in vain;]{lang="en"}\
  [我如同囚奴，掙扎枉然；]{lang="zh"}
- [But I received a glorious freedom,]{lang="en"}\
  [但我今得著奇妙的釋放，]{lang="zh"}
- [When Jesus broke my fetters in twain.]{lang="en"}\
  [就是主耶穌打碎鎖鍊。]{lang="zh"}
