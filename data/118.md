# 1

- [At Your feet we fall, mighty risen Lord,]{lang="en"}\
  [全能的主神，你腳前俯伏，]{lang="zh"}
- [As we come before Your throne to worship You.]{lang="en"}\
  [我們來到你座前獻上敬拜。]{lang="zh"}
- [By Your Spirit’s power You now draw our hearts,]{lang="en"}\
  [藉你的靈力，使我們心服，]{lang="zh"}
- [And we hear Your voice in triumph ringing clear.]{lang="en"}\
  [你的得勝聲音清晰地傳來：]{lang="zh"}

# 1-chorus

- [I am He that liveth, that liveth and was dead.]{lang="en"}\
  [“我是那存活的；曾死過、又活了。]{lang="zh"}
- [Behold I am alive for evermore.]{lang="en"}\
  [看哪，我直活到永永遠遠！”]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [There we see You stand, mighty risen Lord,]{lang="en"}\
  [全能的主神，何等的威容！]{lang="zh"}
- [Clothed in garments pure and holy, shining bright.]{lang="en"}\
  [身穿神聖、潔白、明亮的衣裳，]{lang="zh"}
- [Eyes of flashing fire, feet like burnished bronze,]{lang="en"}\
  [眼目如火焰，腳像光明銅，]{lang="zh"}
- [And the sound of many waters is Your voice.]{lang="en"}\
  [聲音如同眾水的聲音響亮。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Like the shining sun in its noonday strength,]{lang="en"}\
  [全能的主神，何等的面貌！]{lang="zh"}
- [We now see the glory of Your wondrous face.]{lang="en"}\
  [榮耀且奇妙，如同烈日放光！]{lang="zh"}
- [Once that face was marred, but now You’re glorified,]{lang="en"}\
  [這臉曾被唾，今已得榮耀！]{lang="zh"}
- [And Your words like a two-edged sword have mighty power.]{lang="en"}\
  [你的話如兩刃利劍般鋒芒。]{lang="zh"}
