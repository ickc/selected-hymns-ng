# 1

His Name is Wonderful;
祂的名是奇妙，
His Name is Counselor;
祂的名是策士，
His Name The Mighty God,
祂是全能的神，
Jesus my Lord.
永在的父；
A Child and Son is He;
有一嬰孩降生，
Eternal Father He;
一子賜給我們，
The Prince of Peace to me,
祂是和平的君，
Jesus my Lord.
耶穌我主。
Praise the Creator
讚美創造主，
Jesus our Savior,
耶穌我救主，
Life-giving Spirit now.
今成賜生命的靈；
In spirit worship Him,
在靈裏敬拜祂，
Love and adore Him;
愛祂並事奉祂，
His Name is Wonderful,
祂的名是奇妙，
Jesus my Lord.
耶穌我主。
