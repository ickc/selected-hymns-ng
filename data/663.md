# 1

- [There is a stream which issues forth]{lang="en"}\
  [從神和羔羊的寶座，]{lang="zh"}
- [From God’s eternal throne]{lang="en"}\
  [流出一道水河；]{lang="zh"}
- [And from the Lamb, a living stream,]{lang="en"}\
  [河水明亮如同水晶，]{lang="zh"}
- [Clear as the crystal stone.]{lang="en"}\
  [滿有生命供應。]{lang="zh"}
- [In it doth grow the tree of life,]{lang="en"}\
  [其中有生命樹長著，]{lang="zh"}
- [It makes the ransomed sing:]{lang="en"}\
  [使神贖民歡樂；]{lang="zh"}
- [Its living draught revives the heart;]{lang="en"}\
  [飲此活泉蘇醒人心，]{lang="zh"}
- [Hence all the joys do spring.]{lang="en"}\
  [喜樂湧流不盡。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Such joys as are unspeakable,]{lang="en"}\
  [如此喜樂無法發表，]{lang="zh"}
- [And full of glory too;]{lang="en"}\
  [並且滿了榮耀；]{lang="zh"}
- [Such splendid riches, meat and drink,]{lang="en"}\
  [其中豐富可吃可喝，]{lang="zh"}
- [As worldlings do not know.]{lang="en"}\
  [世人未曾嘗過。]{lang="zh"}
- [Eye hath not seen, nor ear hath heard,]{lang="en"}\
  [眼未曾見、耳未曾聞，]{lang="zh"}
- [For fancy ’tis concealed,]{lang="en"}\
  [人未曾曆其境；]{lang="zh"}
- [What Thou, Lord, hast laid up for Thine]{lang="en"}\
  [乃為屬主者所保藏，]{lang="zh"}
- [And hast to me revealed.]{lang="en"}\
  [今已向我顯彰。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [I feel Thy touch, I hear Thy voice,]{lang="en"}\
  [我覺你摸、我聞你言，]{lang="zh"}
- [I taste Thy sweetest grace;]{lang="en"}\
  [我嘗你恩甘甜；]{lang="zh"}
- [My soul doth leap: but, oh, that I]{lang="en"}\
  [我魂跳躍、我心所羨，]{lang="zh"}
- [May see Thee face to face!]{lang="en"}\
  [親眼看見你面！]{lang="zh"}
- [Then with the saints my soul shall feast]{lang="en"}\
  [與眾聖徒享你豐筵，]{lang="zh"}
- [On joys that always last:]{lang="en"}\
  [此樂長久不變；]{lang="zh"}
- [Blest be my God, the God of joy,]{lang="en"}\
  [喜樂之神賜恩何豐，]{lang="zh"}
- [That gives here such a taste!]{lang="en"}\
  [配得永遠讚頌！]{lang="zh"}
