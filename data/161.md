# 1

- [O Jesus Lord, when present at Thy table,]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，主耶穌，我們在你前聚集，]{lang="zh"}
- [And on the bread and on the wine we gaze;]{lang="en"}\
  [為著餅、杯我們感激不已；]{lang="zh"}
- [We praise Thee, Lord, that Thou as food art able]{lang="en"}\
  [同心讚美，你竟作生命之糧，]{lang="zh"}
- [To be enjoyed by man in many ways.]{lang="en"}\
  [將你自己多方供人飽嘗。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [O what a shame—when Christ with all His riches,]{lang="en"}\
  [何等可憐——當基督來到人群，]{lang="zh"}
- [Has come into the world, life to supply—]{lang="en"}\
  [滿帶豐盛，作人生命供應，]{lang="zh"}
- [That man would live, not by Him, but by teachings,]{lang="en"}\
  [人竟拒絕，反倚靠道理教訓，]{lang="zh"}
- [So powerless these riches to apply.]{lang="en"}\
  [不肯到祂跟前來得生命，]{lang="zh"}

# 2-chorus

- [But all we need to do is eat the Lord;]{lang="en"}\
  [我們所需乃是把你享受，]{lang="zh"}
- [He’s marvelous, He’s wonderful!]{lang="en"}\
  [何等奇妙，何等豐厚！]{lang="zh"}
- [And as we eat, we’re inwardly restored;]{lang="en"}\
  [如此享受，裏面就得飽足，]{lang="zh"}
- [Christ grows in us, grows to the full.]{lang="en"}\
  [長大成人，活出基督。]{lang="zh"}

  [哦，主耶穌，你降卑來到塵世，]{lang="zh"}
