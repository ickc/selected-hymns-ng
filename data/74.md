# 1

- [How beauteous were the marks divine,]{lang="en"}\
  [主，你溫柔所留印記，]{lang="zh"}
- [That in Thy meekness used to shine,]{lang="en"}\
  [照耀神聖，何其美麗！]{lang="zh"}
- [That lit Thy lonely pathway, trod]{lang="en"}\
  [在你所行孤單路中，]{lang="zh"}
- [In wondrous love, O Son of God!]{lang="en"}\
  [你腳留下愛的行蹤。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [O who like Thee, so mild, so bright,]{lang="en"}\
  [你這人子，光中之光，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou Son of man, Thou Light of light?]{lang="en"}\
  [有誰像你柔細、明亮？]{lang="zh"}
- [O who like Thee did ever go]{lang="en"}\
  [有誰像你如此忍耐，]{lang="zh"}
- [So patient, through a world of woe?]{lang="en"}\
  [經過這一憂患塵埃？]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [O who like Thee so humbly bore]{lang="en"}\
  [被人辱罵向不還口，]{lang="zh"}
- [The scorn, the scoffs of men, before?]{lang="en"}\
  [遭受苦害從無怨尤；]{lang="zh"}
- [So meek, so lowly, yet so high,]{lang="en"}\
  [何其溫柔、何其謙卑，]{lang="zh"}
- [So glorious in humility?]{lang="en"}\
  [謙和之中又何高貴！]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [And death, that sets the prisoner free,]{lang="en"}\
  [為使罪囚得著自由，]{lang="zh"}
- [Was pang, and scoff, and scorn to Thee;]{lang="en"}\
  [囚犯之死甘願忍受；]{lang="zh"}
- [Yet love through all Thy torture glowed,]{lang="en"}\
  [藉此你愛顯明無留，]{lang="zh"}
- [And mercy with Thy life-blood flowed.]{lang="en"}\
  [憐憫隨著你血湧流。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [O wondrous Lord, my soul would be]{lang="en"}\
  [奇妙的主，我心渴望：]{lang="zh"}
- [Still more and more conformed to Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [更多模成你的形像，]{lang="zh"}
- [And learn of Thee, the lowly One,]{lang="en"}\
  [像你謙卑，學你式微，]{lang="zh"}
- [And like Thee, all my journey run.]{lang="en"}\
  [你的腳蹤終生跟隨。]{lang="zh"}
