# 1

- [How firm a foundation, ye saints of the Lord,]{lang="en"}\
  [穩固的根基，為信徒已建立，]{lang="zh"}
- [Is laid for your faith in His excellent word!]{lang="en"}\
  [在神話語中，其穩固勝天地。]{lang="zh"}
- [What more can He say than to you He hath said,]{lang="en"}\
  [你既投靠主，如投到避難所，]{lang="zh"}
- [To you who for refuge to Jesus have fled?]{lang="en"}\
  [神話就保證你永遠是穩妥。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [“Fear not, I am with thee, O be not dismayed,]{lang="en"}\
  [“我與你同在，無需喪膽、恐懼，]{lang="zh"}
- [For I am thy God, and will still give thee aid;]{lang="en"}\
  [因我是你神，仍是你的幫助；]{lang="zh"}
- [I’ll strengthen thee, help thee, and cause thee to stand,]{lang="en"}\
  [我加力給你，助你站穩不移，]{lang="zh"}
- [Upheld by My righteous, omnipotent hand.]{lang="en"}\
  [我公義能手，永遠將你扶住。”]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [“When through the deep waters I call thee to go,]{lang="en"}\
  [“我要帶領你，經過深水、幽谷，]{lang="zh"}
- [The rivers of sorrow shall not overflow;]{lang="en"}\
  [憂愁的水流，必不將你淹埋；]{lang="zh"}
- [For I will be with thee, thy troubles to bless,]{lang="en"}\
  [我必看顧你，苦難轉為祝福，]{lang="zh"}
- [And sanctify to thee thy deepest distress.]{lang="en"}\
  [在你受壓時，使你聖別、開懷。”]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [“When through fiery trials thy pathway shall lie,]{lang="en"}\
  [“你被試煉時，我要為你開路，]{lang="zh"}
- [My grace, all sufficient, shall be thy supply;]{lang="en"}\
  [足夠的恩典，要作你的幫助；]{lang="zh"}
- [The flame shall not hurt thee; I only design]{lang="en"}\
  [火不至傷你，它只燒去雜物，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy dross to consume, and thy gold to refine.]{lang="en"}\
  [我安排這火，要將精金煉出。”]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [“E’en down to old age all My people shall prove]{lang="en"}\
  [“一直到年老，我子民必證實]{lang="zh"}
- [My sovereign, eternal, unchangeable love;]{lang="en"}\
  [我主宰權柄，永遠不變的愛；]{lang="zh"}
- [And then, when grey hairs shall their temples adorn,]{lang="en"}\
  [他們的白髮，要作頭上裝飾，]{lang="zh"}
- [Like lambs they shall still in My bosom be borne.]{lang="en"}\
  [如同眾羊羔，安詳躺臥我懷。”]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [“The soul that on Jesus hath leaned for repose,]{lang="en"}\
  [“凡靠在我懷尋求安息之人，]{lang="zh"}
- [I will not, I will not desert to his foes;]{lang="en"}\
  [我必不將他撇棄給他仇敵；]{lang="zh"}
- [That soul, though all hell should endeavor to shake,]{lang="en"}\
  [陰府的全軍，雖想搖動這魂，]{lang="zh"}
- [I’ll never, no never, no never forsake!”]{lang="en"}\
  [但我必永不，永不將他丟棄！”]{lang="zh"}
