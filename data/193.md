# 1

- [Oh, Jesus, Lord, when Thou on earth]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，主耶穌，當你在地，]{lang="zh"}
- [Accompanied Thy faithful band,]{lang="en"}\
  [他們與你多年同處；]{lang="zh"}
- [It seemed they knew, but knew Thee not,]{lang="en"}\
  [但是他們對你自己，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thyself they did not understand.]{lang="en"}\
  [似識不識，似悟不悟。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [They heart Thy voice, beheld Thy form,]{lang="en"}\
  [他們聽過你的聲音，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy beauteous stature unforgot;]{lang="en"}\
  [他們見過你的丰姿，]{lang="zh"}
- [They crowded ‘round Thee, met Thy gaze,]{lang="en"}\
  [他們擠過你的肉身，]{lang="zh"}
- [But who Thou art, they knew it not.]{lang="en"}\
  [但你是誰，似知不知。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [As they of old we too are veiled;]{lang="en"}\
  [我們好像蓋重幔子，]{lang="zh"}
- [We’ve known Thee long apparently;]{lang="en"}\
  [彷彿知道，又不透明，]{lang="zh"}
- [Yet if we say we know, at best]{lang="en"}\
  [說不認識，早已認識，]{lang="zh"}
- [We know Thee not transparently.]{lang="en"}\
  [說已認識，認識不清。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [But as the Spirit now Thou art,]{lang="en"}\
  [但你今日已在靈裏，]{lang="zh"}
- [Another Comforter become;]{lang="en"}\
  [成為另一位保惠師，]{lang="zh"}
- [Reveal Thyself within my heart,]{lang="en"}\
  [並且住在我的心裏，]{lang="zh"}
- [Since to Thy temple Thou hast come.]{lang="en"}\
  [將你自己向我啟示。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [With Thy blest Spirit, Lord, fill me,]{lang="en"}\
  [但願你用聖靈充滿]{lang="zh"}
- [Fill every corner of my soul;]{lang="en"}\
  [在我全人每一角落，]{lang="zh"}
- [May Thou inspire my every part,]{lang="en"}\
  [沒有一處不受你感，]{lang="zh"}
- [Oh may Thou touch, renew the whole.]{lang="en"}\
  [沒有一處不被你摸。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [The Spirit then revealing make]{lang="en"}\
  [求你自己藉靈顯現，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thyself more real within my heart,]{lang="en"}\
  [加倍實在，在我心懷；]{lang="zh"}
- [That ear not hear, nor eye may see]{lang="en"}\
  [無耳所聽，無目所見，]{lang="zh"}
- [How very real to me Thou art.]{lang="en"}\
  [無手所摸，如此實在。]{lang="zh"}

# 7

- [When Thou in mercy dost reveal]{lang="en"}\

- [And give Thyself as Spirit thus,]{lang="en"}\
  [將你自己給了我們，]{lang="zh"}
- [What in the world can be more real,]{lang="en"}\
  [世上有誰比你更實？]{lang="zh"}
- [More true than what Thou art to us?]{lang="en"}\

# 8

- [Thy Spirit, Lord, in mine, I pray,]{lang="en"}\
  [求你用靈從我的靈，]{lang="zh"}
- [O’erflow my being as a flood,]{lang="en"}\
  [如同洪水漫溢全人：]{lang="zh"}
- [That every part with glory shine]{lang="en"}\
  [到處榮耀，到處光明，]{lang="zh"}
- [And everywhere be Thee and God.]{lang="en"}\
  [到處是你，到處是神。]{lang="zh"}
