# 1

- [Through all the changing scenes of life,]{lang="en"}\
  [歷經人世諸多變故，]{lang="zh"}
- [In trouble and in joy,]{lang="en"}\
  [喜樂或是痛苦，]{lang="zh"}
- [The praises of my God shall still]{lang="en"}\
  [我心、我口仍要讚美]{lang="zh"}
- [My heart and tongue employ.]{lang="en"}\
  [我神所賜恩惠。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Oh, magnify the Lord with me,]{lang="en"}\
  [與我同將恩主稱頌，]{lang="zh"}
- [With me exalt His name;]{lang="en"}\
  [同來高舉主名；]{lang="zh"}
- [When in distress to Him I called,]{lang="en"}\
  [當我為難，向祂呼求，]{lang="zh"}
- [He to my rescue came.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂立前來拯救。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [The hosts of God encamp around]{lang="en"}\
  [神的使者四圍安營，]{lang="zh"}
- [The dwellings of the just;]{lang="en"}\
  [衛護義人居所；]{lang="zh"}
- [Deliverance He affords to all]{lang="en"}\
  [凡信靠祂救拔的人，]{lang="zh"}
- [Who on His succor trust.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂必拯救解脫。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Oh, make but trial of His love,]{lang="en"}\
  [所有試煉出自祂愛，]{lang="zh"}
- [Experience will decide]{lang="en"}\
  [故當憑信依賴，]{lang="zh"}
- [How blest they are, and only they,]{lang="en"}\
  [事實經驗都必證實，]{lang="zh"}
- [Who in His truth confide.]{lang="en"}\
  [靠祂何等有福。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Fear Him, ye saints, and you will then]{lang="en"}\
  [聖徒們哪，當敬畏祂，]{lang="zh"}
- [Have nothing else to fear;]{lang="en"}\
  [纔能無所懼怕；]{lang="zh"}
- [Make you His service your delight,]{lang="en"}\
  [當以服事祂為喜樂，]{lang="zh"}
- [Your wants shall be His care.]{lang="en"}\
