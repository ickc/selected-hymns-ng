# 1

- [O Master, let me walk with Thee]{lang="en"}\
  [求主容我與你同行，]{lang="zh"}
- [In lowly paths of service free;]{lang="en"}\
  [卑微路上甘心事奉；]{lang="zh"}
- [Tell me Thy secret, help me bear]{lang="en"}\
  [告我祕訣，助我忍受]{lang="zh"}
- [The strain of toil, the fret of care.]{lang="en"}\
  [勞苦、緊張、煩惱、憂愁。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Help me the slow of heart to move]{lang="en"}\
  [求主助我愛中說話，]{lang="zh"}
- [By some clear, winning word of love;]{lang="en"}\
  [好將遲鈍之心感化；]{lang="zh"}
- [Teach me the wayward feet to stay,]{lang="en"}\
  [教我穩定別人腳步，]{lang="zh"}
- [And guide them in the homeward way.]{lang="en"}\
  [將人引到回家道路。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Teach me Thy patience; still with Thee]{lang="en"}\
  [求主教我學你忍耐，]{lang="zh"}
- [In closer, dearer company,]{lang="en"}\
  [使我更近你的胸懷；]{lang="zh"}
- [In work that keeps faith sweet and strong,]{lang="en"}\
  [在工作上信心剛強，]{lang="zh"}
- [In trust that triumphs over wrong.]{lang="en"}\
  [靠你勝過錯誤情況。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [In hope that sends a shining ray]{lang="en"}\
  [盼望之光，照我前途，]{lang="zh"}
- [Far down the future’s broadening way,]{lang="en"}\
  [使我不致偏離正路；]{lang="zh"}
- [In peace that only Thou canst give,]{lang="en"}\
  [在你所賜平安之中，]{lang="zh"}
- [With Thee, O Master, let me live.]{lang="en"}\
  [與你同行，隨你行動。]{lang="zh"}
