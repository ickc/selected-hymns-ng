# 1

- [O Lord my God, when I in awesome wonder,]{lang="en"}\
  [當我思念，我主，你創造大工，]{lang="zh"}
- [Consider all the works Thy hand hath made;]{lang="en"}\
  [我心立即肅然讚歎驚奇！]{lang="zh"}
- [I see the stars, I hear the mighty thunder,]{lang="en"}\
  [我見眾星，又聞那雷聲隆隆，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy power through out the universe displayed.]{lang="en"}\
  [宇宙到處顯示你的能力！]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [When through the woods and forest glades I wander,]{lang="en"}\
  [當我漫步，在林間樹下徘徊，]{lang="zh"}
- [And hear the birds sing sweetly in the trees:]{lang="en"}\
  [鳥語啾啾，柔美唱和樹梢；]{lang="zh"}
- [When I look down from lofty mountain grandeur,]{lang="en"}\
  [當我佇立，從山巔俯視四圍，]{lang="zh"}
- [And hear the brook and feel the gentle breeze:]{lang="en"}\
  [澗水潺潺，和風息息拂到。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [And when I think, that God, His son not sparing,]{lang="en"}\
  [當我思想，神竟不吝祂愛子，]{lang="zh"}
- [Sent Him to die, I scarce can take it in:]{lang="en"}\
  [差祂受死，我真難以領會；]{lang="zh"}
- [That on the cross, my burden gladly bearing,]{lang="en"}\
  [十字架上，甘為我擔罪不辭，]{lang="zh"}
- [He bled and died to take away my sin:]{lang="en"}\
  [流血至死，使我得蒙赦罪。]{lang="zh"}

- [When Christ shall come, with shout of acclamation,]{lang="en"}\
