# 1

- [Before the throne of God above]{lang="en"}\
  [遠在高天神寶座前，]{lang="zh"}
- [I have a strong, a perfect plea;]{lang="en"}\
  [我有一位強力中保；]{lang="zh"}
- [A great High Priest, whose Name is Love,]{lang="en"}\
  [這大祭司，祂名是愛，]{lang="zh"}
- [Who ever lives and pleads for me.]{lang="en"}\
  [長遠活著，為我代禱。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [My name is graven on His hands,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂將我名刻在祂手，]{lang="zh"}
- [My name is written on His heart;]{lang="en"}\
  [又將我名記在心裏；]{lang="zh"}
- [I know that while in heaven He stands]{lang="en"}\
  [祂今在天站立神右，]{lang="zh"}
- [No tongue can bid me thence depart.]{lang="en"}\
  [誰也不能將我驅離。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [When Satan tempts me to despair,]{lang="en"}\
  [撒但在我心裡控告，]{lang="zh"}
- [And tells me of the guilt within,]{lang="en"}\
  [企圖使我喪氣、絆跌，]{lang="zh"}
- [Upward I look, and see Him there]{lang="en"}\
  [但我向祂舉目仰望，]{lang="zh"}
- [Who made an end of all my sin.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂已除去我的罪孽。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Because the sinless Savior died,]{lang="en"}\
  [無罪之主既已受死，]{lang="zh"}
- [My sinful soul is counted free;]{lang="en"}\
  [我這罪人就得自由；]{lang="zh"}
- [For God, the Just, is satisfied]{lang="en"}\
  [公義的神今已滿足，]{lang="zh"}
- [To look on Him and pardon me.]{lang="en"}\
  [看見救主，祂就赦宥。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Behold Him there, the risen Lamb!]{lang="en"}\
  [看哪，復活羔羊在天，]{lang="zh"}
- [My perfect, spotless Righteousness,]{lang="en"}\
  [是我完全、無疵義裳，]{lang="zh"}
- [The great unchangeable I AM,]{lang="en"}\
  [是那永不改變“我是”，]{lang="zh"}
- [The King of glory and of grace.]{lang="en"}\
  [是我榮耀、恩典君王。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [One with Himself, I cannot die;]{lang="en"}\
  [與祂合一，我不再死，]{lang="zh"}
- [My soul is purchased by His blood;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的寶血將我贖回；]{lang="zh"}
- [My life is hid with Christ on high,]{lang="en"}\
  [我的生命與主同藏，]{lang="zh"}
- [With Christ, my Savior and my God.]{lang="en"}\
  [藏在救主、我神之內。]{lang="zh"}
