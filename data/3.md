# 1

- [Holy, Holy, Holy, Lord, God Almighty!]{lang="en"}\
  [聖哉、聖哉、聖哉，全能的主神！]{lang="zh"}
- [Unto everlasting days our song shall rise to Thee;]{lang="en"}\
  [我們向你揚聲，頌讚直到永遠；]{lang="zh"}
- [Holy, Holy, Holy, Merciful and Mighty!]{lang="en"}\
  [聖哉、聖哉、聖哉，全能而又慈憐！]{lang="zh"}
- [God in Three Persons, blessed Trinity!]{lang="en"}\
  [一神別三位，三位是一神！]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Holy, Holy, Holy! all the saints adore Thee;]{lang="en"}\
  [聖哉、聖哉、聖哉，眾聖都下拜；]{lang="zh"}
- [Heaven’s elders cast their crowns down by the glassy sea;]{lang="en"}\
  [天上長老冠冕，玻璃海前拋開；]{lang="zh"}
- [Cherubim and seraphim worship too before Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [基路伯，撒拉弗，也都一同敬拜]{lang="zh"}
- [Who wert, and art, and evermore shalt be.]{lang="en"}\
  [永遠常存主，昔在又今在。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Holy, Holy, Holy! though the darkness hide Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [聖哉、聖哉、聖哉，你藏身黑暗，]{lang="zh"}
- [Though the eye of sinful man Thy glory may not see,]{lang="en"}\
  [縱然罪人不能看見你的榮光，]{lang="zh"}
- [Only Thou art holy, there is none beside Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [惟你乃是至聖，無何與你相像；]{lang="zh"}
- [Perfect in power, in love, and purity.]{lang="en"}\
  [愛、純潔、能力，惟獨你完全！]{lang="zh"}
