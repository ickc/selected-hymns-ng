# 1

- [O God of love to Thee we bow,]{lang="en"}\
  [愛的神阿，在你座前，]{lang="zh"}
- [And pray for these before Thee now.]{lang="en"}\
  [我們今為新人禱祈；]{lang="zh"}
- [That, closely knit in holy vow,]{lang="en"}\
  [求使他們緊緊相聯，]{lang="zh"}
- [They may in Thee be one.]{lang="en"}\
  [你裏合而為一。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [When days are filled with pure delight,]{lang="en"}\
  [有時道路平順無險，]{lang="zh"}
- [When paths are plain and skies are bright,]{lang="en"}\
  [天色明麗，充滿歡喜；]{lang="zh"}
- [Walking by faith and not by sight,]{lang="en"}\
  [憑信前進不憑眼見，]{lang="zh"}
- [May they in Thee be one.]{lang="en"}\
  [因在你裏合一。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [When stormy winds fulfill Thy will,]{lang="en"}\
  [有時遭遇狂風暴雨，]{lang="zh"}
- [And all their good seems turned to ill,]{lang="en"}\
  [一切福樂變為憂戚；]{lang="zh"}
- [Then, trusting Thee completely, still]{lang="en"}\
  [全心靠主，一無畏懼，]{lang="zh"}
- [May they in Thee be one.]{lang="en"}\
  [仍在你裏合一。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Whate’er in life shall be their share]{lang="en"}\
  [共度此生，不問禍福，]{lang="zh"}
- [Of quick’ning joy or burd’ning care,]{lang="en"}\
  [同證主恩，不論憂喜；]{lang="zh"}
- [In pow’r to do and grace to bear,]{lang="en"}\
  [從主得力，堅忍相助，]{lang="zh"}
- [May they in Thee be one.]{lang="en"}\
  [永在你裏合一。]{lang="zh"}
