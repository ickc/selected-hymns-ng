# 1

- [Jesus, cast a look on me;]{lang="en"}\
  [主阿，求你將我看，]{lang="zh"}
- [Give me sweet simplicity;]{lang="en"}\
  [賜我甜美的簡單；]{lang="zh"}
- [Make me poor, and keep me low,]{lang="en"}\
  [靈中貧窮，心卑微，]{lang="zh"}
- [Seeking only Thee to know.]{lang="en"}\
  [尋求猶如向日葵。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Weaned from all my lordly self,]{lang="en"}\
  [脫離作威的自己，]{lang="zh"}
- [Weaned from all the miser’s pelf,]{lang="en"}\
  [脫離財奴之所倚，]{lang="zh"}
- [Weaned from all the scorner’s ways,]{lang="en"}\
  [脫離顯揚的興趣，]{lang="zh"}
- [Weaned from all the lust of praise.]{lang="en"}\
  [脫離受誇的私欲。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [All that feeds my busy pride,]{lang="en"}\
  [所有供我驕傲的，]{lang="zh"}
- [Cast it evermore aside;]{lang="en"}\
  [求你為我全拋棄；]{lang="zh"}
- [Bid my will to Thine submit,]{lang="en"}\
  [使我意志服你的，]{lang="zh"}
- [Lay me humbly at Thy feet.]{lang="en"}\
  [謙卑順服到死地。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Make me like a little child,]{lang="en"}\
  [使我回轉像嬰孩，]{lang="zh"}
- [Of my strength and wisdom spoiled;]{lang="en"}\
  [將我智、力全毀壞；]{lang="zh"}
- [Seeing only in Thy light,]{lang="en"}\
  [只在你的光中行，]{lang="zh"}
- [Walking only in Thy might;]{lang="en"}\
  [只靠你的能力動。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Leaning on Thy loving breast,]{lang="en"}\
  [倚在你愛的胸膛，]{lang="zh"}
- [Where a weary soul may rest;]{lang="en"}\
  [好像斷奶的仰望；]{lang="zh"}
- [Feeling well the peace of God]{lang="en"}\
  [感到神賜的平安，]{lang="zh"}
- [Flowing from Thy precious blood.]{lang="en"}\
  [充滿在我全人間。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [In this posture let me live,]{lang="en"}\
  [讓我活在這姿態，]{lang="zh"}
- [And hosannas daily give;]{lang="en"}\
  [天天滿出讚美來，]{lang="zh"}
- [In this temper let me die,]{lang="en"}\
  [如此到死也不變，]{lang="zh"}
- [And hosannas ever cry.]{lang="en"}\
  [但我等候你顯現。]{lang="zh"}
