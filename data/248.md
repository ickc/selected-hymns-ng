# 1

- [Oh, the Lord is quickly coming,]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，我們主必要快臨，]{lang="zh"}
- [He has spoken in His word;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂已在話中申明；]{lang="zh"}
- [And confirmed it by the churches,]{lang="en"}\
  [教會也將此事印證，]{lang="zh"}
- [For His Spirit we have heard.]{lang="en"}\
  [因聖靈如此說明。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Not to all the seven churches]{lang="en"}\
  [但並非全體七教會，]{lang="zh"}
- [Did the Lord speak in this way;]{lang="en"}\
  [都聽到主如此說；]{lang="zh"}
- [To the church in Philadelphia]{lang="en"}\
  [惟獨非拉鐵非教會，]{lang="zh"}
- [Did He choose these words to say.]{lang="en"}\
  [主纔如此對她說。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Philadelphia, Philadelphia,]{lang="en"}\
  [非拉鐵非，非拉鐵非，]{lang="zh"}
- [Church so full of brotherly love,]{lang="en"}\
  [你滿了弟兄相愛，]{lang="zh"}
- [You’re the church to bring the Bridegroom]{lang="en"}\
  [惟你能將新郎帶回，]{lang="zh"}
- [Back to earth from heav’n above.]{lang="en"}\
  [從高天到地上來。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [He that hath the key of David,]{lang="en"}\
  [那拿著大衛的鑰匙，]{lang="zh"}
- [He that shuts and opens too,]{lang="en"}\
  [掌管開和關的說；]{lang="zh"}
- [Knows thy works, O Philadelphia;]{lang="en"}\
  [“我知道你行為，”故此]{lang="zh"}
- [That’s why you’re His Bride so true.]{lang="en"}\
  [作祂新婦配得過。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Not so strong by worldly standards,]{lang="en"}\
  [你雖只有一點力量，]{lang="zh"}
- [Hidden from religion’s fame;]{lang="en"}\
  [沒有屬世的名聲；]{lang="zh"}
- [Jesus loves you, Philadelphia,]{lang="en"}\
  [卻是主所愛的對象，]{lang="zh"}
- [For you’re faithful to His name.]{lang="en"}\
  [因你對主的忠誠。]{lang="zh"}

- [By His Word you’re overcoming—]{lang="en"}\

  [阿們聲響徹雲天；]{lang="zh"}
- [Just hold fast to what you’ve gotten,]{lang="en"}\
  [只要持守你的所有，]{lang="zh"}
