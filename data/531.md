# 1

- [Out of the depths of darkness, death, and anguish,]{lang="en"}\
  [從黑暗、死亡和苦悶的深淵，]{lang="zh"}
- [Christ lifted me and brought me to His throne.]{lang="en"}\
  [主將我提升到祂寶座前；]{lang="zh"}
- [No more enslaved by Satan’s dark delusions,]{lang="en"}\
  [不再受撒但權勢奴役、欺騙，]{lang="zh"}
- [With Christ transcendent, I am His alone.]{lang="en"}\
  [與主一同超越，在祂身邊。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Out of the depths of weakness and frustration,]{lang="en"}\
  [從軟弱、挫折和不安的深域，]{lang="zh"}
- [Set on the mount of praise and perfect peace;]{lang="en"}\
  [登上讚美高峰，毫無憂慮；]{lang="zh"}
- [Resting in Him, I know the sweet fulfillment]{lang="en"}\
  [安息於祂，祂生命是我依據：－]{lang="zh"}
- [Of life within that flows in blest release.]{lang="en"}\
  [湧出有福釋放，成就有餘。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Out of the depths of fear and disappointment,]{lang="en"}\
  [從懼怕、受壓和失望的深谷，]{lang="zh"}
- [Set on the mount of splendor, joy, and grace;]{lang="en"}\
  [興起喜樂，受恩典的鼓舞；]{lang="zh"}
- [Reigning with Christ, I taste His satisfaction;]{lang="en"}\
  [與基督同作王，豫嘗祂滿足；]{lang="zh"}
- [Feasting with Him, I glimpse the Bridegroom’s face.]{lang="en"}\
  [與祂同席，新郎榮臉得睹。]{lang="zh"}
