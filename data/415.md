# 1

- [Search me, O God, and know my heart today;]{lang="en"}\
  [懇求我主，鑒察我的心思，]{lang="zh"}
- [Try me, O Savior, know my thoughts, I pray.]{lang="en"}\
  [求主察驗我，知道我心事；]{lang="zh"}
- [See if there be some wicked way in me;]{lang="en"}\
  [看我裡面，有何噁心存留？]{lang="zh"}
- [Cleanse me from every sin and set me free.]{lang="en"}\
  [洗去每一罪汙，使我自由。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [I praise Thee, Lord, for cleansing me from sin;]{lang="en"}\
  [讚美我主，你已潔淨我罪，]{lang="zh"}
- [Fulfill Thy Word and make me pure within.]{lang="en"}\
  [實現你應許，純淨我心內。]{lang="zh"}
- [Fill me with fire where once I burned with shame;]{lang="en"}\
  [求以靈火，潔淨已往羞情，]{lang="zh"}
- [Grant my desire to magnify Thy name.]{lang="en"}\
  [我只渴慕能夠榮耀你名。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Lord, take my life and make it wholly Thine;]{lang="en"}\
  [主，佔有我，使我完全歸你，]{lang="zh"}
- [Fill my poor heart with Thy great love divine.]{lang="en"}\
  [以你大愛，充滿貧窮心裡；]{lang="zh"}
- [Take all my will, my passion, self and pride;]{lang="en"}\
  [奪我深處驕傲、情欲、意志，]{lang="zh"}
- [I now surrender, Lord—in me abide.]{lang="en"}\
  [我今降服，求主與我同止。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [O Holy Ghost, revival comes from Thee;]{lang="en"}\
  [懇求聖靈，賜下復興之火，]{lang="zh"}
- [Send a revival—start the work in me.]{lang="en"}\
  [差來復興——先在我心工作；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy Word declares Thou wilt supply our need;]{lang="en"}\
  [你既宣告供給我們需要，]{lang="zh"}
- [For blessings now, O Lord, I humbly plead.]{lang="en"}\
  [為這祝福，我今迫切禱告。]{lang="zh"}
