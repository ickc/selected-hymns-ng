# 1

- [Blest be the tie that binds]{lang="en"}\
  [福哉以愛聯繫，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our hearts in Christian love,]{lang="en"}\
  [聖徒心心相契；]{lang="zh"}
- [The fellowship our spirit finds]{lang="en"}\
  [彼此交通一情一意，]{lang="zh"}
- [Is like to that above.]{lang="en"}\
  [真如在天無異。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Before our Father’s throne,]{lang="en"}\
  [在父恩座之前，]{lang="zh"}
- [We pour our ardent pray’rs;]{lang="en"}\
  [同心尋求眷憐；]{lang="zh"}
- [Our fears, our hopes, our aims are one--]{lang="en"}\
  [有望同盼，有苦同擔，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our comforts and our cares.]{lang="en"}\
  [同憂、同樂、同安。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [We share our mutual woes;]{lang="en"}\
  [軟弱彼此體諒，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our mutual burdens bear;]{lang="en"}\
  [重擔互相擔當；]{lang="zh"}
- [And often for each other flows]{lang="en"}\
  [一人心傷，眾人淚淌，]{lang="zh"}
- [The sympathizing tear.]{lang="en"}\
  [充滿同情心腸。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [When we asunder part,]{lang="en"}\
  [每逢彼此分離，]{lang="zh"}
- [It gives us inward pain;]{lang="en"}\
  [總是難免依依，]{lang="zh"}
- [But we shall still be joined in heart,]{lang="en"}\
  [但身雖離，靈乃一起，]{lang="zh"}
- [And hope to meet again.]{lang="en"}\
  [且有再會之期。]{lang="zh"}
