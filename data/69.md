# 1

- [Meekness and majesty, manhood and Diety,]{lang="en"}\
  [溫和而又君尊，人性透著神性，]{lang="zh"}
- [In perfect harmony, the Man who is God.]{lang="en"}\
  [和諧顯在一身，祂是神而人。]{lang="zh"}
- [Lord of eternity, dwells in humanity;]{lang="en"}\
  [永遠常在的主，在肉身裏居住，]{lang="zh"}
- [Kneels in humility and washes our feet.]{lang="en"}\
  [甚至謙卑蹲俯，為我們洗足。]{lang="zh"}

# 1-chorus

- [O what a mystery, meekness and majesty;]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，何等的奧祕，溫和而又君尊；]{lang="zh"}
- [Bow down and worship, for this is your God.]{lang="en"}\
  [當屈膝拜禮，因這是我神。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Father’s pure radiance, perfect in innocence;]{lang="en"}\
  [父的純潔光輝，完全聖潔、無罪；]{lang="zh"}
- [Yet learns obedience to death on a cross.]{lang="en"}\
  [順服神旨所給，嘗十架苦味。]{lang="zh"}
- [Suff’ring to give us life, conquering through sacrifice;]{lang="en"}\
  [受苦為賜生命，犧牲以至得勝；]{lang="zh"}
- [And as they crucify prays, “Father, forgive.”]{lang="en"}\
  [十字架上受釘，猶為人求懇。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Wisdom unsearchable, God, the invisible;]{lang="en"}\
  [神的智慧無邊，活神，眼不能見，]{lang="zh"}
- [Love indestructible in frailty appears.]{lang="en"}\
  [竟因無限慈憐，在肉身顯現。]{lang="zh"}
- [Lord of infinity, stooping so tenderly;]{lang="en"}\
  [原為無限之神，今竟俯就、親近；]{lang="zh"}
- [Lifts our humanity to the heights of His throne.]{lang="en"}\
  [提昇我的人性，座前永沐恩。]{lang="zh"}
