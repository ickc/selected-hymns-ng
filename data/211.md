# 1

- [In the secret of His presence]{lang="en"}\
  [在祂同在的隱密處，]{lang="zh"}
- [How my soul delights to hide!]{lang="en"}\
  [我得安居何甘美！]{lang="zh"}
- [Oh, how precious are the lessons]{lang="en"}\
  [在祂面前時常俯伏，]{lang="zh"}
- [Which I learn at Jesus’ side!]{lang="en"}\
  [所學功課何寶貴！]{lang="zh"}
- [Earthly cares can never vex me,]{lang="en"}\
  [憂慮不能將我攪擾，]{lang="zh"}
- [Neither trials lay me low;]{lang="en"}\
  [試煉也不能壓住；]{lang="zh"}
- [For when Satan comes to tempt me,]{lang="en"}\
  [每逢撒但引誘吼叫，]{lang="zh"}
- [To the secret place I go.]{lang="en"}\
  [我就到祂隱密處。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [When my soul is faint and thirsty,]{lang="en"}\
  [當我帶著疲倦、乾渴，]{lang="zh"}
- [‘Neath the shadow of His wing]{lang="en"}\
  [來到祂的翅膀間，]{lang="zh"}
- [There is cool and pleasant shelter,]{lang="en"}\
  [就得蔭庇，涼爽、快樂，]{lang="zh"}
- [And a fresh and crystal spring;]{lang="en"}\
  [活泉清澈而新鮮；]{lang="zh"}
- [And my Savior rests beside me,]{lang="en"}\
  [在此投身救主胸懷，]{lang="zh"}
- [As we hold communion sweet;]{lang="en"}\
  [彼此交通滿香甜；]{lang="zh"}
- [If I tried, I could not utter]{lang="en"}\
  [我雖嘗試，也說不來]{lang="zh"}
- [What He says when thus we meet.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂那祕密的語言。]{lang="zh"}

  [如果祂是從來也未]{lang="zh"}
