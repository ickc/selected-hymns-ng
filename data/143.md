# 1

- [Majestic sweetness sits enthroned]{lang="en"}\
  [“甜美”登極救主額上，]{lang="zh"}
- [Upon the Savior’s brow;]{lang="en"}\
  [神聖、尊貴、莊嚴；]{lang="zh"}
- [His head with radiant glories crowned,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂頭披戴榮耀之光，]{lang="zh"}
- [His lips with grace o’erflow.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂口溢出恩典。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [No mortal can with Him compare]{lang="en"}\
  [地上所有將亡之人，]{lang="zh"}
- [Among the sons of men;]{lang="en"}\
  [與祂相比，誰配？]{lang="zh"}
- [Fairer is He than all the fair]{lang="en"}\
  [天上所有天使天軍，]{lang="zh"}
- [That fill the heavenly train.]{lang="en"}\
  [有誰比祂更美？]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [He saw me plunged in deep distress,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂見我陷痛苦深淵，]{lang="zh"}
- [He flew to my relief;]{lang="en"}\
  [迅速前來救助；]{lang="zh"}
- [For me He bore the shameful cross]{lang="en"}\
  [為我忍受十架苦難，]{lang="zh"}
- [And carried all my grief.]{lang="en"}\
  [背負我的愁苦。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [To God, the Father, my abode,]{lang="en"}\
  [向著父神——我的居所，]{lang="zh"}
- [He brings my weary feet;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂帶我的腳步；]{lang="zh"}
- [Shows me the glories of my God,]{lang="en"}\
  [將神榮耀指示與我，]{lang="zh"}
- [And makes my joys complete.]{lang="en"}\
  [使我喜樂滿足。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Since from His bounty I receive]{lang="en"}\
  [我從祂的神聖厚恩，]{lang="zh"}
- [Such proofs of love divine,]{lang="en"}\
  [得知祂愛之大；]{lang="zh"}
- [Had I a thousand hearts to give,]{lang="en"}\
  [倘若我有千萬的心，]{lang="zh"}
- [Lord, they should all be Thine.]{lang="en"}\
  [也必全都歸祂。]{lang="zh"}
