# 1

- [Lord Jesus! when we think of Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [主耶穌，當我們想到]{lang="zh"}
- [Of all Thy love and grace,]{lang="en"}\
  [你的一切恩愛，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our spirits long and fain would see]{lang="en"}\
  [我們的靈盼望最好]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy beauty face to face.]{lang="en"}\
  [當面見你丰采。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [And though the wilderness we tread,]{lang="en"}\
  [雖然我們行在野地，]{lang="zh"}
- [A barren, thirsty ground,]{lang="en"}\
  [寂寞、乾渴、駭驚，]{lang="zh"}
- [With thorns and briars overspread,]{lang="en"}\
  [左右荊棘，前後蒺藜，]{lang="zh"}
- [Where foes and snares abound;]{lang="en"}\
  [四圍仇敵陷阱。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Yet in Thy love such depths we see,]{lang="en"}\
  [我們卻從深處著想，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our hearts o’erflow with praise—]{lang="en"}\
  [知道你愛價值；]{lang="zh"}
- [Content ourselves, while, Lord to Thee]{lang="en"}\
  [我們因此心裏明亮，]{lang="zh"}
- [A joyful song we raise.]{lang="en"}\
  [讚美你恩不置。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Our Lord, our Life, our Rest, our Shield,]{lang="en"}\
  [你是我們生命、力量、]{lang="zh"}
- [Our Rock, our Food, our Light;]{lang="en"}\
  [盾牌、磐石、詩歌，]{lang="zh"}
- [Each thought of Thee doth constant yield]{lang="en"}\
  [無論怎樣把你思想，]{lang="zh"}
- [Unchanging, fresh delight.]{lang="en"}\
  [總叫我們快樂。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Blest Savior, keep our spirits stayed]{lang="en"}\
  [可愛的主，保守我們]{lang="zh"}
- [Hard following after Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [緊緊跟隨你行，]{lang="zh"}
- [Till we, in robes of white arrayed,]{lang="en"}\
  [直到我們進入天門，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy face in glory see.]{lang="en"}\
