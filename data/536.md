# 1

- [My home is God Himself; Christ brought me there,]{lang="en"}\
  [我的居所乃是神的自己，]{lang="zh"}
- [And bade me dwell in Him, rejoicing there;]{lang="en"}\
  [我主基督將我帶到這裡；]{lang="zh"}
- [He bore me where no foot but His hath trod,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂帶我走祂所走過的路，]{lang="zh"}
- [Within the holiest at home with God.]{lang="en"}\
  [到這至聖所裡與神同住。]{lang="zh"}
- [O holy place! O home divinely fair!]{lang="en"}\
  [聖別所在！神聖榮美之家，]{lang="zh"}
- [And we, God’s little ones, abiding there.]{lang="en"}\
  [在此與神同住，至美絕佳！]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [A long, long road I traveled night and day,]{lang="en"}\
  [夜以繼日走過漫長的路，]{lang="zh"}
- [And sought to find within myself some way,]{lang="en"}\
  [自己裡面想要找到一途；]{lang="zh"}
- [Aught I could do, or feel to bring me near;]{lang="en"}\
  [一無所成，也無任何進步，]{lang="zh"}
- [Self effort failed, and I was filled with fear,]{lang="en"}\
  [自我努力不過全是失誤，]{lang="zh"}
- [And then I found Christ was the only way]{lang="en"}\
  [而後得知主是惟一道路，]{lang="zh"}
- [That I must come to Him and in Him stay.]{lang="en"}\
  [我當藉祂、到神裡面居住。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [O wondrous place! O home divinely fair!]{lang="en"}\
  [奇妙住處，神聖榮美之家！]{lang="zh"}
- [And I, God’s little one, safe hidden there.]{lang="en"}\
  [與神同住，安穩無以復加。]{lang="zh"}
- [Lord, as I dwell in Thee and Thou in me,]{lang="en"}\
  [哦神，你我既是互為居所，]{lang="zh"}
- [So make me dead to everything but Thee;]{lang="en"}\
  [求你使我只是向你活著；]{lang="zh"}
- [That as I rest within my home most fair,]{lang="en"}\
  [在這最美之家，得享安康，]{lang="zh"}
- [I’ll share my God in all and everywhere.]{lang="en"}\
  [隨事隨在，將你與人分享。]{lang="zh"}
