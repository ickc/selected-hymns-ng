# 1

- [In the wilderness for God!]{lang="en"}\
  [曠野之中為著神！]{lang="zh"}
- [Just a common bush a flame!]{lang="en"}\
  [一叢荊棘在火焚！]{lang="zh"}
- [Thus may I be, blessed Lord,]{lang="en"}\
  [願我如此蒙你恩，]{lang="zh"}
- [For the glory of Thy Name.]{lang="en"}\
  [使你榮耀顯我身。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

  [僅是平常的荊棘，]{lang="zh"}

- [Something thru which God can speak,]{lang="en"}\
  [神能憑它顯能力，]{lang="zh"}
