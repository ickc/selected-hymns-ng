# 1

- [How sweet, how heav’nly is the sight,]{lang="en"}\
  [愛主之人所顯景象，]{lang="zh"}
- [When those who love the Lord]{lang="en"}\
  [何甘甜，何屬天！]{lang="zh"}
- [In one another’s peace delight,]{lang="en"}\
  [實現主話，喜樂歡暢，]{lang="zh"}
- [And so fulfill His Word;]{lang="en"}\
  [彼此和睦平安。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [When each can feel his brother’s sigh,]{lang="en"}\
  [一人傷心，眾人同感，]{lang="zh"}
- [And with him bear a part;]{lang="en"}\
  [重擔，彼此分擔；]{lang="zh"}
- [When sorrow flows from eye to eye,]{lang="en"}\
  [憂傷之情，眼目相傳，]{lang="zh"}
- [And joy from heart to heart;]{lang="en"}\
  [喜樂，心心相感。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [When, free from envy, seem and pride,]{lang="en"}\
  [脫離嫉妒、譏嘲、驕傲，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our wishes all above,]{lang="en"}\
  [我們所望在天；]{lang="zh"}
- [Each can his brother’s failings hide,]{lang="en"}\
  [弟兄跌倒，扶持、代禱，]{lang="zh"}
- [And show a brother’s love;]{lang="en"}\
  [顯出愛心無限。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [When love, in one delightful stream,]{lang="en"}\
  [愛如水流，何等可悅，]{lang="zh"}
- [Through every bosom flows;]{lang="en"}\
  [流過眾人心胸；]{lang="zh"}
- [When union sweet, and dear esteem,]{lang="en"}\
  [愛的尊重、香甜聯結，]{lang="zh"}
- [In every action glows.]{lang="en"}\
  [顯在每一舉動。]{lang="zh"}
