# 1

- [On Christ, salvation rests secure;]{lang="en"}\
  [救恩穩固在於基督；]{lang="zh"}
- [The Rock of Ages must endure;]{lang="en"}\
  [永久磐石堅定可靠；]{lang="zh"}
- [Nor can that faith be overthrown]{lang="en"}\
  [信心安在“活石”之上，]{lang="zh"}
- [Which rests upon the “Living Stone.”]{lang="en"}\
  [就不至於失敗、跌倒。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [No other hope shall intervene;]{lang="en"}\
  [除祂之外，別無盼望；]{lang="zh"}
- [To Him we look, on Him we lean;]{lang="en"}\
  [向祂仰望，將祂倚賴；]{lang="zh"}
- [Other foundations we disown,]{lang="en"}\
  [建造在這“活石”之上，]{lang="zh"}
- [And build on Christ, the “Living Stone.”]{lang="en"}\
  [別無根基可以取代。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [In Him it is ordained to raise]{lang="en"}\
  [神已命定在祂裏面]{lang="zh"}
- [A temple to Jehovah’s praise,]{lang="en"}\
  [建造聖殿，好得榮耀；]{lang="zh"}
- [Composed of all His saints, who own]{lang="en"}\
  [“活石”所救一切信徒，]{lang="zh"}
- [No Savior but the “Living Stone.”]{lang="en"}\
  [是這殿的建造材料。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [View the vast building, see it rise:]{lang="en"}\
  [看這建造何等偉大：]{lang="zh"}
- [The work how great! the plan how wise!]{lang="en"}\
  [工程何鉅！設計何智！]{lang="zh"}
- [O wondrous fabric! pow’r unknown:]{lang="en"}\
  [奇妙結構，莫測大能，]{lang="zh"}
- [That rests it on the “Living Stone.”]{lang="en"}\
  [全都安置於那“活石”。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [But most adore His precious name:]{lang="en"}\
  [最可稱頌，祂的尊名：]{lang="zh"}
- [His glory and His grace proclaim!]{lang="en"}\
  [說出祂的榮耀、恩典！]{lang="zh"}
- [For us, the lost, condemned, undone,]{lang="en"}\
  [失喪、定罪，不堪如我，]{lang="zh"}
- [He gave Himself, the “Living Stone.”]{lang="en"}\
  [竟得蒙這“活石”眷念！]{lang="zh"}
