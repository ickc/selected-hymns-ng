# 1

- [We have a most glorious King;]{lang="en"}\
  [我們有一最榮耀王；]{lang="zh"}
- [The heavens, He says, are His throne;]{lang="en"}\
  [諸天乃是祂的寶座；]{lang="zh"}
- [All worlds are His mighty domain,]{lang="en"}\
  [諸世界是祂的領疆，]{lang="zh"}
- [All kingdoms His scepter shall own.]{lang="en"}\
  [一切國度由祂掌握。]{lang="zh"}
- [He dwells with His people below,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂與地上子民同住，]{lang="zh"}
- [He loves in their trials to share;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂樂分擔他們苦楚；]{lang="zh"}
- [We dwell with the King I His work,]{lang="en"}\
  [為祂事工，與王同住，]{lang="zh"}
- [His burden we willingly bear.]{lang="en"}\
  [甘心背負祂的託付。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [I’m dwelling with Jesus my King;]{lang="en"}\
  [我與耶穌我王同住，]{lang="zh"}
- [I’ve found where He dwells with His own;]{lang="en"}\
  [我已尋到祂的居所；]{lang="zh"}
- [I’ve opened the door of my heart;]{lang="en"}\
  [開我心門，祂已進入；]{lang="zh"}
- [He’s made it His temple and throne.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂使我心成祂寶座。]{lang="zh"}
- [Like Mary I sit at His feet,]{lang="en"}\
  [像馬利亞坐祂腳前，]{lang="zh"}
- [Like John I recline on His breast;]{lang="en"}\
  [又像約翰躺祂懷中；]{lang="zh"}
- [His presence is fullness of joy,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的同在，使我心歡，]{lang="zh"}
- [His bosom is infinite rest.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的胸懷，使我安寧。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [I dwell with the King for His work,]{lang="en"}\
  [為祂事工，與王同住，]{lang="zh"}
- [I’ve part in His glorious plan]{lang="en"}\
  [有分於祂榮耀計劃，]{lang="zh"}
- [To bring in His kingdom to earth]{lang="en"}\
  [在地帶進祂的國度，]{lang="zh"}
- [And tell His salvation to man.]{lang="en"}\
  [將祂救恩向人傳達。]{lang="zh"}
- [The world has its work and rewards,]{lang="en"}\
  [世界有其工作、賞賜，]{lang="zh"}
- [I count them but folly and loss;]{lang="en"}\
  [但我算作愚昧、無益；]{lang="zh"}
- [My business is only His work,]{lang="en"}\
  [惟祂事工，是我職事，]{lang="zh"}
- [My message is only His cross.]{lang="en"}\
  [惟祂十架，是我信息。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [I dwell with the King for His work,]{lang="en"}\
  [為祂事工，與王同住，]{lang="zh"}
- [The work, it is His and not mine;]{lang="en"}\
  [是祂事工，不是我的；]{lang="zh"}
- [He plans and prepares it for me]{lang="en"}\
  [祂來計劃，祂來鋪路，]{lang="zh"}
- [And fills me with power divine.]{lang="en"}\
  [充滿我以神聖能力。]{lang="zh"}
- [So duty is changed to delight,]{lang="en"}\
  [因此責任變作悅事，]{lang="zh"}
- [And prayer into praise as I sing;]{lang="en"}\
  [禱告變作歌唱、讚美；]{lang="zh"}
- [I dwell with my King for His work]{lang="en"}\
  [為祂事工，與王同住，]{lang="zh"}
- [And work in the strength of my King.]{lang="en"}\
  [靠王能力，有所作為。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [We’ll dwell with the King for His work]{lang="en"}\
  [為祂事工，與王同住，]{lang="zh"}
- [And work thru each day of the year.]{lang="en"}\
  [日以繼夜，殷勤作工；]{lang="zh"}
- [Perhaps ere it passes, the King]{lang="en"}\
  [也許事工尚未止住，]{lang="zh"}
- [In glory Himself shall appear.]{lang="en"}\
  [我王即已榮耀再臨。]{lang="zh"}
- [Oh, then in some closer embrace,]{lang="en"}\
  [與祂更近，何等有福，]{lang="zh"}
- [Oh, then in some nobler employ]{lang="en"}\
  [事工更尊，何等權柄；]{lang="zh"}
- [We’ll dwell with the King for His work]{lang="en"}\
  [為祂事工，與王永住，]{lang="zh"}
- [In endless, ineffable joy!]{lang="en"}\
  [難言喜樂，永無止境。]{lang="zh"}
