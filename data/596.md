# 1

- [Why should I fear the darkest hour,]{lang="en"}\
  [為何懼怕黑暗時光，]{lang="zh"}
- [Or tremble at the tempter’s power?]{lang="en"}\
  [或被試探能力驚慌？]{lang="zh"}
- [Jesus vouchsafes to be my tower.]{lang="en"}\
  [既有耶穌在我身旁。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Though hot the fight, why quit the field?]{lang="en"}\
  [戰爭雖凶，為何退縮？]{lang="zh"}
- [Why must I either fly or yield,]{lang="en"}\
  [為何必須投降逃脫？]{lang="zh"}
- [Since Jesus is my mighty shield?]{lang="en"}\
  [既有耶穌擋住敵火。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [When creature comforts fade and die,]{lang="en"}\
  [人的安慰，逐漸消失，]{lang="zh"}
- [Worldlings may weep, but why should I?]{lang="en"}\
  [世人哀哭，我何如斯？]{lang="zh"}
- [Jesus still lives, and still is nigh.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂仍親近，祂仍在此。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Though all the flocks and herds were dead,]{lang="en"}\
  [棚內無牛、圈內絕羊，]{lang="zh"}
- [My soul a famine need not dread,]{lang="en"}\
  [但我魂仍不懼饑荒，]{lang="zh"}
- [For Jesus is my living bread.]{lang="en"}\
  [因耶穌是我生命糧。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [I know not what may soon betide,]{lang="en"}\
  [我不知將遇何事故，]{lang="zh"}
- [Or how my wants shall be supplied;]{lang="en"}\
  [或我缺乏如何應付，]{lang="zh"}
- [But Jesus knows, and will provide.]{lang="en"}\
  [但祂知道，並能補足。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [Though sin would fill me with distress,]{lang="en"}\
  [雖然罪惡令我苦惱，]{lang="zh"}
- [The throne of grace I dare address,]{lang="en"}\
  [但我仍敢座前祈禱，]{lang="zh"}
- [For Jesus is my righteousness.]{lang="en"}\
  [因有耶穌作我中保。]{lang="zh"}

# 7

- [Though faint my prayers and cold my love,]{lang="en"}\
  [禱告無力，愛心冷淡，]{lang="zh"}
- [My steadfast hope shall not remove,]{lang="en"}\
  [但我盼望堅定不變，]{lang="zh"}
- [While Jesus intercedes above.]{lang="en"}\
  [因主為我代求在天。]{lang="zh"}

# 8

- [Against me earth and hell combine;]{lang="en"}\
  [世界、地獄聯手攻擊，]{lang="zh"}
- [But on my side is power divine;]{lang="en"}\
  [神聖能力伴我不離，]{lang="zh"}
- [Jesus is all, and He is mine!]{lang="en"}\
  [主是一切，祂是我的！]{lang="zh"}
