# 1

- [Jesus, Thy head, once crown’d with thorns,]{lang="en"}\
  [從前那戴荊棘的頭，]{lang="zh"}
- [Is crown’d with glory now;]{lang="en"}\
  [今戴榮耀冠冕；]{lang="zh"}
- [Heaven’s royal diadem adorns]{lang="en"}\
  [救主耶穌，我們元首，]{lang="zh"}
- [The mighty Victor’s brow.]{lang="en"}\
  [你今已升高天。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Thou glorious light of courts above,]{lang="en"}\
  [你是地上聖徒之樂，]{lang="zh"}
- [Joy of the saints below,]{lang="en"}\
  [你是天上之光；]{lang="zh"}
- [To us still manifest Thy love,]{lang="en"}\
  [你今領我飲於愛河，]{lang="zh"}
- [That we its depths may know.]{lang="en"}\
  [使我知其深廣。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [To us Thy cross with all its shame,]{lang="en"}\
  [主，將你的羞辱、權柄，]{lang="zh"}
- [With all its grace be giv’n;]{lang="en"}\
  [一並賜給我們；]{lang="zh"}
- [Though earth disowns Thy lowly name,]{lang="en"}\
  [地雖否認你的微名，]{lang="zh"}
- [God honors it in heav’n.]{lang="en"}\
  [神卻使你高升。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Who suffer with Thee, Lord, below]{lang="en"}\
  [凡肯與你在世同苦，]{lang="zh"}
- [Shall reign with Thee above;]{lang="en"}\
  [也要同榮在天，]{lang="zh"}
- [Then let it be our joy to know]{lang="en"}\
  [所以求主使我堅固，]{lang="zh"}
- [This way of peace and love.]{lang="en"}\
  [鄙視世界恩典。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [To us Thy cross is life and health;]{lang="en"}\
  [十架於你雖是辱、死，]{lang="zh"}
- [’Twas shame and death to Thee;]{lang="en"}\
  [在我卻是生命！]{lang="zh"}
- [Our present glory, joy and wealth,]{lang="en"}\
  [也是我的榮耀、權勢！]{lang="zh"}
- [Our everlasting stay.]{lang="en"}\
  [我的永遠安寧！]{lang="zh"}
