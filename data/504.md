# 1

- [Abide in Thee! in that deep love of Thine,]{lang="en"}\
  [住在你裡面，這是我心願，]{lang="zh"}
- [My Jesus, Lord, Thou Lamb of God divine,]{lang="en"}\
  [住在你裡面，時刻不間斷；]{lang="zh"}
- [Down, closely down, as living branch with tree,]{lang="en"}\
  [枝子如何與葡萄樹相聯，]{lang="zh"}
- [I would abide, my Lord, my Christ, in Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [我也深願，主，住在你裡面。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Abide in Thee! my Savior God, I know]{lang="en"}\
  [住在你裡面，你富我享受，]{lang="zh"}
- [How love of Thine so vast in me may flow,]{lang="en"}\
  [你所是一切，得藉我流露；]{lang="zh"}
- [My empty vessel, running o’er with joy,]{lang="en"}\
  [願作你身上常新的枝子，]{lang="zh"}
- [Must overflow to Thee without alloy.]{lang="en"}\
  [彰顯你生命，結果永不止。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Abide in Thee! nor doubt, nor self, nor sin,]{lang="en"}\
  [住在你裡面，罪、已失權能，]{lang="zh"}
- [Can e’er prevail with Thy blest life within;]{lang="en"}\
  [再無法勝過你內住生命；]{lang="zh"}
- [Joined to Thyself, communing deep, my soul]{lang="en"}\
  [與你相結聯，深處互交通，]{lang="zh"}
- [Knows naught besides its motions to control.]{lang="en"}\
  [靈就能管治魂一切活動。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Abide in Thee! ’tis thus I only know]{lang="en"}\
  [住在你裡面，得知你心意，]{lang="zh"}
- [The secrets of Thy mind e’en while below—]{lang="en"}\
  [摸著你同在，進入你秘密；]{lang="zh"}
- [All joy and peace, and knowledge of Thy word,]{lang="en"}\
  [喜樂與平安要將我充溢，]{lang="zh"}
- [All pow’r and fruit, and service for the Lord.]{lang="en"}\
  [你話的供應也作我能力。]{lang="zh"}
