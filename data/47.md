# 1

- [Lift that Name high! That glorious Name,]{lang="en"}\
  [高舉主名！那榮耀名！]{lang="zh"}
- [Let heav’n and earth its pow’r proclaim;]{lang="en"}\
  [天地宣告祂的大能；]{lang="zh"}
- [Our mighty, conqu’ring, coming King,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂已得勝，祂要再臨，]{lang="zh"}
- [Earth yet shall with His praises ring.]{lang="en"}\
  [全地都要稱頌祂名。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Lift that Name high! To that high tower]{lang="en"}\
  [高舉主名！祂是高臺，]{lang="zh"}
- [We flee in every trial hour,]{lang="en"}\
  [每逢試煉，免我受害，]{lang="zh"}
- [Safe, sheltered, satisfied and free,]{lang="en"}\
  [安全蔭蔽，自由滿意，]{lang="zh"}
- [For Jesus’ Name is victory.]{lang="en"}\
  [因耶穌名就是勝利。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Lift that Name high! Until one day]{lang="en"}\
  [高舉主名！直到那日，]{lang="zh"}
- [His mighty Name the earth shall sway,]{lang="en"}\
  [萬口都要認祂為主，]{lang="zh"}
- [And sin and death, distress and pain]{lang="en"}\
  [憂愁、歎息、罪惡、死亡，]{lang="zh"}
- [Shall be no more, for Christ shall reign.]{lang="en"}\
  [不再存留，因主是王。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Lift that Name high! Jesus shall reign,]{lang="en"}\
  [高舉主名！耶穌是主，]{lang="zh"}
- [And kings shall follow in His train;]{lang="en"}\
  [所有君王都要歸服；]{lang="zh"}
- [Lift that Name high, all names above,]{lang="en"}\
  [高舉主名！超越之名！]{lang="zh"}
- [The Name of Him we own and love.]{lang="en"}\
  [我們寶愛祂這尊名。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Lift that Name high! For every knee]{lang="en"}\
  [高舉主名！耶穌是主，]{lang="zh"}
- [Shall bow to Him; Jesus shall see]{lang="en"}\
  [萬民屈膝向祂降服；]{lang="zh"}
- [Fruit of His Cross, when earth shall bring]{lang="en"}\
  [十架果效終於看見，]{lang="zh"}
- [Her tribute to her Lord and King.]{lang="en"}\
  [因祂是主、是王無間。]{lang="zh"}
