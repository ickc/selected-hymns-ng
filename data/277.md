# 1

- [O perfect Love, all human thought transcending,]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，主你大愛，遠超人所能讚！]{lang="zh"}
- [Lowly we kneel in prayer before Thy throne,]{lang="en"}\
  [我們今謙恭，懇求你座前：]{lang="zh"}
- [That theirs may be the love which knows no ending,]{lang="en"}\
  [使二人因你，彼此相愛無間，]{lang="zh"}
- [Whom Thou forevermore dost join in one.]{lang="en"}\
  [並使他們與你永遠相聯。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [O perfect Life, be Thou their full assurance]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，完全生命，願成他們確據：]{lang="zh"}
- [Of tender charity and steadfast faith,]{lang="en"}\
  [不移的信心，並你的柔愛，]{lang="zh"}
- [Of patient hope, and quiet, brave endurance,]{lang="en"}\
  [如孩童信靠，苦或死均無懼，]{lang="zh"}
- [With childlike trust that fears nor pain nor death.]{lang="en"}\
  [不息的盼望，無聲的忍耐。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Grant them the joy which brightens earthly sorrow,]{lang="en"}\
  [求賜你喜樂，消除一切憂怯，]{lang="zh"}
- [Grant them the peace which calms all earthly strife,]{lang="en"}\
  [並賜你平安，平息諸爭競；]{lang="zh"}
- [And to life’s day the glorious, unknown morrow]{lang="en"}\
  [願他們共度一生未知歲月，]{lang="zh"}
- [That dawns upon eternal love and life.]{lang="en"}\
  [聯於這永遠的愛與生命。]{lang="zh"}
