# 1

- [One day when heaven was filled with His praises,]{lang="en"}\
  [一日，當天上充滿讚美之聲，]{lang="zh"}
- [One day when sin was as black as could be,]{lang="en"}\
  [一日，當罪惡深染全人類，]{lang="zh"}
- [Jesus came forth to be born of a virgin—]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌就降世，為一童女所生，]{lang="zh"}
- [Dwelt among men, my example is He!]{lang="en"}\
  [住在人中間，顯出神光輝！]{lang="zh"}

  [降生，為愛我；受死，為救我；]{lang="zh"}

  [埋葬，為帶去我一切罪過；]{lang="zh"}
- [Rising, He justified freely forever:]{lang="en"}\
  [復活，為白白的永遠稱義我：]{lang="zh"}
- [One day He’s coming—O glorious day!]{lang="en"}\
  [一日，祂要來，哦，榮日接我！]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [One day they led Him up Calvary’s mountain,]{lang="en"}\
  [一日，人將祂帶到加略山上，]{lang="zh"}
- [One day they nailed Him to die on the tree;]{lang="en"}\
  [一日，人把祂釘木上剪除；]{lang="zh"}
- [Suffering anguish, despised and rejected;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂備嘗痛苦，被神擊打、壓傷，]{lang="zh"}
- [Bearing our sins, my Redeemer is He.]{lang="en"}\
  [擔當我罪過，作我救贖主！]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [One day they left Him alone in the garden,]{lang="en"}\
  [一日，人撇祂獨自在那園中，]{lang="zh"}
- [One day He rested, from suffering free;]{lang="en"}\
  [一日，祂安息，不再有痛苦；]{lang="zh"}
- [Angels came down o’er His tomb to keep vigil;]{lang="en"}\
  [天使從天來，夜間守祂墓洞；]{lang="zh"}
- [Hope of the hopeless, my Savior is He.]{lang="en"}\
  [無望者之望，乃是我救主！]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [One day the grave could Conceal Him no longer,]{lang="en"}\
  [一日，那墳墓不能將祂留藏，]{lang="zh"}
- [One day the stone rolled away from the door;]{lang="en"}\
  [一日，那大石輥開不封堵；]{lang="zh"}
- [Then He arose, over death He had conquered;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂已復活了，祂已勝過死亡，]{lang="zh"}
- [Now is ascended, my Lord evermore.]{lang="en"}\
  [升到高天上，永遠作我主！]{lang="zh"}
