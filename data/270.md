# 1

- [Flow through me, Lord, a weak and earthly vessel,]{lang="en"}\
  [主，流過我，從這個軟弱瓦器，]{lang="zh"}
- [Cleansed by Thy blood, and quickened at Thy call.]{lang="en"}\
  [用血洗淨，發聲使我醒起。]{lang="zh"}
- [Flow through me, Lord, all utterly abandoned,]{lang="en"}\
  [主，流過我，將一切完全捨棄，]{lang="zh"}
- [To Thy dear service, great, or high, or small.]{lang="en"}\
  [事主、愛主，事無大、小、高、低。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Flow through me, Lord, with overflowing fullness,]{lang="en"}\
  [主，帶著你滿溢豐盛流過我，]{lang="zh"}
- [Make me to know Thine own deep love for souls.]{lang="en"}\
  [你熱切的愛將我心開擴。]{lang="zh"}
- [Work in me, Lord, the faith that moveth mountains,]{lang="en"}\
  [作在我身，使我有移山信心，]{lang="zh"}
- [As power, Almighty power, my being holds.]{lang="en"}\
  [用你大能，將我全人握繄。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Flow through me, Lord, till sin in all its blackness]{lang="en"}\
  [主，流過我，直到那被囚之人，]{lang="zh"}
- [To captives bound by Satan is revealed.]{lang="en"}\
  [看見罪惡可怖、可棄、可恨。]{lang="zh"}
- [Flow through me, Lord, till hearts are stricken, broken,]{lang="en"}\
  [主，流過我，直到硬心被破碎，]{lang="zh"}
- [And in Thy cleansing fountain saved and healed.]{lang="en"}\
  [在你洗淨泉源得救歸回。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Flow through me, Lord, till lagging souls are quickened,]{lang="en"}\
  [主，流過我，直到失敗的聖徒]{lang="zh"}
- [Till lives of failure sing the victory song,]{lang="en"}\
  [被復甦並高唱凱歌歡呼；]{lang="zh"}
- [Till praise from lips long dumb and silent, open,]{lang="en"}\
  [直到長久沉寂的人再張嘴]{lang="zh"}
- [And join with rapture earth’s glad, cleansed throng.]{lang="en"}\
  [隨同歡樂聖眾讚美。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Flow through me, Lord, till waters from Thy fountain]{lang="en"}\
  [主，流過我，直到你活水泉源]{lang="zh"}
- [Shall slake the thirst of souls in deserts drear.]{lang="en"}\
  [來滋潤荒漠乾渴的心田。]{lang="zh"}
- [Flow through me, Lord, till and places blossom]{lang="en"}\
  [主，流過我，直到枯乾之地上，]{lang="zh"}
- [With roses fresh and sweet, and wondrous fair.]{lang="en"}\
  [長滿鮮花，何等甜美、馨香。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [Flow through me, Lord, set all my heart-strings thrilling,]{lang="en"}\
  [主，流過我，用你那無比、完全]{lang="zh"}
- [With Thine own love, so matchless, perfect, free.]{lang="en"}\
  [的大愛來鼓動我的心弦。]{lang="zh"}
- [Flow through me, Lord, till naught in earth or heaven,]{lang="en"}\
  [主，流過我，直到在天地之間，]{lang="zh"}
- [Can satisfy my being, Lord, but Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [別無他物，惟你滿我心願。]{lang="zh"}
