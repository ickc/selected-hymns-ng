# 1

- [O for a thousand tongues to sing]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，願我有千萬舌頭，]{lang="zh"}
- [My great Redeemer’s praise,]{lang="en"}\
  [前來讚美救主，]{lang="zh"}
- [The glories of my God and King,]{lang="en"}\
  [說祂恩典何等深厚，]{lang="zh"}
- [The triumphs of His grace.]{lang="en"}\
  [榮耀何等豐富。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [My gracious Master and my God,]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌這名，慰我苦情，]{lang="zh"}
- [Assist me to proclaim,]{lang="en"}\
  [驅盡我的驚怯，]{lang="zh"}
- [To spread through all the earth abroad,]{lang="en"}\
  [是我安息、是我生命，]{lang="zh"}
- [The honors of Thy name.]{lang="en"}\
  [成為我的音樂。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Jesus! the name that charms our fears,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂因愛我，竟願經歷]{lang="zh"}
- [That bids our sorrows cease;]{lang="en"}\
  [人世所有苦楚；]{lang="zh"}
- [’Tis music in the sinner’s ears,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂已消滅罪的能力，]{lang="zh"}
- [’Tis life, and health, and peace.]{lang="en"}\
  [釋放罪的囚徒。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [His love my heart has captive made,]{lang="en"}\
  [我每靜念救我的愛，]{lang="zh"}
- [His captive would I be,]{lang="en"}\
  [立即感覺不配；]{lang="zh"}
- [For He was bound, and scourged and died,]{lang="en"}\
  [不知祂為甚麼恩待]{lang="zh"}
- [My captive soul to free.]{lang="en"}\
  [我這人中罪魁。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [He breaks the power of canceled sin,]{lang="en"}\
  [我今作祂愛的俘虜，]{lang="zh"}
- [He sets the prisoner free;]{lang="en"}\
  [甘心作到永久；]{lang="zh"}
- [His blood can make the foulest clean;]{lang="en"}\
  [因祂為我受死、忍辱，]{lang="zh"}
- [His blood availed for me.]{lang="en"}\
  [使我得以自由。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [So now Thy blessed Name I love,]{lang="en"}\
  [我既從你可愛的名，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy will would e’er be mine.]{lang="en"}\
  [知你待我美意；]{lang="zh"}
- [Had I a thousand hearts to give,]{lang="en"}\
  [假若我有千萬的心，]{lang="zh"}
- [My Lord, they all were Thine!]{lang="en"}\
