# 1

- [Sun of my soul, Thou Savior dear,]{lang="en"}\
  [救主耶穌，我的太陽，]{lang="zh"}
- [It is not night if Thou be near;]{lang="en"}\
  [有你同在，夜也明亮；]{lang="zh"}
- [Oh, may no earthborn cloud arise]{lang="en"}\
  [勿讓人世有雲遮掩]{lang="zh"}
- [To hide Thee from Thy servant's eyes.]{lang="en"}\
  [僕人心眼不見慈顏。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [When the soft dews of kindly sleep]{lang="en"}\
  [倦眼欲合，頻頻催眠，]{lang="zh"}
- [My wearied eyelids gently steep,]{lang="en"}\
  [睡如甘露，潤我夕薦，]{lang="zh"}
- [Be my last thought how sweet to rest]{lang="en"}\
  [深願今晚最後思念，]{lang="zh"}
- [Forever on my Savior's breast.]{lang="en"}\
  [乃是永世息你胸前。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Abide with me from mom till eve,]{lang="en"}\
  [與我同在自晨至夕，]{lang="zh"}
- [For without Thee I cannot live;]{lang="en"}\
  [離你我覺生無意義，]{lang="zh"}
- [Abide with me when night is nigh,]{lang="en"}\
  [無你我懼死蔭臨到，]{lang="zh"}
- [For without Thee I dare not die.]{lang="en"}\
  [求你相偕自晚至朝。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Come near, and bless us when we wake,]{lang="en"}\
  [當我睡醒，求主賜福，]{lang="zh"}
- [Ere through the World our way we take;]{lang="en"}\
  [扶我行走人生路途，]{lang="zh"}
- [Till in the ocean of Thy love]{lang="en"}\
  [直至那日被提你旁，]{lang="zh"}
- [We lose ourselves in Thee above.]{lang="en"}\
  [溶化在你愛的海洋。]{lang="zh"}
