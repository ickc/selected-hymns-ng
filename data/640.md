# 1

- [Our Lord is now rejected, And by the world disowned,]{lang="en"}\
  [我主今被人棄絕，又被世界否認；]{lang="zh"}
- [By the many still neglected, And by the few enthroned;]{lang="en"}\
  [眾人將祂漠視、忽略，只被少數崇尊；]{lang="zh"}
- [But soon He’ll come in glory! The hour is drawing nigh,]{lang="en"}\
  [但祂不久將榮歸！時日已經迫近，]{lang="zh"}
- [For the crowning day is coming By-and-by.]{lang="en"}\
  [因為不久加冠之日即將臨。]{lang="zh"}

- [O the crowning day is coming, Is coming by-and-by,]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，那加冠之日已近，不久即將來臨，]{lang="zh"}
- [When our Lord shall come in power And glory from on high.]{lang="en"}\
  [那時主將在大能榮耀中從天降臨。]{lang="zh"}
- [O the glorious sight will gladden Each waiting, watchful eye,]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，榮耀情景將令守望者歡喜不置，]{lang="zh"}
- [In the crowning day that’s coming By-and-by.]{lang="en"}\
  [在那不久即將臨加冠之日。]{lang="zh"}
