# 1

- [Sound ye the trumpet-call; Heralds proclaim]{lang="en"}\
  [響起福音號聲，你這信徒；]{lang="zh"}
- [Jesus as Lord of all, Sound forth His fame;]{lang="en"}\
  [宣揚祂的名聲，耶穌是主！]{lang="zh"}
- [Tell of His great renown, Lift high the kingly crown,]{lang="en"}\
  [傳說祂的大名，高舉祂的尊貴，]{lang="zh"}
- [Let every knee bow down At His blest name.]{lang="en"}\
  [萬膝因祂大名，無不下跪。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Who will go forth for Him? Who will arise?]{lang="en"}\
  [誰肯為祂前去，誰願興起？]{lang="zh"}
- [Though eyes with tears are dim, Severed love’s ties:]{lang="en"}\
  [雖因親人分離，淚流滴滴；]{lang="zh"}
- [Counting all things but loss, Earth’s highest gain but dross,]{lang="en"}\
  [但以十架為榮，視萬事如糞土，]{lang="zh"}
- [And glorying in the cross, Who will arise?]{lang="en"}\
  [所得一切虛空；誰肯站住？]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Go, for the crowning day Draws ever near;]{lang="en"}\
  [去罷，得講之日，已快臨近；]{lang="zh"}
- [Time will soon pass away, Jesus be here:]{lang="en"}\
  [時日即將消逝，耶穌快臨。]{lang="zh"}
- [Raise ye the cross where now Nations to idols bow;]{lang="en"}\
  [高舉十架前往，向拜偶像之民，]{lang="zh"}
- [Dawn o’er the mountain’s brow Tells He is near.]{lang="en"}\
  [將此信息宣揚：耶穌快臨。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Hark to the trumpet-blast! Jesus is King!]{lang="en"}\
  [聽哪，號聲已響，耶穌為王！]{lang="zh"}
- [He comes to reign at last, All conquering:]{lang="en"}\
  [政權歸祂執掌，永世無疆。]{lang="zh"}
- [Then the wide world shall own, Bending before His throne,]{lang="en"}\
  [全地向祂服降，座前俯伏頌揚，]{lang="zh"}
- [Jesus is King alone, Jesus is King!]{lang="en"}\
  [惟獨耶穌為王，耶穌為王！]{lang="zh"}
