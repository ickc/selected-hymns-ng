# 1

- [Oh, Christ in all His glory put on humanity]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，基督在祂榮耀裡穿上了人性，]{lang="zh"}
- [So He could be my Person, and live inside of me.]{lang="en"}\
  [藉此祂作我主人，並且內住於我；]{lang="zh"}
- [A man in life and being, He fully fits my case,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的生命與所是，對我祂全適應，]{lang="zh"}
- [So all His glorious Person can me replace.]{lang="en"}\
  [故祂這榮耀主人，取代了我。]{lang="zh"}

# 1-chorus

- [O Lord, O Lord, You are the man for me!]{lang="en"}\
  [哦主，哦主，你是那人為我！]{lang="zh"}
- [I take You as my Person, as my full identity.]{lang="en"}\
  [我接受你作我主人，作我的完全真體；]{lang="zh"}
- [O Lord, O Lord, subdue my every part,]{lang="en"}\
  [哦主，哦主，征服我每一部，]{lang="zh"}
- [And every moment, every place, make home in all my heart.]{lang="en"}\
  [每一時刻，每一處所，安家在我心裡。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Oh, Christ became the Spirit to speak inside of me,]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，基督成了那靈，在我裡面說話，]{lang="zh"}
- [And as the speaking Spirit He speaks unceasingly.]{lang="en"}\
  [作為那說話的靈，祂不停地說話。]{lang="zh"}
- [His speaking is the water that washes me within,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的說話如水在裡面將我沖刷，]{lang="zh"}
- [Discharging all my oldness, impaiting Him.]{lang="en"}\
  [清除我所有老舊，並分賜祂。]{lang="zh"}

# 2-chorus

- [O Lord, O Lord, speak all You want to me.]{lang="en"}\
  [哦主，哦主，儘量向我說話，]{lang="zh"}
- [Your speaking is Your presence—I must have it constantly.]{lang="en"}\
  [你說話是你的同在，我必接受不息；]{lang="zh"}
- [O Lord, O Lord, speak-wash my every part]{lang="en"}\
  [哦主，哦主，說、洗我的各部，]{lang="zh"}
- [And by this metabolic change, make home in all my heart.]{lang="en"}\
  [藉新陳代謝的變化，安家在我心裡。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Oh, Christ will come, the Bridegroom, for us, His glorious Bride,]{lang="en"}\
  [新郎基督就要來娶祂榮耀新婦，]{lang="zh"}
- [By coming from within us the church is glorified.]{lang="en"}\
  [藉從我們裡面將教會榮耀發表；]{lang="zh"}
- [His Person is the glory expanding now in us;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂這主人是榮耀在我裡面展布，]{lang="zh"}
- [This glory is our Person—how glorious!]{lang="en"}\
  [這是我們的主人，何其榮耀！]{lang="zh"}

# 3-chorus

- [O Lord, O Lord, by Your economy]{lang="en"}\
  [哦主，哦主，借著你的經營，]{lang="zh"}
- [So flood us with Your glory, saturate us thoroughly.]{lang="en"}\
  [將你榮耀徹底滿溢、浸透我們全人；]{lang="zh"}
- [Come out, come out, break forth in glory here,]{lang="en"}\
  [來罷，來罷，在榮耀裡顯現，]{lang="zh"}
- [And from the church, O glorious One, in glory soon appear.]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，那尊榮者藉教會，在榮耀裡顯身。]{lang="zh"}
