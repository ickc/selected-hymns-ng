# 1

- [Lord, accept our feeble song!]{lang="en"}\
  [主，接納我們的詩歌，]{lang="zh"}
- [Pow’r and praise to Thee belong;]{lang="en"}\
  [雖然聲音頂柔弱；]{lang="zh"}
- [We would all Thy grace record,]{lang="en"}\
  [我們述說你的恩篤，]{lang="zh"}
- [Holy, gracious, loving Lord!]{lang="en"}\
  [因你是我們救主。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Rich in glory, Thou didst stoop,]{lang="en"}\
  [因你捨去榮耀、豐富，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thence is all Thy people’s hope;]{lang="en"}\
  [你的信徒纔得福；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou wast poor, that we might be]{lang="en"}\
  [你變貧窮，叫你信徒]{lang="zh"}
- [Rich in glory, Lord, with Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [因你享榮耀、豐富。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Wherefore Thou high heaven didst spurn?]{lang="en"}\
  [天上有何使你心厭？]{lang="zh"}
- [Wherefore Thou to earth didst turn?]{lang="en"}\
  [世界有何使你羨？]{lang="zh"}
- [Why leave heav’n to come to earth]{lang="en"}\
  [因而你就離天臨世，]{lang="zh"}
- [Lonely, scorned, e’en suffering death?]{lang="en"}\
  [孤單、淒涼直到死？]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Thou in heav’n-the glorious One!]{lang="en"}\
  [你在天上何等榮耀！]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou on earth-the outcast Man!]{lang="en"}\
  [你在世上何蕭條！]{lang="zh"}
- [Though this suffering Thou didst know,]{lang="en"}\
  [你早已知此行苦惱，]{lang="zh"}
- [Love would come to bear our woe.]{lang="en"}\
  [只因愛我竟甘冒。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [When we think of love like this,]{lang="en"}\
  [當我想到你的良善，]{lang="zh"}
- [Joy and shame our hearts possess;]{lang="en"}\
  [就不禁又喜又慚！]{lang="zh"}
- [Joy, that Thou couldst pity thus;]{lang="en"}\
  [喜，因你能這樣愛好；]{lang="zh"}
- [Shame, for such returns from us.]{lang="en"}\
  [慚，因我這樣還報。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [Yet we hope the day to see]{lang="en"}\
  [但我們望那日快到，]{lang="zh"}
- [When from every hindrance free,]{lang="en"}\
  [脫盡所有的阻撓；]{lang="zh"}
- [When to Thee, in glory, brought,]{lang="en"}\
  [那時我們進榮耀裏，]{lang="zh"}
- [We shall serve Thee as we ought.]{lang="en"}\
  [要照本分服事你。]{lang="zh"}

# 7

- [Now, O Lord, we wait for Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [現今我們等在這裏，]{lang="zh"}
- [Wait “the blessed hope” to see.]{lang="en"}\
  [因這盼望受策勵；]{lang="zh"}
- [May we ever for Thee live,]{lang="en"}\
  [主，使我們活著為你，]{lang="zh"}
- [Till Thy saints Thou dost receive.]{lang="en"}\
  [直到你前同聚集。]{lang="zh"}
