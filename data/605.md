# 1

- [Take time to be holy, Speak oft with Thy Lord,]{lang="en"}\
  [用功追求聖潔， 多和主對講，]{lang="zh"}
- [Abide in Him always, And feed on His Word.]{lang="en"}\
  [常住在祂裡面， 以祂話為糧。]{lang="zh"}
- [Wait thou in His presence, Submissive and meek,]{lang="en"}\
  [等候在祂面前， 柔順且降服，]{lang="zh"}
- [Forgetting in nothing His blessing to seek.]{lang="en"}\
  [每件事上不忘, 尋求祂祝福。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Take time to be holy, The world rushes on;]{lang="en"}\
  [用功追求聖潔，　　事務雖忙碌；]{lang="zh"}
- [Spend much time in secret With Jesus alone.]{lang="en"}\
  [多花時間單獨　　　和主在密處。]{lang="zh"}
- [By looking to Jesus Like Him thou shalt be;]{lang="en"}\
  [借著仰望耶穌，　　變成祂形像；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy friends, in thy conduct, His likeness shall see.]{lang="en"}\
  [在你的行為上，　　人見祂模樣。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Take time to be holy, Let Him be thy guide;]{lang="en"}\
  [用功追求聖潔，　　讓祂作領導；]{lang="zh"}
- [And run not before Him Whatever betide;]{lang="en"}\
  [任何事情發生，　　不在祂前跑；]{lang="zh"}
- [In joy or in sorrow Still follow thy Lord,]{lang="en"}\
  [無論是樂是苦，　　始終跟隨主，]{lang="zh"}
- [And, looking to Jesus, Still trust in His Word.]{lang="en"}\
  [始終相信祂話，　　常仰望耶穌。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Take time to be holy, Be calm in thy soul,]{lang="en"}\
  [用功追求聖潔，　　內心須寧靜；]{lang="zh"}
- [Each thought and each temper Beneath His control.]{lang="en"}\
  [讓祂前來管理　　　意念和性情。]{lang="zh"}
- [Thus led by His Spirit To fountains of love,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂靈把你帶到　　　愛的發源所，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou soon shalt be fitted For service above.]{lang="en"}\
  [如此你纔配作　　　屬天的工作。]{lang="zh"}
