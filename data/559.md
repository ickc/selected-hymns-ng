# 1

- [Life out of death—dear Master, is it Spoken]{lang="en"}\
  [由死而生——恩主，這乃是說明，]{lang="zh"}
- [Of the life here, or in the better land?]{lang="en"}\
  [生命在此，或是在於來世？]{lang="zh"}
- [Nay, wherefore wait? The vessel marred and broken,]{lang="en"}\
  [不必等候！毀壞破碎的器皿，]{lang="zh"}
- [Shall now be moulded by the Potter’s hand.]{lang="en"}\
  [即今可在陶人手中陶制。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Life out of death—oh, wondrous resurrection!]{lang="en"}\
  [由死而生——何等奇妙的復活！]{lang="zh"}
- [Seed sown in conscious weakness, raised in power;]{lang="en"}\
  [種時明感軟弱，起時強壯；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy life lived out in days of toil and friction,]{lang="en"}\
  [你的生命顯於勞碌和折磨；]{lang="zh"}
- [”Not I, but Christ” in me from hour to hour.]{lang="en"}\
  [時刻非我，乃是基督我王。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Life out of death—a pilgrim path and lonely,]{lang="en"}\
  [由死而生——客旅道路何孤單，]{lang="zh"}
- [Trodden by those who glory in the Cross.]{lang="en"}\
  [認識十架者卻都願同行，]{lang="zh"}
- [They live in fellowship with “Jesus only,”]{lang="en"}\
  [效法祂死，他們與祂同苦難，]{lang="zh"}
- [And for His sake count earthly gain but loss.]{lang="en"}\
  [因祂，就將萬事當作有損。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [從天上的清澈、純淨江河，]{lang="zh"}
- [帶著活泉，生命洋溢又豐盛，]{lang="zh"}
- [告訴渴人，從茲無需再渴。]{lang="zh"}
