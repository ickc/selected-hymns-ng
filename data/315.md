# 1

- [In loving kindness Jesus came]{lang="en"}\
  [慈愛憐憫，祂來尋我，]{lang="zh"}
- [My soul in mercy to reclaim,]{lang="en"}\
  [降世為要將我得著。]{lang="zh"}
- [And from the depths of sin and shame]{lang="en"}\
  [祂從罪惡、羞恥深壑，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thro’ grace He lifted me.]{lang="en"}\
  [藉恩救拔了我。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [He called me long before I heard,]{lang="en"}\
  [我不識祂，祂來召我，]{lang="zh"}

  [激動我心，開我心鎖，]{lang="zh"}
- [But when I took Him at His word,]{lang="en"}\
  [發出恩言，免我罪過，]{lang="zh"}
- [Forgiv’n, He lifted me.]{lang="en"}\
  [是祂救拔了我！]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [His brow was pierced with many a thom,]{lang="en"}\
  [頭上荊冕，將祂刺傷，]{lang="zh"}
- [His hands by cruel nails were tom,]{lang="en"}\
  [手腳被釘，槍扎肋旁！]{lang="zh"}
- [When from my guilt and grief, forlorn,]{lang="en"}\
  [赦我罪愆，擔我憂傷，]{lang="zh"}
- [In love He lifted me.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂愛救拔了我。]{lang="zh"}
