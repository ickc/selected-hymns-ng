# 1

- [Christ is grace, coming to us from the Father above,]{lang="en"}\
  [基督是恩典，從那源頭——天父而來，]{lang="zh"}
- [Bringing us back to God, the source, who is love.]{lang="en"}\
  [帶我們歸神，因為——祂是愛。]{lang="zh"}
- [We partake, through our Savior, of the nature divine—]{lang="en"}\
  [因救主，我們竟得有分於神性情，]{lang="zh"}
- [Oh, how sublime!]{lang="en"}\
  [何等恩情！]{lang="zh"}
- [Sisters: By the grace, Oh, the love]{lang="en"}\
  [（姊妹）借著恩，進入愛，]{lang="zh"}
- [Brothers: that is flowing what a treasure,]{lang="en"}\
  [（弟兄）我們蒙恩,恒久不衰；]{lang="zh"}
- [Everyone: And the essence divine is saturating our soul.]{lang="en"}\
  [（全體）神聖性情浸透我們胸懷；]{lang="zh"}
- [Sisters: Into love Full and vast,]{lang="en"}\
  [（姊妹）哦，這愛，何豐厚，]{lang="zh"}
- [Brothers: we are growing; without measure,]{lang="en"}\
  [（弟兄）我心感戴；無法測透；]{lang="zh"}
- [Everyone: Will possess us and bring us to God’s goal.]{lang="en"}\
  [（全體）帶我們到神前來。]{lang="zh"}

- [Sisters: to the light Shining forth]{lang="en"}\
