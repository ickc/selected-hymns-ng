# 1

- [Joyful, joyful, we adore Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [快樂，快樂，我們敬拜]{lang="zh"}
- [God of glory, Lord of love;]{lang="en"}\
  [榮耀之神、愛之主；]{lang="zh"}
- [Hearts unfold like flow’rs before Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [到主面前，心如花開；]{lang="zh"}
- [Opening to the sun above.]{lang="en"}\
  [主如旭日，光耀目。]{lang="zh"}
- [Melt the clouds of sin and sadness;]{lang="en"}\
  [愁雲罪霧，求主消去；]{lang="zh"}
- [Drive the dark of doubt away;]{lang="en"}\
  [疑惑黑蔭求散開；]{lang="zh"}
- [Giver of immortal gladness,]{lang="en"}\
  [永遠快樂，求主賜予，]{lang="zh"}
- [Fill us with the light of day!]{lang="en"}\
  [旭日光輝滿我懷！]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [All Thy works with joy surround Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [主的造物全都歡樂，]{lang="zh"}
- [Earth and heav’n reflect Thy rays,]{lang="en"}\
  [天地反映主光輝，]{lang="zh"}
- [Stars and angels sing around Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [群星、天使，圍繞謳歌，]{lang="zh"}
- [Center of unbroken praise.]{lang="en"}\
  [不斷向祂獻讚美。]{lang="zh"}
- [Field and forest, vale and mountain,]{lang="en"}\
  [高山、幽谷、森林、平原，]{lang="zh"}
- [Flowery meadow, flashing sea,]{lang="en"}\
  [草場青翠、波平靜，]{lang="zh"}
- [Chanting bird and flowing fountain]{lang="en"}\
  [清歌小鳥，輕注流泉，]{lang="zh"}
- [Call us to rejoice in Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [感動我心樂無盡。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Thou art giving and forgiving,]{lang="en"}\
  [主既施恩，又施赦免，]{lang="zh"}
- [Ever blessing, ever blest,]{lang="en"}\
  [永遠賜福，我敬愛；]{lang="zh"}
- [Wellspring of the joy of living,]{lang="en"}\
  [生活喜樂，活水泉源，]{lang="zh"}
- [Ocean depth of happy rest!]{lang="en"}\
  [歡樂、安息似深海！]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou our Father, Christ our Brother—]{lang="en"}\
  [神是我父，基督我兄，]{lang="zh"}
- [All who live in love are Thine;]{lang="en"}\
  [凡活愛裏皆主民；]{lang="zh"}
- [Teach us how to love each other,]{lang="en"}\
  [指教我們愛裏交通，]{lang="zh"}
- [Lift us to the joy divine.]{lang="en"}\
  [同享神聖的歡欣。]{lang="zh"}
