# 1

- [“Be all at rest, my soul!” Oh! blessed secret]{lang="en"}\
  [“你當安息！”這是有福的秘訣，]{lang="zh"}
- [Of the true life that glorifies thy Lord;]{lang="en"}\
  [使你生活真能討主喜悅；]{lang="zh"}
- [Not always doth the busiest soul best serve Him,]{lang="en"}\
  [並非最忙的人，最能事奉神，]{lang="zh"}
- [But he who resteth on His faithful word.]{lang="en"}\
  [乃是安息在祂話上的人。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [“Be all at rest!” for rest is highest service;]{lang="en"}\
  [“你當安息！”安息乃最高服事；]{lang="zh"}
- [To the still heart God doth His secrets tell;]{lang="en"}\
  [神的秘密平靜的心得知；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thus shalt thou learn to wait, and watch, and labor,]{lang="en"}\
  [故當學習等候、儆醒並勞力；]{lang="zh"}
- [Strengthened to bear, since Christ in thee doth dwell.]{lang="en"}\
  [從主得力，因祂住在你裡。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [“Be all at rest!” for rest alone becometh]{lang="en"}\
  [“你當安息！”惟獨安息方證實，]{lang="zh"}
- [The soul that casts on Him its every care;]{lang="en"}\
  [你已將一切掛慮交托主；]{lang="zh"}
- [“Be all at rest!” so shall thy life proclaim Him]{lang="en"}\
  [“你當安息！”如此生活將見證：]{lang="zh"}
- [A God who worketh and who heareth prayer.]{lang="en"}\
  [是神工作，是神聽人求懇。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [“Be all at rest!” so shalt thou be an answer]{lang="en"}\
  [“你當安息！”當人問說：“誰是神？]{lang="zh"}
- [To those who question, “Who is God, and where?”]{lang="en"}\
  [祂在那裡？”你就是個覆訊；]{lang="zh"}
- [For God is rest, and where He dwells is stillness,]{lang="en"}\
  [神是安息，祂居住在安靜裡，]{lang="zh"}
- [And they who dwell in Him that rest shall share.]{lang="en"}\
  [凡住祂裡，就必分享安息。]{lang="zh"}
