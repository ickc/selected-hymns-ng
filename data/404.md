# 1

- [Bless’d are the pure in heart,]{lang="en"}\
  [清心的人有福，]{lang="zh"}
- [For they shall see our God;]{lang="en"}\
  [他們必得見神；]{lang="zh"}
- [The secret of the Lord is theirs,]{lang="en"}\
  [主的秘訣他們握住－]{lang="zh"}
- [Their heart is Christ’s abode.]{lang="en"}\
  [讓主安家於心。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [The Lord, who left the heavens,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂離天上居處，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our life and peace to bring,]{lang="en"}\
  [帶來生命、平安，]{lang="zh"}
- [To dwell in lowliness with men,]{lang="en"}\
  [降卑自己，與人同住，]{lang="zh"}
- [Their pattern and their King:]{lang="en"}\
  [作人榜樣、君王。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [He to the lowly soul]{lang="en"}\
  [來向卑微的人，]{lang="zh"}
- [Doth still Himself impart,]{lang="en"}\
  [將祂自己分賜；]{lang="zh"}
- [And for His dwelling and His throne]{lang="en"}\
  [揀選那些清心的人，]{lang="zh"}
- [Chooseth the pure in heart.]{lang="en"}\
  [使祂安家、管治。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Lord, we Thy presence seek;]{lang="en"}\
  [我尋求你同在，]{lang="zh"}
- [May ours this blessing be;]{lang="en"}\
  [願主賜此福氣；]{lang="zh"}
- [Give us a pure and lowly heart,]{lang="en"}\
  [賜我清潔謙卑、心懷，]{lang="zh"}
- [A temple meet for Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [供你居住、安息。]{lang="zh"}
