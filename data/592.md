# 1

- [A little bird I am,]{lang="en"}\
  [我是一隻籠中小鳥，]{lang="zh"}
- [Shut from the fields of air,]{lang="en"}\
  [遠離天空、曠闊野地；]{lang="zh"}
- [And in my cage I sit and sing]{lang="en"}\
  [是祂將我安置於此，]{lang="zh"}
- [To Him who placed me there;]{lang="en"}\
  [我願向祂歌頌不已；]{lang="zh"}
- [Well pleased a prisoner to be,]{lang="en"}\
  [如此被囚，我甚歡欣，]{lang="zh"}
- [Because, my God, it pleaseth Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [因這，我神，使你稱心。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Nought have I else to do,]{lang="en"}\
  [禁中我無他事可作，]{lang="zh"}
- [I sing the whole day long;]{lang="en"}\
  [終日只是歡然歌唱；]{lang="zh"}
- [And He whom most I love to please]{lang="en"}\
  [我所使之稱心的神，]{lang="zh"}
- [Doth listen to my song;]{lang="en"}\
  [也在傾聽我的頌揚；]{lang="zh"}
- [He caught and bound my wandering wing;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂捆綁了我的翅膀，]{lang="zh"}
- [But still He bends to hear me sing.]{lang="en"}\
  [卻愛俯首聽我歌唱。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Thou hast an ear to hear]{lang="en"}\
  [哦神，你是有耳能聽，]{lang="zh"}
- [A heart to love and bless;]{lang="en"}\
  [你也有心施愛賜福；]{lang="zh"}
- [And though my notes were e’er so rude,]{lang="en"}\
  [我的音調雖然粗陋，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou wouldst not hear the less;]{lang="en"}\
  [你卻毫不鄙棄厭惡；]{lang="zh"}
- [Because Thou knowest as they fall,]{lang="en"}\
  [因你知這音調之弦，]{lang="zh"}
- [That love, sweet love, inspires them all.]{lang="en"}\
  [乃是甜美之愛所彈。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [My cage confines me round;]{lang="en"}\
  [這籠將我四面禁錮，]{lang="zh"}
- [Abroad I cannot fly;]{lang="en"}\
  [我難外飛任意遨遊；]{lang="zh"}
- [But though my wing is closely bound,]{lang="en"}\
  [我的翅膀雖被捆住，]{lang="zh"}
- [My heart’s at liberty;]{lang="en"}\
  [我心我靈仍是自由；]{lang="zh"}
- [For prison walls cannot control]{lang="en"}\
  [監牢牆垣，不能阻擋]{lang="zh"}
- [The flight, the freedom of the soul.]{lang="en"}\
  [心靈所有釋放、翱翔。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [O it is good to soar]{lang="en"}\
  [我心超越監牢之閂，]{lang="zh"}
- [These bolts and bars above!]{lang="en"}\
  [我靈騰飛何其自在！]{lang="zh"}
- [To Him whose purpose I adore,]{lang="en"}\
  [向著心愛之主騰飛，]{lang="zh"}
- [Whose providence I love;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的旨意我所敬拜；]{lang="zh"}
- [And in Thy mighty will to find]{lang="en"}\
  [在你堅定旨意之中，]{lang="zh"}
- [The joy, the freedom of the mind.]{lang="en"}\
  [我靈得到自由、歡騰。]{lang="zh"}
