# 1

- [Is it Thy will that I should be]{lang="en"}\
  [我今受浸，是你旨意，]{lang="zh"}
- [Buried, in symbol, Lord, with Thee;]{lang="en"}\
  [象徵與你一同埋葬；]{lang="zh"}
- [Owning Thee by this solemn sign,]{lang="en"}\
  [你是屬我，我也屬你，]{lang="zh"}
- [Telling the world that I am Thine?]{lang="en"}\
  [藉此表記，向世顯彰。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Gladly I yield obedience now;]{lang="en"}\
  [我今歡然順從你命，]{lang="zh"}
- [In all things to Thy will I’d bow;]{lang="en"}\
  [在凡事上，主旨是從；]{lang="zh"}
- [I’ll follow where my Savior led,]{lang="en"}\
  [我已定意隨你而行，]{lang="zh"}
- [And humbly in His footsteps tread.]{lang="en"}\
  [謙卑跟從你的腳蹤。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [This emblematic, watery grave]{lang="en"}\
  [這水乃是象徵墳墓，]{lang="zh"}
- [Shows forth His love—who came to save;]{lang="en"}\
  [表明你愛前來救贖；]{lang="zh"}
- [And as I enter it, I see]{lang="en"}\
  [我一進入就得目睹，]{lang="zh"}
- [The price my Savior paid for me.]{lang="en"}\
  [救主為我罪債已付。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Forth from Thy burial, Lord, I come,]{lang="en"}\
  [從這埋葬，我今進前，]{lang="zh"}
- [For Thou hast triumphed o’er the tomb;]{lang="en"}\
  [因你已經勝過墳墓；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy resurrection life I share—]{lang="en"}\
  [復活生命，長我裡面，]{lang="zh"}
- [My portion is no longer here.]{lang="en"}\
  [我不再被墳墓困住。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Oh, may I count myself to be]{lang="en"}\
  [你已代我眾罪受死，]{lang="zh"}
- [Dead to the sins that wounded Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [我今向罪已算為死；]{lang="zh"}
- [Dead to the pleasures of this earth,]{lang="en"}\
  [向這世樂，我也已死．]{lang="zh"}
- [Unworthy of my heav’nly birth.]{lang="en"}\
  [屬天生命，開始於茲。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [Lord Jesus, when I gaze on Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [主耶穌，我向你注目，]{lang="zh"}
- [And all Thy radiant glory see,]{lang="en"}\
  [只要一見你的榮臉，]{lang="zh"}
- [That joy will far exceed the shame]{lang="en"}\
  [喜樂遠超今生羞辱，]{lang="zh"}
- [I bear on earth for Thy loved name.]{lang="en"}\
  [為你尊名，雖苦亦甜。]{lang="zh"}
