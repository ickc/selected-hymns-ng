# 1

- [Lord of glory, we adore Thee!]{lang="en"}\
  [神的基督、榮耀的主，]{lang="zh"}
- [Christ of God, ascended high!]{lang="en"}\
  [你的心慈、你恩溥！]{lang="zh"}
- [Heart and soul we bow before Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [我們現在全魂、全心，]{lang="zh"}
- [Glorious now beyond the sky;]{lang="en"}\
  [到你面前來朝覲；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thee we worship, Thee we praise—]{lang="en"}\
  [我敬拜你、讚美你，]{lang="zh"}
- [Excellent in all Thy ways.]{lang="en"}\
  [你的道路無可比！]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Anointed King, with glory crowned,]{lang="en"}\
  [受膏的王、一切的主，]{lang="zh"}
- [Rightful Heir and Lord of all!]{lang="en"}\
  [進入榮耀得萬福！]{lang="zh"}
- [Once rejected, seemed, disowned,]{lang="en"}\
  [前曾被人譏笑、厭棄，]{lang="zh"}
- [E’en by those Thou cam’st to call:]{lang="en"}\
  [你欲救人，人絕你；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thee we honor, Thee adore—]{lang="en"}\
  [我敬愛你、稱頌你，]{lang="zh"}
- [Glorious now and evermore.]{lang="en"}\
  [今得尊榮至無極。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Lord of life! to death once subject;]{lang="en"}\
  [你曾死過，生命的主，]{lang="zh"}
- [Blesser, yet a curse once made:]{lang="en"}\
  [祝福人者竟受詛！]{lang="zh"}
- [Of Thy Father’s heart the object.]{lang="en"}\
  [你是父神所愛所鍾，]{lang="zh"}
- [Yet in depths of anguish laid;]{lang="en"}\
  [竟受苦難學順從；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thee we gaze on, Thee recall]{lang="en"}\
  [我仰望你、記念你，]{lang="zh"}
- [Bearing here our sorrows all.]{lang="en"}\
  [為我親歷憂患地。]{lang="zh"}
