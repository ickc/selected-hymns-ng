# 1

- [In all thy work, O Lord, Thou didst]{lang="en"}\
  [主，你所有一切工作，]{lang="zh"}
- [At Calv’ry’s cross once come to rest;]{lang="en"}\
  [並非到了十架為止；]{lang="zh"}
- [Yet Thou art working still today,]{lang="en"}\
  [你今乃是仍舊生活，]{lang="zh"}
- [But in another form expressed.]{lang="en"}\
  [從你另一形態表示。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Thy saving pow’r Thou still dost show;]{lang="en"}\
  [你今仍舊施行拯救，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou still dost speak, enlighten, guide;]{lang="en"}\
  [仍舊說話，仍舊引導；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou and the Spirit in one stream]{lang="en"}\
  [你與聖靈已成一流，]{lang="zh"}
- [Sweep many in Thy living tide.]{lang="en"}\
  [仍舊光照，使人仆倒。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Through Him Thy power’s not withheld;]{lang="en"}\
  [藉祂，你不停止能力，]{lang="zh"}
- [Through Him Thy working does not cease;]{lang="en"}\
  [藉祂，你不停止運行；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou still dost comfort and command,]{lang="en"}\
  [你的感動、你的鼓勵；]{lang="zh"}
- [Encourage, strengthen, and release.]{lang="en"}\
  [你仍安慰，你仍命令。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Since Thou art with the Spirit one]{lang="en"}\
  [因為你已成為那靈，]{lang="zh"}
- [His coming means that Thou hast come,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂來就是你又來地，]{lang="zh"}
- [And His indwelling is Thine own,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的內住是你充盈，]{lang="zh"}
- [Since Thou the Spirit hast become.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂代表你，祂又是你。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [He executes within my heart]{lang="en"}\

- [All Thy desires and Thy demands,]{lang="en"}\
  [你的一切命令、希冀 ；]{lang="zh"}
- [As for the Father here on earth]{lang="en"}\
  [有如從前你在世境，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou hast performed all His commands.]{lang="en"}\

# 6

- [Obeying Him we Thee obey;]{lang="en"}\
  [服祂就是服你旨意；]{lang="zh"}
- [Allowing Him ourselves to fill,]{lang="en"}\
