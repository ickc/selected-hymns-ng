# 1

- [It passeth knowledge, that dear love of Thine,]{lang="en"}\
  [你的大愛，過於人所能度，]{lang="zh"}
- [My Savior, Jesus; yet this soul of mine]{lang="en"}\
  [我救主耶穌！但不堪的我，]{lang="zh"}
- [Would of Thy love in all its breadth and length,]{lang="en"}\
  [真要知道它的高、深、長、闊，]{lang="zh"}

  [好叫它的能力，越顯越多]{lang="zh"}

# 2

  [我救主耶穌！但我的嘴唇]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [It passeth praises, that dear love of Thine,]{lang="en"}\
  [你的奇愛，過於人所能讚，]{lang="zh"}
- [My Savior, Jesus; yet this heart of mine]{lang="en"}\
  [我救主耶穌！但我的心坎]{lang="zh"}
- [Would sing that love, so full, so rich, so free,]{lang="en"}\
  [總要歌唱那愛，這大！這滿！]{lang="zh"}

  [因為惡性如我，也得恩湛，]{lang="zh"}
- [Nigh unto God.]{lang="en"}\
  [來親近神。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [But though I cannot sing, or tell, or know]{lang="en"}\
  [我今雖然不能在這地方]{lang="zh"}

  [但我要帶我的虛空心房，]{lang="zh"}
- [O Thou, who art of love the living spring,]{lang="en"}\
  [來到你這愛的泉源良港，]{lang="zh"}
- [My vessel fill.]{lang="en"}\
  [求你充滿！]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [I am an empty vessel—not one thought]{lang="en"}\

  [因你愛我！]{lang="zh"}

- [Thither may I, in simple faith draw nigh,]{lang="en"}\

  [倚靠、鑑賞、稱羨並享受你，]{lang="zh"}
- [But unto Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [不再他求。]{lang="zh"}
