# 1

- [The living of Christ when He sojourned on earth,]{lang="en"}\
  [當基督寄居在地時的生活，]{lang="zh"}
- [The sixteenth Psalm shows us, was wholly of worth.]{lang="en"}\
  [如詩篇十六篇所記的情形：]{lang="zh"}
- [His attitudes, choices, and interests all tell]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的態度、選擇、喜好，]{lang="zh"}
- [The man who in God’s tabernacle may dwell.]{lang="en"}\
  [都述說住在神帳幕內之人的光景。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [“Preserve me, O God, for in Thee I confide”—]{lang="en"}\
  [“神阿，保佑我，因為我投靠你；”]{lang="zh"}
- [In God He took refuge whate’er did betide.]{lang="en"}\
  [——祂以神為避難所，凡事倚賴。]{lang="zh"}
- [“O Thou art my Lord, I’ve no good beyond Thee”]{lang="en"}\
  [“你是我的主，”祂不斷向神提：]{lang="zh"}
- [He spoke in Himself unto God constantly.]{lang="en"}\
  [“神阿，我的好處不在你以外。”]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [“As for all the saints who are dwelling on earth,”]{lang="en"}\
  [“論到世上的聖民，又美又善；”]{lang="zh"}
- [To Him they’re the people of excellent worth.]{lang="en"}\
  [——祂說他們是祂所最喜悅的；]{lang="zh"}
- [Of them He has said He has all His delight—]{lang="en"}\
  [四福音書都證實這個恩言，]{lang="zh"}
- [In proving this all the four Gospels unite.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂甚至為他們捨棄了自己。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [“The Lord is my portion, the Lord is my cup”—]{lang="en"}\
  [“神是我的產業，我杯中的分；”]{lang="zh"}
- [For everything He to the Father looked up.]{lang="en"}\
  [——無論何事，祂都以父為信託。]{lang="zh"}
- [“The Lord I will bless who my counsel doth give”]{lang="en"}\
  [“我必要稱頌那指教我的神。”]{lang="zh"}
- [Himself He denied and by God’s word did live.]{lang="en"}\
  [——祂否認自己，單靠神話而活。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [His heart thus was glad and His spirit rejoiced,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂因此靈裏快樂、心裏歡喜，]{lang="zh"}
- [And e’en in His death praise to God could be voiced;]{lang="en"}\
  [甚至在死裏仍揚聲讚美神；]{lang="zh"}
- [His soul God would never abandon to hell,]{lang="en"}\
  [神必不使祂魂在陰間被棄，]{lang="zh"}
- [But in resurrection His body would dwell.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的身體要復活不見朽損。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [God raised Him, and He with God’s glory was crowned;]{lang="en"}\
  [神使祂復起，以榮耀為冠冕；]{lang="zh"}
- [Then fullness of joy in God’s presence He found.]{lang="en"}\
  [在神面前尋到滿足的喜樂。]{lang="zh"}
- [E’en now at the Father’s right hand is His seat,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂今已高升坐在神的右邊，]{lang="zh"}
- [Where flowing forever are pleasures complete.]{lang="en"}\
  [從那裏湧流出永遠的福樂。]{lang="zh"}
