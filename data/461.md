# 1

- [Jesus, my Savior! Thou art mine,]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌，我主！你是屬我，]{lang="zh"}
- [The Father’s gift of love divine;]{lang="en"}\
  [是父的愛所賜福分；]{lang="zh"}
- [All Thou hast done, and all Thou art,]{lang="en"}\
  [你的一切所是、所作，]{lang="zh"}
- [Are now the portion of my heart.]{lang="en"}\
  [今都已成我的永分。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Poor, feeble, wretched, as I am,]{lang="en"}\
  [貧窮、軟弱、可憐如我，]{lang="zh"}
- [I now can glory in Thy name;]{lang="en"}\
  [今竟能歸榮耀給你；]{lang="zh"}
- [Now cleansed in Thy most precious blood]{lang="en"}\
  [我今已蒙寶血洗濯，]{lang="zh"}
- [And made the righteousness of God.]{lang="en"}\
  [使我成為神的公義。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [All that Thou hast Thou hast for me,]{lang="en"}\
  [你之所有成我永分，]{lang="zh"}
- [All my fresh springs are hid in Thee;]{lang="en"}\
  [我的泉源藏你裡頭；]{lang="zh"}
- [In Thee I live; While I confess]{lang="en"}\
  [因你我活，歡然承認：]{lang="zh"}
- [I nothing am, yet all possess.]{lang="en"}\
  [我是無有，你是萬有！]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [O Savior, teach me to abide]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，我救主，教我靠近，]{lang="zh"}
- [Close sheltered at Thy wounded side,]{lang="en"}\
  [藏在你的受傷肋邊，]{lang="zh"}
- [Each hour receiving “grace on grace,”]{lang="en"}\
  [時刻領受“恩上加恩”，]{lang="zh"}
- [Until I see Thee face to face.]{lang="en"}\
  [直至面見你的榮臉。]{lang="zh"}
