# 1

- [I dare not be defeated With Calvary in view,]{lang="en"}\
  [我不敢稍微失敗，因有加略在望，]{lang="zh"}
- [Where Jesus conquered Satan, Where all His foes He slew;]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌在彼曾奏凱，勝過黑暗君王；]{lang="zh"}
- [Come, Lord, and give the vision To nerve me for the fight,]{lang="en"}\
  [求主賜給我異象，我纔臨陣奮興，]{lang="zh"}
- [Make me an overcomer Clothed with Thy Spirit’s might.]{lang="en"}\
  [使我作個得勝者，靠著你的大能。]{lang="zh"}
