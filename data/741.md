# 1

- [Lord, I am continually with Thee:]{lang="en"}\
  [主阿，我照常與你同在，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou hast holden me by my right hand,]{lang="en"}\
  [你攙著我的右手，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou shalt guide me,]{lang="en"}\
  [你引導我走路，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou shalt guide me with Thy counsel,]{lang="en"}\
  [以你的訓言引導我；]{lang="zh"}
- [And afterward receive me,]{lang="en"}\
  [並且以後必接我，]{lang="zh"}
- [Receive me to glory.]{lang="en"}\
  [接我到榮耀裡。]{lang="zh"}
- [Whom have I in heaven but Thee?]{lang="en"}\
  [除你以外，在天有誰？]{lang="zh"}
- [And there is none upon the earth]{lang="en"}\
  [除你以外，]{lang="zh"}
- [That I desire beside Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [在地上我也沒有所愛慕的。]{lang="zh"}
- [My flesh and my heart faileth;]{lang="en"}\
  [我肉體、心腸衰殘，]{lang="zh"}
- [But God is the strength of my heart,]{lang="en"}\
  [但神是我心裡力量，]{lang="zh"}
- [And my portion forever.]{lang="en"}\
  [是我福分到永遠。]{lang="zh"}
