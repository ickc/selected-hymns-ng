# 1

- [Arise, my soul, arise!]{lang="en"}\
  [起來，我魂！起來，]{lang="zh"}
- [Shake off thy guilty fears;]{lang="en"}\
  [拋開恐懼心情；]{lang="zh"}
- [The bleeding Sacrifice]{lang="en"}\
  [寶血已流出來，]{lang="zh"}
- [In my behalf appears.]{lang="en"}\
  [替我擔罪受刑。]{lang="zh"}
- [Before the throne my Surety stands,]{lang="en"}\
  [我的中保座前站立，]{lang="zh"}
- [My name is written on His hands.]{lang="en"}\
  [我的名字刻祂掌裡。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [He ever lives above]{lang="en"}\
  [祂是長遠活著，]{lang="zh"}
- [For me to intercede,]{lang="en"}\
  [代求使我也活，]{lang="zh"}
- [His all-redeeming love,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂愛救贖了我，]{lang="zh"}
- [His precious blood to plead.]{lang="en"}\
  [寶血我擔過，]{lang="zh"}
- [His blood was shed for all our race,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂血曾我們流過，]{lang="zh"}
- [And sprinkles now the throne of grace.]{lang="en"}\
  [如今灑於施恩寶座。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Five bleeding wounds He bears,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂在加略山上，]{lang="zh"}
- [Received on Calvary;]{lang="en"}\
  [身受五處創傷，]{lang="zh"}
- [They pour effectual prayers;]{lang="en"}\
  [有效禱告獻上，]{lang="zh"}
- [They strongly speak for me.]{lang="en"}\
  [神前為我求講；]{lang="zh"}
- [Forgive Him, Oh, forgive, they cry,]{lang="en"}\
  [“他已赦免！他已釋放！]{lang="zh"}
- [Nor let that ransomed sinner die!]{lang="en"}\
  [既蒙救贖，當免死亡！”]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [The Father hears Him pray,]{lang="en"}\
  [天父聽祂代求，]{lang="zh"}
- [His dear anointed One;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的受膏愛子；]{lang="zh"}
- [He cannot turn away]{lang="en"}\
  [神不能拒接受，]{lang="zh"}
- [The presence of His Son.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的心愛兒子。]{lang="zh"}
- [His Spirit answers to the blood,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂靈回答寶血求懇，]{lang="zh"}
- [And tells me I am born of God.]{lang="en"}\
  [細語告我是神所生。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [My God is reconciled,]{lang="en"}\
  [我己與神和好，]{lang="zh"}
- [His pardoning voice I hear;]{lang="en"}\
  [聽見祂寬恕聲；]{lang="zh"}
- [He owns me for His child,]{lang="en"}\
  [作神兒女蒙召，]{lang="zh"}
- [I can no longer fear.]{lang="en"}\
  [不讓疑惑叢生；]{lang="zh"}
- [With confidence I now draw nigh,]{lang="en"}\
  [我今坦然滿懷信心，]{lang="zh"}
- [And Father, Abba, Father, cry.]{lang="en"}\
  [前來呼叫：“阿爸！父神！”]{lang="zh"}
