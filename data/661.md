# 1

- [Glorious things of thee are spoken,]{lang="en"}\
  [榮耀聖城必快降臨，]{lang="zh"}
- [Holy city of our God;]{lang="en"}\
  [神的話中早豫定；]{lang="zh"}
- [He whose word cannot be broken]{lang="en"}\
  [祂要永遠居住其中，]{lang="zh"}
- [Formed thee for His own abode;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂話既出不落空。]{lang="zh"}
- [On the Rock of Ages founded,]{lang="en"}\
  [萬古磐石為城根基，]{lang="zh"}
- [What can shake thy sure repose?]{lang="en"}\
  [穩固無人可搖移；]{lang="zh"}
- [With salvation’s walls surrounded,]{lang="en"}\
  [救恩為牆高大雄壯，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou may’st smile at all thy foes.]{lang="en"}\
  [盡可嗤笑眾仇敵。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [See the streams of living waters,]{lang="en"}\
  [生命活水湧流不息，]{lang="zh"}
- [Springing from eternal love,]{lang="en"}\
  [源出永恆不變愛；]{lang="zh"}
- [Well supply thy blessed members,]{lang="en"}\
  [神的兒女享用不盡，]{lang="zh"}
- [And all fear of want remove;]{lang="en"}\
  [缺乏之懼永不再。]{lang="zh"}
- [Who can faint, when such a river]{lang="en"}\
  [生命之河不斷湧流，]{lang="zh"}
- [Ever flows their thirst t’ assuage?]{lang="en"}\
  [有何乾渴不能消？]{lang="zh"}
- [Grace which, like the Lord, the giver,]{lang="en"}\
  [何況我神豐厚恩典，]{lang="zh"}
- [Never fails from age to age.]{lang="en"}\
  [仍然賜給無終了！]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Blest constituents of Zion,]{lang="en"}\
  [借著救主寶血洗淨，]{lang="zh"}
- [Washed in the Redeemer’s blood;]{lang="en"}\
  [組成有福的錫安；]{lang="zh"}
- [Jesus, whom their souls rely on,]{lang="en"}\
  [聖徒作神祭司、君王，]{lang="zh"}
- [Makes them kings and priests to God.]{lang="en"}\
  [因靠耶穌得完全。]{lang="zh"}
- [’Tis His love His people raises]{lang="en"}\
  [提升祂所愛的子民，]{lang="zh"}
- [Over self to reign as kings:]{lang="en"}\
  [身居榮位掌王權；]{lang="zh"}
- [And as priests, His worthy praises,]{lang="en"}\
  [又作祭司神前侍立，]{lang="zh"}
- [Each his thankful offering brings.]{lang="en"}\
  [讚美為祭壇上獻。]{lang="zh"}
