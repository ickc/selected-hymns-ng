# 1

- [Jesus, lover of my soul,]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌，靈魂的愛人，]{lang="zh"}
- [Let me to Thy bosom fly,]{lang="en"}\
  [你懷讓我來藏身；]{lang="zh"}
- [While the nearer waters roll,]{lang="en"}\
  [驚波駭浪滾滾近！]{lang="zh"}
- [While the tempest still is high:]{lang="en"}\
  [狂風暴雨陣陣緊！]{lang="zh"}
- [Hide me, O my Savior, hide,]{lang="en"}\
  [藏我！哦，主，求藏我！]{lang="zh"}
- [Till the storm of life is past;]{lang="en"}\
  [直到今生風波過！]{lang="zh"}
- [Safe into the haven guide;]{lang="en"}\
  [引我安全到路盡，]{lang="zh"}
- [O receive my soul at last.]{lang="en"}\
  [至終求接我靈魂！]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Other refuge have I none,]{lang="en"}\
  [別無避所能藏隱，]{lang="zh"}
- [Hangs my helpless soul on Thee;]{lang="en"}\
  [無助之人托你身；]{lang="zh"}
- [Leave, oh, leave me not alone,]{lang="en"}\
  [不！哦，不要丟棄我！]{lang="zh"}
- [Still support and comfort me.]{lang="en"}\
  [扶持、安慰，望無輟：]{lang="zh"}
- [All my trust on Thee is stayed,]{lang="en"}\
  [所有倚靠寄你身，]{lang="zh"}
- [All my help from Thee I bring;]{lang="en"}\
  [所有救助由你臨；]{lang="zh"}
- [Cover my defenseless head]{lang="en"}\
  [頭無遮護、身無蔽，]{lang="zh"}
- [With the shadow of Thy wing.]{lang="en"}\
  [求你聖翼來覆庇。]{lang="zh"}

- [Just and holy is Thy name,]{lang="en"}\

- [I am all unrighteousness;]{lang="en"}\
  [我全不義，滿罪孽；]{lang="zh"}

  [我是邪惡，沒良善，]{lang="zh"}

  [恩典、真理，你充滿。]{lang="zh"}
