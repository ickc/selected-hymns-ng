# 1

- [All hail the pow’r of Jesus’ name!]{lang="en"}\
  [何等權能！耶穌尊名！]{lang="zh"}
- [Let angels prostrate fall;]{lang="en"}\
  [天使全都俯伏，]{lang="zh"}
- [Bring forth the royal diadem,]{lang="en"}\
  [獻上冠冕，同心尊敬，]{lang="zh"}
- [And crown Him Lord of all!]{lang="en"}\
  [加冠祂萬有主；]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Ye chosen seed of Israel’s race,]{lang="en"}\
  [以色列民被選族類，]{lang="zh"}
- [Ye ransomed from the fall,]{lang="en"}\
  [墮落，蒙贖聖徒，]{lang="zh"}
- [Hail Him who saves you by His grace,]{lang="en"}\
  [靠恩得救，應當讚美，]{lang="zh"}
- [And crown Him Lord of all!]{lang="en"}\
  [加冠祂萬有主！]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Let every kindred, every tribe,]{lang="en"}\
  [但願地上萬族、萬民，]{lang="zh"}
- [On this terrestrial ball,]{lang="en"}\
  [都向救主俯伏，]{lang="zh"}
- [To Him all majesty ascribe,]{lang="en"}\
  [榮耀、尊貴都歸祂名，]{lang="zh"}
- [And crown Him Lord of all!]{lang="en"}\
  [加冠祂萬有主！]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [O that with yonder sacred throng]{lang="en"}\
  [億萬群眾歌聲雷動，]{lang="zh"}
- [We at His feet may fall!]{lang="en"}\
  [我也一同歡呼，]{lang="zh"}
- [We’ll join the everlasting song,]{lang="en"}\
  [直到永遠敬拜、稱頌，]{lang="zh"}
- [And crown Him Lord of all!]{lang="en"}\
  [加冠祂萬有主！]{lang="zh"}
