# 1

- [I cannot breathe enough of Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [涼爽，你像柔和微風，]{lang="zh"}
- [O gentle breeze of love;]{lang="en"}\
  [怎能將你吸夠！]{lang="zh"}
- [More fragrant than the myrtle tree]{lang="en"}\
  [芬芳勝過所有花叢，]{lang="zh"}
- [The Henna-flower is to me,]{lang="en"}\
  [如同沒藥在我懷中，]{lang="zh"}
- [The Balm of Heaven above.]{lang="en"}\
  [又像天上膏油！]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [滋潤，你像清晨甘露，]{lang="zh"}
- [怎能被你浸夠！]{lang="zh"}
- [新鮮、清涼、消幹、解枯、]{lang="zh"}
- [滋我心情、潤我深處；]{lang="zh"}
- [願你將我浸透。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [I cannot gaze enough on Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [你是美麗中的至美，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou Fairest of the Fair;]{lang="en"}\
  [怎能將你賞夠！]{lang="zh"}
- [My heart is filled with ecstasy,]{lang="en"}\
  [你的面容所發光輝，]{lang="zh"}
- [As in Thy face of radiancy]{lang="en"}\
  [使我更感你美可貴：]{lang="zh"}
- [I see such beauty there.]{lang="en"}\
  [你是美中之首！]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [I cannot yield enough to Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [你是救主、我主、我友，]{lang="zh"}
- [My Savior, Master, Friend;]{lang="en"}\
  [怎能將你侍夠！]{lang="zh"}
- [I do not wish to go out free,]{lang="en"}\
  [我真不願隨意自由，]{lang="zh"}
- [But ever, always, willingly,]{lang="en"}\
  [甘心服事，殷勤不休，]{lang="zh"}
- [To serve Thee to the end.]{lang="en"}\
  [直到一生盡頭！]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [I cannot sing enough of Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [你名香甜遠超眾名，]{lang="zh"}
- [The sweetest name on earth;]{lang="en"}\
  [怎能將你唱夠！]{lang="zh"}
- [A note so full of melody]{lang="en"}\
  [我心充滿甜美感應，]{lang="zh"}
- [Comes from my heart so joyously,]{lang="en"}\
  [深願向你不住頌稱！]{lang="zh"}
- [And fills my soul with mirth.]{lang="en"}\
  [喜樂湧自心頭。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [I cannot speak enough of Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [你的自己可愛、饒優，]{lang="zh"}
- [I have so much to tell;]{lang="en"}\
  [怎能將你說夠！]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy heart it beats so tenderly]{lang="en"}\
  [你心柔細，同情、俯就，]{lang="zh"}
- [As Thou dost draw me close to Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [吸引我心向你倚投，]{lang="zh"}
- [And whisper, “All is well.”]{lang="en"}\
  [細語：“有你就夠！”]{lang="zh"}
