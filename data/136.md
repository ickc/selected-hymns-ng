# 1

- [Oh, the deep, deep love of Jesus,]{lang="en"}\
  [深哉，深哉，耶穌的愛，]{lang="zh"}
- [Vast, unmeasured, boundless, free;]{lang="en"}\
  [無限廣闊，無限量！]{lang="zh"}
- [Rolling as a mighty ocean,]{lang="en"}\
  [浩浩蕩蕩有如汪洋，]{lang="zh"}
- [In its fullness over me,]{lang="en"}\
  [主愛臨我何週詳！]{lang="zh"}
- [Underneath me, all around me,]{lang="en"}\
  [把我蔭庇，把我環繞，]{lang="zh"}
- [Is the current of Thy love;]{lang="en"}\
  [是主滔滔大慈愛；]{lang="zh"}
- [Leading onward, leading homeward,]{lang="en"}\
  [導我前行，領我回家，]{lang="zh"}
- [To my glorious rest above.]{lang="en"}\
  [安息天家到萬代。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Oh, the deep, deep love of Jesus,]{lang="en"}\
  [深哉，深哉，耶穌的愛，]{lang="zh"}
- [Spread His praise form shore to shore;]{lang="en"}\
  [當得讚美、當傳開！]{lang="zh"}
- [How He loveth, ever loveth,]{lang="en"}\
  [何等的愛，永久的愛，]{lang="zh"}
- [Changeth never, never more;]{lang="en"}\
  [世世代代不更改！]{lang="zh"}
- [How He watcheth o’er His loved ones,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂常看顧祂的兒女，]{lang="zh"}
- [Died to call them all His own;]{lang="en"}\
  [為召他們，祂捨身；]{lang="zh"}
- [How for them He intercedeth,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂為他們，父前代求，]{lang="zh"}
- [Watcheth o’er them from the throne.]{lang="en"}\
  [寶座之上常施恩。]{lang="zh"}
