# 1

- [Lamb of God! our souls adore Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [神的羔羊，我敬拜你！]{lang="zh"}
- [While upon Thy face we gaze;]{lang="en"}\
  [當我注視你榮面，]{lang="zh"}
- [There the Father’s love and glory]{lang="en"}\
  [你臉閃耀明亮光輝，]{lang="zh"}
- [Shine in all their brightest rays;]{lang="en"}\
  [全是父愛所彰顯。]{lang="zh"}
- [Thine Almighty pow’r and wisdom]{lang="en"}\
  [天地萬物都見證你，]{lang="zh"}
- [All creation’s works proclaim;]{lang="en"}\
  [智慧、能力何無限，]{lang="zh"}
- [Heav’n and earth alike confess Thee]{lang="en"}\
  [並且證實你乃“我是”，]{lang="zh"}
- [As the ever great “I AM.”]{lang="en"}\
  [永遠常在、不改變！]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Lamb of God! Thy Father’s bosom]{lang="en"}\
  [神的羔羊，父的愛子！]{lang="zh"}
- [Ever was Thy dwelling place;]{lang="en"}\
  [原本住在父懷裏 ；]{lang="zh"}
- [His delight, in Him rejoicing,]{lang="en"}\
  [你是父的喜樂、滿足，]{lang="zh"}
- [One with Him in pow’r and grace;]{lang="en"}\
  [同有父的恩與力；]{lang="zh"}
- [O what wondrous love and mercy!]{lang="en"}\
  [竟然為了愛憐我們，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou didst lay Thy glory by;]{lang="en"}\
  [離開榮耀的天域，]{lang="zh"}
- [And for us didst come from heaven]{lang="en"}\
  [作神羔羊，代人受死，]{lang="zh"}
- [As the Lamb of God to die.]{lang="en"}\
  [配得我們的拜禮。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Lamb of God! When we behold Thee]{lang="en"}\
  [神的羔羊，降生人世，]{lang="zh"}
- [Lowly in the manger laid,]{lang="en"}\
  [馬槽起始歷艱苦，]{lang="zh"}
- [Wand’ring as a homeless stranger]{lang="en"}\
  [飄泊人間，被人輕視，]{lang="zh"}
- [In the world Thy hands had made;]{lang="en"}\
  [誰識你是創造主？]{lang="zh"}
- [When we see Thee in the garden]{lang="en"}\
  [客西馬尼，汗如血滴，]{lang="zh"}
- [In Thine agony of blood,]{lang="en"}\
  [知否為誰向父哭？]{lang="zh"}
- [At Thy grace we are confounded,]{lang="en"}\
  [奇恩臨我，使我覺醒，]{lang="zh"}
- [Holy, spotless Lamb of God!]{lang="en"}\
  [羔羊乃是救我主！]{lang="zh"}
