# 1

- [你在地上所有行徑，]{lang="zh"}
- [何其仁慈、美麗！]{lang="zh"}
- [你在人性不凡生平，]{lang="zh"}
- [令人歎賞、驚奇！]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [What grace, O Lord, and and beauty shone]{lang="en"}\
  [你在艱苦歷程顯出]{lang="zh"}
- [Around Thy steps below!]{lang="en"}\
  [何等忍耐的愛！]{lang="zh"}
- [What patient love was seen in all]{lang="en"}\
  [艱苦至死亦所不顧，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy life and death of woe!]{lang="en"}\
  [忍、愛，誰能比賽！]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Forever on Thy burdened heart]{lang="en"}\
  [你心受創，重負難減，]{lang="zh"}
- [A weight of sorrow hung,]{lang="en"}\
  [常是憂患重重；]{lang="zh"}
- [Yet no ungentle, murm'ring word]{lang="en"}\
  [然而從無不美怨言，]{lang="zh"}
- [Escaped Thy silent tongue.]{lang="en"}\
  [出你緘默口中。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Thy foes did hate, despise, revile,]{lang="en"}\
  [敵者妒恨，拒者辱罵，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy friends Unfaithful prove;]{lang="en"}\
  [從者顯為不忠，]{lang="zh"}
- [Unwearied in forgiveness still,]{lang="en"}\
  [你仍不煩，寬赦有加，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy heart could only love!]{lang="en"}\
  [你心惟愛是從。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [哦！主，在地你的生平，]{lang="zh"}
- [各方真如素祭：]{lang="zh"}
- [柔細、香甜、完全、均衡，]{lang="zh"}
- [神、人共賞不已。]{lang="zh"}
