# 1

- [Jesus! how much Thy name unfolds]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌！你名何等芬芳，]{lang="zh"}
- [To ev’ry opened ear;]{lang="en"}\
  [在蒙恩人身上！]{lang="zh"}
- [The pardoned sinner’s mem’ry holds]{lang="en"}\
  [得救罪人，心裏所念，]{lang="zh"}
- [None other half so dear.]{lang="en"}\
  [無一像你這甜。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Thy name encircles every grace]{lang="en"}\
  [神來為人，所表恩義，]{lang="zh"}
- [That God as man could show;]{lang="en"}\
  [你名包含無遺，]{lang="zh"}
- [There only could He fully trace]{lang="en"}\
  [天上生命、世間生活，]{lang="zh"}
- [A life divine below.]{lang="en"}\
  [神在你身顯著！]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Jesus—it speaks a life of love,]{lang="en"}\
  [“耶穌”這名表明大愛，]{lang="zh"}
- [Of sorrows meekly home;]{lang="en"}\
  [如何忍受禍災，]{lang="zh"}
- [It tells of sympathy above,]{lang="en"}\
  [這名說出我們零丁，]{lang="zh"}
- [Whatever makes us mourn.]{lang="en"}\
  [天上如何同情。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Jesus, the One who knew no sin,]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌無罪，為我成罪，]{lang="zh"}
- [Made sin to make us just;]{lang="en"}\
  [使我蒙恩受惠；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou gav’st Thyself our love to win,]{lang="en"}\
  [你捨你身，來得我心，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our full confiding trust.]{lang="en"}\
  [今後你重我輕。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [The mention of Thy name shall bow]{lang="en"}\
  [題起你名，我心即伏，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our hearts to worship Thee;]{lang="en"}\
  [敬拜救主我主；]{lang="zh"}
- [The chiefest of ten thousand Thou,]{lang="en"}\
  [你愛救我，我今歸你]{lang="zh"}
- [Whose love has set us free.]{lang="en"}\
  [這超人上的你！]{lang="zh"}
