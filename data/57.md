# 1

- [How sweet the Name of Jesus sounds]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌，這名甜美、芬芳，]{lang="zh"}
- [In a believer’s ear!]{lang="en"}\
  [在蒙恩人耳中！]{lang="zh"}
- [It soothes his sorrow, heals his wounds,]{lang="en"}\
  [消除憂愁，醫治創傷，]{lang="zh"}
- [And drives away his fear.]{lang="en"}\
  [驅盡所有驚恐。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [It makes the wounded spirit whole,]{lang="en"}\
  [這名醫治憂傷的靈，]{lang="zh"}
- [And calms the troubled breast;]{lang="en"}\
  [安慰痛苦心情；]{lang="zh"}
- [’Tis manna to the hungry soul,]{lang="en"}\
  [是飢餓者的生命餅，]{lang="zh"}
- [And to the weary rest.]{lang="en"}\
  [是疲倦者安寧。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Dear Name! the rock on which we build;]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌這名是我磐石，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our shield and hiding-place;]{lang="en"}\
  [我盾牌、避難所；]{lang="zh"}
- [Our never-failing treasury, filled]{lang="en"}\
  [這名積蓄無窮恩賜，]{lang="zh"}
- [With boundless stores of grace.]{lang="en"}\
  [取用永不短絀。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Jesus, our Savior, Shepherd, Friend,]{lang="en"}\
  [是我牧人、朋友、救主，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our Prophet, Priest, and King;]{lang="en"}\
  [先知、祭司、君王；]{lang="zh"}
- [Our Lord, our Life, our Way, our End,]{lang="en"}\
  [是我生命、標竿、道路，]{lang="zh"}
- [Accept the praise we bring.]{lang="en"}\
  [我要向祂頌揚。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Weak is the effort of our heart,]{lang="en"}\
  [我心疲弱，祂來鼓舞，]{lang="zh"}
- [And cold our warmest thought;]{lang="en"}\
  [冷淡，祂來焚燒；]{lang="zh"}
- [But when we see Thee as Thou art,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂之所是，如此豐富，]{lang="zh"}
- [We’ll praise Thee as we ought.]{lang="en"}\
  [配得我口稱道。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [Till then we would Thy love proclaim]{lang="en"}\
  [我要高聲，盡情稱頌，]{lang="zh"}
- [With every fleeting breath;]{lang="en"}\
  [宣告祂愛無極；]{lang="zh"}
- [And triumph in that blessed Name]{lang="en"}\
  [在祂名裏，我要誇勝，]{lang="zh"}
- [Which quells the pow’r of death.]{lang="en"}\
  [消除死亡能力。]{lang="zh"}
