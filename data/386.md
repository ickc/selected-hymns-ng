# 1

- [My Jesus, as Thou wilt!]{lang="en"}\
  [主阿，照你旨意，]{lang="zh"}
- [Oh, may Thy will be mine!]{lang="en"}\
  [願我能以順從！]{lang="zh"}
- [Into Thy hand of love]{lang="en"}\
  [在你愛的手裏，]{lang="zh"}
- [I would my all resign;]{lang="en"}\
  [凡事我都聽從；]{lang="zh"}
- [Through sorrow, or through joy,]{lang="en"}\
  [無論憂、樂臨我，]{lang="zh"}
- [Conduct me as Thine own,]{lang="en"}\
  [求主親自引領，]{lang="zh"}

  [助我仍能唱說：]{lang="zh"}

  [“主，你旨意得成。”]{lang="zh"}
