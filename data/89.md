# 1

- [Alas! and did my Savior bleed?]{lang="en"}\
  [哎喲！救主真曾流血？]{lang="zh"}
- [And did my Sov’reign die,]{lang="en"}\
  [真曾捨命亡躬？]{lang="zh"}
- [Would He devote that sacred head]{lang="en"}\
  [祂肯犧牲祂的超越，]{lang="zh"}
- [For such a worm as I?]{lang="en"}\
  [為我這個小蟲？]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Was it for sins that I had done]{lang="en"}\
  [祂在木上那樣哀歎，]{lang="zh"}
- [He groaned upon the tree?]{lang="en"}\
  [可是為我罪愆？]{lang="zh"}
- [Amazing pity! grace unknown!]{lang="en"}\
  [憐憫何滿！慈愛何汎！]{lang="zh"}
- [And love beyond degree!]{lang="en"}\
  [恩典何其無邊！]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Well might the sun in darkness hide,]{lang="en"}\
  [難怪太陽立變暗烏，]{lang="zh"}

  [隱藏一切榮光；]{lang="zh"}

  [當神基督造物的主，]{lang="zh"}
