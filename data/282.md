# 1

- [Loved with everlasting love,]{lang="en"}\
  [永遠的愛已愛我，]{lang="zh"}
- [Led by grace that love to know;]{lang="en"}\
  [這愛藉恩我賞識；]{lang="zh"}
- [Spirit, breathing from above,]{lang="en"}\
  [聖靈從上來吹著，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou hast taught me it is so.]{lang="en"}\
  [為要如此來指示。]{lang="zh"}
- [Oh, this full and perfect peace!]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，這豐滿的平安！]{lang="zh"}
- [Oh, this transport all divine!]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，這神聖的歡樂！]{lang="zh"}
- [In a love which cannot cease,]{lang="en"}\
  [在這不息愛裏面，]{lang="zh"}
- [I am His, and He is mine.]{lang="en"}\
  [我是屬祂，祂屬我。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Heaven above is softer blue,]{lang="en"}\
  [頭上之天何蔚藍，]{lang="zh"}
- [Earth around is sweeter green;]{lang="en"}\
  [四周之地也青綠；]{lang="zh"}
- [Something lives in every hue]{lang="en"}\
  [有一景色更鮮艷，]{lang="zh"}
- [Christless eyes have never seen:]{lang="en"}\
  [無主之目從未睹：]{lang="zh"}
- [Birds with gladder songs o’erflow,]{lang="en"}\
  [鳥鳴使我更心悅，]{lang="zh"}
- [Flow’rs with deeper beauties shine,]{lang="en"}\
  [花美使我更快活，]{lang="zh"}
- [Since I know, as now I know,]{lang="en"}\
  [自從我心能領略：]{lang="zh"}
- [I am His, and He is mine.]{lang="en"}\
  [我是屬祂，祂屬我。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Things that once were wild alarms]{lang="en"}\
  [有事曾使我驚恐，]{lang="zh"}
- [Cannot now disturb my rest;]{lang="en"}\
  [今不再擾我安息；]{lang="zh"}
- [Closed in everlasting arms,]{lang="en"}\
  [靠在永久膀臂中，]{lang="zh"}
- [Pillowed on the loving breast.]{lang="en"}\
  [枕在愛的胸懷裏。]{lang="zh"}
- [Oh, to lie forever here,]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，願永遠臥於此，]{lang="zh"}
- [Doubt and care and self resign,]{lang="en"}\
  [憂疑自己，全逃脫，]{lang="zh"}
- [While He whispers in my ear.]{lang="en"}\
  [當祂柔聲的指示：]{lang="zh"}
- [I am His, and He is mine.]{lang="en"}\
  [我是屬祂，祂屬我。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [His forever, only His:]{lang="en"}\
  [我是永遠只屬祂，]{lang="zh"}
- [Who the Lord and me shall part?]{lang="en"}\
  [誰能使祂與我分？]{lang="zh"}
- [Ah, with what a rest of bliss]{lang="en"}\
  [祂在我心來安家，]{lang="zh"}
- [Christ can fill the loving heart.]{lang="en"}\
  [有福、安息滿我心。]{lang="zh"}
- [Heaven and earth may fade and flee,]{lang="en"}\
  [天地可以都廢去，]{lang="zh"}
- [Firstborn light in gloom decline;]{lang="en"}\
  [亮光也可全衰落，]{lang="zh"}
- [But, while God and I shall be,]{lang="en"}\
  [但主與我永同居，]{lang="zh"}
- [I am His, and He is mine.]{lang="en"}\
  [我是屬祂，祂屬我。]{lang="zh"}
