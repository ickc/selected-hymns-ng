# 1

- [Thy mighty love, O God, constraineth me,]{lang="en"}\
  [哦神，你的大愛來激勵我，]{lang="zh"}
- [As some strong tide it preseth on its way,]{lang="en"}\
  [如同潮水湧進我的心懷，]{lang="zh"}
- [Seeking a channel in my self-bound soul,]{lang="en"}\
  [如活水江河從我魂流過，]{lang="zh"}
- [Yearning to sweep all barriers away.]{lang="en"}\
  [沖去我裡面一切的障礙。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Shall I not yield to that constraining power?]{lang="en"}\
  [我怎能不服你大愛能力？]{lang="zh"}
- [Shall I not say, O tide of love, flow in?]{lang="en"}\
  [我怎能不讓愛潮進我心？]{lang="zh"}
- [My God, Thy gentleness hath conquered me,]{lang="en"}\
  [你的溫柔滿了降服能力，]{lang="zh"}
- [Life cannot be as it hath hither been.]{lang="en"}\
  [使我生命從此有了更新。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Break through my nature, mighty heavenly love,]{lang="en"}\
  [屬天的愛破碎我的天然，]{lang="zh"}
- [Clear every avenue of thought and brain,]{lang="en"}\
  [清掃我心中的每一思路，]{lang="zh"}
- [Flood my affections, purify my will,]{lang="en"}\
  [純淨我意志，滿溢我情感，]{lang="zh"}
- [Let nothing but Thine own pure life remain.]{lang="en"}\
  [除你純淨生命，別無他物。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Thus wholly mastered and possessed by God,]{lang="en"}\
  [願我全人被神佔有、管理，]{lang="zh"}
- [Forth from my life, spontaneous and free,]{lang="en"}\
  [使你生命從我不絕流出，]{lang="zh"}
- [Shall flow a stream of tenderness and grace,]{lang="en"}\
  [恩典、柔愛之河暢流不息，]{lang="zh"}
- [Loving, because God loved, eternally.]{lang="en"}\
