# 1

- [Jesus! that name we love, Jesus, our Lord!]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌！我愛這名，耶穌我主！]{lang="zh"}
- [Jesus, all names above, Jesus, the Lord!]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌！遠超萬名，耶穌我主！]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou, Lord, our all must be;]{lang="en"}\
  [主，你作我一切，]{lang="zh"}
- [Nothing that’s good have we,]{lang="en"}\
  [你外我無基業，]{lang="zh"}
- [Nothing apart from Thee, Jesus, our Lord!]{lang="en"}\
  [有你我無所缺，耶穌我主！]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [As Son of Man it was, Jesus, the Lord!]{lang="en"}\
  [你曾成為人子， 耶穌我主！]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou gav’st Thy life for us, Jesus, our Lord!]{lang="en"}\
  [你曾替我受死， 耶穌我主！]{lang="zh"}
- [Great was indeed Thy love,]{lang="en"}\
  [你愛真是殊優，]{lang="zh"}
- [All other loves above,]{lang="en"}\
  [遠超人世所有，]{lang="zh"}
- [Love Thou didst dearly prove, Jesus, our Lord!]{lang="en"}\
  [救恩因你成就， 耶穌我主！]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Righteous alone in Thee, Jesus, the Lord!]{lang="en"}\
  [我惟因你得生， 耶穌我主！]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou wilt a refuge be, Jesus, our Lord!]{lang="en"}\
  [我惟靠你得勝， 耶穌我主！]{lang="zh"}
- [Whom, then, have we to fear,]{lang="en"}\
  [我們還怕甚麼]{lang="zh"}
- [What trouble, grief, or care,]{lang="en"}\
  [憂慮、苦難、鬼魔！]{lang="zh"}
- [Since Thou art ever near, Jesus, our Lord!]{lang="en"}\
  [因為有你相佐， 耶穌我主！]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Soon Thou wilt come again, Jesus, the Lord!]{lang="en"}\
  [不久你要再臨， 耶穌我主！]{lang="zh"}
- [We shall be happy then, Jesus, our Lord!]{lang="en"}\
  [我們快要歡欣， 耶穌我主！]{lang="zh"}
- [When Thine own face we see,]{lang="en"}\
  [那時我們見你，]{lang="zh"}
- [Then shall we like Thee be,]{lang="en"}\
  [我們就要像你，]{lang="zh"}
- [Then evermore with Thee, Jesus, our Lord!]{lang="en"}\
  [並要永遠偕你， 耶穌我主！]{lang="zh"}
