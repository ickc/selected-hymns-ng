# 1

- [In the heart of Jesus There is love for you,]{lang="en"}\
  [在主耶穌心頭，有愛為著你；]{lang="zh"}
- [Love most pure and tender, Love most deep and true;]{lang="en"}\
  [這愛純潔、溫柔，且深切無比。]{lang="zh"}
- [Why should you be lonely, Why for friendship sigh,]{lang="en"}\
  [為何你感孤單，常歎無友伴？]{lang="zh"}
- [When the heart of Jesus Has a full supply?]{lang="en"}\
  [因在主心裡面，正將你顧念。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [In the mind of Jesus There is thought for you,]{lang="en"}\
  [在主耶穌心上， 常將你關注；]{lang="zh"}
- [Warm as summer sunshine, Sweet as morning dew;]{lang="en"}\
  [溫暖有如陽光， 甘甜如晨露；]{lang="zh"}
- [Why should you be fearful, Why take anxious thought,]{lang="en"}\
  [為何你感不安， 常掛慮、憂愁？]{lang="zh"}
- [Since the mind of Jesus Cares for those He bought?]{lang="en"}\
  [因主耶穌思念, 凡祂所拯救。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [In the field of Jesus There is work for you;]{lang="en"}\
  [在主耶穌田裡， 有份工為你；]{lang="zh"}
- [Such as even angels Might rejoice to do;]{lang="en"}\
  [天使也都願意， 能一同參與。]{lang="zh"}
- [Why stand idly sighing For some life-work grand,]{lang="en"}\
  [為何閑站歎息， 嚮往作大事？]{lang="zh"}
- [While the field of Jesus Seeks your reaping hand?]{lang="en"}\
  [正當主的田裡， 需你來盡職。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [In the Church of Jesus There’s a place for you;]{lang="en"}\
  [在主的教會裡， 有處所為你；]{lang="zh"}
- [Glorious, bright, and joyous, Right and peaceful too;]{lang="en"}\
  [榮耀、光明、公義，喜樂又安息。]{lang="zh"}
- [Why then, like a wand’rer, Roam with weary pace,]{lang="en"}\
  [為何在外徘徊， 如無家可歸？]{lang="zh"}
- [If the Church of Jesus Holds for you a place?]{lang="en"}\
  [正當在主教會， 有處為你備。]{lang="zh"}
