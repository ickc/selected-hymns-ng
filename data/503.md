# 1

- [Thou hast said Thou art the Vine, Lord,]{lang="en"}\
  [我主，你說你是真樹，]{lang="zh"}
- [And that I’m a branch in Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [我是你的真枝子；]{lang="zh"}
- [But I do not know the reason]{lang="en"}\
  [但我不知為何緣故，]{lang="zh"}
- [Why I should so barren be.]{lang="en"}\
  [我是如此不結實。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Bearing fruit is my deep longing,]{lang="en"}\
  [我心羡慕結果更多，]{lang="zh"}
- [More Thy life to manifest,]{lang="en"}\
  [更能彰顯你生命；]{lang="zh"}
- [To Thy throne to bring more glory,]{lang="en"}\
  [更能榮耀歸與寶座，]{lang="zh"}
- [That Thy will may be expressed.]{lang="en"}\
  [更能禱告得答應。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [But I fail to understand, Lord,]{lang="en"}\
  [但是，主阿，我不明白]{lang="zh"}
- [What it means—“abide in me”]{lang="en"}\
  [“常在裡面”的聖言；]{lang="zh"}
- [For the more I seek “abiding,”]{lang="en"}\
  [我越追求要去“常在”，]{lang="zh"}
- [More I feel I’m not in Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [我越發現在外邊。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [How I feel I’m not abiding;]{lang="en"}\
  [我不覺得我在裡面，]{lang="zh"}
- [Though I pray and strongly will,]{lang="en"}\
  [也禱告並立志！]{lang="zh"}
- [Yet from me Thou seemest distant]{lang="en"}\
  [但你都像隔開很遠，]{lang="zh"}
- [And my life is barren still.]{lang="en"}\
  [生命仍然無果子。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Yet Thou art the Vine, Thou saidst it.]{lang="en"}\
  [但你是說，你已是樹，]{lang="zh"}
- [And I am a branch in Thee;]{lang="en"}\
  [我也已是你枝子；]{lang="zh"}
- [When I take Thee as my Savior,]{lang="en"}\
  [當我信你作我救主，]{lang="zh"}
- [Then this fact is wrought in me.]{lang="en"}\
  [聖靈已成此事實。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [Now I’m in Thee and I need not]{lang="en"}\
  [我今已是在你裡面，]{lang="zh"}
- [Seek into Thyself to come,]{lang="en"}\
  [再也不必求加入；]{lang="zh"}
- [For I’m joined to Thee already,]{lang="en"}\
  [我是已經與你結聯，]{lang="zh"}
- [With Thy flesh and bones I’m one.]{lang="en"}\
  [完全合一的骨肉。]{lang="zh"}

# 7

- [Not to “go in” is the secret,]{lang="en"}\
  [秘訣不是要我“去在”，]{lang="zh"}
- [But that I’m “already in”!]{lang="en"}\
  [事實是我“已在”內；]{lang="zh"}
- [That I ne’er may leave I’d ask Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [所求乃是不要離開，]{lang="zh"}
- [Not how I may get within.]{lang="en"}\
  [不是如何得地位。]{lang="zh"}

# 8

- [I am in, already in Thee!]{lang="en"}\
  [我已在內，已在你內！]{lang="zh"}
- [What a place to which I’m brought!]{lang="en"}\
  [這是神作的事實！]{lang="zh"}
- [There’s no need for prayer or struggling,]{lang="en"}\
  [不必禱告，不必行為，]{lang="zh"}
- [God Himself the work has wrought.]{lang="en"}\
  [乃是神手的設施。]{lang="zh"}

# 9

- [Since I’m in, why ask to enter;]{lang="en"}\
  [我已在內怎求入內！]{lang="zh"}
- [O how ignorant I’ve been!]{lang="en"}\
  [我是何等的愚昧，]{lang="zh"}
- [Now with praise and much rejoicing]{lang="en"}\
  [現今我只歡樂讚美，]{lang="zh"}
- [For Thy Word, I dwell therein.]{lang="en"}\
  [相信聖言的無偽。]{lang="zh"}

# 10

- [Now in Thee I rest completely,]{lang="en"}\
  [我今只有完全安息，]{lang="zh"}
- [With myself I gladly part;]{lang="en"}\
  [知道你是我一切，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou art life and Thou art power,]{lang="en"}\
  [你是生命、你是能力，]{lang="zh"}
- [All in all to me Thou art.]{lang="en"}\
  [而我一切都了結。]{lang="zh"}
