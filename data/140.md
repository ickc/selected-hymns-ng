# 1

- [I have a Friend, whose faithful love]{lang="en"}\
  [我有一位奇妙救主，]{lang="zh"}
- [Is more than all the world to me:]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的愛情舉世莫如，]{lang="zh"}
- [‘Tis higher than the heights above,]{lang="en"}\
  [深海雖深，它還要深；]{lang="zh"}
- [And deeper than the soundless sea;]{lang="en"}\
  [高天雖高，它還要高；]{lang="zh"}
- [So old, so new, So strong, so true;]{lang="en"}\
  [亦新、亦陳、又摰、又真，]{lang="zh"}
- [Before the earth received its frame,]{lang="en"}\
  [不分境地、不理晚早；]{lang="zh"}
- [He loved me—Blessed be His name!]{lang="en"}\
  [祂愛我，讚美祂的名！]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [He held the highest place above,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂在天上位分至尊，]{lang="zh"}
- [Adored by all the sons of flame,]{lang="en"}\
  [天使、天軍無不拜頓，]{lang="zh"}
- [Yet such His self-denying love,]{lang="en"}\
  [卻因情愛非常堅強，]{lang="zh"}
- [He laid aside His crown and came]{lang="en"}\
  [以至願意撇棄穹蒼，]{lang="zh"}
- [To seek the lost, And at the cost]{lang="en"}\
  [來尋迷羊，並且受戕，]{lang="zh"}
- [Of heavenly rank and earthly fame]{lang="en"}\
  [捨了天福，又失世賞，]{lang="zh"}
- [He sought me—Blessed be His name!]{lang="en"}\
  [祂尋我，讚美祂的名！]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [It was a lonely path He trod,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的道路實在孤單，]{lang="zh"}
- [From every human soul apart;]{lang="en"}\
  [沒有同心、沒有同伴；]{lang="zh"}
- [Known only to Himself and God]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的所有患難、憂愁，]{lang="zh"}
- [Was all the grief that filled His heart,]{lang="en"}\
  [惟祂自己和神知道；]{lang="zh"}
- [Yet from the track He turned not back]{lang="en"}\
  [但祂前走，不稍退後，]{lang="zh"}

  [直等祂到我的囚牢，]{lang="zh"}
- [He found me—Blessed be His name!]{lang="en"}\
  [祂見我，讚美祂的名！]{lang="zh"}

# 4

  [祂被人賣，祂被人告，]{lang="zh"}

  [祂就如此——零丁孤苦，]{lang="zh"}
