  [寶座上的羔羊；]{lang="zh"}
- [Hark! How the heav’nly anthem drowns]{lang="en"}\
  [聽哪！聖歌自天而降，]{lang="zh"}
- [All music but its own!]{lang="en"}\
  [掩蓋其他聲響。]{lang="zh"}
- [Awake, my soul, and sing]{lang="en"}\
  [我魂！醒起歌唱，]{lang="zh"}
- [Of him who died for thee;]{lang="en"}\

- [Rich wounds, yet visible above,]{lang="en"}\
  [渾身鞭傷仍然可望，]{lang="zh"}
- [In beauty glorified;]{lang="en"}\
  [顯於屬天榮光。]{lang="zh"}
- [No angel in the sky]{lang="en"}\
  [無一天使能夠]{lang="zh"}
- [Can fully bear that sight,]{lang="en"}\

- [But downward bends his wond’ring eye]{lang="en"}\
