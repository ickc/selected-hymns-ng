# 1

- [Be still, my heart! these anxious cares]{lang="en"}\
  [我心，安靜！這些掛慮，]{lang="zh"}
- [To thee are burdens, thorns and snares;]{lang="en"}\
  [是你重擔、陷阱、荊棘；]{lang="zh"}
- [They cast dishonor on the Lord,]{lang="en"}\
  [使主蒙羞，聖靈擔憂，]{lang="zh"}
- [And contradict His gracious word.]{lang="en"}\
  [使主恩言不得成就。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Brought safely by His hand thus far,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂手領你至今安然，]{lang="zh"}
- [Why wilt thou now give place to fear?]{lang="en"}\
  [是何使你畏懼膽寒？]{lang="zh"}
- [How canst thou want if He provide,]{lang="en"}\
  [有祂供應，何來缺乏？]{lang="zh"}
- [Or lose thy way with such a Guide?]{lang="en"}\
  [有祂引領，必不會差！]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [When first before His mercy-seat]{lang="en"}\
  [當你初次到祂恩座，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou didst to Him thine all commit;]{lang="en"}\
  [已將一切全都交托；]{lang="zh"}
- [He gave thee warrant from that hour]{lang="en"}\
  [那時祂已給你保證，]{lang="zh"}
- [To trust His wisdom, love, and power.]{lang="en"}\
  [信靠祂的愛心、權能。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [And has He not His promise passed,]{lang="en"}\
