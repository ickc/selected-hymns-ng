# 1

- [Will your anchor hold in the storms of life,]{lang="en"}\
  [你人生航程，忽起大風暴，]{lang="zh"}
- [When the clouds unfold their wings of strife?]{lang="en"}\
  [你所拋之錨，是否可靠？]{lang="zh"}
- [When the strong tides lift, and the cables strain,]{lang="en"}\
  [狂浪排天來，繩索似不牢，]{lang="zh"}
- [Will your anchor drift or firm remain?]{lang="en"}\
  [你錨將隨波或不動搖？]{lang="zh"}

- [We have an anchor that keeps the soul]{lang="en"}\
  [我心堅定因我錨牢靠，]{lang="zh"}
- [Steadfast and sure while the billows roll,]{lang="en"}\
  [在風浪中仍穩定不搖，]{lang="zh"}
- [Fastened to the Rock which cannot move,]{lang="en"}\
  [因它緊扣在磐石之上，]{lang="zh"}
- [Grounded firm and deep in the Savior’s love.]{lang="en"}\
  [深固主愛裏，無比的剛強。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [It is safely moored, ‘twill the storm withstand,]{lang="en"}\
  [安居良港內，能不懼風浪，]{lang="zh"}
- [For ’tis well secured by the Savior’s hand;]{lang="en"}\
  [有主的手臂，我永穩當；]{lang="zh"}
- [And the cables passed from His heart to mine,]{lang="en"}\
  [我心已與祂，永遠相聯結，]{lang="zh"}
- [Can defy the blast, through strength divine.]{lang="en"}\
  [神聖的能力，保守一切。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [It will firmly hold in the straits of fear,]{lang="en"}\
  [經險惡海峽，仍勇往無懼，]{lang="zh"}
- [When the breakers have told the reef is near;]{lang="en"}\
  [礁上碎浪聲，表明險域；]{lang="zh"}
- [Though the tempest rave and the wild winds blow,]{lang="en"}\
  [波浪雖兇猛，狂風雖咆哮，]{lang="zh"}
- [Not an angry wave shall our bark o’erflow.]{lang="en"}\
  [無任何波濤，把我心搖。]{lang="zh"}
