# 1

- [How true it is no heart may comprehend]{lang="en"}\
  [神為屬祂的人所備榮耀，]{lang="zh"}

  [並這世代末日將生何事，]{lang="zh"}
- [And what will happen when this age shall end;]{lang="en"}\
  [實在無人能以領會、知曉；]{lang="zh"}
- [But yet in vision Jesus hath made known]{lang="en"}\
  [但主在異象中早已啟示：]{lang="zh"}
- [How fair and holy shall the Church descend,]{lang="en"}\
  [教會從天降，美麗且榮耀，]{lang="zh"}

  [光輝明亮有如碧玉寶石。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [And He shall make His Church all heavenly fair,]{lang="en"}\
  [主將使祂教會全然美麗，]{lang="zh"}
- [With-gold and pearls, and every radiant stone,]{lang="en"}\
  [如精金、珍珠，並閃亮寶石；]{lang="zh"}
- [And reign in holiness and glory there,]{lang="en"}\
  [在聖潔裡為王，榮耀無比，]{lang="zh"}
- [And shine as suns and stars have never shone;]{lang="en"}\
  [所發光輝遠勝天上星日；]{lang="zh"}
- [And He shall lead His Bride, His joy and care,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂將領祂新婦，祂心所喜，]{lang="zh"}
- [With blissful singing to His Father’s throne.]{lang="en"}\
  [同到父的座前頌揚不止。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [And then the blest communion shall have come,]{lang="en"}\
  [那時，神的眾子都將會集，]{lang="zh"}
- [Of God’s dear children meeting from afar;]{lang="en"}\
  [那有福的交通將要來臨；]{lang="zh"}
- [Within His burning love they blend as one,]{lang="en"}\
  [在祂的大愛中相調為一，]{lang="zh"}
- [Yet each, according as His counsels are,]{lang="en"}\
  [各人顯出他特出的光景，]{lang="zh"}
- [Shall have peculiar glory of his own,]{lang="en"}\
  [都按照祂的智慧所定意，]{lang="zh"}
- [As one star differeth from another star.]{lang="en"}\
  [正如這星榮光異於那星。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

  [在那日，神是一切的一切，]{lang="zh"}

- [Their stream of life, their beautiful array,]{lang="en"}\
  [是生命源，是美麗和喜悅，]{lang="zh"}
- [Their food, their joy, their radiance, Christ the Lord;]{lang="en"}\
  [主是他們的食物和榮光；]{lang="zh"}
- [The music of their wondrous song shall say,]{lang="en"}\
  [喜樂難言，惟用美妙音樂、]{lang="zh"}

  [詩章來表達其中的歡暢。]{lang="zh"}

- [Of resurrection life which He hath given:]{lang="en"}\
