# 1

- [O God and Father, we our praises bring,]{lang="en"}\
  [親愛父神，我們向你歌唱，]{lang="zh"}
- [For who more worthy of our praise could be]{lang="en"}\
  [有誰比你更配我們頌揚。]{lang="zh"}
- [Than Thou, who seekest worshipers who sing]{lang="en"}\
  [你今要人以靈以「真」\^\[「真」指基督\]敬拜，]{lang="zh"}
- [In spirit and in truth\^\["truth" denote Christ\] adoringly!]{lang="en"}\
  [我們照此向你獻上感戴。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [All worlds Thou hast created by the Son,]{lang="en"}\
  [所有世界是你藉子造成，]{lang="zh"}
- [All things are held by His unmeasured power;]{lang="en"}\
  [萬有也都由祂大能托撐；]{lang="zh"}
- [Yet we approach Thee in that glorious One:]{lang="en"}\
  [但我卻是在你愛子裏面，]{lang="zh"}
- [What cause for worship in this holy hour!]{lang="en"}\
  [與你親近，敬拜何其甘甜。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [' Tis He who leads us in this blessed sphere,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂今在這蒙服範圍領唱，]{lang="zh"}
- [In the assembly singing praise to Thee;]{lang="en"}\
  [在你教會唱詩向你頌揚；]{lang="zh"}
- [What joy to Him that we should thus be near]{lang="en"}\
  [何等歡樂，祂有弟兄隨同，]{lang="zh"}
- [As suited and in perfect liberty!]{lang="en"}\
  [與祂同享自由，向你歌頌。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Here we unite, our song of joy is one!]{lang="en"}\
  [我們和祂在此同聲歌唱，]{lang="zh"}
- [And Thou, O God, art fully satisfied.]{lang="en"}\
  [哦父，使你滿足、供你欣賞；]{lang="zh"}
- [Amidst the praises led by Christ, Thy Son,]{lang="en"}\
  [在你愛子所領讚美之中，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou hast a dwelling ever to abide.]{lang="en"}\
  [你可安居，永遠享受稱頌。]{lang="zh"}
