# 1

- [Not where we elect to go,]{lang="en"}\
  [不是我們隨意走，]{lang="zh"}
- [But where Jesus leads the way,]{lang="en"}\
  [乃是隨主的引領；]{lang="zh"}
- [There the living waters flow,]{lang="en"}\
  [那裏活水方湧流，]{lang="zh"}
- [There our darkness turns to day.]{lang="en"}\
  [那裏心中方光明。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Not our self-appointed task]{lang="en"}\
  [不是自擇的工作，]{lang="zh"}
- [Will the Lord’s approval win,]{lang="en"}\
  [就能博得祂嘉許；]{lang="zh"}
- [But the work we did not ask,]{lang="en"}\
  [乃是完成祂委託，]{lang="zh"}
- [Finished humbly, just for Him.]{lang="en"}\
  [纔可領受祂稱譽。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Not the prayer we long to plead]{lang="en"}\
  [不是我們隨自己，]{lang="zh"}
- [When we bend before the Throne,]{lang="en"}\
  [就能座前獻禱告；]{lang="zh"}
- [But the touching deeper need]{lang="en"}\
  [乃是聖靈的歎息，]{lang="zh"}
- [Of the Spirit’s wordless groan.]{lang="en"}\
  [摸著更深的需要。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Not the gift we proudly lay]{lang="en"}\
  [如果我們答應“不”，]{lang="zh"}
- [On His altar will He heed,]{lang="en"}\
  [當祂輕說“我需要”，]{lang="zh"}
- [If our hearts have said Him, “Nay,”]{lang="en"}\
  [就是壇上有禮物，]{lang="zh"}
- [When He whispered, “I have need.”]{lang="en"}\
  [也不能使祂稱好。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Thus we die, and dying live]{lang="en"}\
  [我們如此向己死，]{lang="zh"}
- [In the heavenlies with the Lord;]{lang="en"}\
  [與祂一同活天上，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thus we serve, and pray, and give,]{lang="en"}\
  [如此奉獻而服事，]{lang="zh"}
- [Christ Himself our great Reward.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂將自己作恩賞。]{lang="zh"}
