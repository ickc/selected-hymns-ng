# 1

- [Strong are the walls around me,]{lang="en"}\
  [四圍牆垣堅而固，]{lang="zh"}
- [That hold me all the day;]{lang="en"}\
  [終日將我禁閉；]{lang="zh"}
- [But they who thus have bound me,]{lang="en"}\
  [但那關閉我的人，]{lang="zh"}
- [Cannot keep God away:]{lang="en"}\
  [不能使神遠離：]{lang="zh"}
- [My very dungeon walls are dear,]{lang="en"}\
  [監牢牆垣全變可愛，]{lang="zh"}
- [Because the God I love is here,]{lang="en"}\
  [因為我神在此同在。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [They know, who thus oppress me,]{lang="en"}\
  [關閉我者都知道，]{lang="zh"}
- [’Tis hard to be alone;]{lang="en"}\
  [甚難使我孤單；]{lang="zh"}
- [But know not One can bless me,]{lang="en"}\
  [但是他們卻不知]{lang="zh"}
- [Who comes through bars and stone;]{lang="en"}\
  [是祂來獄慰安；]{lang="zh"}
- [He makes my dungeon’s darkness bright,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂使牢中黑暗變明，]{lang="zh"}
- [And fills my bosom with delight.]{lang="en"}\
  [並用喜樂充滿我靈。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Thy love, O God, restores me]{lang="en"}\
  [哦神，你愛激動我，]{lang="zh"}
- [From sighs and tears to praise;]{lang="en"}\
  [悲歎轉為頌贊；]{lang="zh"}
- [And deep my soul adores Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [我從深處敬拜你，]{lang="zh"}
- [Nor thinks of time or place:]{lang="en"}\
  [不管時間、地點；]{lang="zh"}
- [I ask no more, in good or ill,]{lang="en"}\
  [或順或逆，都無所求，]{lang="zh"}
- [But union with Thy holy will.]{lang="en"}\
  [只求和你旨意相投。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [’Tis that which makes my treasure,]{lang="en"}\
  [這個成為我寶貝，]{lang="zh"}
- [’Tis that which brings my gain;]{lang="en"}\
  [這個使我得益；]{lang="zh"}
- [Converting woe to pleasure,]{lang="en"}\
  [為我將禍變祝福，]{lang="zh"}
- [And reaping joy from pain.]{lang="en"}\
  [使我苦中歡喜。]{lang="zh"}
- [Oh, ’tis enough, whate’er befall,]{lang="en"}\
  [不論何事都可臨到，]{lang="zh"}
- [To know that God is All in all.]{lang="en"}\
  [只要有神我就夠了。]{lang="zh"}
