# 1

- [“Forward!” be our watchword, Steps and voices joined;]{lang="en"}\
  [前進!是我口號， 步聲相接聯；]{lang="zh"}
- [Seek the things before us, Not a look behind;]{lang="en"}\
  [向著標竿直跑， 永不回頭看。]{lang="zh"}
- [‘Burns the fiery pillar At our army’s head;]{lang="en"}\
  [軍前火柱燦爛， 照明我路程；]{lang="zh"}
- [Who shall dream of shrinking, By our Captain led?]{lang="en"}\
  [誰還畏縮不前， 基督在率領。]{lang="zh"}
- [Forward through the desert, Through the toil and fight;]{lang="en"}\
  [前進！經過曠野， 經過苦戰爭；]{lang="zh"}
- [Heaven’s Kingdom waits us, Forward into light.]{lang="en"}\
  [迦南不再隱約， 錫安放光明。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Glories upon glories Hath our God prepared,]{lang="en"}\
  [榮耀又加榮耀， 我神已豫備；]{lang="zh"}
- [By the souls that love Him, One day to be shared:]{lang="en"}\
  [凡愛神者必要 有分於此內：]{lang="zh"}
- [Eye hath not beheld them, Ear hath never heard;]{lang="en"}\
  [眼睛未曾看見， 耳朵未曾聽，]{lang="zh"}
- [Nor of these hath uttered Thought or speech or word;]{lang="en"}\
  [榮耀達到極點， 心想也未曾。]{lang="zh"}
- [Forward, marching forward Where the kingdom’s bright,]{lang="en"}\
  [前進！一直前進！ 直到國度顯，]{lang="zh"}
- [Till the veil be lifted, Till our faith be sight!]{lang="en"}\
  [直到幔子不存， 信心變眼見。]{lang="zh"}
