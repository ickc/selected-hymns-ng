# 1

  [耶穌，到底是否可能？]{lang="zh"}
- [A mortal man ashamed of Thee?]{lang="en"}\
  [竟然有人羞認你名？]{lang="zh"}
- [Ashamed of Thee, Whom angels praise,]{lang="en"}\
  [你名乃是天使所頌，]{lang="zh"}
- [Whose glories shine thro’ endless days?]{lang="en"}\
  [榮耀光輝照耀不停！]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Ashamed of Jesus! sooner far]{lang="en"}\
  [竟然有人羞認主名，]{lang="zh"}
- [Let night disown each radiant star:]{lang="en"}\
  [無異黑夜羞認明星！]{lang="zh"}
- [’Tis midnight with my soul, till He.]{lang="en"}\
  [我心本來暗如夜半，]{lang="zh"}
- [Bright Morning Star’, bid darkness flee.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂這晨星，使暗變明。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Ashamed of Jesus! O as soon]{lang="en"}\
  [竟然有人羞認主名，]{lang="zh"}
- [Let morning blush to own the sun;]{lang="en"}\
  [無異清晨羞認朝陽！]{lang="zh"}
- [He sheds the beams of light divine,]{lang="en"}\
  [主是朝陽，光華四射，]{lang="zh"}
- [O’er this benighted soul of mine.]{lang="en"}\
  [照明我的如夜天良。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Ashamed of Jesus! that dear friend]{lang="en"}\
  [竟然有人羞認主名，]{lang="zh"}
- [On whom my glorious hope depends?]{lang="en"}\
  [我們屬天盼望所賴；]{lang="zh"}
- [No, when blush, be this my shame,]{lang="en"}\
  [我心慚愧，我臉含羞，]{lang="zh"}
- [That I no more revere His Name.]{lang="en"}\
  [因我不覺祂名可愛。]{lang="zh"}
