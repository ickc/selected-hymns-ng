# 1

- [I have a Savior, He’s pleading in glory,]{lang="en"}\
  [我有一救主，在天為我祈求，]{lang="zh"}
- [A dear, loving Savior, tho’ earth-friends be few;]{lang="en"}\
  [可親又可愛，雖朋友與我疏；]{lang="zh"}
- [And now He is watching in tenderness o’er me,]{lang="en"}\
  [現今祂時時看顧我，情愛深厚；]{lang="zh"}
- [But O, that my Savior were your Savior too!]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，願我救主也成為你救主！]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [I have a Father; to me He has given]{lang="en"}\
  [我有一天父，住在光明之家，]{lang="zh"}
- [A hope for eternity, blessed and true;]{lang="en"}\
  [許我一盼望，既永遠，又有據；]{lang="zh"}
- [And soon He will call me to meet Him in Heaven,]{lang="en"}\
  [不久要接我到天上去會見祂：]{lang="zh"}
- [But O, that He'd let me bring you with me too!]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，願祂施恩，你也能同我去！]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [I have a joy, rolling as the sea billows,]{lang="en"}\
  [我有一喜樂，這樂湧如海波，]{lang="zh"}
- [Since Jesus my Savior took my sins away;]{lang="en"}\
  [因為我救主已擔去我罪惡，]{lang="zh"}
- [And now He lives in me-what joy divine o'erflows!]{lang="en"}\
  [並且祂現今還在我裡面活著：]{lang="zh"}
- [But O, that He'd give you this same joy today.]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，願我救主也賜你這喜樂！]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [I have a peace; it is calm as a river,]{lang="en"}\
  [我有一平安，靜如湖水一般，]{lang="zh"}
- [A peace that the friends of this world never knew;]{lang="en"}\
  [奇妙的平安，世上友未知曉；]{lang="zh"}
- [My Savior alone is its author and giver,]{lang="en"}\
  [惟有主耶穌，祂是這平安根源：]{lang="zh"}
- [And O, could I know it was given for you!]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，願這平安，你也能親嘗到！]{lang="zh"}

- [Then pray that your Savior may bring them to glory,]{lang="en"}\
