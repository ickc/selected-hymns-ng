# 1

- [Fill Thou my life, O Lord my God,]{lang="en"}\
  [主阿，求你來充滿我，]{lang="zh"}
- [In ev ‘ry part with praise,]{lang="en"}\
  [讚美洋溢各部，]{lang="zh"}
- [That my whole being may proclaim]{lang="en"}\
  [使我全人能夠傳播]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy being and Thy ways.]{lang="en"}\
  [你所是和道路。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Not for the lip of praise alone,]{lang="en"}\
  [不僅是嘴唇的稱祝，]{lang="zh"}
- [Nor e’en the praising heart,]{lang="en"}\
  [或僅心中感銘，]{lang="zh"}
- [I ask, but for a life made up]{lang="en"}\
  [乃是全人內裡各部]{lang="zh"}
- [Of praise in every part:]{lang="en"}\
  [都由讚美組成。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Fill every part of me with praise;]{lang="en"}\
  [日常生活所遭事物，]{lang="zh"}
- [Let all my being speak]{lang="en"}\
  [雖或微小、平凡，]{lang="zh"}
- [Of Thee and of Thy love, O Lord,]{lang="en"}\
  [每一作為，雖不特出，]{lang="zh"}
- [Poor though I be and weak.]{lang="en"}\
  [仍能讚美不斷。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Praise in the common things of life,]{lang="en"}\
  [求用讚美滿我各部，]{lang="zh"}
- [Its goings out and in;]{lang="en"}\
  [我雖又貧又弱，]{lang="zh"}
- [Praise in each duty and each deed,]{lang="en"}\
  [仍能用我全人說出]{lang="zh"}
- [However small and mean.]{lang="en"}\
  [你和你愛所作。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [So shall no part of day or night]{lang="en"}\
  [無論晝夜不分時刻，]{lang="zh"}
- [From sacredness be free,]{lang="en"}\
  [完全分別為聖，]{lang="zh"}
- [But all my life, in every step,]{lang="en"}\
  [願我一生每一時刻，]{lang="zh"}
- [Be fellowship with Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [與你交通更深。]{lang="zh"}
