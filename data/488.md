# 1

- [Jesus is all the world to me:]{lang="en"}\
  [基督就是我的世界：]{lang="zh"}
- [My life, my joy, my all.]{lang="en"}\
  [生命、喜樂、一切！]{lang="zh"}
- [He is my strength from day to day;]{lang="en"}\
  [是我能力，時時提挈，]{lang="zh"}
- [Without Him I would fall.]{lang="en"}\
  [離祂我就僕跌。]{lang="zh"}
- [When I am sad, to Him I go;]{lang="en"}\
  [當我愁苦我來就祂，]{lang="zh"}
- [No other one can cheer me so.]{lang="en"}\
  [無人如此安撫接納；]{lang="zh"}
- [When I am sad, He makes me glad;]{lang="en"}\
  [使我心樂，解我心憂，]{lang="zh"}
- [He’s my Friend.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂是我友！]{lang="zh"}
