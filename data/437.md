# 1

- [Down in the valley with my Savior I would go,]{lang="en"}\
  [我今願跟隨救主，不論走何路：]{lang="zh"}
- [Where the flowers are blooming and the sweet waters flow;]{lang="en"}\
  [或是平坦大路，或是崎嶇窄途；]{lang="zh"}
- [Everywhere He leads me I would follow, follow on,]{lang="en"}\
  [既有救主親自相輔，我就不躊躇，]{lang="zh"}
- [Walking in His footsteps till the crown be won.]{lang="en"}\
  [一路跟隨耶穌，直到進國度。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Down in the valley with my Savior I would go,]{lang="en"}\
  [我今願跟隨救主，不論在何處：]{lang="zh"}
- [Where the storms are sweeping and the dark waters flow;]{lang="en"}\
  [或在明媚樂土，或在死蔭幽谷；]{lang="zh"}
- [With His hand to lead me I will never, never fear,]{lang="en"}\
  [既有救主親自照護，我就不感苦；]{lang="zh"}
- [Dangers cannot fright me if my Lord is near.]{lang="en"}\
  [處處跟隨耶穌，直到末一步。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Down in the valley, or upon the mountain steep,]{lang="en"}\
  [我今願跟隨救主，不論時何如：]{lang="zh"}
- [Close beside my Savior would my heart ever keep;]{lang="en"}\
  [或是陽光滿目，或是黑雲密佈；]{lang="zh"}
- [He will lead me safely in the path that He has trod,]{lang="en"}\
  [既有救主親自部署，我就無所顧；]{lang="zh"}
- [Up to where they gather in the rest of God.]{lang="en"}\
  [時時跟隨耶穌，一直到天曙。]{lang="zh"}
