# 1

- [Father, Thy Son beloved leads our praise]{lang="en"}\
  [宴筵方過，我們尚感甜美，]{lang="zh"}
- [After the banquet feast is had and done;]{lang="en"}\
  [父，你愛子率領我們讚美；]{lang="zh"}
- [How dear, how sweet to Thee the praise He leads,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂領詩歌，於你何親、何甜，]{lang="zh"}
- [And in His praises joining we are one.]{lang="en"}\
  [我們與祂一致，和聲頌讚。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Father, we need no goodness more than Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [哦父，你外我們不需別惠，]{lang="zh"}
- [Nor do we seek a love that’s less than Thine;]{lang="en"}\
  [有何比你恩愛更為寶貴！]{lang="zh"}
- [How we adore Thee now that here we know]{lang="en"}\
  [我們敬拜，因為我們嘗到]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy searchless, uncreated life divine.]{lang="en"}\
  [你那無窮生命豐美味道。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Father, within Thy love our love to Thee]{lang="en"}\
  [在你愛中我愛對你增長，]{lang="zh"}
- [Grows, far transcending all our earthly ties;]{lang="en"}\
  [超越所有人情、屬地捆綁；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thine own dear love in power leads our hearts]{lang="en"}\
  [你愛藉著大能，帶領我心]{lang="zh"}
- [Where Thine own presence deeply satisfies.]{lang="en"}\
  [享受你的同在與日俱深。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Father, by joining with Thy Son beloved,]{lang="en"}\
  [父阿，我們跟隨你的愛子，]{lang="zh"}
- [We all with gladness praise Thee now again;]{lang="en"}\
  [在此向你讚美頌揚不止；]{lang="zh"}
- [Joyfully feasting on Thy holy love,]{lang="en"}\
  [飽享你的聖愛，心滿意足，]{lang="zh"}
- [Tasting, as sons, the sweetness of Thy name.]{lang="en"}\
  [眾子嘗到你名所有豐富。]{lang="zh"}
