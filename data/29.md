  [阿爸，我們來頌讚你！]{lang="zh"}
- [Humbly now our homage pay;]{lang="en"}\
  [我們謙卑敬拜你！]{lang="zh"}
- [‘Tis Thy children’s bliss to know Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [我們現今得認識你，]{lang="zh"}
- [None but children “Abba” say]{lang="en"}\
  [真是我們的福氣。]{lang="zh"}
- [This high honor we inherit,]{lang="en"}\
  [我們得著這樣權柄，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy free gift through Jesus’ blood;]{lang="en"}\
  [是因耶穌的尊名；]{lang="zh"}
- [God the Spirit, with our spirit,]{lang="en"}\
  [你靈也和我們的靈，]{lang="zh"}

  [證明我們有生命。]{lang="zh"}

  [得你生命的恩賜。]{lang="zh"}
