# 1

- [Jesus spreads His banner o’er us,]{lang="en"}\
  [主舉愛旗遮蓋我們，]{lang="zh"}
- [Cheers our famished souls with food;]{lang="en"}\
  [同來享受祂愛筵；]{lang="zh"}
- [He the banquet spread before us,]{lang="en"}\
  [我們感激救贖大恩，]{lang="zh"}
- [Of His mystic flesh and blood.]{lang="en"}\
  [全心虔誠來記念。]{lang="zh"}
- [Precious banquet, bread of heaven,]{lang="en"}\
  [桌上陳設這餅和杯，]{lang="zh"}
- [Wine of gladness, flowing free;]{lang="en"}\
  [都是表明你的愛；]{lang="zh"}
- [May we taste it, kindly given,]{lang="en"}\
  [我們一同領你恩惠，]{lang="zh"}
- [In remembrance Lord, of Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [我們一同來敬拜。]{lang="zh"}
