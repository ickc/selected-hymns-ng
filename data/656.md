- [We’ll mix His Word with faith by answering “Amen!”]{lang="en"}\
  [對主話說“阿們”是把信心調入，]{lang="zh"}

- [His sanctifying work in us go on and on,]{lang="en"}\

  [我們的靈、魂、身體，要完全成聖，]{lang="zh"}
