# 1

- [There’s a wideness in God’s mercy]{lang="en"}\
  [神的憐憫何等深廣，]{lang="zh"}

  [闊逾洋海難測量；]{lang="zh"}

  [祂是公義卻又慈良，]{lang="zh"}
- [Which is more than liberty.]{lang="en"}\
