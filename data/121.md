# 1

- [Gazing on the Lord in glory,]{lang="en"}\
  [我們的靈伏著注視]{lang="zh"}
- [While our hearts in worship bow,]{lang="en"}\
  [在榮耀裏的耶穌，]{lang="zh"}
- [There we read the wondrous story]{lang="en"}\
  [我們在彼讀祂故事，]{lang="zh"}
- [Of the cross—its shame and woe.]{lang="en"}\
  [如何在世受羞辱。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Every mark of dark dishonor]{lang="en"}\
  [你的聖首滿了羞辱，]{lang="zh"}
- [Heaped upon the thorn-crowned brow,]{lang="en"}\
  [世人竟然大歡暢；]{lang="zh"}
- [All the depths of Thy heart’s sorrow]{lang="en"}\
  [今日榮耀反面說出：]{lang="zh"}
- [Told in answ’ring glory now.]{lang="en"}\
  [當日你心的憂傷。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [On that cross, alone, forsaken,]{lang="en"}\
  [當你掛在十字架時，]{lang="zh"}
- [Where no pity’ng eye was found;]{lang="en"}\
  [孤單被棄無人憐；]{lang="zh"}
- [Now, to God’s right hand exalted,]{lang="en"}\
  [眾聖今則向你唱詩，]{lang="zh"}
- [With Thy praise the heavens resound.]{lang="en"}\
  [你則高升到父前。]{lang="zh"}
