# 1

- [“Within the Veil”: be this, belov’d, thy portion,]{lang="en"}\
  [“進入幔內！”願這福氣你有分，]{lang="zh"}
- [Within the secret of thy Lord to dwell;]{lang="en"}\
  [到主隱密處，在祂蔭下住；]{lang="zh"}
- [Beholding Him, until thy face His glory,]{lang="en"}\
  [瞻祂直至你臉、你生命、你唇，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy life His love, thy lips His praise shall tell.]{lang="en"}\
  [流露祂榮耀、祂愛、祂稱祝。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [“Within the Veil,” for only as thou gazest]{lang="en"}\
  [“進入幔內，”惟在此至聖之殿，]{lang="zh"}
- [Upon the matchless beauty of His face,]{lang="en"}\
  [方能注視祂無比的榮美；]{lang="zh"}
- [Canst thou become a living revelation]{lang="en"}\
  [你纔可能成為祂活的彰顯]{lang="zh"}
- [Of His great heart of love, His untold grace.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂愛的泱漭，祂恩的廣沛。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [“Within the Veil,” His fragrance poured upon thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [“進入幔內，”讓主馨香將你熏，]{lang="zh"}
- [Without the Veil, that fragrance shed abroad;]{lang="en"}\
  [出到幔外去，發出祂香味；]{lang="zh"}
- [“Within the Veil,” His hand shall tune the music]{lang="en"}\
  [“進入幔內，”讓祂手調整音韻，]{lang="zh"}
- [Which sounds on earth the praises of Thy Lord.]{lang="en"}\
  [使你歡奏出，愛祂的讚美。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [“Within the Veil,” thy spirit deeply anchored,]{lang="en"}\
  [“進入幔內，”在主裡將錨拋定，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou walkest calm above a world of strife;]{lang="en"}\
  [使你能勝過，世俗的翻騰；]{lang="zh"}
- [“Within the Veil,” thy soul with Him united,]{lang="en"}\
  [“進入幔內，”與主聯合成一靈，]{lang="zh"}
