# 1

- [O God! we see Thee in the Lamb]{lang="en"}\
  [父神阿，你在羔羊裏]{lang="zh"}
- [To be our hope, our joy, our rest;]{lang="en"}\
  [是我的盼望和安息，]{lang="zh"}
- [The glories that compose Thy name]{lang="en"}\
  [你名所表一切慈仁，]{lang="zh"}
- [Standing engaged to make us blest.]{lang="en"}\
  [都是為著賜福我們。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Thou great and good! Thou just and wise!]{lang="en"}\
  [偉大、智慧、公義、慈仁，]{lang="zh"}
- [Hail! as our Father and our God!]{lang="en"}\
  [你是我父，又是我神；]{lang="zh"}
- [For we are Thine by sacred ties,]{lang="en"}\
  [我們屬你，是藉寶血，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy sons and daughters—bought with blood.]{lang="en"}\
  [作你兒女，永遠聯結。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Then, Oh! to us this grace afford,]{lang="en"}\
  [既是這樣，願你施恩，]{lang="zh"}
- [That far from Thee we ne’er may move;]{lang="en"}\
  [好叫我們親近你身；]{lang="zh"}
- [Our guard—the presence of the Lord;]{lang="en"}\
  [你的同在作我居所，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our joy—Thy perfect present love.]{lang="en"}\
  [你的大愛作我喜樂。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [This gives us ever to rejoice,]{lang="en"}\
  [這叫我們永遠歡騰，]{lang="zh"}
- [Turning to light our darkest days;]{lang="en"}\
  [這叫黑夜變為光明；]{lang="zh"}
- [And lifts on high each feeble voice,]{lang="en"}\
  [若有微息尚能稱頌，]{lang="zh"}
- [While we have breath to pray or praise.]{lang="en"}\
  [必引弱聲向你歌誦。]{lang="zh"}
