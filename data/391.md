# 1

- [Jesus, Savior, pilot me]{lang="en"}\
  [主，你作我的領港，]{lang="zh"}
- [Over life’s tempestuous sea;]{lang="en"}\
  [來過此人生風浪；]{lang="zh"}
- [Unknown waves before me roll,]{lang="en"}\
  [前面是未識水道，]{lang="zh"}
- [Hiding rock and treach’rous shoal;]{lang="en"}\
  [浮沙外加上暗礁；]{lang="zh"}
- [Chart and compass come from Thee;]{lang="en"}\
  [你當賜南針圖狀，]{lang="zh"}

  [主，你作我的領港。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

  [你也能鎮靜狂海；]{lang="zh"}
- [Boist’rous waves obey Thy will]{lang="en"}\
  [當你說應當安靜，]{lang="zh"}
- [When Thou say’st to them, “Be still.”]{lang="en"}\
  [風和浪立聽命令；]{lang="zh"}
- [Wondrous Sov’reign of the sea,]{lang="en"}\
  [你真是奇妙的王，]{lang="zh"}
- [Jesus, Savior, pilot me.]{lang="en"}\
  [主，你作我的領港。]{lang="zh"}
