  [他等候一座城，卻住在帳棚，]{lang="zh"}
- [A pilgrim to glory right onward he went;]{lang="en"}\
  [這天城的旅客，一直奔前程；]{lang="zh"}
- [God’s promise his solace, so royal his birth,]{lang="en"}\
  [他有美好證據，前途實堪誇，]{lang="zh"}
- [No wonder he sought not the glories of earth.]{lang="en"}\
  [難怪他不尋求地上的榮華。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [He looked for a city, his God should prepare;]{lang="en"}\
  [他等候一座城，他神的住處，]{lang="zh"}

  [他沒有，也不求地上的房屋；]{lang="zh"}
- [For had not God told him, that royal abode]{lang="en"}\
