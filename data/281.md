# 1

- [I’ve found a Friend, oh, such a Friend!]{lang="en"}\
  [我有一友，何等之友！]{lang="zh"}
- [He loved me ere I knew Him;]{lang="en"}\
  [愛我遠在創世前；]{lang="zh"}
- [He drew me with the cords of love,]{lang="en"}\
  [慈繩愛索將我牽引，]{lang="zh"}
- [And thus He bound me to Him.]{lang="en"}\
  [使我和祂永相聯。]{lang="zh"}
- [And round my heart still closely twine]{lang="en"}\
  [此愛堅強，圍繞我心，]{lang="zh"}
- [Those ties which naught can sever,]{lang="en"}\
  [無何能將其分離；]{lang="zh"}
- [For I am His, and He is mine,]{lang="en"}\
  [我是屬祂，祂也屬我，]{lang="zh"}
- [Forever and forever.]{lang="en"}\
  [直到永世不稍渝！]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [I’ve found a Friend, oh, such a Friend!]{lang="en"}\
  [我有一友，何等之友！]{lang="zh"}
- [He bled, He died to save me;]{lang="en"}\
  [流血、捨命拯救我；]{lang="zh"}
- [And not alone the gift of life,]{lang="en"}\
  [不僅給我生命恩賜，]{lang="zh"}
- [But His own self He gave me.]{lang="en"}\
  [連祂自己也給我。]{lang="zh"}
- [Naught that I have my own I call,]{lang="en"}\
  [我今不再屬我自己，]{lang="zh"}
- [I hold it for the Giver;]{lang="en"}\
  [我獻全人歸祂有；]{lang="zh"}
- [My heart, my strength, my life, my all,]{lang="en"}\
  [我心、我命，我的一切，]{lang="zh"}
- [Are His, and His forever.]{lang="en"}\
  [全都歸祂到永久。]{lang="zh"}
