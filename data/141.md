# 1

- [Lord Jesus, are we one with Thee?]{lang="en"}\
  [主，我是否與你為一？]{lang="zh"}
- [O height, O depth of love!]{lang="en"}\
  [你愛高、深無比！]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou one with us on Calvary,]{lang="en"}\
  [在各各他，你與我一，]{lang="zh"}
- [We one Thee above.]{lang="en"}\
  [在天，我與你一。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Such was Thy grace that for our sake]{lang="en"}\
  [因著你的豐富恩典，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou didst from heaven come down,]{lang="en"}\
  [為我來到世間；]{lang="zh"}
- [With us of flesh and blood partake,]{lang="en"}\
  [為我成了血肉之體，]{lang="zh"}
- [In all our misery one.]{lang="en"}\
  [飽嘗人間苦難。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Our sins, our guilt, in love Divine]{lang="en"}\
  [因你神聖無比大愛，]{lang="zh"}
- [Confessed and home by Thee;]{lang="en"}\
  [承擔我的過犯；]{lang="zh"}
- [The gall, the curse, the wrath were Thine,]{lang="en"}\
  [備嘗苦膽、咒詛、忿怒，]{lang="zh"}
- [To set Thy members free.]{lang="en"}\
  [使你肢體釋放。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Ascended now, in glory bright,]{lang="en"}\
  [如今你已升入榮耀，]{lang="zh"}
- [Head of the Church Thou art;]{lang="en"}\
  [成為教會元首；]{lang="zh"}
- [Nor life nor death, nor depth nor height,]{lang="en"}\
  [或生或死、或天或地，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy saints and Thee can part.]{lang="en"}\
  [難使你我分手。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Oh teach us, Lord, to know and own]{lang="en"}\
  [求主將此奇妙奧祕，]{lang="zh"}
- [This wondrous mystery,]{lang="en"}\
  [向我指教開啟；]{lang="zh"}
- [That Thou in heaven with us art one,]{lang="en"}\
  [就是在天與你為一，]{lang="zh"}
- [And we are one with Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [你我活在一裏。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [Thou shalt to wondering worlds display]{lang="en"}\
  [同坐寶座永遠見證，]{lang="zh"}
