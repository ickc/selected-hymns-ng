# 1

- [Ye sleeping souls, awake]{lang="en"}\
  [沉睡的人，醒起！]{lang="zh"}
- [From dreams of carnal ease!]{lang="en"}\
  [莫再貪享安逸；]{lang="zh"}
- [Your couch of sloth forsake;]{lang="en"}\
  [不要留戀榻上，]{lang="zh"}
- [Nor seek the flesh to please;]{lang="en"}\
  [不求肉體舒暢；]{lang="zh"}
- [Far from this land of shadows flee,]{lang="en"}\
  [遠遠逃離死蔭幽谷，]{lang="zh"}
- [And march towards eternity.]{lang="en"}\
  [快步邁向永生之路。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Aside each burden lay,]{lang="en"}\
  [卸下每一重擔，]{lang="zh"}
- [That does your course restrain,]{lang="en"}\
  [前程不受阻延；]{lang="zh"}
- [And onward urge your way]{lang="en"}\
  [脫離自己、罪愆，]{lang="zh"}
- [From nature, self, and sin;]{lang="en"}\
  [使你快步向前；]{lang="zh"}
- [Prepare the midnight cry to hear,]{lang="en"}\
  [豫備聆聽夜半呼聲：]{lang="zh"}
- [“The Bridegroom comes, behold Him near!”]{lang="en"}\
  [“看哪，新郎已經來臨！”]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Arise, go forth to find]{lang="en"}\
  [起來，進前尋出，]{lang="zh"}
- [Your true and faithful Lord;]{lang="en"}\
  [你的信實之主；]{lang="zh"}
- [And leaving all behind,]{lang="en"}\
  [一切丟在背後，]{lang="zh"}
- [In spirit hear His word.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂話靈裡領受。]{lang="zh"}
- [The pure in heart His presence feel,]{lang="en"}\
  [清心得見祂的同在，]{lang="zh"}
- [’Tis them He doth Himself reveal.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂樂向你啟示出來。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Within your souls retired,]{lang="en"}\
  [將你深處敞開，]{lang="zh"}
- [His precious love enjoy;]{lang="en"}\
  [享受祂的大愛；]{lang="zh"}
- [And with His Spirit tlr’d,]{lang="en"}\
  [被祂聖靈焚燒，]{lang="zh"}
- [Your time in prayer employ;]{lang="en"}\
  [隨時多方禱告；]{lang="zh"}
- [And purchase oil, whilst yet you may,]{lang="en"}\
  [趁著今日，多多買油，]{lang="zh"}
- [Lest it should fail you on that day.]{lang="en"}\
  [以免那日，使你蒙羞。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Give God alone Thy heart,]{lang="en"}\
  [此心只獻給神，]{lang="zh"}
- [For vain is all disguise.]{lang="en"}\
  [萬物虛空不真；]{lang="zh"}
- [O Lord Thy love impart,]{lang="en"}\
  [哦主，賜下你靈，]{lang="zh"}
- [The oil that makes us wise!]{lang="en"}\
  [將愛澆灌我心！]{lang="zh"}
- [To light our souls with holy fire,]{lang="en"}\
  [但願聖火點亮我魂，]{lang="zh"}
- [Which shall not, e’en in death, expire.]{lang="en"}\
  [永不熄滅，至死仍焚！]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [Jesus, Thyself excite]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌，求你激勵]{lang="zh"}
- [Our feet to run the race—]{lang="en"}\
  [我腳奔跑不息－]{lang="zh"}
- [To watch, and pray, and fight,]{lang="en"}\
  [儆醒、禱告、爭戰，]{lang="zh"}
- [Till we behold Thy face!]{lang="en"}\
  [直到瞻你榮臉！]{lang="zh"}
- [Nor let us ever tire, nor rest,]{lang="en"}\
  [不要使我疲倦、止息，]{lang="zh"}
- [Till with Thy full possession blest!]{lang="en"}\
  [直到完滿達你心意！]{lang="zh"}
