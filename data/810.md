# 1

- [Thy name is sweet as ointment poured forth;]{lang="en"}\
  [你名似膏香，你愛比酒美，]{lang="zh"}
- [Better Thy love than wine, O draw Thou me!]{lang="en"}\
  [你若來吸引，有誰不跟隨？]{lang="zh"}
- [If we the footsteps follow of the flock,]{lang="en"}\
  [我們若跟隨羊群的腳蹤，]{lang="zh"}
- [Entered Thy fellowship of love we'll be.]{lang="en"}\
  [我們必進入你愛的交通。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [He's my Beloved, I am His own love;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂是我良人，我是祂佳偶，]{lang="zh"}
- [He draweth me, pursue I after Him.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂將我吸引，我將祂追求；]{lang="zh"}
- [Fragrant as myrrh, I'd hide Him in my heart;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂香如沒藥，我願藏心房，]{lang="zh"}
- [Beauteous as henna, I'd be clothed with Him;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂美似鳳仙，我願戴身上。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Bathe in His love, and of His fatness taste,]{lang="en"}\
  [進入祂愛裡，享受祂肥甘，]{lang="zh"}
- [Lie on His breast, His sweetness there enjoy;]{lang="en"}\
  [躺在祂懷中，飽嘗祂香甜；]{lang="zh"}
- [His love the banner, His affection shown]{lang="en"}\
  [祂愛為旌旗，將我身遮藏，]{lang="zh"}
- [Tenderly soothes my heart to purest joy.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂情似美物，使我心舒暢。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Oh, my Beloved's mine, and I am His;]{lang="en"}\
  [良人屬於我，我也屬於祂，]{lang="zh"}
- [I am a lily and my Shepherd He;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂是牧養者，我是百合花。]{lang="zh"}
- [May daybreak come, the shadows flee away,]{lang="en"}\
  [惟願天快曙，黑影全飛散，]{lang="zh"}
- [Him on the mountains as a hart I'd see.]{lang="en"}\
  [見祂如朝鹿，顯在比特山。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Myrrh of the death with Him and frankincense,]{lang="en"}\
  [同死的沒藥，復活的乳香，]{lang="zh"}
- [The resurrection, permeate my heart;]{lang="en"}\
  [來將我熏透，好給祂欣賞；]{lang="zh"}
- [North wind awake, and let the south wind blow,]{lang="en"}\
  [北風阿，興起，南風阿，吹來，]{lang="zh"}
- [Make my heart's garden pleasure to His heart.]{lang="en"}\
  [來使我心園，成為祂心愛。]{lang="zh"}
