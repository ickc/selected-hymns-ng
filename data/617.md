# 1

- [Stand up! stand up for Jesus! Ye soldiers of the cross;]{lang="en"}\
  [興起，興起，為基督，興起，十架精兵；]{lang="zh"}
- [Lift high His royal banner, It must not suffer loss:]{lang="en"}\
  [舉起主旗揚威武，永遠將它高撐。]{lang="zh"}
- [From vict’ry unto vict’ry His army shall He lead,]{lang="en"}\
  [主必率領祂大軍，得勝而又得勝，]{lang="zh"}
- [Till every foe is vanquished And Christ is Lord indeed.]{lang="en"}\
  [直到仇敵全滅盡，人尊主名為聖。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Stand up! stand up for Jesus! The trumpet call obey;]{lang="en"}\
  [興起，興起，為基督，興起，聽主號令；]{lang="zh"}
- [Forth to the mighty conflict In this His glorious day.]{lang="en"}\
  [走上疆場齊步武，靠主得勝大名。]{lang="zh"}
- [Ye that are men, now serve Him Against unnumbered foes;]{lang="en"}\
  [仇敵雖多，又兇險，也必向其進攻；]{lang="zh"}
- [Let courage rise with danger, And strength to strength oppose.]{lang="en"}\
  [愈險，愈攻，愈勇敢，攻到仇敵消蹤 。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Stand up! stand up for Jesus! Stand in His strength alone;]{lang="en"}\
  [興起，興起，為基督， 興起，靠主大力；]{lang="zh"}
- [The arm of flesh will fail you; Ye dare not trust your own.]{lang="en"}\
  [莫靠自己而貽誤，莫用血氣兵器。]{lang="zh"}
- [Put on the Gospel armor, And, watching unto prayer,]{lang="en"}\
  [穿上屬靈的軍裝，禱告隨時多方；]{lang="zh"}
- [Where duty calls, or danger, Be never wanting there.]{lang="en"}\
  [無論何處需抵擋，都能隨靈前往。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Stand up! stand up for Jesus! The strife will not be long:]{lang="en"}\
  [興起，興起，為基督，戰爭必不會長；]{lang="zh"}
- [This day the noise of battle, The next the victor’s song;]{lang="en"}\
  [今日殺聲雖四布， 明日凱歌響亮。]{lang="zh"}
- [To him that overcometh A crown of life shall be;]{lang="en"}\
  [凡是靠主得勝者，必得榮耀冠冕，]{lang="zh"}
- [He, with the King of glory, Shall reign eternally.]{lang="en"}\
  [必與基督同快樂， 同王直到永遠。]{lang="zh"}
