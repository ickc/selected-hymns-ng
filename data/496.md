# 1

- [How tedious and tasteless the hours]{lang="en"}\
  [當我不見基督容華，]{lang="zh"}
- [When Jesus no longer I see!]{lang="en"}\
  [時間何等無味、可厭！]{lang="zh"}
- [Sweet prospects, sweet birds and sweet flow’rs,]{lang="en"}\
  [活潑的鳥、美麗的花，]{lang="zh"}
- [Have all lost their sweetness to me.]{lang="en"}\
  [對我就不再是甘甜；]{lang="zh"}
- [The midsummer sun shines but dim,]{lang="en"}\
  [可愛太陽如失光線，]{lang="zh"}
- [The fields strive in vain to look gay;]{lang="en"}\
  [田野枉欲表現歡喜；]{lang="zh"}
- [But when I am happy in Him]{lang="en"}\
  [當我快樂在祂裡面，]{lang="zh"}
- [December’s as pleasant as May.]{lang="en"}\
  [陰霾也如晴空美麗。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [His name yields the richest perfume,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂名發出最美香味，]{lang="zh"}
- [And sweeter than music His voice;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂聲成為最甜音樂，]{lang="zh"}
- [His presence disperses my gloom,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的同在展我愁眉，]{lang="zh"}
- [And makes all within me rejoice.]{lang="en"}\
  [使我裡面盡都歡悅；]{lang="zh"}
- [I should, were He always thus nigh,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂若如此不斷親密，]{lang="zh"}
- [Have nothing to wish or to fear;]{lang="en"}\
  [我就無何可怕、可求，]{lang="zh"}
- [No mortal so happy as I;]{lang="en"}\
  [無人快樂與我可比，]{lang="zh"}
- [My summer would last all the year.]{lang="en"}\
  [我的春日常年長有。]{lang="zh"}
