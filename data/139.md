# 1

- [O blessed Savior, is Thy love]{lang="en"}\
  [我的救主，你愛為何]{lang="zh"}
- [So great! So full! So free!]{lang="en"}\
  [這摯！這寬！這博！]{lang="zh"}
- [Fain would we have our thoughts, our hearts,]{lang="en"}\
  [不然，我心、我命、我力，]{lang="zh"}
- [Our lives, engaged with Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [那肯完全歸你！]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [We love Thee for the glorious worth]{lang="en"}\
  [我們愛你，因你自己]{lang="zh"}
- [Which in Thyself we see;]{lang="en"}\
  [上下千秋無匹；]{lang="zh"}
- [We love Thee for that shameful cross,]{lang="en"}\
  [我們愛你，因你忍受]{lang="zh"}
- [Endured so patiently.]{lang="en"}\
  [十字架的恥羞。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [No man of greater love can boast]{lang="en"}\
  [人愛所誇，不過就是]{lang="zh"}
- [Than for His friend to die;]{lang="en"}\
  [代友受死的事；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou for Thine enemies wast slain!]{lang="en"}\
  [但你卻為仇敵被殺！]{lang="zh"}
- [What love with Thine can vie?]{lang="en"}\
  [誰曾如此愛法？]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Though in the very form of God,]{lang="en"}\
  [雖然你有神的形像，]{lang="zh"}
- [With heav’nly glory crowned,]{lang="en"}\
  [安居榮耀天鄉；]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou didst a servant’s form assume,]{lang="en"}\
  [但你卻成奴僕樣式，]{lang="zh"}
- [Beset with sorrow round.]{lang="en"}\
  [親歷憂患塵世。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Thou wouldst like wretched man be made]{lang="en"}\
  [你願凡事都像世人，]{lang="zh"}
- [In everything but sin,]{lang="en"}\
  [（除了罪惡無分），]{lang="zh"}
- [That we as like Thee might become]{lang="en"}\
  [好叫我們完全像你，]{lang="zh"}
- [As we unlike had been:]{lang="en"}\
  [雖然前此不一。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [Like Thee in faith, in meekness, love,]{lang="en"}\
  [像你溫柔、像你堅剛，]{lang="zh"}
- [In every beauteous grace;]{lang="en"}\
  [像你在凡事上！]{lang="zh"}
- [From glory unto glory changed,]{lang="en"}\
  [榮上加榮，天天更變，]{lang="zh"}
- [Till we behold Thy face.]{lang="en"}\
  [直到與你相見。]{lang="zh"}

# 7

- [O Lord, we treasure in our hearts]{lang="en"}\
  [我們的主，我們的心]{lang="zh"}
- [The memory of Thy love;]{lang="en"}\
  [珍藏你的愛情；]{lang="zh"}
- [And ever shall Thy name to us]{lang="en"}\
  [今後我們對你尊名，]{lang="zh"}
- [A grateful odor prove.]{lang="en"}\
  [永要感激恭敬。]{lang="zh"}
