# 1

- [O Father of glorify, now grant unto me]{lang="en"}\
  [哦,榮耀的父神，求現在賜我]{lang="zh"}
- [A spirit of wisdom, a spirit to see;]{lang="en"}\
  [那智慧的靈，使我靈能看見；]{lang="zh"}
- [O give unto me the full knowledge of Him,]{lang="en"}\
  [照明我心中的眼睛，好使我]{lang="zh"}
- [Enlightn’ing the eyes of my heart deep within.]{lang="en"}\
  [對主的認識能充分且完全。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [O show me that Christ is the hope of Thy call—]{lang="en"}\
  [使我看見祂是恩召的指望——]{lang="zh"}
- [O glorious hope, Christ will fill all in all!]{lang="en"}\
  [榮耀的盼望，祂要充滿萬有！]{lang="zh"}
- [Reveal Him—the riches of glory in us—]{lang="en"}\
  [祂這榮耀的豐富藏我身上，]{lang="zh"}
- [As God’s own inheritance. How glorious!]{lang="en"}\
  [作神的基業，供神人同享受。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [O God of our Lord Jesus, let me perceive]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，主耶穌的神，讓我能明白]{lang="zh"}
- [Th’ exceeding great pow’r unto us who believe,]{lang="en"}\
  [向信祂的人所顯超絕大能，]{lang="zh"}
- [The power that raised Christ, Thy Son, from the dead]{lang="en"}\
  [這能力使基督從死裏復活，]{lang="zh"}
- [And made Him o’er all, to His Body, the Head.]{lang="en"}\
  [為教會元首，遠超一切執政。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [O God, Thou hast wrought all this pow’r into Christ,]{lang="en"}\
  [神已將這能力作到基督裏，]{lang="zh"}
- [Who now lives in me as my Person, my life.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂今活在我裏作我的生命。]{lang="zh"}
- [O grant revelation, dear Father, to see]{lang="en"}\
  [這超絕大能是基督在我裏；]{lang="zh"}
- [This transcending pow’r is a Person in me.]{lang="en"}\
  [求父神賞給啟示使我看明。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [O Christ is the hope, He’s the glory and power;]{lang="en"}\
  [基督是我盼望、榮耀和能力；]{lang="zh"}
- [So Christ I must take as my Person each hour.]{lang="en"}\
  [每時刻我須以祂作我生命。]{lang="zh"}
- [Then in me such power will be exercised,]{lang="en"}\
  [我裏面的能力就運行不已，]{lang="zh"}
- [My hope and God’s glory will be realized.]{lang="en"}\
  [我盼望、和神榮耀就被顯明。]{lang="zh"}
