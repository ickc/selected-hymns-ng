# 1

- [I take Thy promise, Lord, in all its length,]{lang="en"}\
  [主，你的應許，我全都接受，]{lang="zh"}
- [And breadth and fulness, as my daily strength;]{lang="en"}\
  [每天來取用，長闊又深厚；]{lang="zh"}
- [Into life’s future fearless I may gaze,]{lang="en"}\
  [面對著將來，我毫無畏懼，]{lang="zh"}
- [For, Savior, Thou art with me all the days.]{lang="en"}\
  [因為我救主，與我永不離。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Days may be coming fraught with loss and change,]{lang="en"}\
  [時日會改遷，得失難預測，]{lang="zh"}
- [New scenes surround my life and fancies strange;]{lang="en"}\
  [景色常更換，幻夢易失色；]{lang="zh"}
- [I thank Thee that no day can ever break,]{lang="en"}\
  [親愛的救主，我心感謝你，]{lang="zh"}
- [Savior, when Thou wilt leave me or forsake.]{lang="en"}\
  [因你永不會把我來丟棄。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [There may be days of darkness and distress,]{lang="en"}\
  [前面的年日，艱苦或黑暗，]{lang="zh"}
- [When sin has power to tempt, and care to press,]{lang="en"}\
  [陰府的權勢，罪惡的試探，]{lang="zh"}
- [Yet in the darkest day I will not fear,]{lang="en"}\
  [縱在黑暗中，我心仍安詳，]{lang="zh"}
- [For, ‘mid the shadows, Thou wilt still be near.]{lang="en"}\
  [因在陰影中，你仍在我旁。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Days there may be of joy and.deep delight,]{lang="en"}\
  [有時喜樂潮，充滿在我心，]{lang="zh"}
- [When earth seems fairest, and her skies most bright,]{lang="en"}\
  [地上的美景，天上的光明，]{lang="zh"}
- [Then draw me closer to Thee, lest I rest]{lang="en"}\
  [勿讓我留此，仍來親近你，]{lang="zh"}
- [Elsewhere, my Savior, than upon Thy breast.]{lang="en"}\
  [不再停他處，只在你懷裡。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [And all the other days that make my life,]{lang="en"}\
  [願我的一生留下的年日，]{lang="zh"}
- [Marked by no special joy or grief or strife,]{lang="en"}\
  [不再求安樂，或掙扎得釋，]{lang="zh"}
- [Days filled with quiet duties, trivial care,]{lang="en"}\
  [每日的生活，恩典必加力，]{lang="zh"}
- [Burdens too small for other hearts to share;]{lang="en"}\
  [人生的重荷，恩主必體恤。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [Spend Thou these days with me, all shall be Thine,]{lang="en"}\
  [願一生一世，永與主同在，]{lang="zh"}
- [So shall the darkest hour with glory shine.]{lang="en"}\
  [縱過死蔭穀，仍不怕遭害。]{lang="zh"}
- [Then when these earthly years have passed away,]{lang="en"}\
  [當我度過這地上的年日，]{lang="zh"}
- [Let me be with Thee in the perfect day.]{lang="en"}\
  [仍與主同在，一直到永世。]{lang="zh"}
