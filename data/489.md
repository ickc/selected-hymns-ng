# 1

- [Lord, Thou hast made Thyself to me]{lang="en"}\
  [主，你已使你的自己，]{lang="zh"}
- [A living, bright reality,]{lang="en"}\
  [成為我們活的實際；]{lang="zh"}
- [More present to faith’s vision keen]{lang="en"}\
  [信心所見榮耀異象，]{lang="zh"}
- [Than any earthly object seen;]{lang="en"}\
  [比諸屬地景物明朗；]{lang="zh"}
- [More dear, more intimately nigh]{lang="en"}\
  [可親可近，其情其意，]{lang="zh"}
- [Than e’en the closest earthly tie.]{lang="en"}\
  [地上最近親人難比。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [And Thou, blest vision of my soul,]{lang="en"}\
  [有福異象我已看見：]{lang="zh"}
- [Hast made my broken nature whole;]{lang="en"}\
  [惟有你是我的完全；]{lang="zh"}
- [Hast purified my base desires,]{lang="en"}\
  [你已純潔我的意願，]{lang="zh"}
- [And kindled passion’s holiest fires;]{lang="en"}\
  [最聖愛火其中點燃；]{lang="zh"}
- [My nature Thou hast lifted up,]{lang="en"}\
  [你已提高我的性情，]{lang="zh"}
- [And filled me with a glorious hope.]{lang="en"}\
  [充滿你的榮耀豐盛。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Nearer and dearer still to me,]{lang="en"}\
  [永活救主，可愛恩人，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou living, loving Savior be;]{lang="en"}\
  [對我你要更親更近；]{lang="zh"}
- [Brighter the vision of Thy face,]{lang="en"}\
  [你的榮面要更光明，]{lang="zh"}
- [More charming still Thy words of grace;]{lang="en"}\
  [你的恩言要更有能；]{lang="zh"}
- [So, life shall be transformed to love,]{lang="en"}\
  [愛中要將我全變化，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy grace and mercy more to prove.]{lang="en"}\
  [憐憫、恩典要更增加。]{lang="zh"}
