# 1

- [Stir me, oh, stir me, Lord, I care not how,]{lang="en"}\
  [激動我心，主，我別無計較，]{lang="zh"}
- [But stir my heart in passion for the world,]{lang="en"}\
  [只求激動我心關懷世人，]{lang="zh"}
- [Stir me to give, to go, but most to pray;]{lang="en"}\
  [使我去給、去傳、更去禱告；]{lang="zh"}
- [Stir till the blood-red banner be unfurled]{lang="en"}\
  [願將血紅救贖大旗高擎，]{lang="zh"}
- [O’er lands that still in heathen darkness lie,]{lang="en"}\
  [傳遍外邦、除去黑暗籠罩，]{lang="zh"}
- [O’er deserts where no cross is lifted high.]{lang="en"}\
  [好使萬民認識十架大道。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Stir me, oh! stir me, Lord, till all my heart]{lang="en"}\
  [激動我心，主，直到我全心]{lang="zh"}
- [Is filled with strong compassion for these souls;]{lang="en"}\
  [充滿同情，關心失喪靈魂，]{lang="zh"}
- [Till Thy compelling word drives me to pray;]{lang="en"}\
  [被你的話催促，為人求懇，]{lang="zh"}
- [Till Thy constraining love reach to the poles]{lang="en"}\
  [被你的愛激勵，到處找尋，]{lang="zh"}
- [Far north and south, in burning deep desire,]{lang="en"}\
  [天南地北，因愛傳揚你恩，]{lang="zh"}
- [Till east and west are caught in love’s great fire.]{lang="en"}\
  [東奔西走，裏面愛火如焚。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Stir me, oh, stir me, Lord, till prayer is pain,]{lang="en"}\
  [激動我心，主，直到我禱告]{lang="zh"}
- [Till prayer is joy, till prayer turns into praise;]{lang="en"}\
  [能從痛苦轉為喜樂、讚美；]{lang="zh"}
- [Stir me, till heart and will and mind, yea, all]{lang="en"}\
  [願我全人都能被你得到，]{lang="zh"}
- [Is wholly Thine to use through all the days.]{lang="en"}\
  [一生歸你使用、永不後悔。]{lang="zh"}
- [Stir, till I learn to pray exceedingly;]{lang="en"}\
  [求你使我學會迫切禱告；]{lang="zh"}
- [Stir, till I learn to wait expectantly.]{lang="en"}\
  [也求使我學會等候、依靠。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Stir me, oh stir me, Lord, Thy heart was stirred]{lang="en"}\
  [激動我心，神，你曾因愛火]{lang="zh"}
- [By love’s intensest fire, till Thou didst give]{lang="en"}\
  [激動，賜下你的獨生愛子，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thine only Son, Thy best beloved One,]{lang="en"}\
  [使祂離開天上榮耀寶座，]{lang="zh"}
- [E’en to the dreadful cross, that I might live.]{lang="en"}\
  [為使我們得生，被釘受死。]{lang="zh"}
- [Stir me to give myself so back to Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [激動我心，叫我為你而活，]{lang="zh"}
- [That Thou canst give Thyself again through me.]{lang="en"}\
  [好叫眾人從我將你得著。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Stir me, oh, stir me, Lord, for I can see]{lang="en"}\
  [激動我心，主，使我能看見，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy glorious triumph-day begin to break:]{lang="en"}\
  [你的榮耀得勝日子已近；]{lang="zh"}
- [The dawn already gilds the eastern sky;]{lang="en"}\
  [晨曦已經隱約顯在天邊；]{lang="zh"}
- [Oh, Church of Christ, arise, awake, awake.]{lang="en"}\
  [主的教會阿，當興起、儆醒。]{lang="zh"}
- [Oh! stir us, Lord, as heralds of that day.]{lang="en"}\
  [黑夜已深，我主即將再顯，]{lang="zh"}
- [For night is past, our King is on His way.]{lang="en"}\
  [我願傳報此訊直到那天。]{lang="zh"}
