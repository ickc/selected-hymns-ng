# 1

- [Jesus, Redeemer and my one Inspirer,]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌我救主，是你感動我心，]{lang="zh"}
- [Heat in my coldness, set my life aglow.]{lang="en"}\
  [使冷淡焚燒，驅盡我陰影，]{lang="zh"}
- [Break down my barriers; draw, yea, draw me nigher.]{lang="en"}\
  [拆毀我障礙，吸引我更親近，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thee would I know, whom it is life to know.]{lang="en"}\
  [使我認識你是我的生命。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Deepen me, rid me of the superficial;]{lang="en"}\
  [更深地挖我，除我膚淺行為；]{lang="zh"}
- [From pale delusion set my spirit free;]{lang="en"}\
  [使我脫困惑，靈自由如飛；]{lang="zh"}
- [All the entangled in me quick unravel;]{lang="en"}\
  [求我主速速解開一切纏累；]{lang="zh"}
- [Pluck forth each thread of insincerity.]{lang="en"}\
  [除盡我身上每一絲虛偽。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Thy vows are on me, oh to serve Thee truly—]{lang="en"}\
  [我有願在身，全心事奉我神－]{lang="zh"}
- [Love perfectly, in purity obey—]{lang="en"}\
  [完全的愛你，單純順服你－]{lang="zh"}
- [Burn, burn, O Fire. O Wind, now winnow throughly;]{lang="en"}\
  [哦，聖風來吹，哦，求聖火來焚；]{lang="zh"}
- [O Sword, awake against the flesh and slay.]{lang="en"}\
  [神寶劍！醒起治死我肉體。]{lang="zh"}
