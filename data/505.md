# 1

You are the vine, We are the branches,
你是葡萄樹，我們是枝子，
Keep us abiding in You;
使我常住你裡面；
You are the vine, We are the branches
你是葡萄樹，我們是枝子，
Keep us abiding in You.
使我常住你裡面。
And we’ll go In Your love
我們因你愛而前往，靠你名而得勝，
And we’ll go In Your name,
因愛前往，靠你名而得勝，
That the world Will surely know
讓世人都能知道，
hat You have power to heal and to save.
在你有醫治權柄能拯
