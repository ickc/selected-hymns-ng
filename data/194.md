# 1

- [We’ve come to love the Bible,]{lang="en"}\
  [我們喜愛這聖經——]{lang="zh"}
- [the precious Word of God;]{lang="en"}\
  [神的寶貴話語；]{lang="zh"}
- [It fills us with the riches and fullness of the Lord.]{lang="en"}\
  [主的豐富與豐盛，在這話裏蘊藏。]{lang="zh"}
- [The grace it is infusing yields holy energy,]{lang="en"}\
  [帶著恩典的供應，產生屬天能力；]{lang="zh"}
- [And life to us transfusing, it sets us free.]{lang="en"}\
  [它所傳輸的生命，將我釋放。]{lang="zh"}

  [話語！話語！我們愛神話語。]{lang="zh"}
- [Its life and light is our delight, it fills us with the Lord.]{lang="en"}\
  [生命、亮光、和主自己，藉它充滿我心。]{lang="zh"}
- [The Word! The Word, the key to all our joy!]{lang="en"}\
  [話語！話語！喜樂從它吸取！]{lang="zh"}
- [Its jewels fine we love to mine, its riches to employ.]{lang="en"}\
  [豐富寶藏，我們掘取，永遠取用不盡。]{lang="zh"}
