# 1

- [Here, O my Lord, I see Thee face to face;]{lang="en"}\
  [主，在此我要與你面對面，]{lang="zh"}
- [Here faith can touch and handle things unseen;]{lang="en"}\
  [在此我要用信把握不見，]{lang="zh"}
- [Here would I grasp with firmer hand Thy grace,]{lang="en"}\
  [在此我要更深認識恩典，]{lang="zh"}
- [And all my weariness upon Thee lean.]{lang="en"}\
  [將我疲勞都息在主腳前。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Here would I feed upon the Bread of God;]{lang="en"}\
  [在此我要吃主所賜美物，]{lang="zh"}
- [Here drink with Thee the royal wine of heav’n;]{lang="en"}\
  [在此我要飲主所遞福杯，]{lang="zh"}
- [Here would I lay aside each earthly load;]{lang="en"}\
  [在此我要忘記一切難處，]{lang="zh"}
- [Here taste afresh the calm of sin forgiv’n.]{lang="en"}\
  [再嘗一次赦罪平安滋味。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [I have no help but Thine; nor do I need]{lang="en"}\
  [除你之外，我無別的幫助；]{lang="zh"}
- [Another arm save Thine to lean upon;]{lang="en"}\
  [有你賜恩，我就不求人惠；]{lang="zh"}
- [It is enough, my Lord, enough indeed;]{lang="en"}\
  [有你的愛，我已心滿意足；]{lang="zh"}
- [My strength is in Thy might, Thy might alone.]{lang="en"}\
  [靠你能力，我要站住地位。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [This is the hour of banquet and of song;]{lang="en"}\
  [這是歡喜飲宴、唱詩時刻；]{lang="zh"}
- [This is the heav’nly table spread for me;]{lang="en"}\
  [這是屬天筵席為我擺設；]{lang="zh"}
- [Here let me feast, and, feasting, still prolong]{lang="en"}\
  [我要在此吃喝，並再吃喝，]{lang="zh"}
- [The brief bright hour of fellowship with Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [享受與你交通甜美時刻。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Too soon we rise; the symbols disappear;]{lang="en"}\
  [席撤何速，表記的物已盡！]{lang="zh"}
- [The feast, though not the love, is past and gone;]{lang="en"}\
  [酒餅雖無，拯救的愛未亡！]{lang="zh"}
- [The bread and wine remove, but Thou art here,]{lang="en"}\
  [宴筵已過，你仍在此親近，]{lang="zh"}
- [Nearer than ever still our Shield and Sun.]{lang="en"}\
  [親近有加，作我萬有君王！]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [Feast after feast thus comes and passes by,]{lang="en"}\
  [上席罷席，次次我們聚散，]{lang="zh"}
- [Yet passing, points to the glad feast above,]{lang="en"}\
  [如此聚散，遙指天上佳筵；]{lang="zh"}
- [Giving sweet foretastes of the festal joy,]{lang="en"}\
  [時雖未至，我們卻已豫嘗，]{lang="zh"}
- [The Lamb’s great bridal-feast of bliss and love.]{lang="en"}\
  [他日天上羔羊婚娶喜宴。]{lang="zh"}
