# 1

- [The Lord is ris’n; and death’s dark judgment flood]{lang="en"}\
  [主已復活，死的審判已過；]{lang="zh"}
- [Is passed, in Him who bought us with his blood.]{lang="en"}\
  [在祂裏面，死亡難再淹沒。]{lang="zh"}
- [The Lord is ris’n: we stand beyond the doom]{lang="en"}\
  [主已復活，我今不再定罪；]{lang="zh"}
- [Of all our sin, through Jesus’ empty tomb.]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的空墓作證最具權威。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [The Lord is ris’n: with Him we also rose,]{lang="en"}\
  [主已復活，我也同祂復活；]{lang="zh"}
- [And in His grave see vanquished all our foes.]{lang="en"}\
  [在祂墓中，仇敵全都埋沒。]{lang="zh"}
- [The Lord is ris’n: beyond the judgment land,]{lang="en"}\
  [主已復活，我得脫離死域；]{lang="zh"}
- [In Him, in resurrection-life we stand.]{lang="en"}\
  [在祂裏面，復活生命是倚。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [The Lord is ris’n: and now redeemed to God,]{lang="en"}\
  [主已復活，我已得蒙拯救，]{lang="zh"}
- [We tread the desert which His feet have trod.]{lang="en"}\
  [今在曠野跟祂腳蹤行走。]{lang="zh"}
- [The Lord is ris’n: the sanctu’ry’s our place,]{lang="en"}\
  [主已復活，我得進至聖所，]{lang="zh"}
- [Where now we dwell before the Father’s face.]{lang="en"}\
  [與祂一同在神面光生活。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [The Lord is ris’n: the Lord is gone before.]{lang="en"}\
  [主已復活，作了幔內先鋒，]{lang="zh"}
- [We long to see Him, and to sin no more.]{lang="en"}\
  [使我營外來作祂的見證。]{lang="zh"}
- [The Lord is ris’n: our triumph-shout shall be,]{lang="en"}\
  [主已復活，我要高唱凱歌：]{lang="zh"}
- [“Thou hast prevailed! Thy people, Lord, are free!”]{lang="en"}\
  [“你已得勝！你民已脫捆索！”]{lang="zh"}
