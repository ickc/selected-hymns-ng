# 1

- [There were ninety and nine that safely lay]{lang="en"}\
  [九十九隻圈中躺臥，]{lang="zh"}
- [In the shelter of the flock,]{lang="en"}\
  [終日平安、快樂；]{lang="zh"}
- [But one was out on the hills away,]{lang="en"}\
  [但有一隻山野飄泊，]{lang="zh"}
- [Far off in the cold and dark;]{lang="en"}\
  [處境艱窘、險惡；]{lang="zh"}
- [Away on the mountains wild and bare,]{lang="en"}\
  [荒山深谷，流蕩、孤苦，]{lang="zh"}
- [Away from the tender Shepherd’s care.]{lang="en"}\
  [遠離慈牧，失卻照護。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [“Lord, Thou hast here Thy ninety and nine;]{lang="en"}\
  [「主，你已經有九十九，]{lang="zh"}
- [Are they not enough for Thee?”]{lang="en"}\
  [難道還嫌不夠？」]{lang="zh"}
- [But the Shepherd made answer: “This of Mine]{lang="en"}\
  [祂說：「迷羊亦我所有，]{lang="zh"}
- [Has wandered away from Me;]{lang="en"}\
  [現在離我飄流，]{lang="zh"}
- [And although the road be rough and steep,]{lang="en"}\
  [山勢雖陡，路雖難走，]{lang="zh"}
- [I go to the desert to find My sheep.”]{lang="en"}\
  [我仍須去將牠營救。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [But none of the ransomed ever knew]{lang="en"}\
  [但是我們不能領會，]{lang="zh"}
- [How deep were the waters crossed;]{lang="en"}\
  [祂涉之水何危，]{lang="zh"}
- [Nor how dark was the night which the Lord]{lang="en"}\
  [所曆黑夜何等可畏，]{lang="zh"}
- [passed through Ere He found His sheep that was lost.]{lang="en"}\
  [為將迷羊尋回。]{lang="zh"}
- [Out in the bleak desert He heard its cry—]{lang="en"}\
  [荒野前行，祂聞哀聲；]{lang="zh"}
- [All bleeding and helpless, and ready to die.]{lang="en"}\
  [迷羊無助，垂死悲鳴。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [“Lord, whence are those blood-drops all the way]{lang="en"}\
  [「主阿，山徑一路血滴，]{lang="zh"}
- [That mark out the mountain’s track?”]{lang="en"}\
  [是誰，為何而流？」]{lang="zh"}
- [“They were shed for one who had gone astray]{lang="en"}\
  [「乃是我為迷羊流的，]{lang="zh"}
- [Ere the Shepherd could bring him back.”]{lang="en"}\
  [為要將牠拯救。」]{lang="zh"}
- [“Lord, whence are Thy hands so rent and torn?”]{lang="en"}\
  [「你的手掌，為何受傷？」]{lang="zh"}
- [“They’re pierced tonight by many a thorn.”]{lang="en"}\
  [乃是昨夜路荊所創。」]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [And up from the rocky steep,]{lang="en"}\
  [穿過懸崖陡坡，]{lang="zh"}

  [「樂哉，迷羊找著！」]{lang="zh"}

  [「樂哉！主已尋回迷羊。」]{lang="zh"}
