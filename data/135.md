# 1

- [Eternal Love! Oh, mighty sea,]{lang="en"}\
  [永遠之愛猶如海洋，]{lang="zh"}
- [That hath not bounds, or shore,]{lang="en"}\
  [浩大無邊、無疆，]{lang="zh"}
- [I gaze upon Thy tideless waves,]{lang="en"}\
  [當我觀看不息波浪，]{lang="zh"}
- [I know the Love that loving saves,]{lang="en"}\
  [頓悟你大愛來救亡，]{lang="zh"}
- [I worship, and adore.]{lang="en"}\
  [不盡讚美、歡唱！]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Eternal Love, Eternal Love,]{lang="en"}\
  [永遠之愛，永遠之愛，]{lang="zh"}
- [That ever shall abide;]{lang="en"}\
  [長存直到萬代，]{lang="zh"}
- [The Love that saves, the Love that keeps,]{lang="en"}\
  [這愛救我，又賜恩待，]{lang="zh"}
- [What vastness in Thy glorious deeps,]{lang="en"}\
  [浩浩大愛，何等胸懷，]{lang="zh"}
- [Oh ocean deep and wide!]{lang="en"}\
  [廣闊猶如深海。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Eternal Love that loveth me,]{lang="en"}\
  [永遠之愛，竟愛了我！]{lang="zh"}
- [And loving, made me love,]{lang="en"}\
  [用愛將我纏裹，]{lang="zh"}
- [That brought my Savior pain and loss,]{lang="en"}\
  [這愛使祂受苦、被剝，]{lang="zh"}
- [The awful anguish of the Cross,]{lang="en"}\
  [十字架上，為我擔過，]{lang="zh"}
- [To lift my soul above!]{lang="en"}\
  [又使我魂超脫。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Eternal Love that bore the curse,]{lang="en"}\
  [永遠之愛，為我受詛，]{lang="zh"}
- [That else must fall on me,]{lang="en"}\
  [為我擔當忿怒，]{lang="zh"}
- [Now to the glory of Thy grace,]{lang="en"}\
  [把我引向恩典、祝福；]{lang="zh"}
- [Mine eyes shall see the Savior’s face]{lang="en"}\
  [仰望你的榮臉不住，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thru all Eternity.]{lang="en"}\
  [從今直到永古。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Eternal Love, O Son of God,]{lang="en"}\
  [永遠之愛，神的愛子，]{lang="zh"}
- [Glorious in all Thy ways!]{lang="en"}\
  [作為榮耀、信實，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou art enthroned in light divine,]{lang="en"}\
  [榮光之中，你登寶座，]{lang="zh"}
- [The light in which Thy saints shall shine]{lang="en"}\
  [你的聖徒，光中生活，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thru everlasting days.]{lang="en"}\
  [永世照耀不輟。]{lang="zh"}
