# 1

- [Utterly abandoned To the Holy Ghost!]{lang="en"}\
  [絕對分別出來，歸於主聖靈！]{lang="zh"}
- [Seeking all His fulness At whatever cost;]{lang="en"}\
  [不惜任何代價，尋求祂豐盛；]{lang="zh"}
- [Cutting all the shorelines, Launching in the deep]{lang="en"}\
  [割斷所有岸索，開到水深處；]{lang="zh"}
- [Of His mighty power, Strong to save and keep.]{lang="en"}\
  [靠著祂的大能，剛強而堅固。]{lang="zh"}

  [答應祂的呼召，尋求祂豐富。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Utterly abandoned To the Holy Ghost!]{lang="en"}\
  [絕對分別出來，歸於主聖靈！]{lang="zh"}
- [Oh! the sinking, sinking, Till the self is lost!]{lang="en"}\
  [沉沒還要沉沒，直到己去淨！]{lang="zh"}
- [Till the vessel’s emptied Broken at His feet;]{lang="en"}\
  [直到器皿倒空，祂腳前破碎；]{lang="zh"}
- [Waiting till His filling Makes the work complete.]{lang="en"}\
  [等候直到充滿，流出主恩惠。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Utterly abandoned To the will of God;]{lang="en"}\
  [絕對分別出來，歸於神旨意！]{lang="zh"}
- [Walking in the pathway That my Master trod;]{lang="en"}\
  [走主所走道路，靠著祂恩力；]{lang="zh"}
- [Leaving ease and pleasure; Making Him my’choice,]{lang="en"}\
  [撇棄安逸、享樂，隨主向前進；]{lang="zh"}
- [Waiting for His guidance, Listening for His voice.]{lang="en"}\
  [等候祂的引導，靜聽祂聲音。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Utterly abandoned! Through eternity;]{lang="en"}\
  [絕對分別出來，一直到永世；]{lang="zh"}
- [My will never choosing, His it e’er shall be;]{lang="en"}\
  [不要我的心意，乃要祂意旨；]{lang="zh"}
- [All my plans and longings Lost in His sweet will,]{lang="en"}\
  [我的計畫、願望，在祂全滅沒；]{lang="zh"}
- [Having nothing, owning All things in Him still.]{lang="en"}\
  [似乎一無所有，萬有實屬我。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [Utterly abandoned! ’Tis so sweet to be]{lang="en"}\
  [絕對分別出來，何等的甜美，]{lang="zh"}
- [Captive in His love-bonds, Yet so wondrous free;]{lang="en"}\
  [作祂愛的俘虜，自由何寶貴；]{lang="zh"}
- [Free from sin’s dominion, Free from doubt and fear,]{lang="en"}\
  [脫離罪的轄制、疑惑與恐懼，]{lang="zh"}
- [Free from every worry, Burden, grief or care.]{lang="en"}\
  [脫離所有重擔、憂愁和掛慮。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [Utterly abandoned! Oh, the rest is sweet,]{lang="en"}\
  [絕對分別出來，何等的安息，]{lang="zh"}
- [Waiting in His presence At His blessed feet;]{lang="en"}\
  [在祂面前等候，照祂的心意；]{lang="zh"}
- [Waiting for the filling Of the Host divine,]{lang="en"}\
  [等候得著充滿，滿有祂聖靈，]{lang="zh"}
- [Who my inward parts shall Perfectly refine.]{lang="en"}\
  [將我裡面各部完全都煉淨。]{lang="zh"}

# 7

- [Lo! His Spirit fills me, With His presence sweet!]{lang="en"}\
  [祂靈將我充滿，帶著祂同在！]{lang="zh"}
- [I, in Him, am blessed! I, in Him, complete!]{lang="en"}\
  [在祂我蒙祝福，在祂我蒙愛！]{lang="zh"}
- [Now the light within me Never shall grow dim]{lang="en"}\
  [今後裡面亮光永不再變暗，]{lang="zh"}
- [While abandoned ever, Living unto Him!]{lang="en"}\
  [如此向祂活著，分別到永遠。]{lang="zh"}
