# 1

- [In the cross of Christ I glory,]{lang="en"}\
  [主十字架是我誇耀，]{lang="zh"}
- [Tow’ring o’er the wrecks of time;]{lang="en"}\
  [年代滅沒它獨存；]{lang="zh"}
- [All the light of sacred story]{lang="en"}\
  [神聖事蹟光輝環繞，]{lang="zh"}
- [Gathers round its head sublime.]{lang="en"}\
  [十架巍然是中心。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [When the woes of life o’ertake me,]{lang="en"}\
  [當我遭遇患難折磨，]{lang="zh"}
- [Hopes deceive, and fears annoy,]{lang="en"}\
  [希望成空，憂懼纏；]{lang="zh"}
- [Never shall the cross forsake me,]{lang="en"}\
  [主十字架永不棄我，]{lang="zh"}
- [Lo! It glows with peace and joy.]{lang="en"}\
  [看哪！喜樂與平安。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [When the sun of bliss is beaming]{lang="en"}\
  [有福太陽，以光以愛]{lang="zh"}
- [Light and love upon my way,]{lang="en"}\
  [照耀在我道路上；]{lang="zh"}
- [From the cross the radiance streaming]{lang="en"}\
  [主十字架放射光彩，]{lang="zh"}
- [Adds more luster to the day.]{lang="en"}\
  [使我人生更光亮。]{lang="zh"}
