# 1

- [Lord Jesus Christ, our heart feels sweet]{lang="en"}\
  [哦主耶穌，每想到你，]{lang="zh"}
- [Whene'er we think on Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [我心便覺甘甜；]{lang="zh"}
- [And long that to Thy presence dear]{lang="en"}\
  [深願我能立刻被提，]{lang="zh"}
- [We soon might raptured be!]{lang="en"}\
  [到你可愛身邊！]{lang="zh"}

- [Lord, like the pretty henna-flower,]{lang="en"}\
  [主，你如一棵美麗鳳仙，]{lang="zh"}
- [In vineyards blossoming Thou art;]{lang="en"}\
  [顯在山野葡萄園間，]{lang="zh"}
- [Incomp'rable Thy beauty is,]{lang="en"}\
  [殊姿超群、秀色獨艷，]{lang="zh"}

  [我心依依戀戀。]{lang="zh"}
