- [Father, Thy name our souls would bless,]{lang="en"}\
  [父阿，兒女稱頌你名，]{lang="zh"}

  [是受恩典教訓；]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Sweet is the confidence Thou giv’st,]{lang="en"}\
  [你所給的得救證實，]{lang="zh"}

  [遠超我們讚美；]{lang="zh"}

# 3

  [因在那裏，祂為我們]{lang="zh"}

  [你將生命分給罪人，]{lang="zh"}
