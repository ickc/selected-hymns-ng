# 1

- [Prince of peace, control my will;]{lang="en"}\
  [平安作我心中主；]{lang="zh"}
- [Bid this struggling heart be still:]{lang="en"}\
  [吩咐躁心要安靜；]{lang="zh"}
- [Bid my fears and doubtings cease:]{lang="en"}\
  [憂慮、疑懼皆消除；]{lang="zh"}
- [Hush my spirit into peace.]{lang="en"}\
  [催使我靈入安寧。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Thou hast bought me with Thy blood,]{lang="en"}\
  [你用己血將我買，]{lang="zh"}
- [Opened wide the gate to God;]{lang="en"}\
  [歸神之門已敞開；]{lang="zh"}
- [Peace I ask, but peace must be,]{lang="en"}\
  [我求平安，平安來，]{lang="zh"}
- [Lord, in being one with Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [因我與主不離開。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [May Thy will, not mine be done;]{lang="en"}\
  [願你旨意得成全；]{lang="zh"}
- [May Thy will and mine be one;]{lang="en"}\
  [使你旨成我心願；]{lang="zh"}
- [Chase these doubtings from my heart,]{lang="en"}\
  [心中疑惑你消散，]{lang="zh"}
- [Now Thy perfect peace impart.]{lang="en"}\
  [賜下你完全平安。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Savior, at Thy feet I fall,]{lang="en"}\
  [救主，我伏你腳前，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou, my life, my God, my all;]{lang="en"}\
  [你是我命、我一切；]{lang="zh"}
- [Let Thy happy servant be]{lang="en"}\
  [作你僕人到永遠，]{lang="zh"}
- [One forevermore with Thee.]{lang="en"}\
  [願我與你永聯結。]{lang="zh"}
