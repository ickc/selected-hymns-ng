# 1

- [O Christ, He is the fountain,]{lang="en"}\
  [恩主，你是生命源，]{lang="zh"}
- [The deep, sweet well of life:]{lang="en"}\
  [深而且甜之泉：]{lang="zh"}
- [Its living streams I’ve tasted]{lang="en"}\
  [你的活水我已嘗，]{lang="zh"}
- [Which save from grief and strife.]{lang="en"}\
  [永不再渴再乾。]{lang="zh"}
- [And to an ocean fullness,]{lang="en"}\
  [你的憐憫時擴展，]{lang="zh"}
- [His mercy doth expand;]{lang="en"}\
  [豐滿如同海洋；]{lang="zh"}
- [His grace is all-sufficient]{lang="en"}\
  [你的恩典夠我用，]{lang="zh"}
- [As by His wisdom planned.]{lang="en"}\
  [是你智慧所量。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [O I am my Beloved’s,]{lang="en"}\
  [我乃是屬我良人，]{lang="zh"}
- [And my Beloved’s mine;]{lang="en"}\
  [我的良人屬我；]{lang="zh"}
- [He brings a poor vile sinner]{lang="en"}\
  [你將污穢的罪人，]{lang="zh"}
- [Into His house of wine!]{lang="en"}\
  [帶進你宴筵所。]{lang="zh"}
- [I stand upon His merit;]{lang="en"}\
  [你的功績我所倚，]{lang="zh"}
- [I know no other stand.]{lang="en"}\
  [你外別無可投；]{lang="zh"}
- [I’m hidden in His presence]{lang="en"}\
  [隱藏在你面光中，]{lang="zh"}
- [And held by His own hand.]{lang="en"}\
  [享受你的保守。]{lang="zh"}
