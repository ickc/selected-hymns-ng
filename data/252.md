# 1

- [We gather together to eat the Lord’s supper:]{lang="en"}\
  [我們聚在一起，來吃主的晚餐；]{lang="zh"}
- [By eating and drinking, our oneness we show.]{lang="en"}\
  [藉著這吃喝，來把合一展覽。]{lang="zh"}
- [His presence possessing, our oneness confessing,]{lang="en"}\
  [越發宣告合一，越發有主同在，]{lang="zh"}
- [‘Tis thus we eat and drink, and His blessing we know.]{lang="en"}\
  [深知如此吃喝，蒙主祝福眷愛。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [We’re eating the bread of His body once broken]{lang="en"}\
  [我們所吃的餅 乃是主的身體，]{lang="zh"}
- [As now we’re enjoying His Body made one:]{lang="en"}\
  [它曾被裂開，為使我們合一。]{lang="zh"}
- [One loaf on the table-one Body, we’re able]{lang="en"}\
  [桌上只有一餅——只有一個身體，]{lang="zh"}
- [To testify to all that the Lord’s made us one.]{lang="en"}\
  [為著向人表明，主使我們成一。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [We also are drinking the cup of His blessing,]{lang="en"}\
  [我們所喝的杯，是主立的新約，]{lang="zh"}
- [The church’s true fellowship of the Lord’s blood;]{lang="en"}\
  [我們今一同 交通於主的血。]{lang="zh"}
- [Redeemed by believing, all saints we’re receiving,]{lang="en"}\
  [接納所有聖徒——只要因血得贖，]{lang="zh"}
- [All those who through redemption have come unto God.]{lang="en"}\
  [在神面前稱謝 我們如何蒙福。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Oh, what can we say now except “Hallelujah!”]{lang="en"}\
  [我們別無可說，除說：“阿利路亞！”]{lang="zh"}
- [For such a reality given to us.]{lang="en"}\
  [因主給我們 經歷了這實際。]{lang="zh"}
- [Thy blood we’re employing, Thy Body enjoying;]{lang="en"}\
  [取用主的寶血，享受主的身體，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thus Thou wilt have the church for Thyself glorious.]{lang="en"}\
  [主就得著教會，榮耀歸祂自己。]{lang="zh"}
