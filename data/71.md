# 1

- [Hark! the herald angels sing,]{lang="en"}\
  [聽阿，天使高聲唱：]{lang="zh"}
- [“Glory to the new-born King;]{lang="en"}\
  [“榮耀歸給新生王！]{lang="zh"}
- [Peace on earth, and mercy mild;]{lang="en"}\
  [恩典臨地平安到，]{lang="zh"}
- [God and sinners reconciled.”]{lang="en"}\
  [神人此後能和好。”]{lang="zh"}
- [Joyful, all ye nations, rise,]{lang="en"}\
  [興起！地上萬國民，]{lang="zh"}
- [Join the triumph of the skies;]{lang="en"}\
  [響應天上讚美聲；]{lang="zh"}
- [With angelic hosts proclaim,]{lang="en"}\
  [天唱地和樂歡騰，]{lang="zh"}
- [“Christ is born in Bethlehem.”]{lang="en"}\
  [基督降生伯利恆。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Christ, by highest heav’n adored,]{lang="en"}\
  [基督本有神形像，]{lang="zh"}
- [Christ, the everlasting Lord:]{lang="en"}\
  [基督原是永遠王，]{lang="zh"}
- [Late in time behold Him come,]{lang="en"}\
  [竟在末世從天降，]{lang="zh"}
- [Offspring of a virgin’s womb.]{lang="en"}\
  [生於童女成人狀；]{lang="zh"}
- [Veiled in flesh the Godhead see,]{lang="en"}\
  [神性穿上血肉體，]{lang="zh"}
- [Hail th’incarnate Deity!]{lang="en"}\
  [道成肉身何奧祕，]{lang="zh"}
- [Pleased as man with man to dwell,]{lang="en"}\
  [甘願作人同人住，]{lang="zh"}
- [Jesus our Emmanuel.]{lang="en"}\
  [以馬內利是耶穌。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Hail the heav’n-born Prince of Peace!]{lang="en"}\
  [歡迎，天來和平王！]{lang="zh"}
- [Hail the Sun of righteousness!]{lang="en"}\
  [歡迎，公義的太陽！]{lang="zh"}
- [Light and life to all He brings,]{lang="en"}\
  [帶來生命與亮光，]{lang="zh"}
- [Ris’n with healing in His wings:]{lang="en"}\
  [使人復活，醫人傷；]{lang="zh"}
- [Mild He lays His glory by,]{lang="en"}\
  [虛己，撇下祂榮光，]{lang="zh"}
- [Born that man no more may die;]{lang="en"}\
  [降生救人免死亡，]{lang="zh"}
- [Born to raise the sons of earth;]{lang="en"}\
  [降生使人得重生，]{lang="zh"}
- [Born to give them second birth.]{lang="en"}\
  [降生使人能高升。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Come, Desire of nations, come!]{lang="en"}\
  [萬國“羨慕”萬民“望”，]{lang="zh"}
- [Fix in us Thy humble home:]{lang="en"}\
  [願你安家我心房；]{lang="zh"}
- [Rise, the woman’s conjuring seed,]{lang="en"}\
  [女人後裔——得勝者，]{lang="zh"}
- [Bruise in us the serpent’s head;]{lang="en"}\
  [在我裏面毀古蛇；]{lang="zh"}
- [Adam’s likeness now efface,]{lang="en"}\
  [亞當舊樣全除盡，]{lang="zh"}
- [Stamp Thine image in its place:]{lang="en"}\
  [你的形像將我印；]{lang="zh"}
- [Final Adam from above,]{lang="en"}\
  [末後亞當已降臨，]{lang="zh"}
- [Reinstate us in Thy love.]{lang="en"}\
  [藉愛將我全復興。]{lang="zh"}
