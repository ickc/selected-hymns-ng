# 1

- [Jesus, Thou Joy of loving hearts,]{lang="en"}\
  [哦主，你是人心之樂，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou Fount of life, Thou Light of men,]{lang="en"}\
  [生命之源，人們之光，]{lang="zh"}
- [From the best bliss that earth imparts,]{lang="en"}\
  [從地所給最佳福樂，]{lang="zh"}
- [We turn unfilled to Thee again.]{lang="en"}\
  [空心轉來向你仰望。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Thy truth unchanged hath ever stood;]{lang="en"}\
  [你的真實永存不變，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou savest those that on Thee call;]{lang="en"}\
  [凡求你者，你都提挈：]{lang="zh"}
- [To them that seek Thee, Thou art good,]{lang="en"}\
  [對尋求者，你是良善，]{lang="zh"}
- [To them that find Thee, all in all!]{lang="en"}\
  [對尋得者，你是一切。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [We taste Thee, O Thou living Bread,]{lang="en"}\
  [生命的糧，我已嘗過，]{lang="zh"}
- [And long to feast upon Thee still;]{lang="en"}\
  [仍是切慕嘗食不已；]{lang="zh"}
- [We drink of Thee, the Fountain-head,]{lang="en"}\
  [活水的泉，我飲雖多，]{lang="zh"}
- [And thirst our souls from Thee to fill!]{lang="en"}\
  [仍是渴望更多飲你。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [Our restless spirits yearn for Thee,]{lang="en"}\
  [當那易變之福改變，]{lang="zh"}
- [Where’er our changeful lot is cast;]{lang="en"}\
  [不安的靈對你渴慕；]{lang="zh"}
- [Glad when Thy gracious smile we see]{lang="en"}\
  [歡喜，因見你的笑臉，]{lang="zh"}
- [Blest, when our faith can hold Thee fast.]{lang="en"}\
  [有福，憑信將你抓住。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [O Lord, be Thou our strength and stay!]{lang="en"}\
  [願你作我力量、維持！]{lang="zh"}
- [Make all our moments calm and bright,]{lang="en"}\
  [使我時時寧靜、明亮，]{lang="zh"}
- [Chase all dark thoughts of sin away,]{lang="en"}\
  [驅盡黑暗罪惡之思，]{lang="zh"}
- [Shed o’er us here Thy holy light.]{lang="en"}\
  [向我倒下你的聖光。]{lang="zh"}
