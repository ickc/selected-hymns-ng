# 1

| Jesus, Jesus, Jesus!
| 耶穌，耶穌，耶穌！
| Sweetest Name on earth;
| 地上最甜名；
| How can I, a sinner,
| 像我這樣罪徒，
| Come to know its worth?
| 竟識其恩情。

# 2

| Oh! the sinful sorrow,
| 哦，我真覺羞愧，
| Oh! the strangest shame,
| 哦，我真懊悔，
| That I saw no beauty
| 從前我竟蒙昧，
| In that sacred Name.
| 不知這名美。

# 3

| Never felt the sweetness!
| 從未覺其甘甜，
| Never knew the grace,
| 從未識恩典，
| Never saw the love-pain
| 從未見祂容顏——
| In that wounded face!
| 顯深切愛憐。

# 4

| Never found the mystery
| 也從未曾領悟，
| In that simple word—
| 這名的豐富；
| Jesus, Jesus, Jesus,
| 耶穌，耶穌，耶穌！
| Savior, Lover, Lord.
| 親愛的救主。

# 5

| Now ’tis past and over.
| 我的虧欠、羞恥，
| Gone my guilt and shame;
| 因主的引領，
| Jesus, Jesus did it,
| 現今都已過去，
| Glory to His Name!
| 榮耀歸祂名！

# 6

| Wonderful compassion,
| 哦，奇妙的大愛，
| Reaching even me;
| 竟然臨及我；
| Bows my humbled spirit
| 我靈謙卑下拜，
| In captivity.
| 作愛的俘虜。

# 7

| Jesus! Jesus! Jesus!
| 耶穌，耶穌，耶穌！
| Loved me in my shame.
| 愛不堪如我。
| Oh! the joy and rapture
| 我今歡欣、鼓舞，
| Of that sacred Name.
| 這名真寬博！
