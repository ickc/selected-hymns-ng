# 1

- [The Lord my shepherd is,]{lang="en"}\
  [是愛的神作我牧人，]{lang="zh"}
- [I shall be well supplied:]{lang="en"}\
  [祂常餧養，祂常施恩；]{lang="zh"}
- [Since He is mine, and I am His,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂是屬我，我是屬祂，]{lang="zh"}
- [What can I want beside?]{lang="en"}\
  [何來需要？何來缺乏？]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [He leads me to the place]{lang="en"}\
  [祂帶我到嫩草地場，]{lang="zh"}
- [Where heav'nly pasture grows,]{lang="en"}\
  [使我感覺滿足、歡暢；]{lang="zh"}
- [Where living waters gently pass]{lang="en"}\
  [又帶我到平靜溪流，]{lang="zh"}
- [And full salvation flows.]{lang="en"}\
  [交通不斷，安息無憂。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [If e'er I go astray,]{lang="en"}\
  [我時迷路，祂來截回，]{lang="zh"}
- [He doth my soul reclaim;]{lang="en"}\
  [再側我心，受祂支配；]{lang="zh"}
- [And guides me in His own right way,]{lang="en"}\
  [我常搖動，不甚堅定，]{lang="zh"}
- [For His most holy name.]{lang="en"}\
  [但祂引導，是為己名。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

- [While He affords His aid,]{lang="en"}\
  [何等不明，何等可疑，]{lang="zh"}
- [I cannot yield to fear;]{lang="en"}\
  [怎能行走無所顧忌？]{lang="zh"}
- [Tho' I should walk thro' death's dark]{lang="en"}\
  [你竿扶持，你杖引領，]{lang="zh"}
- [My Shepherd's with me there.]{lang="en"}\
  [你的同在滿我途徑。]{lang="zh"}

# 5

- [In spite of all my foes,]{lang="en"}\
  [你使我能大膽誇勝，]{lang="zh"}
- [Thou dost my table spread;]{lang="en"}\
  [敵雖四布，享恩如恒；]{lang="zh"}
- [My cup with blessings overflows,]{lang="en"}\
  [我頭油潤，我杯酒溢，]{lang="zh"}
- [And joy exalts my head.]{lang="en"}\
  [我神從來不曾吝惜。]{lang="zh"}

# 6

- [The bounties of Thy love]{lang="en"}\
  [你的甘甜、奇妙的愛，]{lang="zh"}
- [Shall crown my future days;]{lang="en"}\
  [測量我的一生年代；]{lang="zh"}
- [Nor from Thy house will I remove,]{lang="en"}\
  [你愛既然永不改變，]{lang="zh"}
- [Nor cease to speak Thy praise.]{lang="en"}\
  [我的讚美還要加添。]{lang="zh"}
