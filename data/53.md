# 1

- [Join all the glorious names]{lang="en"}\
  [匯集一切權能、]{lang="zh"}
- [Of wisdom, love, and pow’r,]{lang="en"}\
  [智慧、愛的尊名，]{lang="zh"}
- [That mortals ever knew,]{lang="en"}\
  [世人所未曾識，]{lang="zh"}
- [That angels ever bore;:]{lang="en"}\
  [天使也未曾知，]{lang="zh"}
- [All are too mean to speak His worth,]{lang="en"}\
  [無法說出祂的價值，]{lang="zh"}
- [Too mean to set my Savior forth.]{lang="en"}\
  [更難表明救主所是。]{lang="zh"}

# 2

- [Great Prophet of my God,]{lang="en"}\
  [我神的大先知，]{lang="zh"}
- [My tongue would bless Thy name;]{lang="en"}\
  [口頌你名不置；]{lang="zh"}
- [By Thee the joyful news]{lang="en"}\
  [神的救恩佳音，]{lang="zh"}
- [Of our salvation came;]{lang="en"}\
  [藉你傳入我心；]{lang="zh"}
- [The joyful news of sins forgiv’n,]{lang="en"}\
  [罪得赦免，大喜信息，]{lang="zh"}
- [Of hell subdued, and peace with heaven.]{lang="en"}\
  [與神和好，我心安息。]{lang="zh"}

# 3

- [Jesus, my great High Priest,]{lang="en"}\
  [耶穌是大祭司，]{lang="zh"}
- [Offered His blood, and died;]{lang="en"}\
  [獻上己血、受死；]{lang="zh"}
- [My guilty conscience seeks]{lang="en"}\
  [我的定罪良心，]{lang="zh"}
- [No sacrifice beside]{lang="en"}\
  [無需別的犧牲：]{lang="zh"}
- [His pow’rful blood did once atone,]{lang="en"}\
  [一次流血，救贖完全，]{lang="zh"}
- [And now it pleads before the throne.]{lang="en"}\
  [神前為我說出美言。]{lang="zh"}

# 4

  [祂的聲音招呼，]{lang="zh"}
- [His watchful eye shall keep]{lang="en"}\
  [祂的眼目看顧，]{lang="zh"}
- [My wand’ring soul among]{lang="en"}\
  [在祂萬千羊中，]{lang="zh"}
- [The thousands of His sheep:]{lang="en"}\
  [關心我的行蹤；]{lang="zh"}
- [He feeds His flock, He calls their names,]{lang="en"}\
  [祂常餵養，按名呼喚，]{lang="zh"}
- [His bosom bears the tender lambs.]{lang="en"}\
